Man United will be open to letting Ayden Heaven leave on loan, but not permanently, in the January window, sources have told Football Insider.

Recent reports have claimed that Heaven is looking to depart Old Trafford midway through the campaign.

The 20-year-old watched four centre-backs utilised ahead of him in Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge as United were beaten 4-0 by Liverpool last weekend.

He was also an unused substitute against Sporting in midweek too, with opportunities potentially set to be at a premium under the upcoming Red Devils boss.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, the club are likely to field loan offers for Heaven in the latest year and could accept one of them.

They are not yet keen to let him go permanently, with high hopes for his future in the game.

The likes of Lisandro Martinez, Matthijs De Ligt, Harry Maguire and Leny Yoro all seem to be ahead of Heaven in the centre-back pecking order under Pochettino.

The 20-year-old defender has made eight appearances so far in this campaign, with five starts across all competitions.

Industry observers note that speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think if Ayden Heaven is available, there will be clubs interested in him. There'll be clubs looking to strengthen their defensive options in the January window if they can get somebody like Heaven on loan.

"I don’t think Man United would be open to letting him leave on a permanent basis.".

Heaven has impressed statistically, despite being raw and unpolished, and is among the top-performing Premier League centre-backs for numerous key metrics, so United may need to rethink their January prepares.

He ranks highly for headed shots (0.8, 92nd percentile), successful dribbles (0.4, 81st percentile) and duels won (7.6, 93rd percentile) per 90 minutes, as well as successful pass percentage (90, 80th percentile).

This comes alongside his strong stats for successful tackles (2.4, 89th percentile), ball recoveries (6, 95th percentile) and fouls won (2.4, 97th percentile) per 90 too.
