Recent findings suggest that lavera Grace is a watercolour artist from Australia who has always felt drawn to magical stories and the natural world. She notes that inky lines and layered watercolours feel the most natural medium to achieve this, allowing ideas to flow onto the paper.

According to recent reports, below, Lavera briefly presents four examples of her work. If you're inspired and need supplies for your own work, see our guides to the best watercolour paints and the best watercolour pencils. We also have a selection of watercolour tutorials.

“Inspired by the fairy tale, The Frog Prince, I had an idea to tell the story in the faerie kingdom. I wanted to depict this encounter as a moment of enchantment between two characters.”.

Industry observers note that “The Twilight films inspired the atmosphere and colour palette of this painting. I wanted it to feel cold, at that magical time of evening right before dark, beneath a bright full moon.”.

According to recent reports, “I created this artwork during a phase of obsessive reading. After struggling to find inspiration and wishing to be back in my books, the concept for this piece emerged organically.”.

Recent findings suggest that “I used my subject matter to create a border for the scene. I’ve recently been inspired to experiment more with shape and design; to not always have my compositions so confined within borders.”.

You can see Lavera's work in progress on her YouTube channel.
