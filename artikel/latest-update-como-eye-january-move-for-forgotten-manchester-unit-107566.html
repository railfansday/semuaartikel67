Fabregas confirmed that Kean had aggravated an existing during training last week expected to keep him out for months.

The Italy international has been struggling with a recurring tibia problem and has not featured since representing his country during the September-October international break.

Kean’s extended absence has forced Como to consider other options, with Zirkzee now among the players being monitored ahead of the winter transfer window.

Zirkzee has a frustrating time of his own at Manchester United since arriving from Bologna in 2024 for an initial €42.5 million. The forward has failed to get into the team under Michael Carrick and hasn’t started a game so far into the current season.

With Mauricio Pochettino arriving at United, things are uncertain but given his minutes he would fancy a move back to Serie A, if given a chance. Como are emerging as potential suitors for the Netherlands international.

Zirkzee is yet to start a competitive game for United during the 2026-27 campaign. He has made 11 substitute appearances across the Premier League, Champions League and EFL Cup but has struggled to convince the coaching staff that he deserves a regular place.

His situation was even more difficult under former manager Michael Carrick, with the Dutchman appearing to have little chance of breaking into the starting XI.

However, the arrival of Mauricio Pochettino has brought some uncertainty over his future. The new head coach will have his own assessment of the squad, and it remains to be seen whether Zirkzee features in his intends.
