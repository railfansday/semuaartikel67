Canva Has a Fresh Kill Switch for AI: ‘We Know AI Doesn’t Suit Every Task’

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, it’s a great day for designers who hate AI. Canva, the online design and publishing platform, revealed on Thursday that it’s introducing two fresh settings to opt out of seeing and using AI tools. One lets you turn off AI tools in Canva, and the other lets you filter out AI-created content from Canva’s library of templates and other elements.

Recent findings suggest that the settings are available now for free and paying people globally, except for those in India, the Philippines and Indonesia. Canva administrators for business and team prepares can enable the two new settings for their entire organization.

It has been revealed that to turn off Canva’s AI, go to your Profile > Settings > Preferences and toggle Enable generative AI features on or off. To filter out AI in your Canva library, go to Profile > Your account > AI personalization and enable the feature that notes show less AI-generated content.

Sources indicate that it’s hard not to compare Canva’s new opt-out features to Adobe, which spent all of last week at Adobe Max trying to convince creative professionals to embrace its AI tools. Adobe’s chief technology officer told CNET that he thought the problem was slop, not AI.

According to recent reports, as criticisms of AI mount, it’s smart, albeit rare, to see a tech manufacturer give its users a definitive, one-click solution to filter out AI tools. We will have to put Canva’s settings to the test to see how effectively they work, though.

Sources indicate that if you do want to use AI, Canva has no shortage. Its magic layers tool lets you break apart an AI-generated image into editable layers, a hugely helpful capability for fixing errors and customizing content. Its foundational models, along with other third-party models, can help you generate new image, video and audio content. Outside of graphic design, Canva has added AI tools for spreadsheet, document and presentation work over the past year. And like every other tech company, Canva claims it aims to continue investing in AI.
