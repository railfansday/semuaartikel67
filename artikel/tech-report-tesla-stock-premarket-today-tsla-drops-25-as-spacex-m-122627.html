📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that tesla shares fell 2.51% in premarket trading on Wednesday to $375.03, down $9.65 from Tuesday’s close of $384.68, as investors weighed the details emerging around a possible merger with SpaceX. Tesla board chair Robyn Denholm, Kathleen Wilson-Thompson and Jack Hartung are leading Tesla’s side of the talks, while Roelof Botha and Randy Glein represent SpaceX, the Financial Times reported. Advisers are now working on the exchange ratio, which will decide how much of the combined firm each set of shareholders owns. Texas governance rules keep Elon Musk out of active negotiations, since he runs both firms. Musk holds about 84% of SpaceX’s voting power and about 20% at Tesla, and the deal could hand him majority control. Approval would need more than half of all eligible Tesla votes, with abstentions counting against it. Short seller Jim Chanos warned a merger could reduce SpaceX’s projected earnings per share by nearly 40%. J.P. Morgan remarked a deal could speed up SpaceX’s entry into the S&P 500, with index funds potentially buying about 10% of its tradable shares.
