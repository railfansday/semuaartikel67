📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The BBC reports that Arion Kurtaj, who hacked Rockstar and dumped a massive trove of content from Grand Theft Auto 6 more than a year before it was formally revealed, has pleaded guilty to that and other hacks carried out while he was a member of the Lapsus$ hacking group.

Kurtaj's hack of Rockstar was notable not for the GTA 6 infodump that followed, but because of how it was carried out: With an Amazon Fire Stick, a mobile phone, and a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse, connected to a TV in a Travelodge hotel room where he was being held under police protection because of threats against him made by rival hackers over stolen bitcoin.

According to recent reports, A 2023 assessment found Kurtaj had "acute autism" and was determined to return to "cybercrime" as soon as possible, despite the fact that he was literally standing trial for doing cybercrimes. Because of that, the jury was asked to determine if he had committed the hacks (it found he had) but not whether he had done so with criminal intent; he was subsequently sentenced to an indefinite stay at a secure hospital, where he would remain unless and until doctors determined that he was no longer a danger.

It has been revealed that as we noted at the time, "danger" might seem a bit of a strong word for someone who leaked videogame footage, but the Lapsus$ group had also hacked Microsoft, Samsung, Nvidia and UK telecom company BT&EE, and stole millions in cryptocurrency from individuals. Kurtaj was also reportedly violent while in hospital custody, causing "dozens" of reports of injuries or property damage. In July 2026, that 'no longer a danger' determination was presumably made, as Kurtaj was released from hospital and moved to prison to await retrial.

Industry observers note that he has now pleaded guilty to multiple hacks carried out when he was 16 and 17 years old, including the Rockstar hack that led to the GTA 6 leak. He also pleaded guilty to assaults on workers while in custody, which the BBC report says included "multiple biting incidents.".

As I mentioned when Kurtaj was moved from hospital to prison, the whole thing strikes me as odd. The major question is how Kurtaj's state of mind at the time of the crimes will influence the outcome of his guilty plea, given that a previous court determined that he needed multiple years of institutionalization. BBC tech reporter Joe Tidy, who's been following the case since Kurtaj's transfer, also described it as "an unusual move" in his report.

According to recent reports, coincidentially, one must assume, Grand Theft Auto 6 debuts for consoles on November 19. Kurtaj will be sentenced at a future date. The leaker who goes by Cyberleek, who recently distributed several GTA 6 gameplay videos apparently taken with a development build of the game, remains at large.
