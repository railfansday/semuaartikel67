According to recent reports, CEO Josh D’Amaro highlighted a Fortnite tie-in as a prime example of how Disney can connect theme parks to its digital business. The manufacturer's annual filing reveals a larger stake in Epic Games in fiscal 2026.

Disney’s stake in Fortnite studio Epic Games grew in fiscal 2026 as the company works to link digital play to real-world visits.

Language in Disney’s annual filing says its ownership in Epic Games increased from 8% to 10% during the fiscal year that ended early October, although it’s unclear what drove the increase and whether Disney made an additional investment.

In a recent development, the filing lists Disney’s Epic Games stake at $2.2 billion as of Oct. 3, up from $1.6 billion a year earlier. Its cash flow statement shows $548 million in net “acquisitions and purchase of investments” for the year, though it doesn’t break out how much, if any, went to Epic.

Disney did not respond to a request for comment in time for publication.
