EXCLUSIVE: Mosquito Coast star Melissa George is leading a BBC and BritBox detective series set around Scotland’s most famous mystery.

The Loch Ness Mysteries also stars Jamie Sives (Dept Q) and Ashley Storrie (Dinosaur).

Industry observers note that george is DCI Vanessa ‘Nessie’ Keen, a provocative London detective whose life is thrown off course when a brush with death leaves her signed off work and persona non grata, until a peculiar personal mystery summons her to the shores of Loch Ness. From Scotland Yard to Scotland, Nessie arrives in the remote Highland district of Monaruadh, only to find herself drawn into a dark and perplexing case alongside local detective DS Rory Ramsay (Sives), whose knowledge of the idiosyncratic local community prove as valuable as Nessie’s razor-sharp instincts. Despite their different approaches and clashes, the two have an undeniable chemistry, forming a duo alongside PC Donna Duff (Storrie). Further casting will be announced in due course. Recent findings suggest that the six-part series derives its name from the notorious monster, also known as Nessie, a mythical creature in Scottish folklore mentioned to inhabit the Scottish Highlands. It is created and led by writer Ed Sellek (Sister Boniface Mysteries) and comes from Deborah Sathe’s nascent BBC Studios-backed label CliffEdge Entertainment, which is already making The Detection Club for the BBC and BritBox. Watch on Deadline.

George is an Australian star of Apple TV’s The Mosquito Coast, ABC’s Alias and NBC’s The Slap. She was Golden Globe nominated for playing Laura Hill in HBO’s In Treatment.

George noted: “Rarely do stories like this come across, with a brilliant and developed character like Nessie. I was immediately drawn to this world and it has been a joy to have a hand in shaping it. I cannot wait to step into these shoes and get on set.”.

Sellek added: “I couldn’t be more stoked for the wickedly talented cast and crew we’re assembling for this show. It’s a murderer’s row of great performers, writers and a production team that’s second-to-none. I think we’re building something really special here.”.

The Loch Ness Mysteries (6×48) was commissioned by Louise Thornton for the BBC and Jon Farrar for BritBox. George is an EP on the show outside the UK and Ireland. Other EPs are Gavin Smith for the BBC, Sathe and Sarah Creasey for CliffEdge and Stephen Nye for BritBox. The six-part series is created and led by Sellek with additional episodes written by Scottish writers Ciara Conway (The Teacher 3) and Niki Rooney (Malory Towers). Katherine Churcher (Grantchester) is directing the first block of filming with subsequent episodes directed by Brian Kelly (No Way Off) and Ryan Graham (River City). The series producer is Kevin McComiskie, known for his work on River City.

According to recent reports, BBC Studios is handling global sales. Filming will begin before the end of year around the Luss and Glasgow areas.

George is repped by Conway Van Gelder Grant, Inspire Entertainment and GGSSC.
