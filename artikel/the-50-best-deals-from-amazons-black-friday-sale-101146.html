In the good old days, Amazon’s Black Friday sale was one simple (albeit chaotic) day a year; now it’s a 12-day affair with an interesting name, the “Turkey 12” ( for the 12 days between now and Cyber Monday, but I digress...). But is it worth shopping the retailer’s sale now or waiting until after Thanksgiving? As someone whose day job is shopping on Amazon, I’d suggest getting a jump on things so you can spend the holiday weekend playing GTA6 and watching sports.

Shop Amazon’s Black Friday Sale.

In a recent development, here’s the deal: Most items you’re after are probably already discounted. Apple tech, art TVs (including the Samsung Frame), several respectable designer watches, and our all-time favorite T-shirt are already majorly marked down, so you don’t have to wait on those. Bookmark anything that’s still full-price, then take a quick peek on Cyber Monday to see if there are any last-minute markdowns.

Apple doesn’t host a Black Friday sale, so Amazon markdowns tend to sell out quickly. In years past, I’ve seen discounted AirPods and Apple Watches go out of stock before Thanksgiving. Apple AirTags and the Apple Watch Series 12 and SE 3 are currently the cheapest they’ve ever been, so shop those deals now if they’re the items you’re after. I’m surprised (and slightly annoyed) that the AirPods 5 are still full price, but it’s nice to see the already-cheap MacBook Neo on sale for the first time.
