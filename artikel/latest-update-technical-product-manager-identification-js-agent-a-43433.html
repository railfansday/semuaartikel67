Fingerprint empowers enterprises to detect and stop online fraud with the world’s most accurate device intelligence. We lead our industry with bleeding-edge identification capabilities and work on turning latest ideas and discoveries in the fraud detection space into reality. Our customers range from innovative startups to leading enterprise companies, including Plaid, Dropbox, and Booking.com.

According to recent reports, fingerprint is a globally dispersed, 100% remote company. We were named on on the 2026 Forbes Best Startup Employers list and ranked #803 on the 2026 Inc. 5000 list of America’s fastest-growing private companies.

We have raised $77M and are backed by Craft Ventures (Tesla, Facebook, Airbnb ), Nexus Venture Partners ( Postman, Apollo.io, MinIO, Druva) and Uncorrelated Ventures ( Redis, Rollbar,  Gradle).

Sources indicate that fingerprint is looking for a Technical Product Manager (P3) to help turn product strategy into clear, well-executed product delivery. This role is ideal for someone who is building strong product management fundamentals, enjoys working closely with engineering and design, and is energized by keeping product work organized, well-documented, and moving toward release.

Sources indicate that in this role, you will own specific product features or workstreams under the guidance of a Sr Technical Product Manager. You will help translate product intent into clear user stories, jobs-to-be-done, acceptance criteria, release aims, documentation, and operational updates. You will work across Product, Engineering, Design, Product Analytics, Customer Success, Sales, and Product Marketing to ensure product records are accurate, dependencies are visible, stakeholders are informed, and shipped work meets the expected customer and business outcomes.

This role blends TPM expectations with the Delivery Product Owner operating model: the Sr TPM sets product direction, priorities, success metrics, and major tradeoffs; the TPM helps ensure that direction becomes shippable, well-understood, and well-supported product increments.
