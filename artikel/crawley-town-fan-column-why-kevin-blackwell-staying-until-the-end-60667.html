Industry observers note that so, Crawley Town have made two substantial announcements: first, that Kevin Blackwell will be staying on as interim manager until the end of the season, and second, that the owners have released an open letter addressing fans’ concerns about the club. Here are my thoughts on both.

Kevin Blackwell staying on is the right call.

Blackwell staying at the club until the end of the season doesn’t really surprise me, as there have been plenty of signs that he would be staying a lot longer than just a couple of games.

From the lack of news about a latest manager, to the short-term deals the club has made for players like Connor Wickham and Ayman Benarous, those aren't the kind of signings you make for a manager who is about to leave.

Recent findings suggest that also, keeping him until the end of the season makes sense. It gives the club the stability we desperately need while also buying us time to find the right long-term permanent manager, and we have looked a lot better since he came into the club, so this feels like a good move to me.

The Owners’ Open Letter: A step in the right direction.

In a recent development, the other massive development was that the owners have released an open letter on the club website. This was much needed, as there has been silence from the club and the owners since the departures of manager Colin Kazim-Richards and director of football Erdem Konyar last month, and this has understandably frustrated a lot of fans.

In the letter, they do acknowledge that mistakes have been made, and for me, that’s a good start. They are saying the right things and it shows that the owners are listening to supporters’ concerns. But actions speak louder than words, and after so many poor decisions as of late, it will take a lot more than a letter to rebuild trust, with only time will tell whether these words are hollow or not.

Sources indicate that overall, I see both announcements as a step in the right direction, as they give more clarity over the team's management and more openness from the owners. Hopefully, this is a turning point, though the results and decisions that follow will show whether it truly is, and hopefully that starts on Saturday against Fleetwood.
