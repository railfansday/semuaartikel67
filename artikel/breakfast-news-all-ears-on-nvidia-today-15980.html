For 33 years, Jensen Huang was Nvidia (NVDA -0.63%). Today after the market closes, the manufacturer reports its fiscal 2027 third-quarter results, followed by an earnings call led by Colette Kress, interim CEO and CFO.

Industry observers note that the backward-looking figures still matter, but will only serve as a closing tally of the Huang era. The call will be Nvidia’s real post-founder stability test. Here’s what we’ll be watching out for:.

Sources indicate that nvidia’s business is excellent, but the stock now carries three open questions: who leads, how revenue is reported, and what the investigation finds. Wait for answers before making your next move.

The Nasdaq fell 2.49% yesterday. The S&P 500 wasn’t far behind, slipping 1.92%.

Weeks like this tempt investors to do one of two things. Some sell a long-held winner (e.g. Nvidia) because the headlines look scary. Others rush into the hottest new name (like Anthropic). Either move can matter as much as which stocks you own.

That’s why the Rule Breakers approach begins with behavior. This week, two of the six habits deserve extra attention.

Invest. For At Least Three Years.

It has been revealed that picture two investors who bought Garmin (GRMN -0.40%) on March 17, 2022. That’s the day Team Rule Breakers recommended it in Stock Advisor, at $111.93. Within months, sales started slipping. The stock fell to the low $80s. It didn’t climb back above $111.93 until late 2023. The first investor gave up during the slump and sold. The second gave the business time. Today, the shares trade around $283. The second investor’s patience paid off.

Sitting through the slump was only half the job. By late 2024, Garmin had roughly doubled from its recommendation price. That’s when many investors sell half to “play with the house’s money.” Anyone who cashed out at the double, around $224, have missed about another 25% since. Massive winners rarely rise in a straight line. Letting one run means sitting through the swings.

According to recent reports, you can’t control how Nvidia’s report lands or how Anthropic’s shares trade on day one. But you can choose how you respond.

Nvidia itself shows why that matters. It has been a standout pick for both Team Rule Breakers and Team Hidden Gems over the years. Hidden Gems’ May 2024 recommendation is still about 25 percentage points ahead of the S&P 500, despite the recent slide. Investors who let it run have been well rewarded.

Sources indicate that every Garmin investor in this story owned a good pick, too. Their results came down to what they did next.

The company’s AI-powered Falcon threat intelligence platform works to detect and prevent breaches before they start.

Given the prevalence of AI-powered threats, the tech giant’s strong industry position, and continued impressive growth, we believe there’s a long runway of growth ahead for CrowdStrike.
