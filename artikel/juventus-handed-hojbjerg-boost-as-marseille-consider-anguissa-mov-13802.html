La Gazzetta dello Sport reports that Marseille’s Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg remains a primary midfield target for Juventus, with the Bianconeri’s hopes boosted by Marseille’s interest in Napoli’s André-Frank Zambo Anguissa.

Juventus will look for further reinforcements in the January transfer window, and according to Gazzetta, Marseille midfielder Hojbjerg remains high on the Old Lady’s agenda.

The Dane is a long-time target of the Bianconeri, who have already held talks with his club in previous transfer windows.

This time, however, Juventus’ hopes of signing the ex-Tottenham man seem more concrete than ever, as Marseille have already identified an ideal replacement.

According to Gazzetta, the French side is interested in Napoli’s Anguissa, who has already played for Marseille and is eager to leave the Stadio Maradona in January.

Anguissa’s contract expires in June 2027, but talks with the Partenopei are stalling, prompting the Cameroon international to consider leaving six months before his deal ends.

According to Gazzetta, Angussa is open to returning to Marseille, but his mid-season transfer would also require Napoli’s green light.

Juventus are dealing with an injury crisis, especially in midfield, as Khephren Thuram and Manuel Locatelli have been unavailable for several months and are not expected to return before 2027.

Locatelli in particular won’t return to action until February or March next year, so the Bianconeri will need to sign a new central midfielder during the winter transfer window.
