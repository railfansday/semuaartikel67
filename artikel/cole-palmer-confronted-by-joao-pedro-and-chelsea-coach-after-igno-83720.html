Cole Palmer was involved in a heated exchange with Joao Pedro after Chelsea's disappointing 4-1 home defeat by Leeds United.

Xabi Alonso's side were beaten at Stamford Bridge on Saturday, with Leeds scoring three times in the second half to secure a memorable, and very rare, victory in the capital.

Emmanuel Emegha opened the scoring for Chelsea after 25 minutes, but Noah Okafor equalised three minutes later.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin put Leeds ahead eight minutes into the second half before Okafor added his second of the afternoon. Brenden Aaronson then sealed the win with a fine solo effort in stoppage time, moving Leeds above Chelsea in the Premier League table.

Joao Pedro was absent from the Chelsea squad as he continues to recover from a thigh injury which has kept him out of the club's past five matches, but in attendance at Stamford Bridge on Saturday and appeared to become involved in a tense exchange with Palmer after the final whistle.

According to recent reports, footage shared on social media showed Palmer approaching his team-mate, covering his mouth with his hand as he spoke. Joao Pedro then placed his arms on Palmer's shoulders and shrugged in response, before Palmer walked away looking frustrated.

Willie Isa, a player support officer at Chelsea, can also be seen trying to talk to Palmer, with the Daily Mail reporting that the player did not acknowledge the home fans after the heavy defeat. The same publication claims Palmer instead walked down the tunnel after his exchange with Joao Pedro.

Asked about the incident after the game, Alonso mentioned: “We were disappointed, the fans were disappointed because the expectations from them are high and we feel sorry for them and we need to go through together and to find solutions to that and now, as I said, for sure it’s not the easiest moment.

“It’s a fragile moment but we need to work together and stick together because there is still so long way to go.”.
