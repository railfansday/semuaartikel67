Atmos Rewards is the loyalty program of Alaska and Hawaiian, and that includes three co-branded credit cards. There’s only one business credit card in the portfolio, and that’s the Atmos™ Rewards Visa Signature® Business Card.

According to recent reports, along those lines, there’s a major upgrade, as the card has just undergone a full refresh. The bad news is that the card’s annual fee has gone up considerably (at least for most people), and the benefits have also changed. Let’s take a look, because as you’d expect, the changes are a mixed bag.

The good news is that there’s a huge incentive to apply for the card, given the massive limited-time welcome bonus of 90,000 Atmos Rewards points after spending $6,000 within the first 90 days.

Link: Learn more about the Atmos™ Rewards Visa Signature® Business Card.

Sources indicate that the Atmos Rewards Business Card is undergoing some major updates. This includes a higher annual fee, new rewards structure, and new set of perks. However, these changes only apply to new card members, as existing card members will keep the old benefits (and fee).

It has been revealed that let me emphasize that the card has a huge limited time welcome bonus of 90,000 Atmos Rewards points after spending $6,000 within the first 90 days. So that’s a major incentive to apply for the card, if you’re eligible. I value Atmos Rewards points at 1.5 cents each, so to me, that’s worth $1,350.

With that out of the way, let’s go over the details….

According to recent reports, the Atmos Rewards Business Card now has a flat $195 annual fee, which covers the primary card member and any employee cards. Previously, the card had an annual fee of $70 per company, plus $25 per employee, so the minimum annual fee you’d pay would be $95.

It goes without saying that this change represents a significant increase in the annual fee, unless you had more than four employee credit cards on your account.

According to recent reports, what has changed here is the new 10x points for booking through Atmos for Business (great for those who can use it), as well as a full overhaul of the 2x points categories. Previously the card offered 2x points on eligible gas, EV charging stations, shipping, and local transit purchases (including rideshare), while now it just offers 2x points on dining. As before, the card offers one status point for every $3 spent, with no cap.

The Atmos Rewards Business Card is picking up quite a few new benefits that it didn’t previously offer:.

The refreshed card also keeps a first checked bag free and preferred boarding on Alaska and Hawaiian flights for the card member and up to six guests on the same reservation when the trip is paid with the card, a 20% rebate on eligible inflight purchases, a 10% rewards bonus for cardholders with an eligible Bank of America small business account, and no foreign transaction fees.

Let me emphasize that the card will no longer offer a $99 companion fare, so that’s being replaced by the new 50% off vouchers that can be earned with spending.

As of November 18, 2026, customers who apply for a new Atmos Rewards Business Card will be receiving the refreshed card, with the new benefits and fee.

It has been revealed that applications submitted before November 18 will continue under the prior card offering. What’s interesting is that current cardholders will keep their existing benefits, and will not be automatically moved over. However, you can call up Bank of America if you’d like to see about the opportunity of switching.

As of now, it sounds like there are no intends to transition people to the newly introduced system, so existing card members shouldn’t be negatively impacted by this.
