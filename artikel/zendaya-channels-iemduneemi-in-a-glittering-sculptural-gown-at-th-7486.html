According to recent reports, zendaya is back on the awards show circuit in a glittering vintage gown meant to kick off her Dune: Part Three press tour. The actress attended this evening’s Governors Awards in a sculptural look from Giles Deacon’s fall/winter 2006 collection. She accessorized with a vintage Rolex watch and jewelry from Chopard.

Her stylist, Law Roach, wrote on Instagram that it’s her first Dune press tour look. The dress is even more stunning in motion:.

The star posed alone, though her husband, Tom Holland, was at the event, too.

It marks her first awards show appearance since the Emmys in September, where she wore a glitzy Prada gown.

According to recent reports, dune: Part Three is the last of Zendaya’s five projects to come out this year. She first promoted The Drama and Euphoria season 3, which had April release dates, then The Odyssey and Spider-Man: Brand Fresh Day, which debuted in July. In her ELLE Summer Issue cover story, she acknowledged it’s been a jam-packed 2026 with all the press tours.

“It’s going to be a long year, but what’s great is that I’m really proud of everything. I just really love my job,” she stated. “But I think that after this, I’m going to have to take a little bit of a break. [Though] as soon as I take a break, I start to go crazy. And I’m like, ‘I need to work.’”.

Industry observers note that she also recalled filming all the projects and juggling the roles. “I remember being on set for Euphoria; it was a night shoot at a ranch,” she mentioned. “I was so tired, but I was also learning my Chakobsa lines for Dune. And then I started writing out my lines to memorize for that quick turnaround trip I was going to make to Iceland [for The Odyssey]. It’s not like I have a lot of lines in The Odyssey, but I was working with Christopher Nolan! The most embarrassing thing in life would be messing up my lines, which did happen once.”.

The Odyssey director Christopher Nolan, meanwhile, had nothing but praise for Zendaya’s performance, telling ELLE, “We would be in the maddest, craziest situations, just all of us fighting the elements, tearing our hair out, all these things going on, and she would sort of parachute in from her other job with this sense of true grace and poise.”.
