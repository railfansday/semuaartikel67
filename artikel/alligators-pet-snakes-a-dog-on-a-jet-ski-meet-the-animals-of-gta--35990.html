Industry observers note that grand Theft Auto VI launches Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, and as the game unlocks around the world, players are already sharing clips of their first encounters with the local wildlife.

Here's what we know about the animals in GTA 6 so far, and what you can do when you find them.

According to recent reports, the wildlife in Leonida gets pretty specific to Florida. There are the animals you'd probably expect, like flamingos, manatees, and alligators, plus a few that might surprise you, like green iguanas, cane toads, and rhesus macaques. Yes, Florida really does have monkeys. And if you head up to Mount Kalaga, it’s worth stopping to see what’s going on around you. One of Rockstar's screenshots highlights a cougar stalking a group of deer, so mind your surroundings.

Recent findings suggest that so far, people have spotted everything from armadillos, beavers, and raccoons to black bears, bobcats, and boars. There are also foxes, otters, skunks, squirrels, frogs, turtles, and snakes, plus cows, horses, cats, and dogs. And if you're the kind of person who stops to pet every dog you pass, there are already reports of Australian shepherds, Chihuahuas, Dobermans, German shepherds, Labradors, and Rottweilers.

There's plenty to look for along the water, too. Think pelicans, herons, and spoonbills, along with ducks, gulls, cormorants, cranes, and those flamingos. Eagles, pigeons, storks, and woodpeckers have also turned up. In the water itself, sightings range from crayfish, eels, and jellyfish to dolphins, sea turtles, manta rays, hammerhead sharks, and whales. If you go scuba diving off the Leonida Keys, some of the marine life even glows.

According to recent reports, A few sightings are still up for debate. GTABase lists possums and rats, for example, while IGN left both off its list.

If you pass by an adorable cat or dog, you can pet both. Petting a cat actually gives Jason a small focus boost, and if a dog's owner gives you permission, you can give it a treat. Some gas stations sell pet treats, according to The Guardian, and you also have the option to praise or scold either animal.

It has been revealed that you can also run into a cat while stealing a car. In one clip, Jason drives off, hears loud meowing, and suddenly has a cat lunging at him. A timed button prompt lets him fend it off, and from there, you can pull over and let the cat out or keep driving. As one user put it, "Cats Are Now Gangsters.".

Apparently, dogs in Leonida like jet skis, too. One player in a post on X swam out to see how far they could get from shore and found a dog sitting on a jet ski that users can ride with. How it got out there is anybody's guess.

In a recent development, back on dry land, Jason has a pet of his own. Dolores, a snake, lives at his house in the Leonida Keys, and feeding her a mouse gives him a small focus boost. So far, she's the only pet Jason can keep in the story, according to Cultured Vultures.

You can also keep track of the animals you see. Open the weapon wheel, select your binoculars, and zoom in on an animal. Then hold R1 on PlayStation or RB on Xbox to add it to your wildlife logbook. There are 62 types of birds to spot, as we covered in our GTA 6 birdwatching guide, and different breeds of cats and dogs get their own entries. Studying any 100 of the 255 entries counts toward 100 percent completion, according to PowerPyx.

There's also a zoo to visit, and you can go fishing or hunting. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told YouTuber TGG that fishing is catch-and-release, so every fish goes back in the water, even during a challenge, as GameSpot reported.

Hunting is a way to earn money. Jason's old Army friend Bret Vogel teaches him how to track deer before sending him after other animals. Some hunting challenges spell out exactly what to look for. According to the outlet, some send players after a boar, a rattlesnake, and a panther.

In a recent development, one challenge, the Legendary Hunter challenge, asks players to hunt eight unique animals, whose identities are still under wraps.
