By LEWIS STEELE, FOOTBALL REPORTER.

Published: 02:00 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 05:29 EST, 23 November 2026.

A Liverpool manager wearing a toothy grin and black puffer jacket as he lapped up the adulation of Anfield with three fist bumps in front of the Kop… where have we seen that before?

Andoni Iraola may not look as natural at the grand gestures or possess the aura of his predecessor Jurgen Klopp just yet – but this still felt like a landmark day for the Basque manager.

Playing a heavy-metal style of football that Klopp would be proud of, Iraola has impressed in his early months at Liverpool but craved a real scalp against a rival to turbocharge the feeling among the fanbase and give his tenure true lift-off.

This was the finest day of the fledgling latest era as Iraola’s relentless Reds were unstoppable and humiliated their biggest rivals – and the boss must be rubbing his hands at his depth of attacking weapons.

Rio Ngumoha, unbelievably still just 18, was the pick of the bunch and United simply could not get near him, while Florian Wirtz put in his best performance in a Reds shirt and Dominik Szoboszlai looked back to his breathtaking best.

Szoboszlai started the rout with a devastating free-kick and swung an imaginary golf club in celebration, then pretended to watch his tee shot fly into the Anfield Road Stand.

Andoni Iraola is playign a style of football that Jurgen Klopp would be proud of at Liverpool.

Sticking on the Hungarian’s theme, the gulf in quality between these two teams felt like that of a professional golfer compared to a rookie who can barely get the ball off the driving range.

Szoboszlai got his driver out to score a thumping strike and continue his role as the Premier League’s best dead-ball specialist – and Liverpool never looked back from there.

All the talk centred around Mauricio Pochettino’s soft side and the embarrassing sequence of events that led to Lenny Yoro’s early red card but let’s take nothing away from Liverpool, who were breathtaking and unstoppable.

It has been revealed that the fact the score was only 4-0 flattered United. To be picky and find criticism in feedback of Liverpool, they were wasteful. This could have been five, six, seven, you name it.

Industry observers note that ngumoha was unplayable. The England international was only four when United won the last of their league titles and Sir Alex Ferguson departed, so he will not remember this team being competent. But the teenager knew the significance of his performance in this fixture.

Industry observers note that his assist for Florian Wirtz was eye-catching, his goal to make it 3-0 deserved after a lung-busting run off the ball. Poor Diogo Dalot will have nightmares for days about Ngumoha’s dancing feet as the Liverpool winger skipped past him time and again.

There were times when Ngumoha played the wrong pass which is the most frightening thing for rivals. This kid is far from the finished product. He will only get better and can be one of the best forwards in the game in a year or so.

He is level-headed unlike some other egotistical young stars and he plays with a smile on his face. Why wouldn’t he?

Rio Ngumoha was unplayable against Manchester United at Anfield and will only get better.

In a recent development, liverpool will pay a maximum of £6.8million for him, based on add-ons after poaching him from Chelsea two years ago. In the week of Grand Theft Auto IV’s release, the heist of Ngumoha feels like the grandest theft of all (though he is only just old enough to play the video game!).

Those around him are all £100million signings but his ceiling is just as high as that of Wirtz, Alexander Isak and Bradley Barcola.

According to recent reports, it helps that all four clicked in this game. Wirtz has been excellent this season but has not performed in the bigger fixtures. Conversations after losses to Manchester City and Arsenal centred around Wirtz not showing up.

According to recent reports, the German was everywhere here and United were left to chase shadows. It is not an exaggeration to label this his best Liverpool performance, with an easy-on-the-eye goal and some moments of creative genius.

Barcola destroyed United with his pace, Szoboszlai was a constant menace, while Isak did everything right until it came to finishing several attacks.

And that is the thing that will excite the head coach and should leave rival fans quaking in their boots. This team is full of virtuoso match-winners but all of them have plenty of room to improve. If they do that and all fulfil their potential, the sky is the limit for Iraola’s Liverpool.
