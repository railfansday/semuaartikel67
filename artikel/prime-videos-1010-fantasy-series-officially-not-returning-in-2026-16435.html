Prime Video's most divisive fantasy release of the year arrived less than a week ago, and its four-episode premiere has already propelled the series to the top of the streaming charts. Set five years after Season 2, The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power Season 3 explores the height of the war between the Elves and the Dark Lord Sauron. The season is set to run through November 25, making it Prime Video's final fantasy release of the year. As a result, no other original fantasy titles will debut on the streamer this year, including one of its most underrated.

Sources indicate that set in the fantasy universe of The Legend of Vox Machina, this series takes place about 20 years later, though you don't need to watch The Legend of Vox Machina to follow it. Titled The Mighty Nein, it centers on a completely different, independent group of outcasts and morally grey characters, adapted from Critical Role's second tabletop campaign. Now, one year after its memorable Season 1 finale, the animated series has finally gotten a Season 2 premiere date, giving fans plenty to look forward to as they return to Exandria.

Industry observers note that unfortunately, The Mighty Nein won't return this year. According to TV Insider, the award-winning series will officially return for its second season on March 24, 2027, with all eight latest episodes dropping at once. The news was revealed on November 15, 2026, during TwitchCon and a special actual-play one-shot titled The Rings of Power: A Middle-Earth One-Shot. The event featured Critical Role cast members Sam Riegel, Travis Willingham, and Liam O'Brien, along with Rings of Power cast members Maxim Baldry and Benjamin Walker. O'Brien, the one-shot's game master, announced the Season 2 release date.

The Mighty Nein Season 2 will bring fan-favorite characters exciting latest adventures. One is a pirate adventure that takes the group across the high seas, where they continue to grapple with their pasts and relationships. The Beacon remains a threat, of course, and longtime Critters familiar with the original Mighty Nein tabletop campaign can expect appearances from new faces.

As fans recall, at the end of The Mighty Nein Season 1, audiences finally saw the misfit antiheroes together for the first time. This included tiefling Jester Lavorre (Laura Bailey), half-orc warlock Fjord Stone (Willingham), goblin Nott the Brave (Riegel), wizard Caleb (O'Brien), Cobalt Soul monk Beauregard Lionett (Marisha Ray), and tiefling bloodhunter Mollymauk Tealeaf (Taliesin Jaffe), all of whom met up with barbarian/mercenary Yasha Nydoorin (Ashley Johnson).

It has been revealed that the Mighty Nein Season 2 premieres in March. Stream past episodes on Prime Video.
