Arsenal winger Bukayo Saka is facing another month on the sidelines as he continues to recover from a thigh injury, taking his absence to eight weeks.

Saka picked up the injury early in a Champions League game against Bayern Munich on 21 October.

Manager Mikel Arteta issued the update at a press conference ahead of the Premier League’s return, with Arsenal heading to Newcastle for their first game since the international break.

Arteta noted Saka had undergone more tests on the concern and was “evolving really well”.

He said: “It looks like it’s going to be an eight-week injury, more or less, so I think it’s four at the moment. Still a while before he's back with the team.”.

Arsenal will also be missing midfielder Martin Zubimendi for a “few weeks” after he picked up a hamstring injury with Spain during the Nations League break, but the Gunners were handed a boost with centre-back William Saliba finally available for selection after a lengthy absence.

The Frenchman has been struggling with a back injury which he was managing at the end of the 2025-26 campaign, and it brought his World Cup to a premature end as he was forced off half an hour into France’s semi-final defeat.

But he did not require surgery and after several months out, has now played two practice matches and is set to be included in the squad.

Arteta said: “He's ready. He's in a good condition. He played two games, one behind closed doors and one more official and he did really well and he's in good condition.

“We want to ramp up his minutes.”.

Sources indicate that on Zubimendi, Arteta said: “He's out. Unfortunately he has a slight problem in his hamstring. He wasn't too concerned after the game but after the MRI scan indicated there's an injury there and he's going to be out for a few weeks.”.

Arteta also responded to the idea that Fantasy Premier League (FPL) selections by top-flight players, staff and family members have contributed towards team news leaks in recent weeks.

He stated: “It's a topic. This is the kind of things that are happening in our society right now and it's something newly introduced, we haven't dealt with it and we have to put new things in place.

Recent findings suggest that “The confidentiality is something critical because it's about trust and what you're trying to do and that should be kept as a huge secret. And we will take measures to make sure that doesn't happen.”.
