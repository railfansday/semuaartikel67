📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jenn Kuzmyk is stepping down as executive director of the Banff World Media Festival after nine years in the post, it was revealed Wednesday.

Kuzmyk on Dec. 11 will exit the annual Rocky Mountains industry retreat, while staying on as a consultant through a short transition. “I want to thank Sean (chair Cohan) and the Banff board of directors for their trust, guidance and commitment to the festival, and our supporters, partners and sponsors, whose belief in Banff makes everything we do possible,” Kuzmyk said in a statement on Wednesday. Among her programming innovations, Kuzmyk introduced a Showrunner Superpanel and Summit Series Keynote sessions that included appearances by Jeffrey Katzenberg, Ted Sarandos, Pearlena Igbokwe, Jesse Armstrong, George Cheeks, Channing Dungey and Rob Wade.

She also helped Banff, held each June in Alberta, to navigate disruptions and hot button challenges arising from the pandemic era and the 2023 Hollywood strikes, and programmed the media festival to reflect the emergence of a creator economy and content being launched on YouTube or TikTok and other digital platforms.

And Kuzmyk introduced at Banff the Country of Honor program, which hosted delegations from South Korea in 2025 and Spain in 2026.

“Under her (Kuzmyk) watch, she has elevated these iconic brands with a consummate blend of strategic leadership and innovation. Building on this exceptional foundation, Banff 2027 is poised for growth under the stewardship of our remarkably talented and dedicated team. We are deeply grateful for Jenn’s contributions and wish her every success in her next chapter,” Russell Goldstein, president and CEO of parent Brunico Communications mentioned in a statement on Wednesday.
