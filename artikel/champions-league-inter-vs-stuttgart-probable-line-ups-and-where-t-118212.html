The in-form Pio Esposito is expected to continue leading the line for Inter as they take on VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League league phase on Wednesday evening.

Inter begin the day in 17th place in the Champions League table after recording two wins and two defeats across their first four league phase matches of the season.

The Nerazzurri picked up a 3-0 victory away against Feyenoord last time out in Europe and head into Wednesday’s tie off the back of a 4-4 draw away against Atalanta in Serie A over the weekend; Pio Esposito providing a hat-trick on the night. The 22-year-old now has eight goals to his name from his last five appearances for club and country.

Stuttgart were beaten 2-0 by Galatasaray last time out in the Champions League and were held to a 0-0 draw by Schalke in the Bundesliga on Saturday.

Cristian Chivu will be without two of his key midfielders in Hakan Calhanoglu and Piotr Zielinski this evening, which should mean opportunities for Petar Sucic and Curtis Jones along with Nicolo Barella. Hakan Calhanoglu and Aleksandar Stankovic are also available in midfield.

It has been revealed that captain and talisman Lautaro Martinez made his return from injury off the bench against Atalanta over the weekend, but it remains to be seen whether he is declared fit to start on Wednesday. Esposito and Marcus Thuram will be the go-to strike partnership if he isn’t.

Inter (3-5-2): J.Martinez; Bisseck, Akanji, C.Augusto; Diouf, Barella, Sucic, Jones, Dimarco; Esposito, Thuram.

Stuttgart (3-4-3): Bredlow; Hendriks, Chabot, Mittelstädt; Leweling, Prömel, Karazor, Führich; Undav, Demirović, El Khannouss.

It has been revealed that tonight’s game will be available to watch live in the UK on TNT Sports 7 and online through HBO Max. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
