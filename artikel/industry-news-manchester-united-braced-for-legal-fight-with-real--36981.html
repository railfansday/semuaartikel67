By CHRIS WHEELER, NORTHERN SPORTS WRITER.

Published: 06:01 EST, 18 November 2026 | Updated: 06:43 EST, 18 November 2026.

Manchester United have expressed their concern over the circumstances surrounding JJ Gabriel’s imminent move to Real Madrid, and threatened legal action if there is any evidence that he was induced to leave.

The 16-year-old winger posted a video on social media on Tuesday night confirming his departure from United as talks with Madrid continue between his new agent Ali Barat and close family at the club’s Valdebebas headquarters.

United sources say they are disappointed the video appeared without any contact with them, but the club will maintain its policy of not punishing Gabriel for what it sees as the actions of adults.

However, they have reiterated that United will fight with every legal means possible if any evidence of inducement from another club comes to light.

Sources indicate that it’s understood that United have been unable to make contact with Gabriel – who has not been seen at the club since September – or his family despite numerous attempts to check on his welfare. They want an opportunity to ensure that he has a say in what could be a life-changing decision.

Gabriel has been training in Madrid and gave an interview to Marca earlier this month saying that he favoured a move to the 15-time European champions over rivals Barcelona, while expressing an ambition to win the Ballon d’Or.

Manchester United are prepared for a legal fight with Real Madrid over JJ Gabriel.

The youngster is on the verge of joining the Spanish giants in a controversial move.

According to recent reports, he is registered as a United player for the rest of the season and the club are refusing to tear up that agreement in the belief that he would still need approval from FIFA to make the move. However, sources stress that there has been no contact from any football authority seeking to de-register him.

In a recent development, gabriel has been at United since the age of 11, and is considered one of the hottest young prospects in European football. He is registered with United's academy until the end of the season but Real Madrid are intending to sign him in January.

There is a FIFA loophole that allows European clubs to tie down young players to professional contracts if they have a European passport which Gabriel qualifies for through both parents.

In a farewell post on Instagram he said: 'Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020. as a 10-year-old boy with major dreams.

'Here I grew as a player and as a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award.

Sources indicate that 'You gave me moments I'll always treasure. To every coach, every member of staff, to my team-mates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow.

According to recent reports, 'As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything. JJ.'.

Gabriel did not make a competitive first-team appearance for United, but did make a senior appearance in United's pre-season friendly defeat to AC Milan this summer.

United’s hierarchy were left unsure what triggered his request to leave given he had become a key part of intends in first team training sessions this season.

United were left rocked in September when Gabriel's family unexpectedly requested to cancel his contract with immediate effect.

Daily Mail Sport revealed at the time that United’s hierarchy were left unsure what triggered such a move given he had become a key part of intends in first team training sessions this season under Michael Carrick.

In a recent development, A week later, Daily Mail Sport revealed that the decline of United since the days of Sir Alex Ferguson was the driving force behind Gabriel's family seeking an exit. United have not won a Premier League title since 2013.

A source close to the family explained that United are 'far away' from the likes of Barcelona, Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, as well as domestic rivals Arsenal and Manchester City, and that they want him to 'learn from the best' to maximise his talents.

'His dad has ambitions for his son to be the best in the world and play with the best and learn from the best and right now that's not current situation at United - far from it,' a source explained.

He has not been seen at the club since he scored a hat-trick for the Under 21s on his UEFA Youth League debut against Sabah.
