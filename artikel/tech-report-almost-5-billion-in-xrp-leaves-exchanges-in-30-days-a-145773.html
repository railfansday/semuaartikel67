It has been revealed that about 5 billion XRP left crypto exchanges over a 30-day period, according to data shared on X by Arab Chain, an on-chain analyst verified by CryptoQuant. At the current price, those tokens are worth almost $5 billion. XRP traded at around $0.95 on Thursday after gaining about 4% over 24 hours, according to CoinMarketCap.

It has been revealed that arab Chain publishes XRP, Bitcoin and Ethereum exchange flow data on CryptoQuant. The analyst wrote the post in Arabic, and according to X's English translation, Binance led the platforms with outflows of approximately 1.439 billion XRP. OKX followed with around 865.27 million XRP, and Bybit recorded approximately 697.65 million XRP. Upbit and Bitget saw outflows of around 159.13 million XRP and 146.16 million XRP, respectively.

According to Arab Chain, XRP outflows from exchanges totaled around 5.7 billion tokens in October.

"The decline in outflows compared with October levels reflects a slower pace of XRP withdrawals from trading platforms, despite continued transfers of large amounts of the cryptocurrency off exchanges," the analyst wrote.

Recent findings suggest that XRP lost 25.4% in November as of Nov. 22, according to CryptoRank. Before 2026, the token's worst November was in 2019, when it fell 23.3%. XRP also lost 13.8% and 11.8% in November of 2025 and 2022, respectively. In November of 2023 and 2024, the token gained 1.4% and 281.7%.

In 2026, XRP has lost value in seven of 11 months so far, according to CryptoRank data.

It has been revealed that earlier in November, XRP fell to $0.895, its lowest price in two years. On Nov. 23, the token rose as high as $1.026 and then dropped back to around $0.93 the next morning. On Wednesday, it traded at $0.908, down 2.23% over 24 hours, according to CoinMarketCap. XRP's record price is $3.65, which it set on July 18, 2025.

According to Arab Chain, large amounts of XRP continue to move away from the direct trading environment.

"These movements may be linked to holding strategies or the redistribution of assets between wallets," the analyst wrote.

Arab Chain added that continued withdrawals may limit the supply available for immediate sale "if they are not matched by similar inflows.".

"However, these movements cannot be considered conclusive evidence of growing investor confidence or reduced selling pressure," the post reads.
