Derby County's 3-0 defeat to Wolverhampton Wanderers at Molineux has only increased the speculation over John Eustace's future at Pride Park.

The Rams started off the encounter in encouraging fashion, but, ultimately, could not convert one of their early chances past the experienced Jose Sa.

According to recent reports, eustace's side had returned from the year's final international break with a 2-0 defeat to promotion-chasing Southampton at St. Mary's, and a fourth away defeat in five was confirmed as Cesar Peixoto's outfit turned up the heat with a brace from Adam Armstrong and a merited goal from highly-rated winger, Mateus Mane.

The heavy loss in the West Midlands, coupled with Preston North End's surprise 4-0 rout over Cardiff City in South Wales means that Derby will kickstart the Championship's 17th matchweek at the foot of the table - a far cry from what many predicted was in store for Eustace's side in pre-season.

The experienced head coach has been under pressure for some time, and now scrutiny over his position has reappeared ahead of a must-win encounter against his former club, Watford, on Saturday.

Just last month, Eustace revealed that he retained the full backing of Derby owner, David Clowes, over the potential to turn the club's fortunes around.

Since then, the Rams have accumulated four points from a possible 18, with a sole victory coming through a 3-1 success over Bolton Wanderers on November 6th.

Speaking to local radio post-match, Eustace reaffirmed the confidence he has in himself and his squad to get out of the current situation, but no word has come from the club since the full-time whistle at Molineux, which only increased speculation.

However, BBC Derby Sport's Dominic Dietrich has since confirmed that, as things stand, Eustace will lead the team for Saturday's clash against the Hornets, who, earlier on Wednesday, confirmed Edward Iordanescu as Alessio Dionisi's successor.

For the second time in three seasons, Derby are staring at the prospect of a Championship relegation battle.

Eustace has now lost 36 of his 81 matches in charge, equating in a win ratio of just 36%, and, unsurprisingly, the fresh enhancement has caused a stir.

It has been revealed that one fan commented: "This is quite disrespectful to the fanbase now. Actually not now. But a few games ago. Two possible reasons both really bad. Financial reasons: I can't believe this. Because relegation will be much more expensive. Blind loyalty: Even worse than financial mismanagement.".

"It’s an insult to the fans that there is no official communication from the club," was another response from a displeased Rams follower.

In a recent development, "Simply kicking the can down the road. Irrespective of any result against Watford, it’ll just be papering over the canyon. Fundamental change is needed," stated another supporter.

Echoing such sentiments, one comment read: "Was only last season the chants of 'Eustace barmy army' were loud and proud. Football is a very fickle game. Clowes holding his nerve. Saturday is going to be interesting....".

Rams fans, how much longer can David Clowes afford to stick with John Eustace? Comment below!

Another supporter claimed: "I could understand him keeping his job if we’re losing games by fine margins and they players were all trying but we’ve been utter crap. Wolves had near on 40 shots last night. And his post-match interviews are insane! There is definitely something going on behind the scenes.".

It has been revealed that "How much worse does it need to get? How much worse can it get? 10 points from 51 in this awful league is a disaster," debated another frustrated fan.

Derby are now five points adrift of safety with 29 matches remaining, and whilst that allows plenty of time for the club's fortunes to change, there have been little signs that such feats will occur under Eustace.

The former Birmingham City and Blackburn Rovers boss does have experience of staving off relegation, but, no doubt some of the frustration felt by supporters stems from the actions taken at their relegation rivals.

Preston have taken seven points from their last three matches under Graham Alexander after he replaced Paul Heckingbottom, whilst Burnley, Watford and now Lincoln City have already opted to make managerial changes.

The Rams have won just five league matches since the start of April, and whilst injuries to key men such as Patrick Agyemang haven't helped Eustace, such circumstances were not expected whatsoever.
