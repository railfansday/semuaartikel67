Both sides had had good wins last time out, Newhaven enjoying a 2-1 victory over Hassocks while Lancing were beating the Lions.

Industry observers note that the Dockers seemed to gain a greater boost from their win as they put Lancing under pressure for long periods. The margin could easily have been greater had Newhaven not wasted some good opportunities, while Lancing failed to give home keeper Ethan Pike much of a test.

It began ominously – as early as the second minute Scott Packer had to show his defensive qualities to prevent a threat and concede a corner, injuring himself in the process.After treatment from physio Callum Morin, he resumed.

Minutes later the Dockers were in front. The first of a number of dangerous long throws was not properly cleared and the ball found its way to Lee Robinson, unmarked on the edge of the six-yard box. The veteran goal ace found the net with aplomb.

Lancing’s first attempt came as a result of a free-kick for a foul on Mo Khedidji. The ball was laid square to him but his effort on goal flew over.

This was followed almost immediately by Lancing’s best move. Emmanuel N’cho wove his way cleverly down the right ,slipping the ball through to Jay Popham, who fired low into the corner to level the scores with 11 minutes gone.

But soon Lancing were punished by a far-post header from Matt Gunn, restoring their lead. Two further chances from the Dockers went begging.

According to recent reports, newhaven were keeping up the pressure and it needed a superb block tackle by Oliver Deda to halt a run towards the Lancing goal. Lancing were conceding corners which were proving difficult for Elliot Dailey to hold on to as he was being obstructed and forced to attempt to punch clear.

Lancing kept battling but could not capitalise on their possession.

The second half began with Lancing inches from an equaliser. Antoine Sharpe showed again what a threat his pace is.

The ball was sent deep down the Dockers left. Sharpe chased it down with Pike in hot pursuit from his goal-line to attempt to clear the ball into touch.Sharpe got there first and with the goal gaping he tried to reach the net from a very tight angle. But the ball struck the near post.

Lancing went close again when Packer got on the end of a free kick and attempted to lob the keeper from close range but his effort lacked height.

Isaac Brown, returning from injury, replaced Rob Deda with Oliver Deda switching to the left of defence.

But in the 78th minute.Billy Oliver shrugged of a few challenges and powered his way through before lifting the ball into the net.

The Dockers came close to a fourth with a header from a free-kick which went wide.

Lancing brought on Dylan Hurst and Dylan Walter for Popham and McGonigleand almost pulled one back when Oliver Deda turned sharply at the edge of the box and fired in a shot that was deflected for a corner.

According to recent reports, so for Lancing, a case of After the Lord Mayor’s Show. They remain in 14th place on17 points and visit AFC Varndeanians on Saturday.
