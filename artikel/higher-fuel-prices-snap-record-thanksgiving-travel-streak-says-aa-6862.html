This year’s gas prices are high enough to dent Thanksgiving travel intends, according to fresh research from AAA.

Higher fuel prices are pushing more travelers to stay home this Thanksgiving, according to a latest report from AAA.

The auto club projects 81.5 million Americans to travel at least 50 miles from home between November 24 and 30, down 0.1% from 81.6 million last year.

Recent findings suggest that it’s a small dip, but excluding 2020, it’s the first time since the 2008 recession that AAA has reported a decline in its Thanksgiving travel projection. (Older projections measured a shorter travel window and are not directly comparable.).

The decline was smaller than AAA anticipated, with nearly a quarter of the U.S. population still planning to travel, according to spokesperson Aixa Diaz,.
