Crystal Palace delivered Thomas Frank a first victory after they came back to beat 10-man Real Sociedad 2-1 in the Europa League.

Dean Henderson was kept busy in the first half, making several stops to ensure it was all to play for after the break in south London, where Frank took charge of a home game for the first time.

Mamadou Sarr broke the deadlock in the 66th minute, but the contest turned on its head when Jon Martin was sent off with a second yellow card for tugging down Jean-Philippe Mateta six minutes later.

The Frenchman fired in the resulting penalty before second-half substitute Dwight McNeil tapped home from Jorgen Strand Larsen’s overhead kick.

Yeremy Pino had a chance to put the game away with a stoppage-time penalty but he was denied by Unai Marrero, and the hosts had to grind out a nervy finish, with Henderson producing his biggest save of the night to deny Orri Oskarsson at the death.

The result moves the Eagles into a play-off position, after starting the evening just outside of it, in 25th.

Sunderland were left frustrated again in their Europa League campaign after drawing 1-1 with Jagiellonia Bialystok at the Stadium of Light.

The Black Cats enjoyed the best of the first-half chances through Malick Fofana and Nilson Angulo, but the visitors had an opportunity when Robin Roefs saved Kajetan Szmyt’s free-kick.

Sunderland struck early in the second half through Timur Tutierov’s header, his second goal in the competition, but Kamil Jozwiak levelled only minutes later after capitalising on a mistake at the back.

A point means the Wearsiders stay in the knockout phase play-off places after being held to their second draw from five games in the competition.

It has been revealed that camilo Duran came to Celtic’s rescue in Europe again after coming off the bench to head a late winner against Torreense in Portugal. The Colombian striker peeled off to the back post to meet a cross from fellow substitute James Forrest and send a downward header into the net in the 85th minute.

Duran’s 15th goal of the season earned a 2-1 victory over the Portuguese second-tier side and put Celtic on six points in the Europa League after his double off the bench inspired a comeback win over Besiktas last time out.

Celtic took the lead in the ninth minute when Javi Vazquez sliced Jota’s cross into his own net with Yang Hyun-jun right behind him.

But the Portuguese Cup winners levelled 10 minutes later after some sloppy defending. Both Kieran Tierney and Colby Donovan had chances to deal with bouncing balls after Alex Alfaro hit the bar from 22 yards but they could not clear and Haiti international Dany Jean volleyed home.

Celtic dominated second-half possession but clear chances were at a premium until Duran struck.
