Grand Theft Auto VI has only been out for a day, but people are already sharing tips.

One early hidden feature — and a nice way to swiftly build those cash reserves — comes courtesy of a liquor store and a talking parrot.

UK streamer Millzaa shared a mini walkthrough of how to find the money in GTA 6 on TikTok and X, where it's been viewed over a million times.

"There's a parrot that leads you to a secret money stash at the start of GTA 6," he indicates in the video. "Talk to your neighbor Shirley and she'll send you to the liquor store for some rum...".

Millzaa explains that once you've bought and delivered the rum for Shirley you can return to Three Sheets Liquor (when you're in the store earlier, the owner tells a bunch of animals in a back room to be quiet. Don't try to enter this room by walking behind the counter (the owner will not like this). If you jump the fence round the side you'll find some criminals you need to fight off — and when you do, you'll discover a talking red parrot in a cage. The parrot mentions cash and a generator, next to which you'll find a grate in the wall.

Hidden inside? Over $1,300. Nice.
