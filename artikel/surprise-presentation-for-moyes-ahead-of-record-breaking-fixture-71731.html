David Moyes was presented with a commemorative frame ahead of Monday night’s Premier League away fixture against Brentford – a record-breaking match for the Everton manager.

The trip to the Gtech Community Stadium will mark Moyes’ 593rd game as Everton manager, taking him ahead of the legendary Harry Catterick in the all-time rankings.

Everton captain James Tarkowski and former Blues midfielder Leon Osman were on hand to present the signed frame – and Moyes said it’s a “privilege and honour” to have reached the historic milestone.

In a recent development, "I'm really thrilled about. I've become the manager who has managed more Everton games than anyone before. That's a privilege and an honour to be put in that position.

"I have to thank the people who brought me to the Club in the first place. When you take a chance on a young manager, you never know it'll be work, but they had great faith and believed in me.

"I'm so thankful for the people around Everton because they've been so good for me.".

Sources indicate that handing over the frame, Osman noted: “It’s a record I don’t think will ever be beaten – and I don’t say that lightly. This is some achievement for a huge football club.

“I’m sure James is like me in saying it’s been an honour to play for you. It’s been an honour, a privilege. It’s been hard! But you’ve made us all better and we wish you many more successes for the upcoming seasons.”.
