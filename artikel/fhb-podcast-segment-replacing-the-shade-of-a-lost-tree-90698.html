According to recent reports, in episode 762 of the Fine Homebuilding Podcast, Rob from North Jersey wonders how to make up for afternoon shade that was lost when a large tree on his property got taken out by a storm. Since losing the tree, he’s noticed the air handler in his unconditioned attic has been working overtime to cool the home. Additionally, afternoon heat in the attic seems to be radiating more into the second floor. Rob asks for advice on providing relief for the air handler, mitigating attic heat, conducting an energy audit, and fortifying his roof. The podcast crew weighs in.
