Latest Update: MSC Armonia Arrives in South Africa for Maiden Season

The MSC Armonia arrived at the Port of Durban earlier this week to kick off its first season in South Africa.

With its repositioning voyage cancelled due to security concerns in the Red Sea, the vessel arrived at its winter homeport with no guests onboard.

For the first sailing of the season, the ship is offering a four-night cruise to Mozambique that features a visit to Pomene, in addition to two days at sea.

For its maiden season in South Africa, the MSC Armonia is scheduled to offer a series of short cruises from Durban through early March 2027.

Before starting a repositioning voyage back to Europe in April 2027, the vessel is also scheduled to operate five cruises from Cape Town.

The itineraries feature visits to destinations in Mozambique and Namibia, such as Portuguese Island, Pomene, Maputo and Walvis Bay.

The MSC Armonia will also offer cruises to nowhere, in addition to a 12-night sailing to the Indian Ocean to celebrate Upcoming Year’s Eve at sea.

Sailing roundtrip from Durban, the longer itinerary features a visit to Portuguese Island before heading to Port Louis in Mauritius and Saint-Denis in Réunion.

For its return to Europe, the MSC Armonia is scheduled to offer a 26-night cruise to Venice, sailing from Durban on April 1, 2026.

The repositioning voyage features visits to destinations in the Indian Ocean, the Red Sea and the Mediterranean.

Highlights of the cruise include a full transit of the Suez Canal, in addition to calls to Diego Suarez in Madagascar, Aqaba in Jordan, Sharm el Sheikh in Egypt and more.

With the Armonia offering winter cruises in the Mediterranean, the MSC Sinfonia is scheduled to sail in South Africa in 2027-28.

The 2,000-passenger vessel will offer short cruises from Durban and Cape Town between November 2027 and March 2028.
