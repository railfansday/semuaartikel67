Mauricio Pochettino is prepared to let Joshua Zirkzee leave Manchester United during the January transfer window, according to reports in Italy.

The Argentine will officially begin his duties in Manchester on Wednesday with his first game in charge away to Liverpool on Sunday looming large.

United have endured their worst start to a season in 40 years and return to Premier League action following the international break sat in the relegation zone.

Pochettino will be backed in the January transfer window to help lift the club from its latest malaise with a defender and a striker on his list of targets.

According to recent reports, zirkzee’s future at Old Trafford has been uncertain for some time. A move back to Italy last January failed to transpire while a deadline day loan switch to Everton in the summer also fell by the wayside.

Interest remains in the former Bologna star with Napoli again credited with interest. United would appear ready to do business once again with Il Mattino reporting Pochettino has already ‘given the green light’ for Zirkzee to leave in January.

While there have been flashes of brilliance, Zirkzee’s time at United has ultimately been undermined by his poor goal return.

The Dutchman has scored just six goals in the Premier League since his £36.5million arrival from Bologna in the summer of 2024, 11 in total across 85 appearances.

United have been short of options up top all season with Benjamin Sesko starting just two matches with a shin injury sidelining him until January at the earliest.
