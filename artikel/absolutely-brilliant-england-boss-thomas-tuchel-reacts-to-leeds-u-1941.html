Thomas Tuchel saluted Leeds United goalkeeper James Trafford following his stellar display in England’s 3-2 Nations League win over Spain.

The Three Lions raced into a three-goal lead in Madrid last night (November 15) as Harry Kane, Ezri Konsa and Ben White weighed in with goals.

Elliot Anderson’s red card buoyed Spain, who hit back through Mikel Oyarzabal and Nico Williams, but a comeback could not be completed.

England boss Tuchel gave Trafford the spot between the sticks and the 24-year-old vindicated the decision, making some excellent stops. He made a particularly impressive double save in stoppage time, denying Fermin Lopez and then keeping Oyarzabal’s follow-up out.

Recent findings suggest that tuchel said: “Very good. That's his job, to close the gap. We want competition in all positions.

"We need to see Trafford in high-pressure moments. The obvious save, where he deserves full credit, is the double save at the very end. Absolutely brilliant.

“There was a lot to do in the deep build-up as well, which he did very, very well, with everyone around him.

“Never lose the concentration, never lose the focus, never lose the vision where to find the free men to overcome the press and put the game to the opponent's half.

“With everyone else, he was part of a fantastic team performance.”.

Trafford’s competitor for the England gloves is Everton’s Jordan Pickford, who has spent the bulk of the last decade as the first-choice stopper.

Across the last two international breaks, playing time has been split evenly between the pair.

In a recent development, last week, Trafford’s Leeds teammate and Pickford’s former Everton teammate Dominic Calvert-Lewin stated: “I think we're fortunate now that we've got two exceptional goalkeepers. One thing that is true for both of them is they're very self-confident and confident in their ability, which is what I think you need from England's number one.

Industry observers note that “I played with Pickers for nine years at Everton, so I've seen him grow and what a career he's had for England. I don't think that can be so quickly disregarded at all.

According to recent reports, “But now I'm playing with James Trafford, who is a younger, confident, top, top goalkeeper, and I'm seeing his quality week in, week out now. I think it's something that should be appreciated by us as a nation.”.
