According to recent reports, as the weather gets cold, you don’t have to pay an arm and a leg just to keep your limbs from freezing off. Instead of heating your entire home, simply plug in a space heater and get direct warmth from a nearby source. If you want a compact yet powerful option, the Lasko Whirlwind Heat Oscillating Space Heater is an ideal choice.

What I like so much about this Lasko is its combination of size, power, and oscillation. It is small enough to sit comfortably on my desk or nightstand without taking up too much space, yet in combination with the dual-axis oscillation and powerful heating, it can warm up an entire room.

It has been revealed that much like the Dreo Whole Room Heater, the Lasko is designed with dual-axis oscillation, meaning it oscillates both vertically and horizontally. It can also oscillate in both directions simultaneously for greater coverage of an entire space. As a result, the heater boasts a total coverage of up to 300 square feet, which should be more than enough for most bedrooms, offices, and even many living rooms.

In a recent development, that design is backed by 1,500 watts of heating power. The heat comes at three different heating levels that adjust the fan power. On its highest level, the heating is almost abrasive up close. In a small room at our office, I felt a tangible difference in temperature around the entire room within minutes, and just as importantly, near-instant hot air from the second I turned it on.

My one complaint is that there isn’t a separate control for both heat levels and fan power, which could lead to a slightly more nuanced level of control. That noted, the auto-eco mode allows you to set a specific temperature, which the space heater will reach, go on low heat mode until it is two degrees above the set temperature, and then shut off automatically.

Industry observers note that this makes it a lot easier to run without direct supervision or accidentally running it for hours on end. Similarly, there is a sleep mode as well where you can set a timer up to 12 hours so it will shut off after a set amount of time, making it great to use once you get in bed.

The Lasko packs all that power in a relatively compact body. In cases of cold streaks in my drafty office building, I can set it up to run on my desk without feeling like I’ve sacrificed all my working space just to stay warm. In fact, I can still have my laptop, books, and assorted gear strewn about.

Placed on the nightstand or on top of a dresser, it works perfectly to warm you up from bed as well. One of the small but notable features is the remote control. While not uncommon, I also love the remote control. I can place it on a stand or even in the corner of a room when I don’t want direct heat and still control the heat level, temperature setting, oscillation, sleep timer, and screen light.

The Lasko Whirlwind doesn’t have any specifically unique safety features, but it has everything you might need for peace of mind. The ceramic safety plug can handle high levels of wattage without overheating to prevent electrical fires, and the space heater will automatically shut off if the motor overheats or the body tips over. The overall housing surrounding the heater stays cool, so you won’t burn your hands if you need to touch it.

In a recent development, the Lasko Whirlwind does exactly what I want it to. I want powerful heat, customizable temperature settings, and quality safety features, and the Lasko provides that. The small size takes that powerful heat and puts it in a container that is compact enough to fit on desks, nightstands, or in the corner of your room.

If you want a device powerful enough to heat up a full room but small enough to fit anywhere, the Lasko Whirlwind is an excellent choice. If you want to heat up large spaces, you might need something a bit larger, but very few space heaters have pound for pound as much power as this one.
