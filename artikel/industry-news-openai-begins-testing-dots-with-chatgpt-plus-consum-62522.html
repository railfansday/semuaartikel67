In September, OpenAI confirmed ChatGPT dot, an always-on, proactive assistant that can handle ongoing tasks, work across connected apps, and make progress on projects even when customers aren’t actively interacting with it.

Additionally, any work delegated to ChatGPT Work or Codex counts toward the allowances for those products.

Here’s OpenAI’s original announcement on how ChatGPT dot works:.

Industry observers note that dots are frontier intelligence that have your back. Powered by GPT-6 Astra, they have their own cloud computer, learn from feedback over time, and can work towards your goals 24/7. Through our ecosystem of plugins, they can readily connect to over 4,000 apps, giving them the tools to help wherever you need them.

Since then, OpenAI has expanded the range of models powering ChatGPT dot, adding GPT-6.1 Terra alongside GPT-6 Astra earlier this month.

More recently, OpenAI added custom instructions, allowing customers to specify how their Dots should behave and respond.

Recent findings suggest that heads up, we’re starting to test dots with a small number of ChatGPT Plus users.
