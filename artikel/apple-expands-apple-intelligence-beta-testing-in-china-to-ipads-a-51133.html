Recent findings suggest that following the arrival of Apple Intelligence on iPhones in China with iOS 27.3 beta 1, Apple is now expanding support to iPads and Macs ahead of the public release of its 27.3 updates. Here are the details.

Apple’s road to releasing Apple Intelligence in China has been a particularly eventful one.

After a significant amount of regulatory back-and-forth, the manufacturer confirmed in late October that Apple Intelligence would make its way to iPhones as of iOS 27.3, beginning with the first beta of the system, released in early November.

Just a few days later, Reuters reported that Apple had cleared another regulatory hurdle in the country by registering Apple Intelligence for use on tablets and laptops.

Today, Apple released the second developer betas of iPadOS 27.3 and macOS 27.3, both of which now include Apple Intelligence support in China, as spotted by MacRumors.

As with the initial iPhone rollout in the first iOS 27.3 beta, Apple Intelligence on iPads and Macs in China does not include Siri AI or the other next-generation AI features confirmed at WWDC 2026.

Instead, owners get access to earlier capabilities such as Writing Tools, Genmoji, Live Translation, and notification summaries, with Alibaba’s Qwen model powering certain features.
