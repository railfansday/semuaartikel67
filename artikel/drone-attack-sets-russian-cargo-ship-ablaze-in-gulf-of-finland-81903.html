Drone attack sets Russian cargo ship ablaze in Gulf of Finland

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (65 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A Russian cargo ship caught fire after ​being hit by drones in the Baltic Sea on Saturday and drifted into Finnish waters. Reporting from Kyiv, FRANCE 24's Gulliver Cragg notes that the ship is affiliated with a major Russian military logistics organization. While Ukrainian authorities typically neither confirm nor deny responsibility, analysts cite a clear motive for the targeting, Cragg explains.
