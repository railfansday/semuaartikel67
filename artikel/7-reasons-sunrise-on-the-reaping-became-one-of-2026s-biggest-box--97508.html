7 Reasons Sunrise On The Reaping Became One Of 2026's Biggest Box Office Successes

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, the newest Hunger Games movie, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, hit theaters just last weekend, and already, it's proven to be a smash hit. In fact, by Sunday, Sunrise on the Reaping had the fifth-best domestic opening in 2026 so far, and its box office success is only going to grow. Reviews of Sunrise on the Reaping have been a bit mixed, with some finding that the franchise has begun to feel a bit too repetitive or wrapped up in its own lore. However, fans have certainly had a different response.

Recent findings suggest that presently, Sunrise on the Reaping has a very impressive 94% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes—and, notably, its critic score isn't terrible either, currently sitting at 68%. Sure, there have been some complaints from fans, such as changes the movie made to the Sunrise on the Reaping book, but for the most part, it is finding plenty of popularity and success. And, in terms of the latter, these seven aspects of the film (including ahead of its release) in particular helped to make that happen.

Although there is plenty that worked for the movie in terms of its own merits, there's no question that Lionsgate was wise to release the movie when it did. Sunrise on the Reaping managed to avoid some of the trickiest release windows of the year, particularly considering that it opted not to release first over the weekend of Thanksgiving, which is a notoriously difficult time of year for competition between movies.

However, this year, things are a bit different, as Thanksgiving won't be the most competitive period for theatrical releases at all. Rather, that title will undoubtedly go to what is being called Dunesday, December 18, on which both Dune: Part Three and Avengers: Doomsday will be released. Had Sunrise on the Reaping debuted in December, it may very well have run into trouble, but with the release window that was chosen, the movie had an even better chance to thrive.

In addition to a wise release schedule, Sunrise on the Reaping benefited enormously from the marketing for the movie. For months ahead of the release, the cast of the movie were giving various interviews and making myriad appearances, and Maysilee Donner actress Mckenna Grace in particular made the press tour work to her advantage, especially with her decision to consistently wear outfits that were callbacks either to previous red carpets for the franchise or outfits from the other movies themselves.

However, even outside of the cast, marketing for the movie was brilliant, including a chillingly well-timed in-universe ad that played during the United States election night this year and came from President Snow himself. With moves like that, Sunrise on the Reaping was getting quite a bit more attention than is arguably typical of a movie like this.

Recent findings suggest that helping the other more traditional forms of marketing for Sunrise on the Reaping was the use of social media. In fact, Lionsgate itself made great use of social media, making what are effectively fan edits for months, and ultimately turning that into an incredibly popular trailer idea, offering fans the opportunity to make a fan edit that would become an official trailer. In general, though, Sunrise on the Reaping was absolutely everywhere on social media, from Instagram to TikTok and beyond.

That presence from Lionsgate also translated to organic chatter on these myriad social media platforms, with many accounts talking about the books, sharing cast interviews and funny moments, speculating about potential additions to the movie, and more in the months leading up to the film's debut.

There's no question that the movie was massively boosted by the star-studded cast. In addition to the fact that the incredibly popular Mckenna Grace was playing Maysilee, Joseph Zada as Haymitch Abernathy had fans beyond excited, as he is a relatively up and coming actor who has started to have a very dedicated following. However, there were many other major names tied to the project, such as Elle Fanning and Maya Hawke, that had fans eager to see the movie as well.

That, in particular, is actually a very impressive aspect of Sunrise on the Reaping, as it is notoriously difficult to recast beloved characters and actually have it work out. For the most part, though, these fresh actors were met with open arms, and their depictions of the characters ended up being celebrated rather than divisive.

While Lionsgate made many wise decisions and the cast members were absolutely excellent, it shouldn't be overlooked that Sunrise on the Reaping also simply had some incredible source material that gave it the foundation it needed to be successful. All of Suzanne Collins' Hunger Games books have been brilliant, but Sunrise on the Reaping became very popular because of its focus on Haymitch, its ability to weave together elements from the previous books and movies, and the way it didn't shy away from the true brutality of the Capitol.

In a recent development, that alone meant that fans of the franchise were going to make their way to theaters this November, as they knew the story and wanted to see it on the big screen. This, again, is impressive, as this book represented Collins' decision to go back and flesh out a fan-favorite character, which is a risky decision—but, clearly, it totally paid off.

According to recent reports, it's true that Sunrise on the Reaping is a great book, but the fact is, the Hunger Games franchise in general is beloved, and that was also going to offer a guarantee of a certain number of theater-goers. For nearly 20 years now, these characters have been immensely popular, and that arguably means that, no matter what Collins or Lionsgate put out, fans are going to want to see it.

It has been revealed that it helps that the very notion of the Hunger Games franchise is a compelling and, in many ways, topical one, even as it is absolutely horrifying and disturbing. These books and movies have staying power for a reason, and Sunrise on the Reaping was able to build upon that and find so much success in part because of it.

Industry observers note that finally, there's very little doubt that the earlier confirmation that Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson were going to appear in the movie contributed to ticket sales and fan excitement. Already, the epilogue of the novel had revealed that Peeta Mellark and Katniss Everdeen play a role in this story, but that didn't automatically mean that these fan-favorite actors were going to appear.

However, just a few weeks before Sunrise on the Reaping hit theaters, Lawrence gave an interview confirming that they would be making an appearance in the movie, and that led to a massive wave of interest and excitement, especially online, from fans. Granted, Katniss and Peeta's cameo has now led to some controversy, but there's nevertheless no question that Lawrence and Hutcherson's role in the movie contributed to the still growing success of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.
