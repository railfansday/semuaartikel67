Mauricio Pochettino is set to begin work with Manchester United imminently, with his backroom staff already in the UK.

The Argentine’s assistants, Jesus Perez, Toni Jimenez and Sebastiano Pochettino (Mauricio’s son), all of whom worked with him in the United States setup, flew to England on Saturday to start their new jobs at club level.

The trio will all join the former Chelsea and Tottenham boss in the dugout at Old Trafford with it being reported that the Premier League outfit paid an additional fee to release them from their contracts with US Soccer, following the £7m outlay to secure Pochettino’s services.

The Athletic reports that Perez, Jimenez and Pochettino Jr., immediately travelled to Manchester after the US’ 6-0 win over Haiti in the Concacaf Nations League quarter-finals at the weekend.

That was the first of their two-legged tie, but US Soccer allowed them to depart prematurely as a result of their aggregate lead being so great.

Pochettino will remain in-post for his final match in charge on Tuesday, when the US take on Haiti once more, this time in Cincinnati.

The 54-year-old will leave the US setup just three months after penning a new four-year deal following the World Cup.

Miguel D’Agostino, who has been one of Pochettino’s longest-serving assistants, has remained with his manager for the second leg, but he is also expected to move to Old Trafford when Pochettino begins work officially on November 18, as was confirmed by United.

Pochettino’s first game in charge of the Red Devils will be a trip to Anfield on Sunday, as he looks to guide his new side away from the relegation zone.
