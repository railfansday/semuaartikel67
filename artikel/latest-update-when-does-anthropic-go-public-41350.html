According to recent reports, anthropic is making its stock market debut today, November 19, 2026, with shares set to trade on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT. The tech giant priced its IPO at $83 per share, exceeding its initial $70–$78 price range and putting its valuation at approximately $1.3 trillion to $1.5 trillion. The offering raised around $81.3 billion through the sale of 980 million shares, making it one of the largest public listings on record. Investors will also be watching Anthropic’s financial position after its latest revenue growth. The company reported approximately $17 billion in Q3 revenue, up from $11.6 billion in the previous quarter. However, it also recorded a $567 million operating loss. Its market debut will give investors their first opportunity to trade Anthropic shares publicly and assess how much demand exists for the AI company’s business.
