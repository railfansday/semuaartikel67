Egyptian artist Laila Gohar is putting her characteristically whimsical spin on a holiday tradition this year, teaming up with The Luxury Collection on a limited-edition advent calendar that takes its design and culinary cues from 24 destinations around the world.

The $218 calendar, available through The Luxury Collection Boutique in the U.S. and Gohar World in select international markets, brings together the talents of illustrator Joana Avillez and Mexican chocolatier Casa Bosques. Designed to resemble the elaborate façade of a grand hotel, the collectible features 24 numbered drawers, each housing a custom chocolate inspired by one of the hotel group’s properties. Sources indicate that casa Bosques founder Rafael Prieto worked with culinary teams across The Luxury Collection’s portfolio to develop the 24 distinct flavors, which include saffron inspired by Dubai, black sesame by Kyoto, toasted coconut by South Caicos and banana and sea salt by Waikiki. Other destinations are represented by hazelnut praline (Paris) and smoked mesquite (Los Cabos).

It has been revealed that “I love the anticipation of opening an advent calendar, especially during the holidays,” Gohar mentioned in a statement. “Each drawer is a small world of its own, inspired by the flavors, traditions, and details that make destinations so special at this time of year. I hope each discovery sparks curiosity and joy.”.

The artist, whose eponymous homewares label Gohar World specializes in surrealist interpretations of traditional tabletop objects, has made a name for herself turning food into elaborate, often fantastical installations.

Recent findings suggest that as part of the collaboration, Gohar has also designed a series of holiday pop-ups modeled after old-world Parisian chocolate shops and Milanese apothecaries. The installations feature theatrical display windows and rows of drawers that echo the design of the calendar itself. At Paris’ Prince de Galles hotel, artist and set designer Alix Bortoli created bespoke vitrines incorporating the raw ingredients used in the chocolates.

According to recent reports, the pop-ups will run throughout the holiday season at Prince de Galles in Paris, The Joseph in Nashville, Solaz in Los Cabos and The Laurus in Singapore. Select properties will also host culinary and design programming tied to the collaboration.

The partnership is part of The Luxury Collection’s ongoing World in Season initiative, which highlights the culinary and cultural traditions across the Marriott-owned brand’s portfolio of more than 135 hotels and resorts in 41 countries and territories.

The limited-edition Laila Gohar x The Luxury Collection advent calendar retails for $218 and is available to purchase online and at participating hotels.
