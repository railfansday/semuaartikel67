Bruno Fernandes told to spearhead Man United dressing room revival - Insider spells out some home truths

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bruno Fernandes has been warned against leaving Manchester United at the end of the season amid their current turmoil.

The recent change in manager, with Mauricio Pochettino appointed to replace Michael Carrick, has led to suggestions of further upheaval within the squad at Old Trafford.

The 32-year-old's future has been a topic of discussion for months now, and there have been suggestions he could be tempted to quit in favour of a move elsewhere.

However, former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that he would be "very disappointed" to see Fernandes "jump ship" come the end of the season.

Instead, the captain has been called upon to help turn the club's recent forunes around, with his role as a key player on the pitch and leader in the dressing room set to be crucial.

Fernandes has less than a year remaining on his current contract, and could be tempted to leave for free, but Man United hold an option to extend which could still be triggered.

Football Insider revealed on 14 November that Pochettino is relying on Fernandes to help win over the Man United dressing room after reports of recent unrest.

Fernandes is among a number of star players who have spoken out about the situation behind the scenes, with the higher-ups and previous coaching staff under fire.

However, amidst all of the turmoil, Fernandes is being backed to aid the new manager in his position and has been called upon to help get things back on track at Man United.

"To put it quite bluntly, Bruno Fernandes has been nowhere near good enough this season," Brown told Football Insider.

Recent findings suggest that "We have to look at the situation as it is now, last season is gone, and he hasn't demonstrated up so far this season. He is as much to blame for this nightmare as anybody is.

"So now, I'm expecting him to step up as a leading influence in the dressing room and on the pitch and make sure things can be turned around.

"He's got to get behind the newly introduced manager, step up his performances, and work hard to get things moving in the right direction again.

"I would be very, very disappointed if he walked away now after the way he has performed and behaved this season, if he decided to jump ship and leave the club.

"He's the captain and he's going to have to play a crucial role in turning this season around, regardless of his contract situation which will be spoken about behind the scenes.".

Fernandes' contract situation has been a talking point behind the scenes at Old Trafford for at least 12 months now, with the player having already turned down moves away.

His role both on and off the pitch make him a vital asset for the Red Devils, who remain keen to keep him around, with suggestions that new contract talks have taken place.

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider on 22 October that Man United are hopeful that Fernandes will sign a latest deal to continue as their best player.

Interest from the Saudi Pro League has lingered, and could cause problems if he is to be tempted away, but there is optimism that they can keep him around.
