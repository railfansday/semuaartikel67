Hertha BSC’s attacking numbers underline impressive start to 2. Bundesliga season

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Berliners have scored 25 goals so far, the joint-third highest total in the division, while their underlying numbers suggest their attacking success is built on more than simply clinical finishing.

According to Bundesliga Match Facts powered by AWS, Hertha have accumulated an expected goals (xGoals) rating of 22.9, the second-highest figure in the division. Only Heidenheim have generated a higher total, highlighting the quality of opportunities the capital club have consistently created.

Recent findings suggest that their shot efficiency rating of 2.1 also places them sixth in the division. Although several teams have been more clinical in front of goal, Hertha’s combination of regular chance creation and effective finishing has established them as one of the 2. Bundesliga’s most dangerous attacking sides.

One of the encouraging aspects of Hertha’s attacking development has been the contribution of different players throughout the squad, having relied heavily on individuals in years gone by.

Sebastian Grønning has provided an important focal point in the final third, while other members of the squad such as Oluwaseun Adewumi, Josip Brekalo and Mads Pedersen have also demonstrated their ability to contribute in attacking areas.

Rather than depending entirely on one goalscorer, Hertha have demonstrated an ability to find contributions from different areas of the pitch.

According to recent reports, their 5-3 victory over Eintracht Braunschweig provided an excellent example of that attacking depth. Despite falling two goals behind, Hertha responded with an impressive comeback, demonstrating their ability to create opportunities and punish opposition mistakes.

Sources indicate that their 2-1 victory over Wolfsburg before the break was another significant result, with Adewumi and Deyovaisio Zeefuik finding the net in front of a packed Olympiastadion. Against one of their promotion rivals, Hertha showed they could translate their attacking quality into notable points.

The relationship between Hertha’s expected goals and actual goals scored is particularly interesting.

Their 22.9 xGoals figure indicates that they have consistently created opportunities worth more than 22 expected goals, while their 25 goals demonstrate that they have also managed to convert those chances effectively.

Although the difference suggests a degree of overperformance in front of goal, the gap is relatively modest compared to the overall volume of opportunities created.

This is key when assessing whether their current position is sustainable. Hertha are not simply benefiting from an unusually clinical spell; their underlying chance creation suggests they have the attacking foundations to remain competitive throughout the campaign.

Ranking second for xGoals, joint-third for goals scored and sixth for shot efficiency, they are performing strongly across all three attacking indicators.

For a club that has endured several frustrating years since dropping out of the Bundesliga in 2023, the current campaign represents a welcome change in fortunes.

Hertha have not only produced the results required to reach the summit but have also developed an attacking profile that compares favourably with the division’s strongest sides.

There is still a considerable amount of football to be played, and maintaining their current standards over an entire campaign will be the real challenge.

It has been revealed that however, the numbers provide plenty of encouragement for supporters in the capital. With consistent chance creation, contributions from across the squad and an increasingly convincing attacking record, Hertha have established themselves as genuine contenders for promotion.

Their position at the top of 2. Bundesliga is no longer simply a reflection of a good run of results. The underlying statistics suggest there are solid reasons to believe their resurgence can continue.
