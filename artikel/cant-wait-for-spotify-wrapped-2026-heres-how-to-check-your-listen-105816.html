We're officially in the final stretch before Spotify Wrapped 2026, which means it's almost time to find out which artists, songs, and albums have been soundtracking our lives for the past year.

But what if you don't want to wait?

In a recent development, maybe you're curious whether your favorite artist has held onto the top spot. Maybe you've spent the year branching out into newly introduced genres and genuinely have no idea what your listening habits look like anymore. Or maybe you're just impatient. (Understandable.).

According to recent reports, while Spotify Wrapped 2026 isn't here just yet, you don't have to wait until December to get a glimpse of your listening habits. Spotify has its own built-in listening statistics, and several third-party services offer even more detailed insights into your musical obsessions.

Here's how to check your Spotify stats before Wrapped arrives.

You might not realize it, but Spotify already has a feature that lets you check which artists and songs you've been listening to the most.

In a recent development, introduced in November 2025, Spotify's Listening Stats feature gives Free and Premium users a personalized snapshot of their recent listening habits, including their top artists and songs from the past four weeks. The feature also offers weekly highlights, which might celebrate a listening milestone, a latest musical discovery, or a particularly devoted fan moment.

Open Spotify on your iPhone or Android device.

Tap your profile picture in the top corner of the screen, or swipe left to open the menu.

Explore your top artists, songs, and personalized listening insights.

Tap "Share" to post your results on social media or send them to friends.

The feature is available in more than 60 markets, although availability and the insights shown may vary.

The catch? Listening Stats focuses on your recent activity, not your entire year. So while it can tell you which artists you've listened to most lately, it won't necessarily predict your official Wrapped rankings.

If you're looking for something a little more comprehensive, stats.fm is one of the better-known third-party tools for analyzing your Spotify listening habits.

After connecting your Spotify account, you can explore your most-played artists, tracks, and genres across different time periods.

For more detailed statistics, such as actual play counts and minutes listened, stats.fm also supports importing your extended Spotify streaming history.

Visit stats.fm or download its app.

Connect your Spotify account and authorize the requested permissions.

Open your top artists or top tracks.

Select a time range to explore your listening habits.

If you've been wondering whether your favorite artist is still your most-played act of the year, this might help you make an educated guess.

Sources indicate that just remember that stats.fm isn't Spotify Wrapped. Its rankings and tracking periods may differ from Spotify's official year-end calculations.

Recent findings suggest that want something a little more fun? Try Receiptify, a web app that transforms your top songs into a receipt-style graphic.

Instead of a colorful Wrapped slideshow, you'll get something resembling a shopping receipt, complete with your most-listened-to tracks.

Visit Receiptify and select Spotify.

Sign in and authorize access to your listening data.

Choose an available time range.

Generate your receipt and save the image to share.

It's a particularly fun option if you're looking for something to post to your Instagram Story while everyone waits for the official Wrapped graphics to arrive.

Receiptify isn't designed to replicate Spotify's complete Wrapped experience, but it does offer a quick, shareable look at the songs you've had on repeat.

And even if your top artists on stats.fm look convincing, don't be surprised if your Wrapped rankings turn out differently.

Recent findings suggest that spotify uses its own methodology to calculate Wrapped results. For example, the tech giant explained in 2025 that top songs are ranked by eligible plays, while top artists are determined using a weighted stream count that gives primary artists more credit than featured performers. Wrapped also uses a specific listening window. In 2025, Spotify counted listening activity from January through mid-November, rather than the full calendar year.

Third-party tools may use different date ranges or ranking methods, meaning their results won't always match.

Industry observers note that still, checking your stats ahead of time can be a fun way to see how your musical tastes have evolved over the year. Think of it as a sneak peek before Spotify delivers its annual judgment on your listening habits.

And if the results surprise you? Well, at least you'll have a little time to emotionally prepare yourself before sharing them with everyone you know.
