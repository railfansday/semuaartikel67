According to recent reports, bitcoin bottom signals are becoming harder to ignore, but calling the market low now would be premature. Supply in loss has moved above 50%, the Bull-Bear Cycle Indicator has slipped below zero, and futures activity is flashing another warning.

The seven-day moving average of supply in loss reportedly reached a 140-day high, moving back above 50%. Analyst Gaah described the reading as a return to a historical bottom zone.

That matters because previous periods with a large share of supply underwater have coincided with market bottoms. Still, the indicator doesn’t confirm that prices have finished falling.

Industry observers note that analyst R3N highlighted another warning. The Bull-Bear Cycle Indicator has dropped to approximately -0.5, ending its brief positive stretch from late summer.
