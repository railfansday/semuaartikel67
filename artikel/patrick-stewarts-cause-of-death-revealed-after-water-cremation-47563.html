More details about Patrick Stewart’s death have come to light.

Less than a month after the X-Men actor died at the age of 86, his official cause of death was determined to be Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia, according to his death certificate obtained by TMZ.

It also listed cardiopulmonary arrest—also known as cardiac arrest—as well as a neurocognitive disorder with Lewy body dementia, which is “a brain disorder that can lead to problems with thinking, movement, behavior, and mood,” per Alzheimers.gov.

The Star Trek: The Next Generation alum’s death certificate stated that his remains underwent alkaline hydrolysis—also known as water cremation—which according to the Cremation Association of North America “uses water, alkaline chemicals, heat, and sometimes pressure and agitation, to accelerate natural decomposition.” The process leaves behind bone fragments and a liquid that can then be safely disposed of.

One day after his Oct. 28 death, Stewart’s family shared that he had passed away at his Los Angeles home “in the loving care of his beloved wife" Sunny Ozell.

It has been revealed that “He has been held in the warm embrace of family and friends, surrounded by live music, prayer, tears, laughter, and the kind of profound emotion that only he could inspire after a lifetime of devotion to us all,” the Oct. 29 post on Stewart’s Instagram read. “We will forever cherish the peaceful ease and reflection on his face, an enduring reminder of his invincible grace.”.

“He will be remembered for the dignity, compassion and humanity he brought to every role,” the statement continued, “in his art and life.”.

Industry observers note that in the weeks following his death, Ozell—whom Stewart married in 2013—shared her own heartbreaking tribute to her late husband, writing on Instagram Nov. 11, “We left nothing unsaid. He knew I loved him, and I know he loved me.”.

Recent findings suggest that “We shared nearly two decades together, and we filled those years with so much *living*,” the 47-year-old added. “I’ve been overwhelmed by the public response to Patrick’s death, and while I’m not at all surprised that he touched so many, I’m sincerely moved by the outpouring of sadness and loss.”.
