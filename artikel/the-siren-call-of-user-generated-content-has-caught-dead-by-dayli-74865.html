Industry observers note that the promise of user-generated content, this idea that you could host a platform that grows infinitely because the people that interact with it make things for it and you don't even have to pay anyone, is an alluring one. And its siren call has come for Dead by Daylight, as developer Behaviour Interactive have outlined the future of the game, which includes a way to make your own bits for the multiplayer horror game.

Sources indicate that "What if you could create your own Dead by Daylight experiences?" explains a blog post recapping the prepares for how the game is going to change. "As one of the most exciting updates coming in 2027, the Dead by Daylight Workshop will open the doors to user-created content in some incredible ways. From designing your own Maps and importing custom assets, to modifying game rules, objectives, and even creating entirely fresh modes, the possibilities are vast.".

The post goes on to note that anything created in the workshop will "exist separately from all our classic modes, with dedicated creation tools and moderation provided to ensure experienced modders have everything they need to do their best work, and all submissions meet our official guidelines." I am always hesitant when it comes to UGC faff, because the ability to make your own custom Debbie Daylight maps might sound promising, but people do actually have to engage with them for the whole thing to work. It's just putting a hat on a hat!

Recent findings suggest that anyway, the workshop isn't the only thing Behaviour have in the works. For one, the game is getting a major visual overhaul. You can expect lighting improvements, more detailed assets and "tons of atmospheric touches," alongside revamps of particular maps like the Temple of Purgation and Backwater Swamp. Killers and survivors will be getting some TLC too by "improving character textures, proportions, hair, and animations to make them look and feel more lifelike. That includes updated character rigs, fresh motion capture, and new facial animation technology designed to better express a character’s fear, pain, and struggles over the course of a Trial.".

Recent findings suggest that beyond all of that, a dynamic weather system is coming to the game, meaning you'll now have to deal with the likes of rain, thunderstorms, snow and heavy wind, all of which can impact matches. Onboarding for newer folks should be getting some improvements too, with the addition of a "more cinematic introduction, clearer explanations and tutorials of game mechanics, and a more gradual introduction," to the game. All of this will be coming in one big enhancement sometime in 2027, so, keep your eyes peeled for whenever that drops.
