Jennifer Lawrence Has Her Swan Moment With a Upcoming It-Bag

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jennifer Lawrence knows better than to let a serious event or outfit get in the way of a very unserious handbag. At WWD’s 2026 Beauty Inc Awards in Newly introduced York on Tuesday evening, the Hunger Games and beauty founder arrived in a sculptural black Dior tuxedo jacket, with exaggerated hips and lapels and a frothy, feathered ivory miniskirt. So far, so classic red carpet.

According to recent reports, her choice of accessory—plucked from her already enviable suite of it-bags—certainly made a statement. Lawrence toted the Dior Cygnet Minaudière, a miniature swan-shaped clutch from Jonathan Anderson’s spring 2027 collection—an objet d’art with a wink, and one Anderson has certainly become known for. The designer has form when it comes to feathered friends: recall the infamous pigeon clutch from his eponymous label.

Lawrence was clearly quite taken with her fresh companion. Earlier that day, she carried the same swan to Beauty Inc’s The Catalysts, where she appeared on stage alongside Jasmina Aganovic, her partner in the recently launched haircare brand mes°. For that engagement, she paired a fluffy white longline cardigan with a simple tank top, relaxed blue jeans, and heels, adding CompletedWorks onyx drop earrings. By evening, the bird had spread its wines to experience the full red-carpet glamour it deserves, accompanied by antique Fred Leighton diamond earrings and black satin Jimmy Choo mules.

Industry observers note that J Law’s handbag collection is already the stuff of fantasy. There are the serious investments—Hermès Kellys, Loewe Puzzles, and Phoebe Philo’s Gig bag—but increasingly, she’s making room for accessories with a little more personality. Consider her leopard-print Prada Galleria, complete with brightly colored ostrich leather accents, or her see-through Gimaguas baguette. The swan is a natural addition to the family.

And the same taste for the fantastical is extending to her outerwear. After the awards, Lawrence emerged in a sumptuous red velvet jacket, carrying another Dior bag: a fuzzy black DiorAlps shearling tote. It joins an increasingly fabulous rotation of novelty coats, from her dramatic fur-trimmed Dior coatdress to the chartreuse Colleen Allen housecoat she recently took out with a pair of slippers.

Industry observers note that jennifer Lawrence may have mastered the art of understated yet expensive-looking dressing, but she’s never lost her appetite for something a little cheeky. Take flight!
