Juventus recovered from a Neto howler to beat Omonia Nicosia 4-1 with Jeff Ekhator, Andrea Cambiaso, Jeremie Boga and Francisco Conceicao on target.

In a recent development, the Bianconeri won three of their first four Europa League games, plus a 2-2 with Stade Rennais, but were missing Pierre Kalulu, Nico Gonzalez, Kerim Alajbegovic, Khephren Thuram, Manuel Locatelli, Arek Milik and Kamil Grabara, while Edon Zhegrova and Daniele Rugani are not on the UEFA list. Neto got his first Juve appearance since May 2017, with Francisco Conceicao’s return after a shoulder injury on October 18.

Omonia had Fotis Kitsos and Mihlali Mayambela injured, while Carel Eiting, Moses Odubajo, David Akintola and Giorgos Viktoros were cup-tied.

See how it all unfolded on the Liveblog.

Francisco Conceicao stung the goalkeeper’s gloves in the opening minutes, but Omonia Nicosia took the lead with their first chance thanks to a howler from Neto.

The goalkeeper was understandably rusty after not playing since last season, and flapped aimlessly at a Muamer Tankovic corner, allowing Lubomir Satka to nod in undisturbed at the back post.

Juve took a while to shake off the shock, Juan Cabal testing Thomas Kaminski from distance, yet Florin Tanase threatened a second by drilling wide from the edge of the area, as did Satka on a free kick, as the jeers started to rain down from the stands.

It needed something to wake them up, and it was provided by Andrea Cambiaso with a ferocious, swerving left-foot finish from distance into the far top corner.

Juventus had turned it around by half-time, as Federico Gatti won it back in midfield and Francisco Conceicao perfectly weighted the pass to send Ekhator clear, beating the on-rushing goalkeeper first-time. It was the young former Genoa striker’s first goal for the Old Lady.

There was almost a third moments later, as Conceicao pulled back from the by-line for Gatti’s angled drive, parried by Kaminski’s boot.

After the restart, Cambiaso forced a one-handed save with his powerful strike, then Boga drilled inches wide of the near post following a give and go with Cambiaso.

Boga also curled wide of the far upright, but Juventus got their third when Zeki Celik won it back, Weston McKennie laid it off for Francisco Conceicao, whose first attempt was parried by Kaminski’s foot, but the rebound was turned in.
