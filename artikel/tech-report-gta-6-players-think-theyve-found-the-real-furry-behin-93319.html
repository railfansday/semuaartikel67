GTA 6 players think they’ve found the real-life fursuit that inspired Chase, the fox-suited character you can save from the cops in a viral random encounter.

Being a furry is illegal in GTA 6’s fictional state of Leonida. An in-game TV ad from the Anti-Furry Association urges residents to report suspected furry activity and warns tourists that hunting season is open. Dexerto previously covered the in-game ban and encounter.

The ban plays out in a nighttime standoff, with police surrounding Chase under a helicopter spotlight. Players can give him a ride, lose the cops, and help him and another fursuited character reach the Furverts’ compound.

Despite furries being illegal in GTA 6, you can actually save them if you wantA furry called Chase offers you $15,000 in cash or a custom SMG in exchange for helping them pic.twitter.com/SkZ21atE5r.

Recent findings suggest that fans comparing Chase with fursuiter FoxiFelix zeroed in on the knees. Both suits have white shooting stars in roughly the same spot on each leg, with three stripes trailing from each star. The fox heads differ, and Felix spotted another resemblance after watching the encounter on YouTube, saying the tails looked alike.

Felix also said Chase meowing in the van reminded them of something they do themselves.

According to recent reports, I've just watched the encounter on YouTube and holy fuck so much is the same it's wild haha. Even the tail is the heccin same ❤️ The fact that chase starts meowing in the van fucking sent me as THATS LITERALLY WHAT I DO 😅.

Sources indicate that YCHsuits, one of the makers Felix credits for their suit, also weighed in. The maker remarked Rockstar used one of its designs for the furry storyline and added that someone else made the fursuit’s head.

I already did – the basis is off of a suit I made back in 2020 x3 (barring the head).

It has been revealed that another fursuiter, Chase the Fox, remarked their DMs blew up after people connected them to the character. The two share a name and wear fox suits, while the shooting-star markings are the detail fans keep pointing to in the FoxiFelix comparison.

My DMs are exploding right now because apparently I'M the furry in GTA VI and I was honestly not prepared to hear that when I woke up today 😭 pic.twitter.com/nTl3KkPvwq.

Rockstar has yet to publicly confirm what inspired Chase’s design.
