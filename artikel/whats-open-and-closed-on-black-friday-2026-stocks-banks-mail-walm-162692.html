Industry observers note that after a national day of Thanksgiving, consumers have traditionally kicked off their holiday shopping on Black Friday. But are stores open and ready for it just one day after employees ate all that turkey? Here’s everything you need to know about what is open and closed on Black Friday. Is Black Friday a federal holiday? No, Black Friday is not a federal holiday, no matter what your shopaholic friends and family members may claim. Are banks open on Black Friday? Most banks are open on Black Friday, but some may have reduced hours because it is Thanksgiving week. Is the stock market open on Black Friday? Yes, both the New York Stock Exchange and Nasdaq are open, but both institutions close early, at 1:00 p.m. ET. Perhaps all those buyers and sellers need a nap after eating some Thanksgiving leftovers. Is the Post Office open on Black Friday? Is mail delivered? Yes. U.S. Postal Service post office locations will be back in action on Black Friday. Mail will also be delivered. Are FedEx and UPS delivering on Black Friday? Yes, UPS is back to normal on Black Friday. Some FedEx locations may have modified hours. Additionally, on-call and drop box pickups may be early. Are schools open on Black Friday? Most schools will be closed for Thanksgiving break but it is a good practice to double-check with your student’s institution. Are restaurants open on Black Friday? Most restaurants will be open to help fuel shoppers for a long day of deal hunting. Call ahead, though, to double-check your favorite mom-and-pop joint. It might take some time off. Are retail stores open on Black Friday? Absolutely. Retail stores such as Best Buy, Walmart, Target, and others desperately want your business. Many sales have already started online ahead of the major day. One notable exception is REI. For more than a decade, the outdoorsy store has promoted its “Opt Outside” initiative, remaining closed on Black Friday. Instead of shopping, it encourages people to take a hike, a walk, or do any outdoor activity. Are pharmacies open on Black Friday? Yes. Most pharmacies are open on Black Friday, including chains like CVS and Walgreens. Are grocery stores open on Black Friday? Yes, if you don’t have any Thanksgiving leftovers and need some grub, most grocery stores are open on Black Friday and happy to help. Membership-based stores like Costco and Sam’s Club will also be open.
