In a recent development, following Thanksgiving weekend, the court’s third session of oral arguments for the 2026-27 term will begin (on Monday, November 30, to be exact). This will include some of the biggest cases of the term, including Viramontes v. Cook County (which we will be liveblogging), Jouppi v. Alaska, and Department of Homeland Security v. D.V.D.

It has been revealed that to remind you, in collaboration with Good Judgment Inc., we are giving SCOTUS obsessives (and non-obsessives) a way to forecast how the court will decide the most notable cases of the term, and to test that collective judgment against AI models. In July, we will provide the names on SCOTUSblog of the three most accurate forecasters, reward them with merch, and compare their forecasts to those of the machines.
