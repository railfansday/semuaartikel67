It has been revealed that after years of waiting, Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and it's already astonishing players with its immaculately detailed environments, unexpected celebrity cameos, and absurdly interactive NPCs. Much like Leonida's various radio stations play all-time bangers and deep cuts, GTA 6 is also packed with references to memorable memes and pop-culture moments, and many of them are easy to miss. For instance, the game contains an homage to a hilarious viral moment from E3 2006, and it's not even a full-on side quest but a random discovery you'll stumble upon as you explore GTA 6's intricate open world.

Recent findings suggest that while the unexpected appearance of a very large crustacean is noteworthy in its own right, "giant enemy crabs" have a special meaning in the world of video games. Back in 2006, during Sony's E3 presentation, one of the developers of Genji 2 explained that the game's stages were based on actual battles that took place in ancient Japan, with hundreds of warriors on either side. Sounds quite gritty and rooted in historical realism, right? Turns out, "based on" is doing some heavy lifting here, because in addition to ordinary men, you'll also be fighting "giant enemy crabs.".

In a recent development, there is sadly little in the historical record to suggest that Japan's coastlines were populated by giant crabs at any point in its history, "ancient" or otherwise. Even so, the cringiness of the presentation made it a viral phenomenon on mid-00s YouTube, inspiring several remixes and blog posts. It even inspired a fan-made Flash browser game. In the years since, the "giant enemy crab" has been lovingly referenced in tons of other games, including League of Legends, Vampire Survivors, Borderlands, Viva Pinata, and Astro Bot. Two decades later, the gag's made it all the way to the biggest video game of all time.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is out now for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
