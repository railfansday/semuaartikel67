Rasmus Hojlund told Manchester City fans ‘there is only one club in Manchester’ after scoring for Napoli in their Champions League clash on Tuesday night.

Hojlund scored the opening goal in the second half at the Etihad Stadiumbut City salvaged a 2-2 draw following Rayan Cherki’s equaliser in the 86th minute.

After scoring his first Champions League goal of the season with a brilliant low finish past Gianluigi Donnarumma, Hojlund celebrating in front of City’s supporters.

But before turning to the half way line, the Denmark international held up one finger and told the crowd: ‘There is only one club in Manchester’.

When asked about his celebration after the game, Hojlund remarked: ‘Some good banter, you know.

Sources indicate that ‘There’s the rivalry between Manchester City and Man United and obviously I have a past in Manchester United. That’s my club. Nothing against City but United is my home.’.

Former Red, Rasmus Hojlund scoring at the Etihad tonight:“There’s only one club in Manchester” 🔴⚪️⚫️😍 pic.twitter.com/mS5eQsMWRA.

Hojlund joined Manchester United in a £72 million deal from Atalanta in 2023 and spent two seasons at Old Trafford before his loan move to Napoli.

The 23-year-old then completed a £38 million permanent move to Napoli this summer as the Serie A club had an obligation to sign him after qualifying for the Champions League.

During his two seasons at United, Hojlund scored 26 goals in 95 appearances.
