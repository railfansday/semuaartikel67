Follow Gerjan "Hungry" Hertgers.

Dreamcatcher and I had an earlier start today, Small Bites is taking his time this morning, he’ll catch up to us later. We hiked into quite a scene today. There is a full wreckage of an airplane crash here! We read about the story and all survived, seeing the remnants of the plane however, I truly wonder how.

Sources indicate that my trail name is Hungry, but this might be the first time I actually am truly getting hungry on the trail. Dreamcatcher needs more food too. We packed a little bit too efficiently I guess. The 100-mile isn’t the best place to do that, since there are no resupply points for days.

We noticed something on our maps that neither of us had heard of before: an AMC hut in the 100-mile wilderness?! It’s a little blue blaze off trail. Considering our situation regarding food, we might as well check it out. Maybe they have something. Small Bites caught up to us again.

Maybe doesn’t look like it, but this was a godsend.

We can’t sit here forever though, so we continued and forded a river and hiked on some nice trail, not the usual rocky and rooty stuff.
