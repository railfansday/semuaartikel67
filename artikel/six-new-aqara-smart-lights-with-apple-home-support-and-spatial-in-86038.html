Aqara has unveiled the global launch of six upcoming smart lighting products with full Apple Home support and what the organization describes as “spatial intelligence.”.

The newly introduced lineup includes a vertical floor lamp with 18 individually addressable lighting zones, and a new LED strip offering seamless color gradients, dynamic flowing effects, and music synchronization ….

All of the new products are Matter compatible, allowing them to fully integrate with Apple Home, in addition to supporting the full range of competing ecosystems: Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Samsung SmartThings, Home Assistant, and Homey.

With a compatible sensor like the FP400, all of the latest products support spatial intelligence.

This enables automation to respond not only to whether someone is present, but also to exactly where people are within a space and how they move through it. For example, the ambient lights can turn on and off based on your movement from the couch to the dining room table.

Industry observers note that its intelligent AI sensing algorithm drastically improves reliability by reducing false triggers caused by environmental factors like pets, airflow, and robotic vacuums. A built-in ambient light sensor also helps trigger smart home scenes based on both human activity and lighting conditions.

Sources indicate that you might initially struggle to spot the new products in the firm’s new releases section on its Amazon store. That’s because instead of each having its own section, you need to click or tap on the product in the top image.

In a recent development, pricing ranges from $24 for upcoming light bulbs through to $320 for the 100-foot-long permanent outdoor rope lights. You can find all the new products here.
