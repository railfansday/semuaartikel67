Martin O'Neill was full of praise for "terrific player" Camilo Duran after he scored the winner in Celtic's Europa League success over Torreense.

According to recent reports, celtic triumphed 2-1 against the Portuguese second-tier side on Thursday, with Duran netting the winner in the 84th minute at Estadio Dr. Magalhaes Pessoa.

An own goal from Javi Vazquez saw Celtic take an early lead, though it was swiftly cancelled out by Dany Jean.

Nevertheless, Duran popped up late on to snatch all three points, with Celtic winning away to Portuguese opposition in major European competition for the first time since beating Boavista in the UEFA Cup semi-finals in April 2003, ending a run of four successive defeats in such matches.

Celtic have also won two away games in major European competitions in a single calendar year for the first time since 2003, having managed just six victories on the road between 2004 and 2025.

Duran, meanwhile, has now scored or assisted in nine of his last 11 Celtic games since the September-October international break, directly contributing to 12 goals in that time (nine goals, two assists).

Industry observers note that "You always have to keep the faith. [Camilo Duran's] flying, he's a terrific player," O'Neill told TNT Sports.

"He didn't start the game, of course you can gamble like that too. He did the same against Besiktas.

Sources indicate that "I'm just delighted to have won. It keeps us going, if nothing else. From an almost impossible position, we've given ourselves some hope.

"Despite the fact we had plenty of possession, I thought we could've accelerated [play] a couple of times.

"We had to take the chance, because the three points were of vital importance.".

Celtic have won back-to-back matches in a single major European campaign for the first time since beating Ferencvaros home and away in the group stage of this competition back in 2021-22.

Torreense, meanwhile, are the first Portuguese side since Belenenses in 1962 to both score and concede in each of their first five matches in major European competition.

Celtic sit 25th in the standings, only outside the play-off places on goal difference, with Torreense being another of the eight teams tied on six points.
