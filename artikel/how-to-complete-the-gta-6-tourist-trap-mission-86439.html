The GTA 6 Tourist Trap mission is a very vital one as it's the beginning of the end of Chapter 1. Without getting into major spoilers just yet, it's fair to say that things go a bit wrong in this mission, and it's all completely unavoidable. That makes it a very linear mission, but it's not always clear where you should be going in all the chaos, so my walkthrough for the Tourist Trap mission in GTA 6 will help you out.

I've kept this guide as spoiler-free as possible, but this is a major moment in GTA 6's story, so you've been warned!

First of all, if you're yet to even start Tourist Trap, make sure you check my list of things to do before Tourist Trap in GTA 6 so that you miss out on as little as possible in Chapter 1.

To get Tourist Trap started, head over to the Lucia icon in the park in the south of Key Lento. A cutscene plays showing Lucia and Jason getting picked up by Mattia and Kat and then driving over to the Grassrivers Vistor Center for a job – retrieve the bag in locker 17 on the second floor. Recent findings suggest that you'll eventually get ambushed by undercover cops and cannot fight back – as far as I'm aware, there's no way to avoid this. Soon after, there's a moment where you must move Jason towards a chair at the interview table, and then it's back into some cutscenes. It all eventually breaks out into a cop-versus-dirty-cop shootout, giving Jason and Lucia a chance to escape:.

Recent findings suggest that once you get to Cal's house, that's the end of Tourist Trap. It's quite a simple mission overall as there are a lot of scripted elements and you've just got to do what the game tells you, with your only real choice being whether you kill or wound all the cops you face. Anyway, once the cutscene with Jason, Lucia, and Cal is over, it's onto the next mission, Home Sweet Home, and eventually Chapter 2.
