As of Nov. 19, 2026, the stock trades at $352.12, having returned -12% over the past year as the business navigates a complex environment of slowing consumer discretionary spending and intense competition.

Since 2006, the pizza stock has delivered more than 2,600% gains for investors as the firm expanded its geographic footprint and leveraged technology to serve customers more efficiently. These characteristics are all reasons Domino's has been a solid defensive investment, but where could the stock go from here?

It has been revealed that domino's next phase of growth will likely rhyme with its first. Geographic expansion, this time internationally, will be critical. The organization already operates in more than 90 international markets, with more international stores than U.S. locations. Markets like China and India still offer significant room to expand. But international growth also adds operational and financial complexity. Digital leadership will also continue to drive DPZ's performance. The company that brought us the "pizza tracker" now relies heavily on digital orders, which typically carry higher average tickets and lower error rates. Continued innovation and investment in this area will be key for its next five years of growth.
