As much as outdoor enthusiasts enjoy spending time in nature and nurturing their bodies and minds, it’s easy to lose space for rest while chasing the next thrill. But not anymore, as recent reports say travelers are consciously seeking experiences that balance adventure with restoration.

According to Hilton’s “The Great Rebalance: How Travelers Are Expecting to Reset and Recalibrate in 2027” survey, which surveyed 14,744 travelers across 14 countries, 65 percent of survey respondents actively book vacations that calm the body and reset the nervous system, and 79 percent say that fresh air and being outdoors help them relax when they travel.

The Global Wellness Institute’s 2026 Wellness Tourism Initiative Trends report stated that, in today’s overstimulating world, “wellness travel is increasingly shifting from performance-driven wellness toward nervous system regulation.” Booking.com noted in its 11th Annual Travel Predictions report that they expect 2027 to be the era of recovery-maxxing.

Industry observers note that all this to say, if you’re ready to book a gorgeous getaway brimming with wellness benefits, we’re here to help. The stays on this list are a bit of a splurge, but they do include an array of rejuvenating amenities in beautiful places. (They’re listed from least to most expensive based on current room rates.) That remarked, read ahead for five U.S. destinations that ace recovery travel.

According to recent reports, four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea has drawn guests to its picture-perfect Wailea shores since opening in 1990 as the brand’s first tropical resort. For decades, the oceanfront resort has been world-renowned as a best-in-class property across accommodations, food, beverage, and hospitality. As of July 2026, next-gen wellness has been added to the list. With the opening of Kai Holo Spa, the resort’s warm, circadian-lit wellness sanctuary (where the lighting is built to regulate guests’ sleep-wake cycles to induce relaxation), guests are invited to zen out on island time, whether amongst the aquathermal circuit, with a Pacific Journeys massage in an open-air massage hale by the ocean, or at the adjoining Next|Health Longevity Clinic, where a hyperbaric chamber and IV therapies await.

You can book your stay here for a minimum of $880 per night.

Recent findings suggest that for a more landlocked wellness experience, head to Texas Hill Country to explore the expansive grounds of Canyon Ranch Austin, where a 27,000-square-foot training center and recovery lab called The Barn welcomes guests to nurture their health with the help of a gym and outdoor field. The resort also includes a sprawling 40,000-square-foot spa, making it the largest spa in Texas, that offers an array of vitality treatments, from zero-gravity neurostimulation (for improved sleep and focus) and red light beds (to reduce training fatigue and pain) to an Ammortal Chamber. The Ammortal Chamber combines both red and near-infrared light, pulsed electromagnetic field (PEMF) therapy that sends electromagnetic waves through the body to ease sore muscles, molecular hydrogen, vibroacoustic sound, and guided breathwork to boost relaxation and rebalance the nervous system. Beyond that, Canyon Ranch Austin’s Women’s Wellness Collective is a dedicated women’s wellness practice that addresses hormonal therapy, brain health, sleep, perimenopause, menopause, and more.

You can book your stay here at a minimum of $1,325 per night (per person) with the Stay Your Way package. There’s a two-night minimum stay. Unlimited meals and snacks are included, along with transportation to and from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport or Horseshoe Bay Resort Airport. With this package, you’ll also receive a $200 nightly spa and fitness credit per person. If you stay three or more nights, you’ll get a $300 health and wellness credit.

Dunton Hot Springs is a romantic, rustic-luxe resort set in an 1800s ghost town. Nestled in Colorado’s San Juan Mountains, Dunton Hot Springs draws on its geography for its wellness offerings. The Wellness in the San Juans workshop is a guided two-hour workshop that combines breathwork, meditation, and mobility to deepen the connection between body and mind. Other ways to recover at the resort include soaking in the 85 degrees Fahrenheit to 106 degrees Fahrenheit calcium bicarbonate waters (which are meant to improve skin health and circulation) in the Bathhouse or under the stars in nature, healing sound baths, facials and massages, and individual, couple’s, and group yoga.

In a recent development, you can book your stay here. There’s a two-night minimum stay, and rates are $1,415 per night. For example, the Echo Cabin, your most affordable option, includes a 252-square-foot cabin, mountain views, a half-bath, and a secluded outdoor shower. If you’re not in the mood to shower in nature, you’re just a short walk from the bathhouse.

Recent findings suggest that the Lodge at Blue Sky, only minutes from Park City, approaches recovery from a different angle, leaning into energy healing and alignment. Meander through a beautiful aspen grove into The Edge Sanctuary, a luxury glamping tent where the resort’s energy healers lead intuitive energy journeys, mesmerizing sound baths, and heart-opening tea ceremonies. For a more traditional recovery experience, pop up to The Edge Spa for indulgent facials and massages overlooking Alexander Creek. The Lodge at Blue Sky guests can also invigorate their senses with contrast therapy, where heat improves circulation and cold therapy supports muscle recovery after athletic pursuits.

You can book your stay here. Current rates start at around $1,924 per night for the 570-square-foot King Sky room, which offers one king bed, a mountain view, a 115-square-foot terrace,  and a soaking tub.

According to recent reports, if you want to explore recovery practices without neglecting your passion for adrenaline-fueled adventure, Alaska’s Tordrillo Mountain Lodge is calling. The remote, all-inclusive luxury adventure lodge incorporates helicopters into much of its programming, from heli-skiing to the newly introduced, first-of-its-kind heli-sauna: the AlpenSauna. Earlier this year, the lodge introduced the AlpenSauna, a fly-in, custom-built sauna that the Tordrillo team moves throughout the Tordrillo Mountain Range. For example, the sauna may be positioned next to an alpine waterfall one month and in the middle of a 28-mile ice field on the Triumvirate Glacier the next. Earlier in 2026, the lodge also debuted a 2,400-square-foot wellness and recovery center co-designed by athletes, guides, and longevity experts.

In a recent development, again, helicopter rides are baked into these experiences; the pricing isn’t cheap, so it is certainly a splurge. For a seven-night stay, the Winter package (February to April) costs $18,500 per person (or $2,643 per night). For a five-night stay with the Kings and Corn package (June), the package costs $16,000 per guest (or $3,200 per night). The least expensive stay comes in their Summer package (July to September). For instance, their “Season of the Salmon” package, which runs from August 13, 2027 to September 13, 2027, costs $10,500 per person (or $3,500 per night) for a Friday-to-Monday, three-night stay. In the summer, unlimited lodge activities include wake surfing, wakeboarding, stand-up paddleboarding, kayaking, and more.
