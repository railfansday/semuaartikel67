Industry observers note that businesses can make their own stablecoins on Cardano [ADA] now! Could this be what the platform’s DeFi scene has been waiting for?

The Cardano Foundation is in the news today after confirming the launch on X.

Businesses on Cardano can now do one of two things. They can create a stablecoin under their own brand, or use stablecoins Brale already supports.

Companies can also swap dollars for stablecoins, cash back out to fiat, and send transfers.

The service reaches 30 blockchains.

It’s powered by CIP-113, which lets issuers attach rules to their tokens. A organization could allow transfers only between approved wallets, or keep the power to freeze tokens if needed. It’s handy for regulated businesses, and it also means apps need to check each token’s rules before working with it.

Here’s what’s sticky though. The Foundation hasn’t mentioned which businesses are issuing tokens at launch. It also didn’t share any figures on expected issuance or payment volume.

For all the hype, ADA’s turned a blind eye to all that.

At press time, the altcoin was valued at $0.138 following a 5% dip in 24 hours. A month ago, it was near $0.21. Most of the damage came later, including the major fall on the 14th.

According to recent reports, on 23rd November, the RSI went up to 32 and the MACD line climbed above the Signal line. It looked like sellers were losing grip for a while, but that didn’t last long. In fact, the RSI has since fallen back to 27, below its own average, and into oversold territory again.

Simply put, ADA may be cheap, but it looks like buyers can’t be plied right now.
