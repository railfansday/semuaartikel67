Is the Galaxy S27 Ultra really going to be a significant downgrade for Samsung fans? It could be, at least when it comes to its cameras.

Industry observers note that earlier this month, Mashable reported on recent leaks suggesting that the Samsung Galaxy S27 lineup wasn't going to get much of an upgrade compared to its predecessors. The Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra fared slightly better than the other models, according to the leaks. It appears the Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra will get a main camera sensor upgrade, along with a better selfie camera and a 5x zoom lens.

However, upcoming leaks suggest the device could also lose a few camera features.

According to recent reports, according to leaker Ice Universe, Samsung may be dropping the laser autofocus module from the Galaxy S27 Ultra. Laser autofocus, also known as laser AF, uses a dedicated sensor that helps the camera rapidly lock focus on a subject. If the leak is accurate, the S27 Ultra would be the first Galaxy S Ultra model without laser autofocus since at least 2021, when Samsung released the Galaxy S21 Ultra.

The removal of laser autofocus appears to be connected to the latest camera layout on the Galaxy S27 Ultra, which was also previously leaked by Ice Universe. The Galaxy S27 Ultra will reportedly move away from the vertical camera setup on the Galaxy S26 Ultra and have a horizontal camera bar instead. The new layout is reportedly the reason behind the removal of laser autofocus.

According to recent reports, that's not the only potential downgrade, as PhoneArena points out. Other leaks have also pointed to the Galaxy S27 Ultra losing its 3x telephoto lens.

If these leaks pan out, will the Galaxy S27 Ultra have other newly introduced features to balance out the downgrades and still lure in current owners looking to upgrade? We'll likely find out at Samsung Galaxy Unpacked early next year.
