Dutch advance, Greece miss out despite win over Serbia.

Florian Wirtz scored twice as Germany delivered a 5-0 rout of the Netherlands in Berlin on Monday, reaching the Nations League quarter-finals as group winners.

Brajan Gruda contributed a goal and two assists, with Derry Scherhant and Nico Schlotterbeck also on the scoresheet as the hosts delivered their most impressive display so far under Jürgen Klopp’s leadership.

It was Germany’s biggest win over their neighbours since a 7-0 victory in 1959, and the Netherlands’ first defeat since Xavi Hernández took over as coach in August. The Dutch have still advanced to the last eight by finishing second, with Germany’s fifth goal taking Klopp’s side into top spot.

Sources indicate that the visitors had the first good chance when Jurriën Timber found Crysencio Summerville unmarked on the penalty spot, but the winger lost his footing before getting a shot away. Germany then went close as the Berlin native Scherhant hit a post after connecting with a cross from his Freiburg teammate Philipp Treu.

Industry observers note that nine minutes later, Scherhant had another chance from the same spot and this time curled a lovely effort across the face of goal and inside the far post. Germany pushed for more goals after the break, and Wirtz delivered with a superb solo effort on the hour, lifting a shot in off the left post from the edge of the penalty area.

Schlotterbeck headed in from Gruda’s free-kick with 65 minutes gone, and the RB Leipzig winger laid on another goal six minutes later when he tore down the right and found Wirtz, who hammered home from close range. Gruda then added one of his own, tapping in a Scherhant shot which rebounded off a post.

Wirtz remarked: “We don’t mind who we play. We want to play attacking football. We want to score goals and dominate the opponent. And I think we did that perfectly … We want to keep going next year. We want to get better. I think we’ve taken some great steps in our development as a team.”.

“Everyone will go home with a smile on their face – and for at least the next three or four months, no-one will be questioning the German national team,” Klopp stated afterwards. “Instead, they’ll be thinking: ‘This could really turn into something.’ We have wonderful footballers, great characters … the lads are even surprising themselves.”.

In a recent development, greece had to settle for third place in Group A2 despite a 1-0 home win over Serbia. Giorgos Masouras scored the second-half winner in Volos, but the hosts missed out on a quarter-final place on head-to-head goal difference, their 7-0 defeat by the Netherlands on Friday proving costly. As group winners, Germany will be seeded in Friday’s draw and will avoid England, France and the winners of Group A4 – probably Portugal – in the last eight.

Sources indicate that switzerland completed a dominant campaign in Group B1 by thrashing Scotland 4-0 in St Gallen. Slovenia finished second in the group, claiming a promotion/relegation playoff place, as Danijel Sturm’s double helped them secure a 3-0 win over North Macedonia.

Max Dowman (pictured) became England Under-21s’ youngest-ever goalscorer by grabbing the late winner in a 3-2 friendly victory over France at Ashton Gate.

Lee Carsley’s side made a lightning start as Tottenham's Mikey Moore gave England an early lead on his first under-21s start. Divin Mubama doubled their lead with a header as Ethan Nwaneri set up both of the hosts' first-half goals.

The Young Lions looked comfortable but Tommy Setford allowed France to pull a goal back after he let Giovani Versini’s cross slip through his fingers. The French winger equalised in style moments later, scoring with a shot from distance.

Industry observers note that carsley had handed a first start to promising Arsenal youngster Dowman and he proved to be the hero, tapping in from Harrison Armstrong's pass to give England the win at only 16 years old. Press Association.

In League C, the Faroe Islands beat Moldova 1-0 to complete an unbeaten campaign and earn a playoff place. Slovakia, already guaranteed Group C3 top spot, earned a 2-2 draw with Kazakhstan thanks to Adrian Kapralik’s last-gasp leveller.

According to recent reports, iceland finished Group C4 unbeaten with a 3-0 home victory against Luxembourg securing first place. Second-placed Estonia beat Bulgaria 2-1 to book a playoff spot.

In League D, Gibraltar earned a 1-1 home draw with Malta thanks to Carlos Richards’s late equaliser, while Lithuania defeated Liechtenstein 2-1 in Kaunas.
