AEW wrestler Adam Copeland, formerly known as Edge in WWE, reportedly fought off a bear while walking his dogs before going on to compete in a major pay-per-view event just days later.

The 53-year-old wrestling legend has faced some intimidating opponents throughout his career, but his latest encounter might be his most dangerous yet.

According to Bryan Alvarez of Wrestling Observer, Copeland found himself face-to-face with a mother bear and her cubs while walking his dogs through a residential neighborhood.

The encounter reportedly happened in the days leading up to AEW Full Gear on November 14, where Copeland was scheduled to challenge Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship.

As reported by 411Mania, Copeland, who plans to retire in 2027, heard a noise nearby and turned to discover a mother bear approaching with her cubs.

The Canadian wrestler knew that making himself appear larger could help deter the animal, but he couldn’t raise his arms because he was holding his dogs’ leashes.

With the bear continuing to approach, Copeland made a split-second decision to kick it underneath the jaw with his shin.

It may not have been his Spear finishing move, but the kick reportedly worked, causing the bear to retreat and allowing Copeland to escape safely with his dogs.

However, the encounter wasn’t entirely painless. Copeland reportedly told people afterward that his shin was extremely sore from striking the animal.

Despite the injury, the former WWE champion still competed at AEW Full Gear in Phoenix, Arizona, on November 14.
