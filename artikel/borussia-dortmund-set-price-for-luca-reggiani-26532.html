After making his breakthrough with 513 minutes across eight Bundesliga appearances for Borussia Dortmund in 2025/26, Luca Reggiani has struggled for playing time this term with Die Schwarzgelben open to selling the Italian defender.

According to recent reports, the Italian defender was already a sales candidate in the summer, and has played just 27 Bundesliga minutes in 2026/27. As such, according to Ruhr Nachrichten, a departure in January is becoming “increasingly likely” with the Signal Iduna Park outfit setting their asking price at around €10m.

As reported in August, Reggiani has fallen down the pecking order following the arrival of Joane Gadou, while Italian compatriot Filippo Mane – who is currently sidelined through injury – has moved ahead of him. Because of this, the 18-year-old has found himself playing for BVB II in the Regionalliga West (fourth tier), as well as the UEFA Youth League as opposed to the UEFA Champions League, and the Premier League International Cup U21.
