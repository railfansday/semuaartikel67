According to recent reports, the 41-date tour will feature special guests Anthrax and Slayer guitarist Kerry King — the Big Four consisted of Metallica, Megadeth, Anthrax and Slayer — and kick off on May 14 at the First Financial Credit Union Amphitheater in Albuquerque, N.M. The routing will then take the bands through Arizona, California, Washington, Utah, Colorado, Illinois, Minnesota, Michigan, Ohio, Virginia, Pennsylvania, Ontario, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, Texas, Idaho, Montana and more. Sources indicate that the Cyber Army presale for the tour will begin at 10 a.m. local time on Tuesday (Nov. 17); click here to join the Cyber Army. A general on sale will then take place on Friday (Nov. 20) at 10 a.m. local time here.

Megadeth are presently wrapping up the Australian leg of their last lap, with reveals in Brisbane on Wednesday (Nov. 18) and Thursday (Nov. 19) before heading to Japan in February and the U.K. in March. Megadeth also got some good news on Monday (Nov. 16) when nominations were declared for the 69th annual Grammy Awards, where they were nominated in the best metal performance category for their song “Let There Be Shred”; Anthrax also scored a nom in that category for “The Edge of Perfection.”.

After waiting nearly four decades, Megadeth scored its first No. 1 album on the Billboard 200 in February with their final, self-titled, studio album.
