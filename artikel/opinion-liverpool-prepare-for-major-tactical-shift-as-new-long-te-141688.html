Andoni Iraola has now managed to get Liverpool to fourth in the Premier League table.

Obviously, this is still miles away from where they need to be but, the fact that only seven points separate them and first-placed Arsenal is excellent news.

Right now, it's unclear where Liverpool are going to finish in England's top flight but, if they keep performing to the standard they demonstrated on Sunday against Manchester United, they'll be storming their way up the table.

But, for them to do this, improvements need to be made to their squad as, all of their other title rivals will be adding extra firepower to their ranks.

So, the fact that the January transfer window is now just over a month away is rather exciting.

Especially with all the players that they've just been linked with.

Liverpool see Gustavo Varela as a 'long-term target'.

Liverpool have several problem areas among their ranks that need addressing.

Obviously, since they're one of the biggest clubs in the world, they won't have any problem finding players who want to join.

However, the prices that Premier League clubs have to pay instantly have extra money added on top of them.

That's why only a certain number of deals can be struck by Liverpool in each window.

Moves for the future are also laid out with a newly introduced update from Football Insider sharing that the Reds' recent links with Monza striker Gustavo Varela are for the 'long-term' rather than the short-term.

"Liverpool will not prioritise a move to sign Monza striker Gustavo Varela in January, sources have told Football Insider," they wrote.

"The 21-year-old has emerged as a target for the Reds, having struck 11 goals from his opening 11 games in Serie A so far this term.

"The Reds are "keeping tabs" on the Portuguese youngster, with several top Italian clubs also having their "eyes fixed on him" at the moment.

"However, sources say that Varela is viewed as more of a long-term option at Anfield, and the club are not likely to make a move for him this winter.

"Monza are relaxed over their frontman's future for now, but they know that further interest from top clubs is inevitable if he continues to perform.".

Liverpool's interest in Gustavo Varela could mean that they're changing to a two striker formation.

Liverpool only have two fit strikers among their ranks at the moment.

Alexander Isak is one of them and, since he was the Reds' record signing, he's obviously going to be Iraola's striker.

Lewis Koumas is the other and, even though his manager likes him, the fact that Hugo Ekitike is edging closer to a return will certainly worry him.

Before he got injured last term, Ekitike was easily Liverpool's best player and, they probably wouldn't have qualified for the Champions League if it wasn't for him.

So, the fact that Varela is now emerging as a potential option for the Reds on their shortlist certainly raises a few questions.

The easiest explanation for this move would be that, eventually, Iraola wants to move to a two striker formation while having Varela and Koumas as his back-up options.

Obviously, there are now guarantees that this will play out like that but, if that is the case, a major tactical change could be on its way to Anfield in the next couple of years.
