If you’ve ever looked at a classic 1929 Bentley Blower and thought, “That looks great, but could it be smaller, electric, and somehow still cost six figures?” then good news: your car has arrived.

Hedley Studios has completed the first 25 production Bentley Blower Jnrs, with the hand-built electric mini-cars now heading to customers. Deliveries are beginning in the UK before expanding across Europe.

The Blower Jnr is an officially licensed collaboration with Bentley, recreating the famous 1929 Bentley 4½ Liter Supercharged Team Car No. 2 at 85% scale. Unlike many scaled-down collector cars, though, this isn’t merely an expensive toy for cruising around your estate. It’s actually road legal.

In the UK and EU, the Blower Jnr is homologated as an L7e quadricycle, reaching over 50 mph (80 km/h) and offering a certified 75-mile (120 km) range. A separate US program is also planned, though it’s not clear exactly what classification the mini-EV would receive stateside.

Industry observers note that underneath the delightfully old-school bodywork is a decidedly newer 48V electric drivetrain producing 31 hp (23 kW). The electric motor sits across the rear axle, while batteries and power electronics are packaged underneath the car.

The carbon-fiber tub rides on a steel frame with leaf springs and period-style friction dampers, while Brembo disc brakes handle stopping duties up front.

Every car that people ever loved will come back in some electric version that they can actually drive and love.

According to recent reports, there are some particularly fun details, too. The charging port is hidden inside a recreation of the original Bentley’s supercharger housing, while modern necessities like indicators, headlights, and even a reversing camera have been incorporated without ruining the 1929 aesthetic.

The first 25 cars are part of a nearly sold-out run of 99 First Editions. Another 250 configurable cars will follow, bringing total production to just 349 units.

Sources indicate that and “Jnr” definitely doesn’t mean junior pricing. Configurable cars start at £112,500 before taxes – roughly $148,000 at current exchange rates.

Then again, if you’re buying a hand-built miniature electric Bentley, value probably wasn’t the first thing on your mind.
