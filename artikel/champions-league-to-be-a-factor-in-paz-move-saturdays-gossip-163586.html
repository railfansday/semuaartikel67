Recent findings suggest that manchester United are still among the favourites to sign Como midfielder Nico Paz, 22, who has remarked he does not want to return to former club Real Madrid - but Champions League qualification could be a factor in the Argentine's decision. However, United could be upstaged by Arsenal with Mikel Arteta reportedly very keen to bring Paz to the Emirates. (ESPN Argentina via Goal), external.

Industry observers note that liverpool are hopeful of securing a January move for Monaco midfielder Lamine Camara, 22, with Chelsea likely to be out of contention after the Senegal international's failed move to Stamford Bridge in the summer. (GiveMeSport), external.

Everton are monitoring Cagliari forward Paul Mendy, 19, but the Senegalese teenager is also attracting interest from Inter Milan. (TeamTalk), external.

It has been revealed that liverpool, Brighton, Bayern Munich, and Barcelona could enter a bidding war for Borussia Monchengladbach's 17-year-old German midfielder Wael Mohya in the summer. (TalkSport), external.

Sources indicate that arsenal are leading the race for Club Brugge and Portugal winger Carlos Forbs, 22, but will likely have to wait until the end of the season. (Football Insider), external.

It has been revealed that chelsea are interested in signing Velez defender Simon Escobar, 17, but face competition from Brighton for the young Argentine. (TeamTalk), external.

Brighton are considering terminating English midfielder Harry Howell's loan deal at Leicester City as they are becoming frustrated with the 18-year-old's lack of game time. (Football Insider), external.
