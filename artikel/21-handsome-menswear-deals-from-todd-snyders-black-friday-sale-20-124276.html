What are the best things to buy at Todd Snyder?

Sources indicate that there really aren’t any weak links at Todd Snyder—if they make it, it’s probably good. We’re especially partial to the tailoring, which spans the silhouette spectrum and mixes classic fabrics with the occasional showstopper. The Wythe suit, in particular, nods to the fuller ’90s cuts without veering into frumpy territory.
