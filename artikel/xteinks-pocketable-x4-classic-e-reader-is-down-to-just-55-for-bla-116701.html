According to recent reports, unsurprisingly, we’re already seeing some solid Black Friday deals on a wide variety of e-readers. Everything from the Kobo Libra Colour to last-gen Kindle Paperwhite is on sale, but if you’re looking for something much cheaper — or considerably smaller — the Xteink X4 Classic is in a league of its own. Thankfully, the tiny e-reader is down to a new low of $54.87 ($55 off) at Amazon, making it an especially affordable alternative to Amazon’s latest Kindle.

Sources indicate that the X4 Classic is an updated version of Xteink’s original pocket-sized e-reader, one that features a miniature 4.3-inch E Ink display that makes it more pocketable than any current Kindle or Kobo model. Like the pricier X4 Pro — which also happens to be on sale direct from Xteink for $84 — it features both physical page-turning buttons and magnets that let you attach it to compatible smartphones. At just 2.4 ounces and 4.9mm thick, it’s also easy to slip into a pocket, while its larger 920mAh battery can last up to 10 days on a single charge.

That mentioned, unlike the X4 Pro, the Classic lacks a touchscreen and built-in reading light, meaning you’ll need an external light to read in the dark. It also uses a proprietary magnetic charging connector instead of USB-C, and transferring books isn’t as straightforward as on a Kindle or Kobo. However, like the Pro, it does support the excellent CrossPoint Reader software, which you can install yourself to improve the reading experience. You can even use it to read books borrowed through Libby or purchased from stores like Kobo, though it takes a few extra steps to do so. Still, if you want an inexpensive, distraction-free way to read on the go, the X4 Classic is hard to beat for less than half the price of a Kindle.
