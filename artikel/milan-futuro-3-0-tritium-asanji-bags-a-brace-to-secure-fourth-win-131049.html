Milan Futuro kept up their excellent recent form by sweeping aside Tritium 3-0, continuing their push towards the top.

The Futuro came into the game in good form, having won three on the spin. A thrilling 3-2 victory over Virtus Verona was followed by a 2-1 victory against Piacenza in the rescheduled suspended game, and then a 3-0 home win over Virtus CiseranoBergamo before the break put them two points off top.

Recent findings suggest that milan’s mission upon the resumption of Serie D was a quite simple one: to build on the three wins in a row and put some pressure on ChievoVerona at the top, a side that they beat away from home a few weeks ago. They faced a Tritium side who started the day second bottom, with just two wins so far.

Sources indicate that it could hardly have been a better start for the Rossoneri, as they took the lead after less than 13 minutes. Levin Asanji was the player who got it to keep his great form going, finishing with an audacious but wonderfully-executed back-heel flick from a cross to the near post by Emanuele Borsani.

Sources indicate that just after Tritium had squandered a good opportunity to get level after a cut-back was miscontrolled, Milan punished them at the other end in the 70th minute. A sweeping counter-attack saw Asanji run in behind, and he unselfishly squared to Lorenzo Ossola who slotted past the goalkeeper to double the advantage.

Industry observers note that with 12 minutes left, Asanji got his second goal of the game to put the result beyond doubt. It was another counter that was the away side’s undoing: this time Alessandro Bonomi hit the post from a square ball, but Asanji reacted fastest to put the rebound into a virtually unguarded net.
