GTA 6 Isn’t Worth Buying A Fancy Controller For

In all the excitement surrounding Grand Theft Auto VI (check out our 10/10 review), it might be tempting to upgrade your setup. For a game 13 years in the making, is now the time to upgrade my TV? Maybe a PS5 Pro is in order? It's a coincidence I finally upgraded my speaker system after 18 years, I swear. One cheaper upgrade than those above you might be considering is a better controller, like a DualSense Edge or Xbox Elite controller. For all of their myriad upgrades, the most essential reason to ever buy one of these, in my opinion, is for the back paddles. Although they are not technically extra buttons, the ability to map an inconveniently placed button to one of them can make an experience far smoother. Some games make an exceptionally strong case for only playing with paddles. You do a lot of sprinting and picking things up in Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, and having to take my thumb off the right stick (thus temporarily locking the camera in place) is something I find to be a constant annoyance. It's such an improvement to be able to do those things while keeping my thumbs where they belong. Grand Theft Auto VI, on the other hand, is not such an example. Rockstar has finally made a change to the default control scheme it uses in its games, mapping sprint to a click of the left stick, rather than the traditional usage of the X (PlayStation) and A (Xbox) buttons. That alone alleviates a potential argument for needing back paddles (at least as much as one can need such a thing). https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rAhUXOYNuH4&t=1s That's not to say there's literally zero use for one of these pro-style controllers in GTA 6. In our experience, tapping a paddle is arguably still an improvement to clicking the left stick to sprint or holding L1/LB to bring up the weapon wheel. Enabling Focus Mode is done by clicking both sticks, which you might find awkward; accomplishing that with paddles might be comfortable for you. GTA 6 doesn't allow its controls to be freely remapped, though there are some options available (such as reverting to that previous style of sprinting). So while there are some reasons you may want to set up the paddles of your pro controller before diving into GTA, it's safe to say that buying one just for this game ain't worth it.
