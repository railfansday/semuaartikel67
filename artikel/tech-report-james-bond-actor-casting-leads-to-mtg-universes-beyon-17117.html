It's official: Robert Pattinson is the next James Bond, but little did we know that this announcement was going to lead to some major Magic: The Gathering speculation, and it's all because of an Instagram comment.

Following the reveal of Pattinson as the next actor to play the iconic British secret agent, the official "wizards_magic" account was spotted commenting on the post, heavily hinting at a future collaboration. Players are split, but that's largely because no one can agree on exactly how this deal may play out.

It has been revealed that over on Instagram, in response to Robert Pattinson's casting as James Bond, the official MTG Instagram account left the comment "Feeling shaken. Something is stirring...".

According to recent reports, this is undoubtedly a tease for some kind of future collaboration, and fans are convinced it will lead to a Universes Beyond crossover; the response on Reddit is largely negative, too. What they can't agree on, however, is whether we're looking at a Secret Lair drop or a full-blown Standard set.

Industry observers note that what we do know is that standard sets are planned years in advance, and sets for 2030 and beyond are already in the pipeline. This comes directly from our conversations with Roy Graham and Mark Rosewater at MagicCon Amsterdam earlier this year.

If there is a future James Bond Universes Beyond set, it's either been in the works for a long time or won't be released until the next 007 film, which isn't expected until sometime in 2028. Because everything would need to line up, some players think a full set simply isn't on the cards.

That leaves us with the possibility of a Secret Lair bundle or superdrop, which feels much more plausible given the faster turnarounds we've seen for those products. The caveat, however, is that it wouldn't include any materials for Pattinson as Bond, and would instead rely on the past 60+ years of Bond actors and other iconic characters and villains.

Sources indicate that timing is everything, though, so Wizards would want to capitalize on the hype of the casting announcement by releasing something before the end of the year, ideally, or waiting again to have it line up with the release of the next film. Given that we've already mentioned that it's looking like 2028 for that, it wouldn't make sense to tease anything until an announcement is imminent.

With a potential Dune Secret Lair already due between now and the end of the year, and also a possible Spider-Man Commander precon, we'd be keen to see where a possible 007 collaboration would even fit into plans.

Broadly speaking, we would be excited to see what a James Bond Secret Lair would look like, and while we're not a fan of realistic art on Magic cards in full sets, we can suspend our disbelief when it comes to Secret Lair, as it feels much more like a completely optional product compared to a full set.
