It has been revealed that happy Thanksgiving! As you're filling your plate with turkey and mashed potatoes, astrology is still doing its thing. Early on November 26, the Moon moves from playful Gemini into nostalgic Cancer. If you're celebrating Thanksgiving with family or longtime friends, you’re likely to be filled with warm fuzzy feelings. Go ahead and tell everyone how much you love them over a piece of pumpkin pie. However, if you're spending Thanksgiving away from home or with latest friends and acquaintances, you might feel a little lonely. Try to schedule some time for a phone call home or simply take a few minutes to reminisce on your favorite holiday memories.

Also going on: Mars moves from outgoing, creative Leo to careful, hard-working Virgo on November 25, the day before Thanksgiving. This is great news for everyone who's in charge of Thanksgiving planning, prepping, cooking, and cleaning, and less great for anyone who's performing in the spotlight (so don't be surprised if the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade falls flat). However, we're also in the first week of Sagittarius season, so there's still plenty of fun to be had. Read your horoscope for Thanksgiving Day 2026:.

Sources indicate that it's all about family, Aries. Whether it's your parents, siblings, or friends who feel like family, you'll have the best Thanksgiving if you can spend time with people you share decades of memories with. If you can't, call or text with a loved one and share some favorite memories. Oh, and you're usually not taking the lead in planning celebrations, but this year, you can handle more responsibility thanks to Mars in Virgo. Get to it.

Talk, talk, talk, Taurus. With the Moon in your 3rd House of communication, you're feeling extra chatty today. Thanksgiving is a great time to catch up with your loved ones, just make sure that you're not dominating the conversation—let other people talk, too! You might also be feeling more creative thanks to Mars in Virgo, so go ahead and volunteer to make the Thanksgiving centerpieces.

Sources indicate that don't blow your budget, Gemini. The Moon in Cancer is shining on your finances, and let's be real, the holidays can be expensive. Try to avoid any Black Friday impulse buys that you don't actually need. With Mars in Virgo, you're in a great position to work through any family-related organizing that needs to be done, like figuring out the Secret Santa proposal or taking the lead on planning the next big family dinner.

Sources indicate that in your feelings, Cancer? With the Moon—your sign's ruler—in your sign, you're at peak Cancer today. That means you're feeling even more emotional and caring today. Make sure you stick some tissues in your purse for when you start tearing up over how fast your niece is growing. With Mars in Virgo, you're feeling more talkative than usual, so use today as an opportunity to reminisce about past holiday memories—one of your favorite things to do.

Trust yourself, Leo. With the Moon in Cancer, your intuition is stronger than ever, which can help you decipher complicated family dynamics or even just when it's time to take the stuffing out of the oven. With Mars in Virgo, your financial smarts are higher than usual right now. If there's any last-minute shopping that needs to be done on Thanksgiving Eve or Thanksgiving, go ahead and volunteer. And those Black Friday sales don't know what's coming for them.

Friendsgiving plans, Virgo? The Moon in Cancer turns your thoughts towards your friends. If you're spending Thanksgiving with your besties, you're all set. But if you have plans with family or a romantic partner, take some time to connect with your pals too, even if it's only over text. And some great news: Mars is in your sign, giving you a welcome boost of confidence and assertiveness that'll last for the rest of the year.

It has been revealed that zoom out, Libra. Thanksgiving's Moon in Cancer has you preoccupied with the impact you'll leave on the world. That's great, but make sure to take time to appreciate this little things, like your mom's gravy, too. Mars in Virgo gives you the opportunity to cut someone or something out of your life for good, like a toxic ex who hits you up on Thanksgiving Eve. The block button is ready for you.

Traveling for Thanksgiving? If yes, that's great: With the Moon in Cancer, you're at peak wanderlust right now. If you're not, take time to reminisce about past favorite vacations, ideally with the people who were traveling with you. Meanwhile, Mars in Virgo gives you the superpower to take your group chat's Friendsgiving or Black Friday shopping aims from idea to actual reality. Don't waste these vibes.

Dessert, Sagittarius? The urge for Thanksgiving sex is strong, thanks to the Moon in Cancer. If you're coupled up or dating someone with potential, this is great: You can have some hot sex and end the holiday feeling even closer than you were before. But if you're looking for something casual, you'll likely catch feelings. When you go back to work after the holiday, Mars in Virgo gets you motivated to prove your worth to your boss. Don't send emails during Thanksgiving dinner, but it's OK to take some time to map out a initiative of attack for a promotion.

It takes two, Capricorn. With the Moon in Cancer, you're feeling extra close to whoever your "other half" is, like a romantic partner or longtime best friend. Take some time to let them know how much you appreciate them. And go ahead and cancel your post-Thanksgiving nap. With Mars in Virgo, you're feeling motivated to use your free time for studying, planning upcoming travel, or reading up on your latest interest.

Sources indicate that it's the little things, Aquarius. The Moon in Cancer makes you appreciate details like your dad's corny jokes or your best friend's memory for your favorite holiday cocktails. Make sure you let them know how much you care. With Mars in Virgo, you have the opportunity to start working towards the next, more evolved version of yourself. It’s not too early to start planning your “fresh year, new Aquarius” strategy.

In a recent development, grab your camera, Pisces. You're already one of the most creative signs of the zodiac, and the Moon in Cancer is boosting your artistic abilities even more. Meanwhile, Mars in Virgo helps you approach any relationship issues with a romantic partner or best friend from a place of calm and confidence.
