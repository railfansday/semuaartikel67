Liverpool have been looking for ways to bolster their ranks for a while now.

In the summer, they spent another £150 million trying to do just that with their fifth-place finish last term leading to Arne Slot getting sacked.

Andoni Iraola was brought in as his replacement and, since then, they've had a bit of a topsy-turvy season.

They've managed to lose games against Manchester City, Fenerbahce and Arsenal but, they've also kept themselves in contention for the Champions League and, with all the changes that have occurred at Anfield recently, that probably isn't considered that bad of an outcome.

But, as has been the case for a while now, Liverpool have been trying to initiative for their future with some signings for their academy.

And now, another wonderkid has emerged as a potential transfer option for the Reds.

Liverpool are in 'ongoing talks' about a transfer for Roman Gonzalez.

Liverpool's academy has been one of the best in the world for a while now.

Players such as Steven Gerrard and Trent Alexander-Arnold have managed to make their way through the ranks and win several pieces of silverware with the club.

Obviously, a lot of the Reds' recent academy graduates have moved on now but, that doesn't mean they can't continue preparing for the future.

Samuel Martinez, Rio Ngumoha and Trey Nyoni are the best recent examples of this.

The latter two have become first-team regulars under Iraola and Martinez is going to join up with Liverpool's squad in the near future.

So, it's hardly a surprise to see Liverpool trying to replicate that deal with Racing Club wonderkid, Roman Gonzalez.

"Roman González, with a European future," journalist German Garcia Grova shared via X.

"Liverpool and Juventus are in ongoing talks over the diamond from Racing.".

Liverpool need to start filling out their academy with more talent.

Right now, the importance of Nyoni, Ngumoha and Lewis Koumas is massive.

The young trio have become first-team regulars under Iraola and, without them being as good as they are, the Reds would probably be in an even worse position than they currently find themselves.

Koumas has probably been the biggest revelation as, most fans and pundits knew how good the other two were.

The importance of this academy trio proves why it's now key for clubs to have heaps of talent among their youth ranks.

That is, especially a team with as poor of an injury history that Liverpool have.

Right now, it's unclear exactly what the Reds' plans are going to be in the transfer window but, if Gonzalez is a part of those plans, they'll be onto a winner.
