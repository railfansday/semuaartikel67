Christian Pulisic revealed his emotions after scoring a brilliant bicycle kick during the USA’s final game under Mauricio Pochettino.

It was known before the USMNT’s two-legged UEFA Nations League quarter-final tie against Haiti that it would be Pochettino’s last two games, given that he has an agreement in place to take over the job at Manchester United vacated following Michael Carrick’s dismissal.

In a recent development, the stars and stripes certainly gave him a fitting send-off, as they won the first leg 6-0 a few days ago and then completed the job with a 2-0 victory in Cincinnati last night. Pulisic got the first goal after a brace in the previous rout, and it was an incredible overhead kick.

Pulisic was actually overlooked by Pochettino for the September-October window – partly for fitness reasons – and he spoke to reporters after the game to share his thoughts after an emotional evening. He was called the ‘best player on the pitch’ by the US media, and his words are via MilanNews.

An overhead kick goal is pretty special….

Industry observers note that “I’ve never scored a goal like that, it was special. Weston crossed the ball and it was the only thing I could really do, so I thought I had to give it a try. It was really nice to see the ball go in.”.

Is it nice to get a victory on Pochettino’s farewell day?

“Yes, obviously he’s leaving now, so it’s nice to end with a good performance and a nice win for the team.”.

How would you describe this particular group, a mix of veterans and young players?

“It’s really nice. It’s been fun working with everyone. We got to meet some of the upcoming guys, even some young talent, and obviously there’s still some of the same core group, so it’s a really great team. I really like where we are right now and I think we’re still growing and have a great future ahead of us.”.

What does Pochettino’s last match leave you with?

In a recent development, “We’re in a good place. Overall, I think the team has improved. I think there’s a good culture within the group, and I think we should be really excited about the future.”.

What kind of an impact has Julian Hall made?

Industry observers note that “It was fantastic playing with him. He scored a goal, but also just for his overall play, making lots of runs and causing problems for the other team. He did a great job, fitting in with this team and making his mark.”.

What was Mauricio Pochettino’s farewell message to you this evening?

Recent findings suggest that “Simply that we have a truly extraordinary culture. We’ve built something truly special and that a bright future awaits us. And to continue that by letting the rest of the team support us.”.

Does it really matter who the coach is at this point?

Sources indicate that “I mean, not that it doesn’t matter. Ultimately, it’s not our choice, as players. We hope whoever arrives will be able to capitalise on the things we do so well and lead us in the right direction.”.
