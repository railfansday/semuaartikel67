Industry News: OpenAI acquires Odessia, travel startup created by Sonder founder

🌐 Source: TechCrunch • 📅 10 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (620 words, 📚 Deep Dive)

  • According to recent reports, terms of the deal were not disclosed, but this looks like an acqui-hire.
  • It has been revealed that an OpenAI spokesperson confirmed the hire to TechCrunch.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, openAI has acquired an AI travel agent called Odessia, according to Odessia’s founder, Francis Davidson. Davidson indicates in a post announcing the deal that he’s now working for OpenAI, helping the AI lab with the future of travel.

According to recent reports, terms of the deal were not disclosed, but this looks like an acqui-hire. Davidson’s announcement focused largely on how he built his AI travel agent Odessia by directing teams of AI agents. The year-old Odessia raised a $6 million pre-seed round led by Sequoia and launched its AI travel agent around five months ago. Davidson says that it had helped early adopters proposal millions of dollars’ worth of travel, but the tool has already been shut down.

It has been revealed that an OpenAI spokesperson confirmed the hire to TechCrunch. “We’re thrilled to welcome Francis to OpenAI. Francis is a proven founder who has built multiple businesses to help people travel, and we’re excited he’s chosen to build with us.”.

However the AI lab has not announced latest travel-specific products or intends. It does, however, count such companies as Bookings.com, Expedia, and KAYAK as customers or partners. Travel is also one of the main areas that people are using personal AI assistants to help with. And, back in July 2025, when OpenAI rolled out its ChatGPT agent, it mentioned travel as one of its potential uses.

According to recent reports, openAI’s spokesperson didn’t have more to share on its plans for travel-related products, except to say that travel is one of the areas that chatbot consumers frequently ask about. “1.2 billion people turn to ChatGPT each week for help with everyday tasks — from understanding their health, figuring out what to make for dinner, building a budget, and even planning a trip. We see a real opportunity to make these experiences even more useful,” the spokesperson remarked.

Acquiring Odessia is interesting for another reason. Davidson is well known in the travel industry after he founded a travel startup called Sonder as a college student in 2014. It was sort of like Airbnb, but it focused on short-term leasing of upscale apartments in major cities worldwide. The pandemic badly hurt it, and as it climbed out of that rut, it went public via a SPAC but didn’t raise as much money as it needed, Davidson later stated in a post announcing his resignation as CEO in June 2025.

When a key partnership with Marriott fell through, the company went into bankruptcy and wound down. The bankruptcy trustee sold Sonder’s name, trademarks, and website domain in 2026 to TravelAI, and that website operates as a curated guide to the kind of travel rental properties that Sonder was once known for. Prior to its downfall, Davidson had built Sonder into a unicorn with over $600 million in revenues. While Sonder was not, ultimately, a success story, Davidson is the kind of battle-scarred founder that Silicon Valley often values.

So we’ll have to wait and see what OpenAI has in store with travel now that he’s joined the organization.
