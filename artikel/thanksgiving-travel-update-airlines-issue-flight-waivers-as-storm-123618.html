Thanksgiving travel update: Airlines issue flight waivers as storms loom

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (172 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Storm systems on both U.S. coasts are expected to disrupt Thanksgiving air travel, with potential delays and cancellations at major airports.
  • Airlines have issued weather-related rebooking policies for affected airports, offering more flexibility to travelers.
  • The West Coast faces heavy rain and wind, while a strong East Coast storm could bring significant delays to major hubs from Atlanta to Boston.
  • Travelers are advised to monitor weather updates, use airline apps for rebooking and notifications, and consider adjusting plans as disruptions may cascade across networks.
  • Weather waivers vary by airline and airport, so check specific policies before making changes.
  • Airlines typically do not cover hotel, meal or transportation costs for weather-related disruptions, but travel insurance or certain credit cards with built-in coverage may help.
  • Thanksgiving Day is usually quieter at airports, but the busiest travel day is the Sunday after the holiday.
