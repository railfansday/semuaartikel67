Ruben Amorim is not a coach who makes a habit of singling out individuals, but he made an exception in Saturday’s press conference.

As MilanNews recall, on the eve of the game against Frosinone, Amorim bluntly admitted that Christian Pulisic and Adrien Rabiot are the ‘irreplaceable’ players in the current squad. Their characteristics and their quality make them unique, in the eyes of the coach and many fans.

In a recent development, when the American and the Frenchman don’t play, the team’s fluidity and effectiveness suffer significantly. There is a stark contract between Milan when Rabiot and Pulisic play versus when both do not feature.

Amorim’s words were clear in his pre-match presser: “Every player appreciates having the club’s trust. And Pulisic’s new contract has helped. When he wasn’t playing, I told you he was still notable for Milan, and we can’t forget about the injured players.

In a recent development, “Pulisic is the only player we can’t replace today: the way he moves between the lines, we don’t have another Pulisic. In the last few European matches, we missed him; we had to manage him. If he’s not on the pitch, given his characteristics, you feel it.

Recent findings suggest that “And you understand how vital he is. He doesn’t talk much, but he speaks a lot on the pitch with his play. He always wants the ball. He and Rabiot always lift the team up. I don’t like to talk about individuals, but I have to.

“I think everyone can understand that. So Pulisic is our present, he will be our future, and I think he has the potential to do even more.”.

It’s worth noting that the coach emphasised the importance of Pulisic’s contract renewal too. This is also why the club are doing everything possible to reach an agreement for Rabiot.

Recent findings suggest that cardinale called him a top player and candidly admitted that he adores him, also emphasising how he respects his family. These statements are not coincidental, given that it is his mother who represents the midfielder.

Speaking of which, discussions with Véronique Rabiot are progressing positively. A meeting took place before the derby, which ended with a 3-1 win with a goal for the former Juventus and Marseille midfielder.

It has been revealed that the player is happy with the Rossoneri and wants to stay: a definitive agreement on the financial terms needs to be reached. Milan are also willing to adjust the player’s salary in order to secure him for a few more years at Milanello.
