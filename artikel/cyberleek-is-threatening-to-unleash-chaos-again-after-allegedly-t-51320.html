Cyberleek is back. The account behind a trove of unprecedented gameplay footage leaks for Grand Theft Auto 6 prior to its launch this week is once again threatening to release an allegedly working build of the game online, and may have already done so. And yes, the account is once again demanding people mine crypto in order for the build to become playable.

“It is done,” began a upcoming announcement posted on the Cyberleek account’s website on November 19. “CL holds the full November 2026 build of Grand Theft Auto VI, and it is going out by torrent. There are reports of files for a build floating around, though Kotaku is unable to verify them and whether they are legit. Cyberleek claims to have withheld the executable file needed to actually run the leaked Xbox build, and will only release that last piece if the market cap for the $CYBERLEEK meme coin reaches $15 million. “If the Xbox build cannot be made to work and support holds, CL will work to get a PS5 build â a fallback, most likely never required,” the post reads. “Either way: the Xbox build is going out. No double dipping for Take-Two/Rockstar.”.

In a recent development, cyberleek threatened to release a build of GTA 6 before back in October. The coin needed to hit $30 million, and the account released a series of escalating leaks, including nude footage of Lucia, one of the two playable characters. But the coin ended up tanking in value instead, and whoever was behind the Cyberleek account disappeared again. It’s unknown if this is the same person who was releasing footage throughout the summer or if multiple people are involved.

Cyberleek already appeared to cash out on the meme coin once before, and it doesn’t seem like Rockstar Games or parent tech giant Take-Two have been able to locate the individuals behind the stolen assets.
