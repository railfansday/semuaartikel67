15 best all-inclusive resorts in the US

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (190 words, 📖 Standard)
  • All-inclusive resorts in the U.S. span a wide range of experiences, from luxury ranches and wellness retreats to beachfront escapes and family-friendly lodges.
  • These properties typically include meals, select drinks and various activities in the nightly rate, but inclusions can vary—especially regarding alcoholic beverages and premium experiences.
  • Many resorts feature unique settings, such as historic hot springs, working farms, private islands and rustic cabins, catering to different travel styles and preferences.
  • Minimum stay requirements, seasonal closures and specific check-in days are common, so flexibility and careful review of what is covered are important when booking.
  • Alcoholic beverages are often excluded or only partially included, with some resorts offering only wine with dinner or charging extra for premium drinks.
  • Certain activities, spa treatments or excursions may incur additional fees beyond the inclusive package, so reviewing the fine print is essential.
  • Some resorts enforce minimum stay durations, select check-in days or have age restrictions, particularly at adults-only or family-focused properties.
