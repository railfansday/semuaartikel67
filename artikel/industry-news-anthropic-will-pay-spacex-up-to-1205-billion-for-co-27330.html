Anthropic's Claude models are at the forefront of the artificial intelligence (AI) software space and have helped the business achieve stellar growth. The tech giant's revenue increased 47% sequentially in the third quarter to $11.7 billion, and revenue in the period was up 1,127% year over year.

According to recent reports, along with the rapid scaling of its AI software services, Anthropic is making massive compute purchases -- and Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +3.16%) has emerged as one of the company's most important partners. Read on for a breakdown of Anthropic's $120.5 billion contract with SpaceX and what it could mean for the two companies.

It has been revealed that the contract size is notably larger than the $85.7 billion that SpaceX raised in its June initial public offering (IPO), underscoring how quickly Anthropic has become one of the company's most vital customers. On the other hand, whether the full $120.5 billion deal is actually realized isn't set in stone.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/17/26.

Industry observers note that the contract has lock-in clauses that extend through the first quarter of 2027, but after that, either party is free to back out with 90 days' notice. That creates a dynamic in which maintaining Anthropic as a customer takes on significant importance for SpaceX and its shareholders.

According to recent reports, for example, SpaceX guided for its first-quarter sales to come in between $33 billion and $37 billion -- and its compute sales to Anthropic should contribute $9.3 billion in revenue for the period. Further out, there are some significant causes for uncertainty.

It has been revealed that if compute prices come down, Anthropic would have plenty of incentive to back out of the deal. Anthropic is also building its own data centers, and it may reduce reliance on SpaceX as it brings more of its own compute capacity online. Alternatively, it's also possible that compute prices could actually increase -- giving SpaceX cause to seek higher rates.

Exactly how the contract's realization shakes out remains to be seen, but the deal sets up a dynamic in which SpaceX and Anthropic's relationship will be very vital to each firm's stock performance in the coming years.
