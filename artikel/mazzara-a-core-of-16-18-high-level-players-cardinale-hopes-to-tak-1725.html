Gerry Cardinale has invested heavily in AC Milan recently and there are no plans to slow down anytime soon.

Since the restructuring of the vendor loan, Cardinale has completely changed his ownership style. More actively involved, the American has seemingly fallen in love with the club and has become desperate to see it succeed.

Glimpses were seen before the mercato, with him regularly visiting the training ground. However, his work since then – spending €150 million on transfers and attending every Serie A game so far – shows the level of dedication he has to the cause.

In January, the American is expected to spend heavily, too. A €70 million bid for Endrick is being prepared, whilst he is also determined to sign Goncalo Inacio. It’s clear that he wants to raise the level of the starters and those who will contribute from the bench.

In an editorial for MilanNews today, Pietro Mazzara looked at the current squad and what Cardinale prepares to do.

Recent findings suggest that “AC Milan’s transfer market is poised for significant events because Gerry Cardinale has no intention of stopping. His comments in recent days, both public and private, are a clear statement of what the AC Milan owner intends to do to bring AC Milan back to the top.

Industry observers note that “Whether he succeeds or not will only be determined by time and the type of decisions he makes, but he certainly has the desire, vision, and financial resources. One can expect anything from someone who beat out Netflix for the Warner Bros. acquisition.

“Among the many things he has learned is that the climate of mediocrity surrounding results has undermined the mentality of a club and an environment that, instead, must strive to always be at the top.

“But returning to those levels requires several factors: a clear decision-making structure (which will be finalised by Markus Krösche, who will always report to Cardinale, who will always have the final say and be involved in negotiations), strong players in the squad, a political presence in the halls of power, a connection with the fans, and credibility.

It has been revealed that “What may seem like slogans in themselves are actually manifestos that one can legitimately believe or not. The only way to evaluate Cardinale’s work is to observe how he operates and then draw conclusions.

“He’s fired up, and between January and next summer there will be some serious transfer campaigns. The reason is simple: there’s a need to significantly raise the squad’s level, creating a core of 16-18 players of a certain caliber who can be joined by young players to develop and develop.

“This is why Milan will be looking for not only technically strong players, but also leaders who can complement those already in the Milan core group.

“It’s crucial to raise the team’s personality, including strong personalities who can provide a different kind of motivation during the day-to-day at Milanello. These players, inevitably, cost money, but Cardinale doesn’t want to set limits.

It has been revealed that “This doesn’t mean Milan will sign a player worth one hundred million euros, but it does mean they can continue along the operational path laid out by Diego Moreira. All of this is in concert and in line with Ruben Amorim, who is fiercely defended by the AC Milan owner and with whom he intends to begin a long-term relationship.

“And the vision is to acquire the Van Dijks in the pre-prime phase rather than signing them at 35 (assuming, for the sake of argument, that I’d sign someone like that now).

It has been revealed that “In my opinion, a Sandro Tonali- style signing is needed. And it’s not out of the question that Milan could try to bring him back in the coming years, even though he presently plays for a club as complex to negotiate with as Tottenham.

“Meanwhile, discussions are ongoing to finalise the renewal of Adrien Rabiot and Alphadjo Cisse.”.
