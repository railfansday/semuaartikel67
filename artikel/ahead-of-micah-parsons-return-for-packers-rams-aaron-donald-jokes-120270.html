Ahead of Micah Parsons' return for Packers-Rams, Aaron Donald jokes: 'Micah, if you want to take one more week …'

Three-time Defensive Player of the Year Aaron Donald said what the Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams might all be thinking: "I wish he would stay out one more week.".

Industry observers note that donald was referring to Packers edge rusher Micah Parsons. A member of the Packers might also have applied the sentiment to Rams edge rusher Myles Garrett.

Instead, the NFL's first Thanksgiving Eve game will feature a matchup of two teams who spent big to upgrade their pass rush recently — before injuries sunk their hopes within months.

The Packers dealt their 2026 and 2027 first-round picks as well as defensive tackle Kenny Clark to the Dallas Cowboys in August 2025 in exchange for Parsons. He raced to 12.5 sacks last year as the Packers were 9-3-1 before Parsons tore his ACL and meniscus last Dec. 14.

After 11 months, the Packers activated him this week with the expectation he'll debut Wednesday in first-year defensive coordinator Jonathan Gannon's defense.

Allow Donald, who emerged from a two-year retirement Aug. 30 to rejoin the Rams, to wish that Parsons would wait another week.

It has been revealed that "A fan of Micah, I think he's one hell of a football player, fast, explosive, plays with an unbelievable motor," Donald told reporters on Sunday. "If he does play this week, that's going to be huge for them. Micah, if you want to take one more week to get yourself 100%, make sure everything is where it needs to be, I'd like to see you on TV.

"I know they're going to be happy to have him back.".

To the Packers' dismay, Garrett is back in the mix as well.

Myles Garrett will play his fourth game with the Rams since joining Los Angeles in June.

Sources indicate that the reigning Defensive Player of the Year missed seven games this season when swelling in his knee dictated in-season surgery. The injury was a major blow after the Rams traded edge rusher Jared Verse alongside their 2027 first-, 2028 second- and 2029 third-round picks to acquire Garrett on June 1. The Rams won the Super Bowl last time it was at their home base, SoFi Stadium. Their all-in actions chased a repeat this season as the game returns to SoFi, and as 38-year-old quarterback Matthew Stafford's career nears its twilight.

Garrett returned Nov. 8 against the Washington Commanders to the tune of three sacks and six pressures. He registered another pressure in 17 snaps the following week against the Arizona Cardinals.

Expect Packers quarterback Jordan Love to be his next target.

It has been revealed that "We played Myles last year, we know what he's about," Love told reporters. "He's a speed guy. He's got power though as well. So he's a great edge rusher, but you got to have a proposal. Obviously we're on the road, so crowd noise will be a little bit of a factor. But you got to be able to be dialed into the snap count and get off on time.

"Because if you miss a step, Myles is the guy that will be right around you.".

Garrett and Parsons are not identical players. Each levies a quick get-off to frustrate opposing offensive linemen, but at 6-foot-4 and 272 pounds, Garrett imposes with size beyond Parsons' 6-3, 250-pound frame.

Each player has flashed against the pass and run. But pass rushing is both players' specialty. One of the NFL's go-to counters for pass rushers of Garrett and Parsons' multi-year All-Pro ilk: establishing the run to keep defenders honest.

That recipe looks far more promising for one team Wednesday than the other.

It has been revealed that while the Rams will hurt from the absence of receivers Puka Nacua (season-ending knee surgery) and Davante Adams (hamstring surgery on Oct. 20, with a 6-10 week recovery), Los Angeles has established a diverse offensive attack that also capitalizes on tight ends and running backs.

Recent findings suggest that in addition to ranking top-10 in passing yards and touchdowns, the Rams rank seventh each in rushing yards and touchdowns. When Parsons chases Stafford, the Rams should have outlets.

"Anytime you got Matthew Stafford, no matter who's out there in the field, you have a great chance to win," Parsons mentioned. "And they got two incredible backs [in Kyren Williams and Blake Corum], they have a great tight end personnel. I mean, you're seeing a similar variation in Buffalo. They're running a lot of 12P (two tight-end personnel) with Dawson Knox and [Dalton] Kincaid with James Cook out of the backfield. So we're seeing that kind of variation across the league with guys who can fit that personnel set. And [the] Rams [have] been running a lot of 13 personnel [three tight ends]. So it really doesn't surprise me. When you have a great offensive coordinator like that and you can adjust to your system and the players you have, that's why [head coach Sean McVay] is one of the great ones.

"Regardless of who plays, we got our hands full on Wednesday.".

The Packers' offense will, too.

Love deserves credit for how he's carried the Packers this year, his 2,838 passing yards ranking third in the league while his 20 passing touchdowns tie for eighth. He has elevated receivers Christian Watson and Matthew Golden into a formidable duo who have combined for 12 touchdowns with each accounting for more than 85 receiving yards per game. Green Bay's challenge, as Minnesota exposed in the Packers' last outing: They have no run game.

Industry observers note that injuries and time on the commissioner's exempt list after allegations of domestic violence have limited running back Josh Jacobs to three games, with Jacobs not expected to return yet this week. Add in a series of injuries to the Packers' offensive line, and the same Green Bay team that ranks third in passing yards (268.1 per game) ranks dead last in rushing yards per game (67) and yards per carry (3.06).

The result: Opponents know the Packers are overwhelmingly going to pass, and defenses pass-rush accordingly. Love has faced the quickest time to pressure through Week 11, at 2.41 seconds, per Next Gen Stats. He's coming off a game in which the Vikings pressured him on average after 2.33 seconds, the second-fastest mark of his career, generating pressure on 40% of his dropbacks — the most of Love's career.

Green Bay could receive offensive line reinforcements in center Sean Rhyan and right guard Kevin Zeitler, each of whom is officially questionable per their game status. But will the need to lean on the pass give Garrett and Donald similar confidence to what the Vikings flashed before the Packers' bye?

Recent findings suggest that "It starts up front," Love noted. "We got to handle business. We got to be able to block the front, give me some time. And in the run game, we got to be able to get hands on people, create some lanes in the run game.

"You look at their team, the front definitely stands out.".

If the regular season ended today, the 6-4 Packers would clinch the final NFC wild card while the 6-4 Rams would miss the cut by two spots (the Seattle Seahawks right now slot eighth).

Those odds will shift after Wednesday.

The Packers' 40% chance to make the playoffs will improve to 62% if Green Bay wins on Wednesday, per The Athletic's NFL playoff simulator that integrates thousands of possible season outcomes. A loss would drop the Packers' chance to 25%, the major gap in large part due to the tiebreaker that Los Angeles would hold.

It has been revealed that the Rams have less upside from a win on Wednesday, per The Athletic's predictor, their 34% chance improving to only 47% if they clinch win No. 7. But they have more downside: A loss to the Packers, and the Rams' chance of advancing to the postseason drops to 16%, per The Athletic. Even with a slew of injuries, that would disappoint a club that spent its offseason making a splash for Garrett, acquiring both of the Chiefs' veteran cornerbacks and reuniting with Donald all to upgrade the defense in hopes of maximizing Stafford's waning time.

It has been revealed that each team is aware of its closing postseason window. And each team is aware that the playoff formula gives no grace on account of injuries — particularly in a crowded NFC.

"I mean, we're in this conference where it's like you have to play perfect," Parsons remarked. "We don't got the ability [like in] basketball to lose by 40 one night and come win by 30 the next week. That's not in our future.

"Every game has to be perfect.".

Will Parsons' return, or Garrett's continued settling into his team, move the needle?

Restrictions on snap count for Parsons in his first game back would not be surprising. But the Packers have worked him through rehabilitation drills and practices, including with pads, assessing his football skills as well as the symmetry of strength between his quads, hips and calves. Parsons' surgery team led by Dr. Neal ElAttrache also has monitored his post-operation recovery with cameras and force plates that detect strength, speed or functional deficits, ElAttrache told Yahoo Sports by text Tuesday night. The goal with these barometers: Return not only healthy but also with a reduced chance of re-injury.

Garrett, meanwhile, said he felt rusty in his return vs. the Commanders. Rams head coach Sean McVay qualified that assessment this week.

Recent findings suggest that "Rusty for him, that's a loose term," McVay said. "I think it's more playing within the system, understanding his opp[ortunities] and understanding who he's rushing with. I think getting the call, applying it, seeing the offense. I think anybody would be rusty. He practiced that whole week [before his return], but before that, he didn't have the accumulation of snaps that a lot of the other guys did or that he would have had if he was healthy the whole time.".

For both teams, and their ramping-back-up edge rushers, the stakes will echo Wednesday night across the very field each club hopes to revisit in February.

In a recent development, "Where we're at in the season, like I remarked, every one matters and you don't want to lose games because you're worried about the outcome," Parsons said. "At this point, we can say every game is going to be the difference between playoffs or Cancun.".
