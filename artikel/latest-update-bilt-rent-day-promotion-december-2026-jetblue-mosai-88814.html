According to recent reports, bilt is the platform known for providing rewards for housing payments. Earlier this year we saw massive changes at Bilt, including an overhaul of the credit card portfolio, plus new ways that housing rewards are offered.

According to recent reports, there are many innovative things about Bilt, including that there’s a Bilt Rent Day promotion on the first day of each month. With a latest month being right around the corner, I’d like to cover the details of the December 2026 Bilt Rent Day offer, as it includes both a JetBlue Mosaic elite status challenge, as well as an up to 100% bonus on statement credit redemptions (which isn’t a very good deal). It’s interesting to see a second month in a row with no transfer bonus….

There are a couple of aspects to the Bilt Rent Day promotion, including the ability to earn bonus points (which is the same every month), plus at least one additional promotion.

Note that Bilt Rent Day promotions are generally valid from 12:00AM ET until 11:59PM PT, so technically you have around 27 hours to take advantage of them.

Bilt has a portfolio of credit cards, including the no annual fee Bilt Blue Card (learn more), $95 annual fee Bilt Obsidian Card (learn more), and $495 annual fee Bilt Palladium Card (learn more) (Rates & Fees). Personally, I think the Bilt Palladium Card is by far the most lucrative.

It has been revealed that on the first day of each month, you earn double those rewards on these cards, and earn up to 1,000 bonus points each month. It could be worth strategically making some purchases on the first day of each month to maximize this as much as possible.

For example, with double points, the Bilt Palladium Card offers 4x points on everyday spending on the first day of the month, so that’s pretty awesome (up to a cap of $500 worth of spending, since that would earn you 1,000 bonus points).

In a recent development, let me cut to the chase — don’t redeem your points this way. It’s a bad value. Ordinarily you get 0.55 cents per Bilt point for statement credit redemptions, so even with a 100% bonus, you’re looking at getting 1.1 cents per point. Hard pass.

In a recent development, regardless of which tier you initially match to, the status you get to keep is based on the activity over the course of those three months. As a reminder, you earn one tile for every $100 spent on JetBlue, or every $1,000 spent on a co-branded JetBlue credit card. So renewing status requires anywhere from $1,000 to $4,000 of airfare spending, or $10,000 to $40,000 of credit card spending.

It has been revealed that this of course comes at a generally useful time for JetBlue elite status, given that the airline is introducing first class, with space available upgrades being an elite perk. However, it’s worth emphasizing that this really isn’t such a special offer, as it’s basically just JetBlue’s standard status match challenge offer. Except in this case, you can unlock it with Bilt elite status rather than airline elite status.

It has been revealed that on the first day of each month, Bilt offers a Rent Day promotion. In addition to earning up to double points for purchases, for December 2026, there are two offers members can take advantage of.

Recent findings suggest that one offer is for up to a 100% bonus on statement credit redemptions, which can get you at most 1.1 cents per point of value. Please don’t redeem your points that way. JetBlue is also offering a status match challenge, whereby Bilt members can receive Mosaic 1 or Mosaic 2 status for a period of 90 days, and can then extend status through early 2028, with the opportunity to earn up to Mosaic 3 status, based on how many tiles are earned.
