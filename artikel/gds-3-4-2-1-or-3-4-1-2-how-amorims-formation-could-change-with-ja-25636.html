AC Milan are tempted by the idea of signing Endrick and Nicolo Tresoldi, but how would Ruben Amorim’s system change?

There has been plenty of talk about January signings, but as much as they would be good to bring, they have to be the right fit. After all, signing a name without thinking ahead will only be problematic. So, the right calculations must be made by Amorim and the recruitment team.

This morning, Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below) looked at how Amorim’s system could change with the arrival of Endrick or Tresoldi and it’s a pretty simple idea.

According to recent reports, with Endrick, there would be ‘fewer adjustments’ thanks to his versatility and also his ability to play in the formation already. So, it would see him playing behind the striker alongside Christian Pulisic or Alphadjo Cisse.

For Tresoldi’s arrival, things would have to change. He’s played most of his career as a lone striker, but that would probably change. Instead, he’d probably make up a two-man strike force, alongside Ramos, and then Christian Pulisic or Alphadjo Cisse would play behind him.

It’s a logical choice, but it would mean Amorim would have to change his ideas mid-season. Ultimately, the mercato will choose the route of the Portuguese’s team.
