iPhone Handoff is a new feature in iOS 27 that lets you use the same phone number across two different iPhones. However, the feature requires carrier support, so it isn’t available to everyone. Here are the carriers that currently support iPhone Handoff and those that have promised support.

Recent findings suggest that with iPhone Handoff, you can use the same phone number on two different iPhones. Your number automatically moves between the two phones, depending on which one you pick up. This includes calls, voicemail, messages, and Find My location.

According to recent reports, simply unlock whichever device you want to use and you’re ready to go. Other data synced with iCloud will automatically upgrade across your devices. Third-party apps work across both devices, following each app’s own syncing behavior.

iPhone Handoff is available on iOS 27 and later. The phones must be connected to Wi-Fi, share the same Apple Account, use Messages in iCloud, and be signed into FaceTime and iMessage with the same Apple Account.

The biggest limiting factor of iPhone Handoff is that it requires carrier support. Currently, just three carriers support the feature:.

What about carriers that are working on the feature? In the United States, Verizon has promised it “will support iPhone Handoff,” but it’s unclear when the feature will launch. Verizon says it will charge $10 per month for the service.

Industry observers note that AT&T, meanwhile, has no plans to support iPhone Handoff. In a statement in September, the carrier said that its focus “remains on delivering innovations that make life simpler and keep customers connected with confidence.” The carrier noted that there “could be potential in capabilities like iPhone Handoff,” but that customers should use “existing eSIM activation and transfer options” instead.

No other carriers have promised support for iPhone Handoff, so it’s unclear how swiftly the feature will expand around the world.

In my testing, iPhone Handoff works really well. I have it set up between my iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo. I use the iPhone Duo 90% of the time, but when I do pick up my iPhone 18 Pro, my number instantly switches to it.
