As a chronic nail biter (gross, I know), I’ve tried just about every type of manicure out there in an attempt to heal my nails and kick my habit. From acrylics and tips to builder gel and dip powder, nothing has ever worked for me. But one simple option has never let me down: press-ons. They provide a natural-looking manicure without damaging your nails, take just minutes to apply, and can be done from the comfort of your own home. There’s no drying, intense filing, or pricey appointments required. What’s not to love?

It has been revealed that recently, I fell down a glue-filled rabbit hole in search of the best press-on nails out there. Every week, I’d apply a new set in the name of research, meticulously assessing each for quality, durability, and longevity. I have very small nail beds, so finding a size, shape, and length that fit my fingertips was essential. After about a year of testing (and an absurd amount of nail glue), there was a clear winner: Glamnetic.

Industry observers note that we praised the product last year, and that praise still holds true. The application is not only simple, but I actually find it quite therapeutic. I start by pushing back my cuticles with the included wooden stick and filing the entire surface of the nails, not just the edges. Doing this helps the press-ons adhere better and last longer. Next, I match each of my fingers to the best-fitting press-on. Finally, I remove any oils from my nails using the alcohol pad, and glue on the set. I start with my pinkies and move toward my thumbs, placing a dot of glue at the base of each press-on and holding it down for about 30 to 45 seconds. Before I know it, I’m left with a perfect manicure that’s affordable, natural-looking, and long-lasting.

Sources indicate that while it’s always fun to experiment with different shapes and lengths, my personal favorite is the brand’s “Short Oval” collection. For under $20, each set comes with 30 nails in varying sizes, and all of the above materials. They are incredibly durable without looking or feeling thick, and each set lasts about two weeks.

Whether you prefer a milky pink shade like Strawberry Icing or a more festive set like Snowfall, Glamnetic is known for its impressive designs. From magnetic cat-eye polish to 3-D chrome appliqués, there’s a set for every style, season, and mood. For a nail biter who has destroyed just about every expensive manicure imaginable, finding a budget-friendly option that can actually keep up with me feels like a small miracle.
