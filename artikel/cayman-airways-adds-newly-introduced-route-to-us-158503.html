📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Cayman Airways will launch a fresh seasonal service to the U.S. next year.

Starting May 21, 2027, the carrier will connect Grand Cayman and Nashville, Tennessee. Flights will operate twice per week, on Mondays and Fridays, through Aug. 16.

Tickets will become available for purchase early next month, officials said.

Cayman Airways is the flag carrier of the Cayman Islands and has its main hub at Owen Roberts International Airport in Grand Cayman.

Its current U.S. destinations are Miami, Tampa, Florida, Austin, Texas, Denver, and Los Angeles.
