My sister is much more committed — she's even taken time off work to play GTA 6. I love playing video games during my downtime, but she’s on another level.

Industry observers note that between the two of us and the rest of my family, there’s usually a game, controller, or accessory on someone’s wish list. With Black Friday coming up, I’m looking through offers at Amazon, the PlayStation Store, and other retailers to see whether it makes sense to buy now or later.

If you’re shopping for a console, you might not find it on sale until Black Friday proper (though there are no guarantees). We haven't seen any deals worth mentioning just yet, but we're keeping an eye out.

Below, I’ve rounded up the early PlayStation deals worth checking, whether you’re buying a gift or just want something new to play.

Whether you’re checking off your gift list or shopping for yourself, buying another controller is almost always a good idea. If the battery dies mid-game, you can swap controllers and charge the other. Plus, my nephews love getting all the different colors — they have a pretty substantial collection now.

In a recent development, right now, the PlayStation DualSense Wireless Controller in Chroma Pearl is $59.99 at Amazon, down from $79.99. That’s $20 off, or 25% in savings. It has haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, a built-in microphone, and a headset jack. This price is for Chroma Pearl, so check the price again if you switch colors before adding it to your cart.

If someone on your gift list already has a PS5 but doesn’t always want to sit in front of the TV, this is def a deal worth considering.
