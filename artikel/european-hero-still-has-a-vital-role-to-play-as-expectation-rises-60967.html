Industry observers note that west Ham’s veteran midfielder has been the subject of an honest interview in his home country on Czech radio coinciding with the release of his autobiography. One of the more revealing comments Tomas Soucek made concerns the pressure and the expectation of playing in front of a major audience – such as West Ham will undoubtedly face tomorrow as the season re-commences with a home tie at London Stadium in front of 60,000 expectant supporters.

Recent findings suggest that unlike several others, from his words it is clear that Soucek seems to thrive on the pressure and the weight of expectation upon him. As one of only a handful of medal winners still at the club from the 2023 campaign, Soucek’s experience should become a steadying influence in the dressing room even though he mostly starts games on the bench these days.

Handling the weight of expectation at London Stadium has been daunting at times.

‘Tornado Tom’ reveals that, far from letting it get to him, he actually enjoys the weight of expectation and pressure :.

Industry observers note that “Yes, obviously I feel it, but I like the pressure. Because football is always up and down, you have so many wins and so many losses – and it’s up to you how you recover from all of it.

“And I love that. As you remarked, it’s the most watched league in the world. And West Ham’s fans are crazy – they’re the best in the league, and it’s such a historic club.

“From the first day until now, I think me and West Ham connection is as good as it could possibly be in all aspects.

“And, yes, the pressure is there, but I try to work with it – and that’s why we play football.” (englishradio.cz).

The pressure will be significantly eased with a clean sheet and three points tomorrow – whether Soucek now, 31 gets into the action or simply acts as a calming figure in the dressing room. He is one of those very rare characters in the West Ham team – a leader on and off the pitch – and in another era could have spent years as the club’s captain.
