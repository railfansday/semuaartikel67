According to recent reports, it’s hard to believe the Lenox Spice Village frenzy started nearly four years ago. Back in 2022, thrifters began showing off their vintage finds on social media, sparking a full-blown obsession with the beloved retro spice jars. By late 2024, Lenox had officially announced the collection’s comeback, and shoppers wasted no time. The sets sold out multiple times throughout 2024 and 2025.

It has been revealed that now, Lenox is tapping into another slice of its vintage kitchenware history with the Lenox Spice Garden: a charming collection of 24 porcelain spice containers first introduced in 1992. Consider this your next collector obsession. Here’s everything you need to know about the 2026 re-release.

Industry observers note that spice displays don't get sweeter than this! We're obsessed with this surprise re-release of the 1992 Spice Garden collection.

34 years after its original debut, the Lenox Spice Garden is officially back—and it’s every bit as charming. The 24-piece collection features cylindrical porcelain spice jars adorned with delicate, hand-drawn botanical illustrations of herbs and spices. Each one is topped with a matching lid finished with the brand’s signature gold trim.

Sources indicate that while it may have the same nostalgic appeal as the Lenox Spice Village, the Spice Garden has a look all its own. Instead of the Village’s squat, house-shaped jars and pastel-painted architectural details, the Garden goes for a more classic botanical aesthetic. Both collections have received modern second-edition reissues, but their shapes, designs and accessories set them apart.

One notable difference? The Spice Garden comes with four matching porcelain spice spoons, while the Spice Village includes jars only. There’s also one swap in the lineup: Chili takes the place of Dill.

Here’s the complete list of spices included in the Lenox Spice Garden: Saffron, Cinnamon, Allspice, Clove, Thyme, Chili, Coriander, Oregano, Marjoram, Parsley, Basil, Chive, Pepper, Bay, Sage, Celery, Nutmeg, Ginger, Mustard, Rosemary, Paprika, Garlic, Onion and Tarragon.

Lenox also brought back the matching Spice Garden Rack, a rustic wooden display with a rich, dark-stained finish and built-in side holsters to keep the spice spoons close at hand. It’s the perfect finishing touch for displaying the collection and a must-have for anyone going all in on it.

Crafted from ivory porcelain and finished with gold accents, this timeless bird beauty makes a thoughtful gift and is an heirloom-worthy piece designed to be treasured for generations.

This one hardly needs an introduction! Just as lovely as the original collection, the Lenox Spice Village brings a little storybook magic to your kitchen with its whimsical pastel hues and irresistible cottagecore charm.
