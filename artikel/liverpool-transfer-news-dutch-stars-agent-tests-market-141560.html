In a recent development, liverpool transfer news: Ryan Gravenberch’s agent, José Fortes Rodriguez, is exploring ways to take his client out of Anfield, according to CaughtOffside. The site relays a claim from the X account @ITKSpace that Fortes has held “loose but direct contact” with several European clubs, none of them named. No formal exit request has been lodged, and the 24-year-old could still stay, so this reads like a camp checking the market rather than a player pushing to leave.

Recent findings suggest that liverpool hold the stronger hand. His six-year extension, signed in March, runs to 2032, so any buyer would face a substantial asking price. Real Madrid have been linked before, though nothing here says they are interested now.

Industry observers note that iraola’s 4-2-3-1 relies on two central midfielders, and Gravenberch, so commanding at the base of Arne Slot’s title-winning side, has struggled to settle. Iraola benched him for three straight games at one stage, he collected a second yellow card in the 3-0 home defeat by Manchester City on 11 October, and a heel problem has since kept him out of the Arsenal and Fenerbahçe games. He has no goals and one assist in the league this term.

His strengths are clear. At 6ft 3in he carries the ball through pressure and starts attacks from deep. Defending space and tracking runners is the weakness, and it highlights when his partner also surges forward.

Gravenberch has admitted he prefers the number eight role, yet Mac Allister and Szoboszlai, who started the 3-1 win at Crystal Palace that lifted Liverpool to fourth, already occupy those spots.
