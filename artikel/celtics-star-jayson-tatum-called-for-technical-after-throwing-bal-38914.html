Jayson Tatum got called for a technical foul at the worst time on Wednesday night, and it cost the Boston Celtics a chance at extending the game.

It has been revealed that tatum was called for a technical foul after he threw a ball toward an official in the final seconds of the Celtics' 105-101 loss to the Denver Nuggets at Ball Arena on Wednesday. While the act itself didn't cost the Celtics the game, it made sure they didn't have a legitimate chance to send the contest into overtime.

Denver star Jamal Murray drained a jumper with just seven seconds left in the game, which put the Nuggets up by three points at the time. Tatum caught the ball after it went through the net, and started to make a move to pass it in as the Celtics called a timeout.

Sources indicate that but, instead of just turning to hand the ball to the official standing right next to him, Tatum turned and tossed the ball toward the official standing near the scorer's table at midcourt. The ball went behind that official, who seemed a bit confused. The official standing right next to Tatum then called him for a technical foul.

Sources indicate that that sent Murray back to the free-throw line. He sank the extra shot, which put the Nuggets up by four and ruined any hopes the Celtics had of forcing overtime.

The throw that Tatum made wasn't egregious by any means. He didn't drill the official with the ball or seem to throw it with any malicious intent. And it even looked like the referee was looking in his direction briefly before turning his back to Tatum once the ball was in the air. Tatum tried to plead his case during the chaotic moments that followed, but the officials weren't having it.

Regardless of intent, throwing a ball at or toward an official like that is often going to result in a technical.

Murray and the Nuggets rallied from an early 15-point hole to beat the Celtics on Wednesday night. Murray had 21 points and seven rebounds in the win, which pushed the Nuggets to 8-5 on the year. Nikola Jokić finished just shy of a triple-double, with 24 points, 9 assists and 8 rebounds. The Nuggets had only 13 fouls called on them all night, compared to the 30 that Boston was hit with.

Sources indicate that tatum led the Celtics with 24 points and eight rebounds in the loss, though he scored only four points in the second half. Neemias Queta finished with 17 points and 10 rebounds. The loss marked the second straight for the Celtics and dropped them to 11-4 on the season.
