Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews fresh releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Industry observers note that john Stewart (Aaron Pierre) is new to the Green Lantern Corps when he joins Hal Jordan (Kyle Chandler) on a murder case in Nebraska. Hal has years of experience with the ring and all the confidence that comes with it — John, on the other hand, has to figure out how to work with him while investigating a death in a town where nobody called for two intergalactic cops. The ring can do a lot for John, but it can’t tell him when to trust the man who’s supposed to show him how to use it.

According to recent reports, that’s what makes pairing the two Lanterns more interesting than giving each of them a separate fight. John has to learn how Hal works while getting enough room to show what he can do on his own. Pierre and Chandler sat together again at HBO’s For Your Consideration panel at the Hammer Museum in Los Angeles on November 9, where Pierre talked about what he wanted to get out of the job and how he felt when it was over.

It has been revealed that in Collider’s exclusive clip from the panel, Pierre says the Lanterns team wrapped filming without worrying about how the show would be received. He put that down to what he’d seen from the people making it alongside him:.

Industry observers note that “And every single department, transpo, wardrobe, catering, crafty, you know, every single department was determined from day zero to pour their hearts into this. So much so that we at the end of it, with the most love and respect, we were like, we were actually not concerned about how it's received, because we have given this everything possible, and we are proud of ourselves.”.

Pierre estimates that the shoot lasted about 116 days and that the production spent less than a fifth of that time on the Burbank lot. He calls it “a traveling tech giant,” which makes transportation a pretty natural place to start when he’s listing the departments he remembers. Most viewers can tell when a scene moves somewhere upcoming, but Pierre was thinking about all the people who had to move with it.

He also claims he came into Lanterns wanting to “just be a sponge.” The two ends of Pierre's answer tell a better story about his time on the show than the line about fan reactions does in isolation. He started the job thinking about how much he could learn from the people around him, but when it came time to talk about the result in a room full of people there to consider it for awards, he was still naming the people he’d learned from.
