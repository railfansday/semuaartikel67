Chris Pratt’s The Terminal List Confirms New Prequel Series After Season 3

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Terminal List is expanding even further.

Chris Pratt's action thriller franchise is getting a upcoming prequel after season 3 was announced, further expanding upon its ever-growing timeline. The Terminal List season 2 was released on Prime Video in October 2026, pulling from the pages of True Believer, the second book in Jack Carr's The Terminal List book series. Following its success on the platform, a follow-up series adapting the third book, Savage Son, was confirmed, with Pratt reprising his role as James Reece for the upcoming series.

In a recent development, now, Deadline has confirmed a new Terminal List prequel series, Cry Havoc, is now in development. Based on Carr's book of the same name, the series will follow James Reece's father, Tom Reece, during his days as a Navy SEAL in the 1960s. Per the novel, the story follows a young Tom during the Vietnam War, wherein he's recruited to a secretive MACV-SOG unit operating in Laos. But, upon learning about the upcoming Tet Offensive, Tom and his team become vital participants in the ongoing conflict.

According to the report, The Terminal List showrunner David Digilio wrote a pilot script with former US Army Ranger, writer, and executive producer Max Adams. The pair will be developing Cry Havoc alongside Carr and Jared Shaw, all of whom will be serving as executive producers on the prequel. In addition, Pratt, Antoine Fuqua, and Kat Samick will serve as executive producers, while Digilio will return as showrunner. The series hasn't been officially greenlit by Amazon MGM Studios, but development is ongoing.

Recent findings suggest that cry Havoc will be the second prequel in the ever-expanding series. The first was the TV original The Terminal List: Dark Wolf, which followed James' former ally Ben Edwards (Taylor Kitsch) becoming recruited to a clandestine CIA organization. The prequel introduced characters like Raife Hastings (Tom Hopper) and Mo Farooq (Dar Salim), both of whom played a major role in The Terminal List season 2. The prequel's interaction with the main show made it vital viewing for the series' interconnected universe.
