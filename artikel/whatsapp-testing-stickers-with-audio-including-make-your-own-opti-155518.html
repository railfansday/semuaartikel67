The feature was spotted by WABetaInfo in the beta version of the Android app.

Sources indicate that whatsApp is developing a newly introduced format to add an audio track to a sticker. This would allow users to send a sticker that plays a sound when the recipient taps it.

You can create your own audio stickers by selecting one of your videos.

It has been revealed that once customers choose a video from their gallery, WhatsApp converts it into an animated sticker automatically. At the top of the editor, users can find a fresh audio button that lets them add a sound to the sticker. If users tap this button, WhatsApp will display a new “Add sound” screen. This section lets users refine the new sound sticker by choosing which audio track to attach to it.

Sounds don’t play automatically, but recipients will see a small audio icon attached to the sticker, and tapping it will play the sound.

As usual with WhatsApp, it’s rolling out first in the Android beta and is likely to reach the iPhone sometime later. This can range from a few weeks to a few months, depending on how well the beta test goes.
