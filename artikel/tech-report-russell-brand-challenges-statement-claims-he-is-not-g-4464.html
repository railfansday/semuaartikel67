Russell Brand issued a statement from prison, insisting he is “not guilty” after being sentenced to 10 years and six months behind bars for rape and sexual assault.

It has been revealed that “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support. As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening,” the comedian, 51, said in a statement shared by his wife, Laura Brand, via his YouTube channel on Sunday, November 15.

According to recent reports, laura, 39, had begun the video by stating that “Russell has asked me to read something to you today” before turning to a notebook to share her husband’s message. The post served as the first time Laura has publicly addressed Russell’s conviction and prison sentence, which was delivered in London’s Southwark Crown Court on Monday, November 9.

Russell’s message continued, “The good news is that Christ is with us when we choose him, and I do. I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey. Little as in small, and hey as in, ‘Hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders prison in Europe.’ The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind and they are dedicated. And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.”.

Russell went to trial for four weeks, ultimately being found guilty by a jury on one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault. Russell had repeatedly denied accusations of wrongdoing, and was found not guilty on two other rape charges and an attempted indecent assault charge.

Industry observers note that laura, who shares three children with Russell, concluded on behalf of her husband, “I don’t know which other men here are not guilty, and the ones that are, they are people that Christ died to save, like you and me. Let us all love, pray for and forgive one another as God forgave us. Please support my treasured wife and family. I feel your prayers. Your brother in Christ, Russell.”.

According to recent reports, since receiving his conviction and sentencing, Russell has been accused of misconduct by six additional women. London’s Metropolitan Police later confirmed to Us that he remains under investigation for additional rape allegations from two unidentified women.

A Met spokesperson told Us, “The suspect has previously been interviewed under caution in connection with the allegations. Files have been submitted to the Crown Prosecution Service in relation to both complainants. Detectives remain in close contact with prosecutors as enquiries continue and further investigative work is carried out.”.

Elsewhere in Sunday’s YouTube video, Laura shared support for her husband, adding that she will continue sharing social media videos after discussing the future of them with Russell.

Industry observers note that “Obviously, [it] will take some getting used to for me to even sit here and not have my companion and my person with me,” she told fans. “Although Russell is not here now in this moment, the Holy Spirit is moving, and the Holy Spirit cannot be restrained. I’m speaking to Russell. He’s OK and he’s praying, unsurprisingly. He’s praying, and we decided for me to continue.”.
