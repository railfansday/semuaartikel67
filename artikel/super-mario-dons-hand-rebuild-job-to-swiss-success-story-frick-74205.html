Aberdeen have appointed former Liechtenstein international Mario Frick as their new head coach.

The 52-year-old will take charge at Pittodrie, subject to the completion of UK visa formalities, following a successful spell with Swiss side FC Luzern.

According to recent reports, frick arrives in the north-east after five years in charge of Luzern, where he transformed the club’s fortunes after taking over in December 2021 with the team rooted to the bottom of the Swiss Super League. He guided them away from relegation trouble before leading them to a fourth-place finish and qualification for European football.

Announcing the appointment, Aberdeen chief executive Adrian Bevington stated the club had undertaken an extensive recruitment process before selecting Frick as the man to lead the Dons forward.

Bevington said: 'We are delighted to welcome Mario Frick and his assistant coach Roman Matter to Aberdeen Football Club.

Industry observers note that 'Mario has significant experience as a head coach and has delivered impressive results with both Vaduz and Luzern. He has a proven track record of building competitive teams and developing young players.

'Throughout our discussions, he demonstrated a clear vision, strong leadership qualities and a deep understanding of what is required to build a successful team.'.

Sporting director Lutz Pfannenstiel also welcomed the appointment and believes Frick has the qualities to make a major impact at Pittodrie.

Pfannenstiel noted: 'Mario is a strong personality with clear ideas about how he wants his teams to play. He brings energy, ambition and a winning mentality, and knows how to build a strong team environment.

Aberdeen have appointed Mario Frick as their latest manager.

'We were looking for the right person to lead the next chapter of Aberdeen FC, and I’m convinced Mario has the qualities, character and vision to make a real impact.'.

Before his spell with Luzern, Frick managed FC Balzers and FC Vaduz, winning promotion to the Swiss Super League with the latter. Across almost 290 senior matches as a head coach, he built a reputation for developing young talent, handing top-flight debuts to more than 30 academy players.

Sources indicate that as a player, Frick represented clubs including FC Basel, FC Zürich, St Gallen, Grasshopper, Siena and Ternana. A striker who could also operate in defence, he won 125 caps for Liechtenstein between 1993 and 2016 and remains his country’s record goalscorer with 16 international goals.

According to recent reports, frick noted: 'I’m excited to be joining Aberdeen FC. It’s a club with a proud history, passionate supporters and big ambitions.

'Together with the staff, my aim is to create an environment where the players can thrive, develop and perform at their very best, while working towards our goals as a team.'.

Frick and assistant coach Matter are expected to arrive in Aberdeen at the start of the week and will begin preparations for next weekend’s Premiership clash with Kilmarnock.
