Team Cherry has confirmed that Hollow Knight: Silksong's Sea of Sorrow DLC will be releasing on December 11, 2026.

Recent findings suggest that this was confirmed in the developer's "Cherry Time! Special Presentation," which featured a newly introduced trailer for the expansion alongside the announcement of its release date (and the showcase of three lovely looking indie titles). And thankfully we're not going to be waiting very long, as Sea of Sorrow is coming out in two weeks, with the devs hyping it up as an "expansion unlike any we've done before.".

As promised, Sea of Sorrow is a "high-seas adventure," with the trailer featuring Hornet arriving on a boat to uncharted shores and taking the fight to pirate bugs who yelp "arghhh" when you beat them with a very Donkey Kong Country 2-coded sound effect. We also saw her blasting out of cannons, climbing net ladders, and getting in scraps with multiple giant pirate bosses.

We knew its release had to be soon as the DLC, which was announced shortly after Silksong launched, was previously given a 2026 release window – although given the reveal to release timeline of the game itself, it's perhaps no surprise that some have not been so trusting, especially when that date vanished on the game's physical copy.

While Silksong famously started out as a DLC for the original Hollow Knight, it's easy to forget that the original got multiple DLC packs, with four free expansions released within its two years. Team Cherry didn't confirm what DLC is still to come for Silksong, but given it's the "first big content update" I'm sure fans will be fed for a while yet.
