Breaking: Puig To Acquire Parfums de Marly and Initio

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, puig has entered into an agreement to acquire Parfums de Marly and its sister brand, Initio, the Spanish beauty and fashion conglomerate announced on Friday. Advent International, the private equity firm that acquired them in 2023, sold its 75% stake in Artessence Group, the holding entity behind Parfums de Marly and Initio Parfums Privés, to Puig for €1.2 billion in cash.

“Niche fragrances offer one of the most attractive opportunities within our core fragrance business,” Puig CEO Jose Manuel Albesa remarked in a statement. “Parfums de Marly brings meaningful growth and an established position in North America, while Initio is a distinctive brand with significant potential ahead. We look forward to welcoming Julien and the Artessence team to Puig and building alongside them.”.

Julien Sprecher founded Parfums de Marly in 2009, inspired by his passion “for the splendor of the French art de vivre in the 18th century”. He sold a majority stake to Advent in 2023, remaining creative director of Parfums de Marly and minority shareholder of the group. Patrice Béliard joined Artessence in October 2025 from The Estée Lauder Companies, taking on the CEO role. Today, Parfums de Marly counts around 25 stores and 1,000 points of sale globally.

Artessence is expected to generate around $420-440 million in revenues and around $180-190 million in EBITDA in the fiscal year 2026-27, ending March 31, 2027, according to the press release.

In a recent development, at its Capital Markets Day in October, Puig signaled its openness to pursuing external growth opportunities. “We count on M&A to accelerate our growth,” Albesa stated. The group also revealed a new set of objectives that includes scaling its niche fragrances (Byredo, Penhaligon’s, L’Artisan Parfumeur and Dries Van Noten). “Niche is no longer niche,” Thomas James, president of niche brands and wellness brands at Puig, said.

In a recent development, albesa added in Friday’s statement: “Together with our other niche houses, [Parfums de Marly and Initio] give us a stronger platform from which to accelerate our ambition in the category, and we look forward to supporting the continued growth of each fragrance house, while preserving the creativity and uniqueness of each of our brands. This transaction will reinforce our core business and is fully aligned with our Vision 2032 ambition to scale what works profitably.”.

Sources indicate that puig and a holding structure established by Sprecher have agreed to an earn-out based on Artessence’s future performance, as well as put and call options over Julien Sprecher’s remaining 25% stake, providing a pathway for Puig to own 100% of the company over time. “Puig shares our belief in the power of distinctive brands and long-term brand building,” Sprecher said. “I am delighted to have the opportunity to continue this journey with a family company that understands fragrance deeply and respects the entrepreneurial and creative culture that has brought us this far.”.

Puig executive chairman Marc Puig stated: “This transaction reflects our selective approach to M&A: we invest in attractive brands with a strong strategic fit that complement our organic growth, while preserving our financial flexibility.”.
