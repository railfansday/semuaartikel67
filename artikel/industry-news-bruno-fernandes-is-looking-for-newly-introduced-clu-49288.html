Bruno Fernandes could explore his options as a free agent as he edges towards leaving Manchester United next summer.

The 32-year-old has been well below his usual standard so far this season, leading to suggestions he may have become disillusioned, and sparking doubts over his future.

With less than a year remaining on his contract, a number of clubs across world football are now keeping a close eye on his situation and could look to tempt him away.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that it would "not be a surprise" if Fernandes left at the end of the season desipte the club hoping to keep him.

The Portugal international has suitors across world football, notably including Saudi Arabia, where Pro League clubs have long been keen to lure him away from United.

There is now a growing feeling that he could leave Man United, despite having held talks over a new contract, with the club entering another new era under Mauricio Pochettino.

Brown told Football Insider on 16 November that Fernandes must step up and inspire the dressing room to help Man United turn their awful start to the season around.

However, there are doubts about whether he will be convinced to stay at the club long-term, with increasing speculation about a move away.

Fernandes will have his pick of a number of suitors should he decide to leave Man United, and despite the club's efforts, looks increasingly likely to move on.

"Unfortunately, I'm not completely convinced Fernandes will want to stay," Brown told Football Insider.

"The club obviously want to keep him, I'm sure Mauricio Pochettino will as well, and maybe he could play a role in convincing him to sign a new contract and extend his stay.

"But I just get the feeling that he's going to be looking elsewhere and maybe the time has come for him to move on, it wouldn't be a surprise if that was the case.

"Just look at the way he's played this season and his attitude on the pitch, he doesn't look like somebody who's happy and willing to keep pushing the club forward.

"As notable as he has been, this is another change in manager and another disappointing season, and how long will he want to stick around while that's happening?

"There are clubs keeping a close eye on him, and I think now could be the time that he is finally convinced to move on.".

Fernandes' future at Old Trafford has been subject to speculation for at least 12 months now, having come close to leaving in 2025, sparking the club to act.

Contract talks have been held this season as they have been keen for the midfielder to extend his stay, given his important role in the side and their long-term looks.

However, Football Insider revealed last month that Man United are now assessing whether they could sell Fernandes at the end of the season.

Despite his contract being set to expire, the club do hold the option to extend his stay by a further year, and could trigger that clause to ensure they can raise funds from his sale.

That would then allow him to move on at the end of the season regardless, while also benefitting the club rather than seeing their best player walk away for free.
