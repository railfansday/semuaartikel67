In a recent development, but thanks to Black Friday, you can see Santa a little earlier than Saturday. A Lego Santa. As of Nov. 26, the Lego Up-Scaled Santa Minifigure is available for $41.99, an $18 discount off the regular list price of $59.99.

Get the Lego Up-Scaled Santa Minifigure for $41.99 and kick off the Christmas season before the Macy's Thanksgiving Parade even gets started.
