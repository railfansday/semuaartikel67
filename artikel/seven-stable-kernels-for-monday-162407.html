In a recent development, greg Kroah-Hartman has announced the 7.3.6, 6.18.65, 6.12.119, 6.6.163, 6.1.193, 5.15.226, and 5.10.275 stable kernels. Each of these contains quite a few patches throughout the tree—6.12.119 has the fewest with more than 1,700 patches, while 6.6.163 has more than 3,300. Owners are advised to upgrade.
