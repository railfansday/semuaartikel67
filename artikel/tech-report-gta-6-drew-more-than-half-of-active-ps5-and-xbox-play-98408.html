Tech Report: GTA 6 Drew More Than Half of Active PS5 and XBOX Players on Launch Day

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A lot of people cleared their schedules for GTA 6, and apparently they meant it. Newly introduced figures show that more than half of the active PlayStation 5 and XBOX players tracked by Circana played Rockstar's game on launch day. The average time spent with it was over eight hours on both platforms.

As Eurogamer reported, Circana analyst Mat Piscatella shared the figures shortly after GTA 6 debuted on November 19. The numbers come from Circana's Player Engagement Tracker, which measures what people are playing rather than how many copies a game has sold.

Industry observers note that according to Piscatella's post on Bluesky, 61% of PS5 players active in the United States on November 19 played GTA 6. On XBOX, the figure was 56% of active players worldwide. That's a huge share of the audience for one game, particularly when people still had all their usual favorites to choose from.

There's an vital distinction in those percentages. The PlayStation figure covers the US, while the XBOX figure is global, so they aren't directly comparable. They also refer to people who were active on their consoles that day, not everyone who owns a PS5 or XBOX. It would be wrong to say that more than half of all console owners bought GTA 6.

The time figures are just as wild. US PS5 players who introduced GTA 6 averaged 513 minutes with the game running, or eight hours and 33 minutes. On XBOX, the average was 494 minutes, or eight hours and 14 minutes. That measures time with the game active, rather than proving everyone was at the controller for every minute.

Eight hours is a full working day, and that was the average time GTA 6 was running for the players Circana tracked. You'd expect plenty of long sessions for a release this major, but those averages are still difficult to get your head around.

Industry observers note that for comparison, Fortnite was the next closest game on November 19, with an engagement rate of about 10% among active players across the tracked console audiences. Even a game that people routinely return to every day wasn't coming close to GTA 6 on Thursday.

According to recent reports, piscatella also looked at what GTA 6 players had been playing in the two weeks before launch. The top five were Fortnite, Call of Duty, GTA 5, Roblox, and NBA 2K27. It's quite a mix, and it says something about GTA's staying power that the previous game was still on that list 13 years after release.

Piscatella stated he couldn't remember another day like it in Circana's tracker, although its daily history only goes back to May 2020. That doesn't establish an all-time record, but it's still a striking result for the period Circana can measure.

Industry observers note that the attention around GTA 6 has been hard to miss even outside gaming circles. Lately, I've heard people who know next to nothing about video games bring it up in conversation. That's not something I can say about most fresh releases, even the ones everyone in the gaming community is excited about.

Rockstar also announced that GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies before release and generated an estimated $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales, including preorders. As DualShockers previously reported, that made for a record-breaking start before a PC version was even available.

Recent findings suggest that sales figures alone don't show whether people actually sat down and played. Circana's data fills in that part. A huge share of active console players had GTA 6 running on November 19, and the PC version isn't even out yet.

It has been revealed that it'll be interesting to see what happens to those figures once the initial excitement dies down. As far as November 19 goes, though, the numbers leave little doubt about where console players were spending their time.
