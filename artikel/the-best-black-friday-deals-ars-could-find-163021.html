Industry observers note that every year has a Prime Day—some years even have two Prime Days!—where we celebrate the life and achievements of the greatest leader the Autobots have ever known: the inimitable Optimus Prime. But just as every dawn is twinned by a dusk that steals its light, so too did Optimus Prime’s bright spark fade, extinguished by the foul hand of Megatron during the Great Decepticon Incursion of 2005.

Recent findings suggest that it is a day that lives in infamy forever, and we all know the tale—how the evil Megatron (cursed be the flow of his energon!) commandeered an Autobot shuttle, cruelly murdered its crew, and launched a cowardly surprise attack against Autobot City—a strategy utterly bereft of honor, as was so typical of Megatron’s schemes. Even as individual Autobots strove to secure Autobot City—heroes, all of them!—hope began to fade under an onslaught that included an attack by the fearsome Devastator, against which there appeared to be no defense.

And then, at the precipice of disaster, Optimus Prime made the fateful declaration whose words still ring out to this day: “Megatron must be stopped, no matter the cost.” The fearless Autobot transformed, rolled out, and got the job done—even though that cost was high. Many brave Autobots besides Optimus Prime lost their lives during the fighting. It was truly a dark day, a black day—and so, every year on the Friday after Thanksgiving, we pause to remember the noble sacrifices made by Prime and by so many others. This is why today is known as Black Friday.
