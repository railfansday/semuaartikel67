Media in Thailand is reporting on an incident that happened early this morning (Sunday, November 22, 2026), whereby a 46-year-old American tourist died at a hotel in Bangkok’s Phra Khanong area, after an altercation with a 39-year-old Italian man, who was reportedly an MMA fighter.

Sources indicate that according to the initial police report, the American had been arguing loudly with the hotel staff at the reception desk, for reasons that are unclear as of now. As an Italian hotel guest walked past him and attempted to enter an elevator, the American reportedly grabbed him and pulled him out.

The confrontation escalated, and turned into a physical struggle, during which the Italian man placed the American man in a chokehold, until he lost consciousness. Hotel staff called emergency services, and while first responders performed CPR, the American couldn’t be revived. Police also found an abrasion on the victim’s forehead.

Industry observers note that the Italian man remained at the scene, and told authorities that he had been acting in self-defense, after being attacked. Police took him into custody, and he potentially faces manslaughter charges, though the case is still being investigated.

The hotel at which this incident happened hasn’t yet been named, but this occurred at Sukhumvit Soi 48 — there are several hotels in that area, with the biggest “brand name” property being a Ramada Plaza by Wyndham.
