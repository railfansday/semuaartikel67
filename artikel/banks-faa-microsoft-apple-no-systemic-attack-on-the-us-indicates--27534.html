A set of major tech outages in close succession is leading some to speculate that they are connected, and that the US is under systemic attack by a hostile state.

All the evidence suggests otherwise, with individual explanations for each of them – and the White House is now saying as much in a bid to put fears to rest ….

On Thursday, a widespread Microsoft 365 outage ran into the following day when two unrelated issues coincided. Friday saw a nationwide ground stop of US air traffic due to a communication issue affecting all air traffic control centers. On Monday, a bug in a Chase bank system saw disruption not only to the bank itself, but also other banks where Chase was a party to a transaction. Finally, yesterday saw many Apple services offline, with retail stores also affected.

The Apple outage doesn’t appear to have been significant enough to have reached the administration’s radar, but CBS News reports secretary of state Marco Rubio saying the others are unrelated.

In a recent development, “The Microsoft incident has nothing to do with what we saw with the FAA. The FAA has nothing to do with what we saw with the banks a few days ago. These issues that we’ve seen from a technical standpoint are completely unrelated one from another,” Rubio remarked […].

“We have zero evidence or any reason to believe that any of these isolated or individual instances have anything to do with nefarious actors, with cyber hackers, with criminals, with another nation state, with a malign actor of any kind. Each of them are distinct, and they’re different in their causation.”.

The statement was backed by Senator Mark Warner.

In a recent development, democratic Sen. Mark Warner, vice chair of the Senate Intelligence Committee, also stated on Tuesday that there’s no indication these incidents are the result of an attack.

Warner went on to say that the ability of AI systems to find vulnerabilities at “unprecedented speed” does, however, mean companies and government agencies alike need to be ready to patch vulnerabilities immediately.
