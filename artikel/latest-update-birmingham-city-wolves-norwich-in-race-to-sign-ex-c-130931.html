Birmingham City returned to winning ways with a comfortable 2-0 away victory over struggling Burnley in the Championship, as head coach Chris Davies has one eye on the January transfer window.

After a disappointing 1-0 defeat to Stoke City last weekend, Birmingham have responded with a 2-0 away victory over lowly Burnley in the Championship to thrust themselves immediately back into automatic promotion contention.

The Blues are looking to secure a first return to the Premier League since 2011, and have already set their sights on one particular player, although they face a threat from two division rivals for their signature.

According to TEAMtalk, Birmingham have joined the likes of Wolverhampton Wanderers and Norwich City in the race for ex-Celtic winger Liel Abada this winter.

Abada, 25, began his professional career in his native Israel with Maccabi Petah Tikva before joining Celtic in the summer of 2021, going on to make 112 appearances across all competitions at Parkhead, scoring 29 and assisting 22.

With the Bhoys, Abada won the Scottish league three times, the Scottish Cup twice, and the Scottish League Cup twice before heading to MLS in March 2024 for around £8 million, linking up with Charlotte.

He'd star against Rangers under the guidance of former Celtic, Tottenham Hotspur, and Nottingham Forest boss Ange Postecoglou, with the Aussie boss describing him as 'outstanding'.

Postecoglou noted: "The fact he gets the amount of goals that he does as a winger, for such a young player, is outstanding.".

TEAMtalk reported that, earlier this year, both Coventry City and Swansea City were monitoring the winger's situation, but opted against making a move for him, as, according to Sky Sports' Lyall Thomas, he may not automatically meet the requirements for a work permit this winter.

This may mean, upon signing for a Championship club, he could head out on loan to a top European league to obtain the required points, as Birmingham have emerged as one of numerous sides tracking his current contract situation.

Abada, since joining Charlotte, initially struggled to show what he was capable of, scoring 12 and assisting twice in 61 MLS appearances during his first couple of years, but has thrown himself into the limelight this year.

With 11 goals and five assists in 28 league appearances, Abada has surpassed his output in his first couple of seasons this year alone, and with his contract set to expire this winter, he could become a free agent as soon as the fresh year begins.

On a free transfer, aged 25, Abada is an interesting prospect for numerous clubs across the globe, as those in the Championship, like Birmingham, are carefully monitoring his situation and weighing up whether to make a move in the next month or so.

Financially, Abada ought to be a no-brainer, as if the winger could obtain the necessary points to play in the English second tier, he could become a real star in the division.

Recent findings suggest that davies, after seeing his side fall short of the play-offs last season, is looking to make no such mistake this time around. With the likes of Demarai Gray, Carlos Vicente, Liam Millar, and Patrick Roberts as wide options, Birmingham boasts some of the best wingers in the division right now.

If Abada does not qualify to play for Blues this winter, he could essentially replace Millar in the squad next summer upon the expiry of the Canadian's loan spell, though whether that is a realistic strategy for the club remains to be seen.

Norwich are also lingering, as are Wolves, as Birmingham do not have a straightforward task of obtaining his signature, with this transfer saga one to keep a close eye on over the coming weeks and months.
