In a recent development, A good keyboard is essential to a good desk setup, and a Thunderbolt 5 dock makes that setup feel much cleaner. Keychron’s keyboards and fresh dock are on sale with up to $48 off during the deal season.

I’ve been using a Keychron Q3 HE for quite some time now. Over the last year or two, I’ve made a lateral move to Gateron Dawn double-rail magnetic switches from the stock Nebula switches it came with. Now, Keychron sells a Q3 HE with improved hardware for an 8K polling rate and with Lime silent switches by default.

It’s an excellent keyboard, and my only complaint is that the white variant I have doesn’t exactly match my desk.

In a recent development, most of Keychron’s deals are good right now, but the standout is the Thunderbolt 5 Dock, which was released earlier this year. I have mine plugged into my Mac Mini for the time being, but I use it on my Windows PC and Googlebook without issue. The Thunderbolt 5 rating means it keeps up with next-gen speeds, but it’s backward-compatible and works with most devices. Just plug in a host device, and you have access to data and displays easily.

It has been revealed that the Keychron Thunderbolt 5 Dock is down to $314, instead of $349. Considering it just rolled out earlier in the year, now is a good time to get one if you’re looking for a docking option.

Right now, Keychron has a number of deals running that are worth checking out.

Industry observers note that the 8K Q3 HE magnetic keyboard from Keychron is down to $206 at 10% off. It was already a great deal for a fully aluminum case with good keycaps and excellent switches, but the new Black Friday deal sweetens the pot a little.

The classic Q3 HE is no slouch, though. I’d recommend it in an instant, and it’s down to $191 instead of $239. That’s $48 down from the traditional price, and a much better deal than the 8K version if you don’t absolutely need the insanely high polling rate.

According to recent reports, the K4 HE is another great option, and it’s a much friendlier $81 on Amazon. That’s down from the $115 it normally is. It’s another hall-effect (magnetic switch) keyboard, but with wood accents and in a smaller 60% layout.

Keychron’s deals won’t last into next month, ending on November 30.
