📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that forget games being praised for releasing in the same month as GTA 6 - there’s an indie game bold enough to launch on the same day. Volvy’s Adventure: Reslimed rolls out on November 19, and if you delete your GTA 6 preload, Devolver Digital might give you the game for free.

For years, Devolver has joked that it would release a game on the same day as GTA 6. Even when GTA 6 got delayed (twice), Devolver altered course along with it. Turns out it wasn’t a joke either, as here we are with two days to go, and Volvy’s Adventure, published by Devolver, will launch on the same day as what might end up being the biggest video game ever.

Industry observers note that GTA 6 has consumed so much of the oxygen in the video game room that, predictably, getting people excited about Volvy has been nigh on impossible. It’s entirely likely this is the first time you’ve even heard of it. That’s why Devolver is turning up the heat and bringing out the big guns by giving Volvy’s Adventure away for free - but only if you delete your GTA 6 preload.

“Repost this tweet with a video of you deleting your GTA 6 pre-load along with #MakeRoomForVolvy for a chance to win a copy of Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed on Steam,” the post on Devolver’s Twitter reads. It’s even paired with a clip of someone, presumably from the publisher's team, showing you how it’s done, which honestly, after how long it took my terrible internet to download all 212 GB of it, sent a shiver down my spine.

Industry observers note that to be clear, if you like the look of Volvy’s Adventure and you’re seriously considering deleting GTA 6 for it, doing so doesn’t guarantee you’ll get a copy for free. Removing GTA 6 from your PS5 or Xbox Series X|S merely enters you into a giveaway to be in with a chance of getting Devolver’s upcoming game for free, and there has been no indication of just how many copies the publisher plans on handing out.

Volvy’s Adventure is also the polar opposite of GTA 6 when it comes to launch platforms. While Rockstar’s massive game will only be available on consoles, Volvy’s Adventure is exclusively available on Steam. If you don’t have a PC or a Steam Machine, then deleting your GTA 6 preload and sharing the evidence with Devolver will, at best, get you a Steam code that’s useless to you. Hey, maybe you can give it to a friend.

Recent findings suggest that if you don’t have GTA 6 loaded up already, Devolver did offer one person the chance to enter by sharing evidence of them biting into a raw onion. I’m unsure if that offer is open to everyone.

Recent findings suggest that it’s also worth noting to those in awe right now that might not fully understand what’s happening here that Devolver isn’t asking you to delete GTA 6 and throw away $80 for the chance to win a game that costs a fraction of that price (it’s $3.74 right now). Once the preload has been deleted, you can just re-download the game. The thing is, a game that big takes a long time to download, and at this late stage, depending on your internet speed, the longer you leave it, the more risk there is of GTA 6 not being ready to play when it unlocks in your region.
