Correction to: Nature https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09655-y Published online 22 October 2025.

In the version of this article initially published, in Fig. 2d, the red triangle depicting vaccine administration at day 17 originally appeared at day 20; in Fig. 5o, the colours of the red and blue traces in the plot were interchanged; in Extended Data Fig. 3h,i, the red and blue colour keys (currently, blue for vaccine, red for no vaccine) were interchanged. In the Fig. 3b legend, in the text now reading “vaccine days 3, 6, 21,” 21 replaces the original “17,” and in the Fig. 3k legend, now reading “vaccine days 3, 6, 20,” 20 replaces the original “21.” In the first sentence of the Methods “Cox proportional hazards regression” section, in the text now reading “For Cox proportional hazards regression, exposures defined relative to an index date...,” “exposures defined relative to an index date” replaces the original “time-dependent variables were defined.” In the first paragraph of the Methods and in the Reporting Summary, data collection timelines have been updated to say “The data-collection cut-off was 1 September 2024 for the NSCLC and melanoma datasets and 1 January 2025 for the tissue agnostic dataset.” Finally, the Acknowledgements have been amended to include the statement that G.A. and N.C. were supported by the CPRIT Research Training Award CPRIT Training Program (RP210028). The changes have been made in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
