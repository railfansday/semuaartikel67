The popular privacy altcoin has lost a large chunk of its trading volume after KuCoin’s surprise delisting.

In a recent development, monero (XMR) got delisted from KuCoin, one of the globe’s largest crypto exchanges. This has pushed the popular privacy altcoin down by 12.8% to trade at roughly $418, hitting latest monthly lows due to the unexpected move from KuCoin’s global exchange.

Right after the bearish XMR news hit, the altcoin witnessed a massive drawdown to find its footing at the July, 2026 lows.

The decision intensified concerns over Monero’s (XMR) liquidity status, as well as the growing restrictions on privacy coins within centralized trading platforms that are curbed by strict laws.

Specifically, it’s the EU’s Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR): Article 79 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624: the strictest measure right now in play that effectively targets privacy coins.

It has been revealed that this comes just a few days after MEXC Global, a popular competing crypto exchange, delisted Monero on Spot markets, keeping the Futures markets services running. Surely, the shrinking support from major platforms could manifest itself in significant-time sell-off sentiment among investors.

The quarterly-low levels on the Bull Bear Power (BBP) metric is already winking at this trend.

The privacy-based altcoin has dipped below the $420 support area with bears overtaking the markets, as depicted in the 4-hour XMR chart-based Bull Bear Power (BBP) metric just below.

If Monero’s (XMR) price manages to restore the $441 confluent resistance level, it can attract the $491 level, dropped yesterday. Judging from the Bollinger Bands (BOLL) on the one-day charts, Monero’s price would need to sustain above $501 for a trend switch confirmation, while the $599 line remains the bullish trend check-point.

Meanwhile, the broader crypto market witnesses another downshift with Bitcoin’s (BTC) pullback below $59,000, briefly dipping below the recent day’s highs of roughly $64,000.
