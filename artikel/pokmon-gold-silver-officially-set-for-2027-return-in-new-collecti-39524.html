Pokémon Gold & Silver Officially Set For 2027 Return In New Collection

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Pokémon Gold Version and Pokémon Silver Version were first released back in 1999 as the first installments in the second generation of the beloved Pokémon franchise. Introducing 100 new species, they are often regarded as some of the most successful in Nintendo's history, and now, almost 30-years on, Gold and Silver are set to return in a stunning 2027 release.

In a recent development, throughout the events of Pokémon Gold & Silver, players will stumble across Legendary Beasts in the Johto region: Entei, Raikou, and Suicune. Now, to celebrate the ongoing 30th anniversary celebrations for the Pokémon franchise, they will return in three new releases which will change the Pokémon Trading Card Game for good.

We all remember first running into these Legendary Beasts in Pokémon Gold Version and Pokémon Silver Version, as they were known to be hard to find when they would exit battle with seemingly no warning. As such, when Pokémon Crystal released in 2000 as the third game in the generation, Suicune became a set encounter. Unfortunately, the same could not be remarked for Entei and Raikou, who continued to elude players for years to come.

Sources indicate that however, Entei, Raikou, and Suicune will be hard to avoid next year, as the Legendary Beasts will be heading to the Pokémon Trading Card Game thanks to three unique sets. This leak comes from PokéBeach, who claims that in Japan, Magma Heartbeat, Lightning Impact, and Aurora Ripples will be added to the game, with each being based on the three Legendaries.

Set to arrive in Japan on May 21, 2027, it is currently unclear if these brand-newly introduced sets will also be receiving an English launch. However, if they do, it is likely that all three sets will be combined as one for their international launch.

In a recent development, as PokéBeach shared, "These are confirmed to be the first sets of the new Pokémon ex★ series, which was teased at Worlds in August. We still don't know what the Pokémon ex★ mechanic will entail. Earlier this month, Creatures indirectly hinted the series would start in May 2027 when they confirmed that booster pack prices would increase at that time.".

Sources indicate that as of now, we do not know any further information about these upcoming Pokémon Trading Card Game sets, but it will be the first time in the card game's history that three sets will be releasing on the same day. As PokéBeach explained, "Traditionally, a new series debuts with dual sets. In English, we then get one combined set.".

So, this is indeed a major day for Pokémon Trading Card Game players, and we can only hope that Magma Heartbeat, Lightning Impact, and Aurora Ripples will also be released internationally at later dates.

According to recent reports, for now, Pokémon fans are gearing up for the beginning of Pokémon Gen 10 with the arrival of Pokémon Winds and Pokémon Waves some time in 2027. Whilst details remain scarce, it will be the next major revision for the franchise, and it is already shaping up to be the most ambitious yet, with a rumored 300+ new Pokémon being added to the roster.
