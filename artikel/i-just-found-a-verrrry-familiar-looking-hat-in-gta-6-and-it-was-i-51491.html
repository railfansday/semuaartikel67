I just found a curious artifact of the Old West in GTA 6, and the best part is I stumbled across it completely by accident.

That's right, partner: Arthur Morgan's cowboy hat is in Grand Theft Auto 6, and you won't find it in some trendy Western fashion shop or in a McMansion as part of a wealthy crypto bro's collection.

If you want to wear it yourself, I'll tell you below where you can find it.

Sources indicate that I was out in Port Gellhorn looking for a particular kind of car for reasons completely unrelated to clothing accessories. While I was driving, I spotted a sign on the side of the road with a balloon tied to it, reading "Yard Sale" with an arrow pointing down the road.

It has been revealed that I followed the sign which led to more yard sale signs and, yup, sure enough, the signs pointed me to an actual yard sale. And among the items for sale was a very familiar-looking hat.

Sources indicate that it's called a "Genuine Cowboy Hat" and, this being a yard sale, it only cost $5. But I knew the second I saw it that this was no ordinary cowboy hat. Here, have a look at it from the back.

Recent findings suggest that yep. No mistaking that. I even got a little pang of sadness thinking about good ol' Arthur Morgan. I miss him. Maybe when I'm done with GTA 6, I'll strap on my spurs and take another ride through Red Dead Redemption 2.

In a recent development, directions: Head to Port Gellhorn. It's in Kelly County on the western side of the state along the coast. In the southern tip of Port Gellhorn, there's a small neighborhood of about five houses just northwest of the Dirt Merchants mod shop.

It has been revealed that look for the driveway filled with yard sale clutter: furniture and electronics and such. There's a bunch of balloons, a cut-out of a nearly naked cowboy, and a big American flag flying. You'll find the hat toward the back sitting on top of a guitar case on a couch. There's also a shirt, a cap, and some shoes for sale that definitely didn't belong to Arthur.
