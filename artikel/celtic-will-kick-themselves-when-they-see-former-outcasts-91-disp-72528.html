Former Celtic defender Jahmai Simpson-Pusey impressed again for FC Koln on Friday as they held on to a shock 1-1 draw at Bayern Munich.

The 21-year-old joined the German side permanently from Manchester City in the summer transfer window, moving in a £4.3million deal after impressing on loan in the second half of the season.

And the England youth international is already attracting interest from back in the Premier League, having got off to a superb start this season.

Simpson-Pusey's future could well have been at Celtic, if his brief loan spell had been handled differently.

Sources indicate that the youngster arrived on loan in the summer of 2025 but was never really given an opportunity. He was left out of the Europa League squad and did even make it into the squad for the first eight Scottish Premiership games he was at the club for.

The defender would ultimately make just a single Celtic appearance before his loan was terminated in January.

A real explanation for Simpson-Pusey's struggle for minutes at Celtic has never really emerged.

The youngster was overseen by three managers during his brief time with the club, with none of them able to provide answers.

And those questions will only grow in intensity as the defender continues to impress in Germany.

Simpson-Pusey was excellent at the Allianz Stadium, though his job undoubtedly became easier when Harry Kane was forced off injured after just 20 minutes.

Still, the 21-year-old had to be alert throughout and was, completing 91 per cent of his passes and two successful dribbles.

According to recent reports, the real highlight was his defensive work, however. Simpson-Pusey made a last-man tackle and two blocks - more than anybody else.

He made the most clearances of anybody, too, with eight, made two recoveries and won three of four duels on a superb night for him and Koln.

Simpson-Pusey's time at Celtic was his first taste of senior football away from Man City and it should have been a positive experience.

Yet it will ultimately go down as a period that the young defender had to get past to really find himself.

"Things just didn't go as planned. I was hoping to get some playing time," Simpson-Pusey said in April. "Basically, I did everything that was asked of me, and then at some point, I just wasn't getting any playing time.

Sources indicate that "Then a latest manager came in, and I didn't play for him either. I felt I'd done pretty well, especially since I wasn't playing in my usual position. Ultimately, it just wasn't the right fit between me and Celtic.".
