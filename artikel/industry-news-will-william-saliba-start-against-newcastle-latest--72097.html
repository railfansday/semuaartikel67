William Saliba is hoping to return to Arsenal’s starting XI against Newcastle today.

Industry observers note that the imperious French defender has yet to feature for the Gunners at senior level this season. He played through a back injury throughout the latter stages of last term and for much of the World Cup in the summer.

But the pain eventually grew unmanageable, and he returned to Arsenal immediately after the tournament for assessment, having been withdrawn from France’s semi-final defeat by Spain in the first half.

Arsenal decided that he should not undergo surgery, instead opting for a lengthy period of rehabilitation at Colney.

But he is now ready to return after months on the sidelines, manager Mikel Arteta has confirmed. Crucially, though, Saliba is not expected to usurp Ezri Konsa for Saturday’s trip to Newcastle.

“He is ready, he’s in a good condition. We want to ramp up his minutes,” Arteta said at his pre-match press conference on Friday. “He played two games, one behind closed doors and one more official. He did really well and he feels in good condition.”.

In a recent development, saliba featured for Arsenal’s Under-21s earlier this month, playing 45 minutes of a 5-2 defeat of Aston Villa in the PL2 at Boreham Wood. Watford were the reported opposition for the behind-closed-doors friendly to which Arteta referred.

In any case, Arteta’s enhancement on Saliba’s condition implies that he is not yet ready to start for the Gunners, meaning Konsa should continue to partner Gabriel in the heart of defence against Newcastle.

Saliba may be limited to a substitute appearance at St James’ Park as he looks to earn his first competitive Arsenal minutes since the Champions League final last season.

With Saliba not expected to start, here is Standard Sport’s Arsenal correspondent Matt Verri’s predicted Gunners XI to face Newcastle.

Predicted Arsenal XI vs Newcastle (4-2-3-1): Raya; Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori; Rice, Guimaraes; Madueke, Odegaard, Tzolis; Havertz.
