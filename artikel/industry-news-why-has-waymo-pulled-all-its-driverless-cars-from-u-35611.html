Industry News: Why has Waymo pulled all its driverless cars from US freeways?

🌐 Source: Co • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (94 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Waymo has temporarily suspended driverless rides on US freeways after California banned the service over safety concerns.
  • It has been revealed that california's Department of Motor Vehicles noted a series of traffic incidents, including robotaxis entering active construction zones, posed an imminent risk to public safety.
  • Waymo right now operates in 15 US cities, though only offered driverless freeway rides in a handful of cities, including San Francisco and Los Angeles.

Waymo has temporarily suspended driverless rides on US freeways after California banned the service over safety concerns.

It has been revealed that california's Department of Motor Vehicles noted a series of traffic incidents, including robotaxis entering active construction zones, posed an imminent risk to public safety. Waymo has now restricted freeway operations in all other US markets while it reviews the performance of its vehicles.

Waymo right now operates in 15 US cities, though only offered driverless freeway rides in a handful of cities, including San Francisco and Los Angeles.
