If you’re looking for Prime Pantry, Amazon closed it in January 2021 and moved its items into the main store.

Industry observers note that say your dishwasher pods arrive every month at $16 a box after the discount, and the price goes up a dollar every three months. A year in, the same box costs $20, 25% more than the first, and each increase revealed up in a reminder email before that box shipped.

So open each reminder email and compare every item’s price with the last delivery’s. If a price has gone up or the last box isn’t used up yet, skip that delivery or move it later, since a box you don’t need yet is money spent early. Cancel an item whose price keeps climbing. Skipping one of five subscriptions in a delivery leaves four, though, and a delivery of four loses the extra 5% on all of them.

Then check the item’s unit price, its price per load, roll, or pound, against what a warehouse club charges and what your grocery store charges on sale. Look up its price history too, to see whether Amazon’s price is low for that item.

Recent findings suggest that set each item’s delivery frequency to match how long one box lasts in your house. Amazon lets you pick anything from every week to every six months and change it at any time.
