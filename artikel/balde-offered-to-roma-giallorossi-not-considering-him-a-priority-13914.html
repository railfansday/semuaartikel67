Among other things, there’s talk of significant fees, especially for Italy.

The Catalans wanted to sell him in the summer but were asking for at least €50 million.

Sources indicate that mendes is at the moment in Barcelona to figure out how to proceed in the coming months. The door remains open for his arrival in Italy, it’s emphasized, only if the format were to change.
