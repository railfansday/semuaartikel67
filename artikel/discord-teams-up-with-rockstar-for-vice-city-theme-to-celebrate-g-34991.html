Discord has officially teamed up with Rockstar Games to introduce a limited-time GTA 6 Vice City theme, allowing users to customize the app’s appearance with colors and designs inspired by the upcoming game.

The collaboration arrives just days before Grand Theft Auto 6’s scheduled November 19 release, which will take players back to Vice City. With anticipation building around Rockstar’s next installment, Discord users can now bring the game’s neon-inspired aesthetic to their accounts.

Discord announced the upcoming customization options in a post on X, writing: “Bring Vice City to your Discord.”.

“Starting today, you can apply the Vice City color theme, app icon, and Display Name Style,” the tech giant explained.

According to recent reports, bring Vice City to your Discord.​Starting today, you can apply the Vice City color theme, app icon, and Display Name Style. ​The Vice City theme is available to everyone through December 13 and to Nitro members (excluding Nitro Basic) through February 20. pic.twitter.com/QKsT1m8Ikq.

The theme is available on both desktop and mobile, allowing owners to customize Discord with colors inspired by Vice City’s signature neon aesthetic.

Recent findings suggest that from November 16 through November 22, consumers can activate the theme directly through Discord’s Quest Home. After that, the color theme and app icon can be found under User Settings > Appearance on desktop or Settings > App Settings on mobile.

The Display Name Style is available separately through Discord’s profile customization settings, allowing users to change how their name appears alongside the fresh app design.
