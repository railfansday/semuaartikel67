Roberto De Zerbi says Luca Williams-Barnett's talent is "bigger than you think" after scoring his first Tottenham goal.

Industry observers note that williams-Barnett was rewarded for two exciting Premier League cameos with his first senior start on Saturday as Ipswich came to town. De Zerbi was rewarded with a goal and an assist from the teenager in a 2-0 win.

The 18-year-old, who joined Tottenham at the age of 10, got the home fans off their seats almost every time he touched the ball.

And De Zerbi mentioned he decided to hand Williams-Barnett his first Premier League start 10 days prior.

The Spurs manager noted: "I told him to play like he plays in the park with his friends, with the same freedom.

Asked if Williams-Barnett could emulate Spanish wonderkid Lamine Yamal, De Zerbi mentioned the Spurs teenager has the passion to reach the top of the game.
