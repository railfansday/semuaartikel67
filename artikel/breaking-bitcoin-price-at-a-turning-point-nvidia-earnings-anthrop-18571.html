Sources indicate that bitcoin price is entering a potentially decisive week as Wall Street prepares for three major events likely to reshape sentiment. Nvidia’s earnings, Anthropic’s enormous IPO, and an unprecedented Trump-Xi-Putin meeting are arriving almost simultaneously. Together, they could trigger a wave of capital rotation, putting pressure on crypto before potentially opening the door to a recovery.

Sources indicate that bitcoin price has already suffered a brutal correction, falling toward $65,000 after losing more than 20% over the past month. But the coming days could introduce a very different market dynamic. Institutional investors are preparing for one of the largest technology listings ever, bigger than SpaceX.

According to recent reports, anthropic upcoming IPO has likely become an enormous liquidity-absorption event, a butterfly effect across traditional markets and crypto. Claude developer has proposed selling 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each. The offering could raise between $57.75 billion and $64.35 billion, implying a fully diluted valuation of $1.4 trillion.

Industry observers note that the capital ambitions are extending beyond the public offering. Anthropic has separately arranged a $26 billion private placement involving major technology companies, including Google, Nvidia, Microsoft, AMD and Broadcom. Combined proceeds could reach $84.6 billion to $93.7 billion.

That is an extraordinary amount of capital competing for investor attention. Reuters reported that institutional investors are already preparing for the IPO alongside Nvidia’s earnings, making both events major tests for an increasingly vulnerable AI trade.

Anthropic’s private-market valuation has also attracted speculative trading on platforms such as Hyperliquid, where pre-IPO exposure has traded at a premium, highlighting just how aggressively investors are positioning for its public debut.

The consequences extend beyond technology stocks. Bitcoin, with its around-the-clock liquidity, could become an unintended source of funding.

But what have we learned from this? Before the June SpaceX IPO, Bitcoin experienced a huge sell-off, falling nearly 20% during the 30-day pre-IPO period. Fast forward a couple of months, Bitcoin price grinded higher as SpaceX’s attention faded.

Now, Anthropic could be just another test. If its IPO absorbs substantial investment capital, Bitcoin could face additional selling pressure. Once allocations settle, however, that temporary competition for liquidity could ease, allowing investors to reconsider previously abandoned positions.

Recent findings suggest that while Anthropic potentially competes for liquidity, Nvidia could provide the catalyst needed to restore confidence across technology markets. Nvidia will report its third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on November 17. London Stock Exchange Group expects $109 billion in revenue and 90% earnings growth.

The expectations are enormous, particularly after Nvidia’s market valuation declined from $5.8 trillion in early October to $3.9 trillion. Investors are looking for evidence that AI infrastructure spending remains sustainable despite the manufacturer’s leadership transition.

A strong earnings report could nevertheless change the narrative. Higher revenue guidance, resilient data center demand, and confidence in future AI spending could help stabilize technology valuations. Nvidia has repeatedly beaten analyst expectations for 8 straight earnings reports.

Why does this matter for Bitcoin? Institutional risk appetite often moves across multiple asset classes. When investors become comfortable taking risks in equities, speculative assets like crypto can benefit from renewed capital allocation.

If Nvidia surprises positively, Bitcoin price could receive an unexpected boost just as the Anthropic IPO reaches its public debut.

Nevertheless, constructive signals between three major geopolitical powers could improve investor confidence.
