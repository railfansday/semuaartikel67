Tech Report: GTA 6 Players Are Encountering a Mysterious Game Breaking Crash

🌐 Source: Polygon • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (559 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Grand Theft Auto 6 is somehow off to an even hotter start than many expected.
  • Sources indicate that many users across Reddit, X, and other platforms have reported this problem, which has an error code of CE-108255-1.
  • Recent findings suggest that others reported the same problem, with some able to work around it by changing their PS5’s resolution from Manual to 1080p in the system settings.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 is somehow off to an even hotter start than many expected. The game sold 32.5 million copies pre-release, bringing in a whopping $3.79 billion on day one. That makes it the biggest entertainment launch in history, according to publisher Take-Two. Unfortunately, though, not everyone has been able to enjoy the fun. Numerous players have reported that they can't progress in the game due to a persistent PlayStation 5 crashthat sends them back to the dashboard.

Sources indicate that many users across Reddit, X, and other platforms have reported this problem, which has an error code of CE-108255-1. The crash happens under a range of circumstances. Some are seeing the error in the prologue, while others don't encounter it until hours in; it can appear at a consistent point or at random. One Redditor wrote that they get the error whenever Jason is about to use a plunger in the Pipework mission, keeping them trapped in purgatory with Mandy. This Redditor said they spoke with Rockstar Games’ customer support about the issue and were told to contact PlayStation’s hardware support team, implying that Rockstar believes this is a PS5 hardware problem.

Recent findings suggest that others reported the same problem, with some able to work around it by changing their PS5’s resolution from Manual to 1080p in the system settings. Other popular “fixes” include clearing the PS5’s cache, rebuilding the database, reinstalling the game, updating the system’s firmware, or restoring the PS5 license. It seems these solutions are working for some but not for others. In some cases, people mentioned that they had encountered the problem before with other games.

According to Sony’s support page, the CE-108255-1 error is a generic code for “An error occurred with the game/application,” and Sony suggests that those who receive the error revision their game or visit the company’s PlayStation Repairs page.

According to recent reports, there are competing theories about why the CE-108255-1 crash seems to be happening to so many GTA 6 players. One prevailing hypothesis is that it is a hardware problem. X user ghost_motley suggested that GTA 6 is so graphically intensive that it’s exposing existing complications with many consumers' PS5s. “This is great news for console repair technicians, who will have a new game to stress test consoles with,” they wrote. Rockstar has touted GTA 6’s detailed environments and ray tracing, saying the game won’t get a 60 fps performance mode on console because it is too graphically demanding. Some have theorized that GTA 6 may be exposing problems with the PS5’s “liquid metal” cooling solution: in some cases, the liquid metal has dried out over time, preventing the console from cooling properly.

It has been revealed that that noted, Gizmodo reported that PlayStation support told at least one person that the problem is a software problem, implying Rockstar is at fault. An X user claimed they went to the trouble of replacing their console’s liquid metal and still ran into the crash. While it seems that Rockstar and Sony's support teams are both at the moment suggesting the other party is to blame, hopefully both are quietly looking into a fix so more people can form their own opinions on the best-reviewed game of all time.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is available now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
