When you're exploring a world as realistic and well-observed as GTA 6's Leonida, it's rare for a piece of benign infrastructure to stick out. But on night one of my playthrough, I clocked some weird streetlights.

Why are some of them turquoise?

Sources indicate that A stylistic choice by Rockstar? Maybe, but it's awfully specific, and I had a feeling there was a deeper intention behind it. So I DMed my buddy Henry, an honest-to-god Floridian who was also playing GTA 6, and asked what's up. At first he thought strange streetlights in the Key Lento area of the map were special lights meant to not disorient turtles, but those are typically red, so probably not. And that doesn't explain the purple ones.

So Henry reached out to friends in south Florida, the region that Leonida's biggest cities are based on, and they cracked the case wide open.

Recent findings suggest that "The purple [lights] are definitely real. We have a ton of them down here because whoever is in charge of that in the government bought a bunch of defective lights," the anonymous Floridian responded.

According to recent reports, yes, actual Florida cities are lit by these dubiously dim, yet undeniably cool purple lamps. According to ClickOrlando, residents first started reporting the purple streetlights in 2023. They put up hundreds of these things across the state before they started malfunctioning—it was such a kerfuffle that the government eventually released a statement saying their vendor really screwed the pooch.

Apparently the cause was a "spectral shift caused by phosphor displacement," which essentially means the LED lights were always blue, but the coating that was supposed to make appear yellow or white or whatever didn't work. The powers that be mentioned the malfunctioning lights would be replaced, but judging by our own sources in the state, they haven't gotten them all.

Sources indicate that so that's fun. If you already heard about this somewhere (other than your Floridian friend), it's probably because YouTuber AnyAustin also clocked these lights in his video published yesterday. Glad I'm not the only one who couldn't resist this rabbit hole.

Recent findings suggest that I like how Rockstar didn't go overboard with these lights—just like the genuine article, they're in and around parts of Leonida, but not everywhere. I'm still not sure how to explain the turquoise ones, though. A lot of local news, Reddit, and YouTube skimming did not produce a clear answer, other than other cities in America that do have green streetlights sometimes. Perhaps Key Lento has lamps busted in a slightly different way, or it's just a calming shade of blue that Rockstar thought looked nice. It does, to be fair.
