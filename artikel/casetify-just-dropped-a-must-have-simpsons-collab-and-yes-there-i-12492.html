TL;DR: Casetify has rolled out a newly introduced The Simpsons tech accessory collection, featuring a 3D Homer-in-the-hedge phone case, Family Charm Cubes, Snappy magnetic grips, and custom sticker cases online and in Casetify Studio stores.

Casetify is officially heading to Springfield.

According to recent reports, launching globally on Nov. 17, The Simpsons by Casetify collection turns the animated family's most recognizable couch gags, chalkboard lines, and viral internet reaction memes into everyday tech gear. Instead of a single basic design, the collection lets fans build custom, mix-and-match setups that pair The Simpsons' classic yellow-and-pink colors with Casetify's protective cases.

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free right now with T-Mobile.

From a 3D Homer hedge case to clip-on charms, here are the best pieces in the drop:.

Homer Simpson — Into the Hedge Phone Case — Homer's most famous disappearing act gets a tactile 3D upgrade, pairing a textured green bush with an embroidered Homer detail peeking out from the leaves.

The Simpsons Family Charm Cube and Collectible Add-ons — Available in two multi-sided designs inspired by Homer, Marge, Bart, Lisa, and Maggie, these collectible cubes pair with matching Snappy magnetic grips, earbuds cases, and clip-on charms.

The DIY Custom Canvas — Fans can design their own sticker-style custom cases using classic show quotes, signature gags, and character layouts to build a personalized Springfield scene from scratch.

Industry observers note that the collaboration arrives right as The Simpsons airs its record-breaking Season 38, adding another pop-culture milestone to a run that already includes 37 Emmy Awards. In addition to online orders, fans can customize pieces in person at Casetify Studio retail locations, which offer on-the-spot accessory personalization across 80 shops worldwide.

Whether you want Homer slowly backing into a hedge on your phone or a custom Springfield sticker setup, the entire collection is now live. Shop The Simpsons by Casetify collection today.
