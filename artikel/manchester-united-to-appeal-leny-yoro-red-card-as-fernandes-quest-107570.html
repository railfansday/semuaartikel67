Manchester United to appeal Leny Yoro red card as Fernandes questions officials

Manchester United have notified the Football Association of their intention to appeal against Leny Yoro’s red card during Sunday’s 4-0 defeat at Liverpool, with captain Bruno Fernandes among those questioning the decision.

According to recent reports, the French defender was dismissed in the eighth minute at Anfield after bringing down Alexander Isak outside the penalty area. Referee Sam Barrott ruled that Yoro had denied an obvious goalscoring opportunity, with Dominik Szoboszlai scoring from the resulting free-kick.

Industry observers note that according to Sky Sports, United intend to challenge the decision, with Isak’s chances of regaining possession central to the dispute. Mauricio Pochettino questioned whether Isak would have reached the ball before goalkeeper Senne Lammens, a view shared by midfielder Kobbie Mainoo.

Fernandes told the BBC after the match that it was “never a red card”.

Industry observers note that “To make a mistake like this can change a game,” he said, as reported by the Press Association. The Portuguese midfielder nevertheless accepted that United were responsible for conceding from the subsequent free-kick.

Former Premier League referee Dermot Gallagher offered a different assessment on Sky Sports’ Ref Watch programme, supporting Barrott’s decision while acknowledging the uncertainty over whether Isak would have reached the ball before Lammens.

“I think most people would have expected a red card,” Gallagher noted.

An attacker’s likelihood of retaining or regaining possession is among the factors referees must consider when assessing whether a foul has denied an obvious goalscoring opportunity.

Sources indicate that united host Brentford at Old Trafford on Saturday in Pochettino’s first home match in charge. Unless the appeal succeeds, Yoro will serve a one-match suspension against the Bees. United sit 19th in the Premier League, having won just one of their opening 11 matches. Yoro remains eligible for Wednesday’s Champions League fixture against Sporting, before attention turns to a crucial domestic fixture for Pochettino’s side.
