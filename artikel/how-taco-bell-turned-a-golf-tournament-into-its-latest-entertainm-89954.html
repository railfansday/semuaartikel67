Three years ago, Taco Bell debuted Live Más Live, an annual showcase revealing upcoming menu items and collaborations. Now, Taco Bell is taking a swing at golf.

Earlier this month, Taco Bell hosted its inaugural Live Más Open in Laguna Beach, Ca, where qualifying entrants competed for the chance to bring a discontinued menu item back to restaurants nationwide.

Hosted by Kevin Hart, the tournament placed fans of the brand alongside celebrities, athletes, and creators on a Taco Bell-inspired golf course to complete in unconventional challenges with snacks along the way. The event streams nationwide on Peacock Nov. 20, followed by its Golf Channel debut Nov.
