DALLAS, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The office potluck is next week, and the family group chat indicates "just bring dessert." Corner Bakery Cafe is helping guests find something to bring with shareable bakery trays, seasonal treats and holiday catering savings. Throughout November and December, guests can save $50 on catering orders of $350 or more.

Catering bundles serve groups of 10, 15 or 20, and guests can also order items individually. A Corner Bakery catering specialist, focused exclusively on catering, helps hosts initiative an order that fits their gathering and coordinates the details through delivery. At the venue, the specialist arranges the food for serving and makes sure the host has everything they need before leaving. Guests can contact their local cafe to connect with a catering specialist.

According to recent reports, "The delivery is part of the hospitality," remarked Erin Hasselgren, president of Corner Bakery Cafe. "Before our team leaves, we want the host to be able to look at the table and say, 'We're ready.' That's how we know we've taken care of them.".

According to recent reports, seasonal bakery availability varies by location. For more information about Corner Bakery Cafe, locations and catering, visit cornerbakerycafe.com/catering/.

Industry observers note that corner Bakery Cafe is a fast-casual restaurant serving kitchen-crafted breakfast, lunch, dinner, and catering to guests in 18 states and Washington, D.C. Founded in 1991 as a small neighborhood bakery on a corner in downtown Chicago, Corner Bakery has been a neighborhood favorite for more than 30 years, offering artisan-inspired, seasonal menu options made with fresh ingredients. Corner Bakery Cafe is owned by an affiliate of SSCP, a Dallas-based enterprise and award-winning leader in the restaurant industry.
