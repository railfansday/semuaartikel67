Industry observers note that MP3 — short for Music Presentation 3 — just dropped its second episode, and with it fresh laughs and wild tracks. As Dropout's first scripted series in seven years, this music video countdown show parodies MTV's long-canceled Total Request Live. As such, MP3 offers quirky VJs and three new music videos each episode.

Saturday Night Live's Jeremy Culhane plays Dane Cartel, a former boy-bander/host with "lightly bad behavior," who is joined by newly minted VJs Dr. Muse-Tina (Skye Townsend) and Chia (Aparna Nancherla).

Behind the music are MP3 co-creators and the podcast Off Book hosts, Zach Reino and Jess McKenna, who penned nine original songs across a wide range of genres for the season. From their long-running improvised emo outfit Every Place I Cry (alongside Nick Mandernach and Ross Bryant) to Joe Cocker-style vocals from Jacob Wysocki, the series showcases real singing from Dropout's roster—including Dropout CEO Sam Reich, Elaine Carroll, and even a surpising cameo from Paul Robalino (aka Paul Refereeno). Plus, the show is packed with Dropout Cinematic Universe Easter eggs, pulling in Smartypants callbacks ("No Thank You, the Ocean") and Very Important People's Princess Emily.

Mashable's Entertainment Editor, Kristy Puchko, sat down with Reino and McKenna in a virtual interview to learn how MP3 came together, why they never mock the music genres they parody, and the real reason a character's face is blurred out in the trailer.

Sources indicate that how to watch: The first two episodes of MP3 are streaming now on Dropout, with the season finale on Dec. 2.
