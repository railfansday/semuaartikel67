GTA 6 reviews are finally being posted, and its Metacritic score has made it the joint best-reviewed game of all time.

It has been revealed that after a 13-year wait since GTA 5, the follow-up arrives with unprecedented hype. It’s expected to break records in terms of revenue, and it’s already been nominated for plenty of Game Awards, including Game of the Year and Best Narrative.

While you won’t know if it hits the mark for you until you visit Vice City for the first time, early reviews are starting to roll out, and it appears that GTA 6 is everything fans hoped it would be.

Following the lifting of the review embargo, GTA 6 sits on a Metacritic score of 99 from 109 reviews, making it the most critically acclaimed release of 2026 and, as it stands, tied for the highest ever score on the platform.

It has been revealed that the only other game that can boast a similar rating is the original The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Check out the full top 10 below:.

Despite only just launching, it’s already beating some absolute heavyweights, including Super Mario Galaxy 2 and GTA 4 and 5. On the 2026 list, it finds itself comfortably in the number one spot, overtaking the Ocarina of Time remake and The Witcher 3 remaster.

Of course, this score could fluctuate as more and more reviews come flooding in. Don’t expect them right away, though, as GTA 6 apparently takes around 80 hours to beat.
