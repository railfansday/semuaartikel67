According to recent reports, we built one of the most powerful computer-use AIs in the world. And made it directly accessible from your browser. On March 31st, we released Holo3, our most advanced computer-use model to date.

Building something powerful is one thing; making it accessible and easy to use is another. We’re doing both.

Recent findings suggest that holoTab is a Chrome extension that navigates the web just like a person would. It automates tasks across any website with zero setup or technical skills required. You describe what you want, and the agent handles it directly inside your browser, navigating interfaces, filling fields, and making decisions the same way you would. The vision models, the action planning, the interface understanding, all of it is running underneath, working for you, and all you ever see is the result.

Some tasks are quick and forgettable, but others are long and repetitive, the kind that reliably eat up your afternoon. You might find yourself cross-referencing competitor pricing across twenty different e-commerce tabs to enhancement a master spreadsheet, or filtering through a dozen job boards and company career pages to find new listings, painstakingly porting the details into a tracking doc to manage your applications.

That's where routines come in. You start a recording, and HoloTab captures what you do in your tab in real time while you click or narrate along the way, demonstrating what you're doing and why. Your screen and your actions give HoloTab everything it needs, enriching the routine with enough context to actually understand the goal.

In a recent development, once the recording stops, a routine is generated. From then on, you can re-run your routine or schedule it whenever you need to, and HoloTab will handle the rest on its own.

In a recent development, computer-use AI is going to change how people work, research, and get things done, both in their professional and personal lives, and that shift is already closer than most people realize. But it shouldn't require a technical background to access, and the benefits shouldn't accrue only to teams with engineers who know how to wire these systems together.

HoloTab is free and ready for everyone to use today: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/holotab/hlaoiikljjgcjdhkakedfngifaopbcop.
