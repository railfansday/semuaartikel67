Man City drop points to Fulham despite superb Anderson volley.

There may have been some eyebrows raised before kick-off when Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca decided to drop club record signing Enzo Fernandez to the bench against Fulham.

Fernandez's lengthy excursions back home to Argentina for their two fixtures during the international break may have been a factor in Maresca's thinking when selecting his starting XI for this game.

According to recent reports, if it was, the Italian refrained from giving that as a reason. "I just think for this game it's better having Ayyoub Bouaddi in there," he told TNT Sports shortly before kick-off.

Recent findings suggest that even so, the move was a significant one as his side were in desperate need of victory after suffering three straight Premier League defeats. That winless streak extended to four after being held to a 1-1 draw.

Maresca had an opportunity to fix the challenges in his set-up during the two-week break and admitted on Friday he was searching for "more balance" in the "defensive line, midfield and up front".

The pairing of the steady Bouaddi and all-action Elliot Anderson in the centre of the park was the crucial call, and it may have given Maresca some idea as to how to approach the forthcoming run of games.

It has been revealed that "Ayyoub with Elliot were both really good," mentioned Maresca. "Today we used Elliot a bit more attacking midfielder, he arrived inside the box and scored a fantastic goal, he had three or four moments around the box where he was really dangerous.

Recent findings suggest that "It is another solution for us. In this moment, we are trying to know each other more as players and also to find the right solution.".

Anderson rescues Man City point against Fulham.

Elliot Anderson and Ayyoub Bouaddi both joined Manchester City in the summer.

Loud jeers greeted the full-time whistle at Etihad Stadium, a culmination of the feeling that was brewing from the home supporters throughout the game.

There was a nervous tension from the beginning, which seemed to seep on to the players on the pitch.

City displayed a flat first-half performance in which they fell behind courtesy of Rodrigo Muniz's superb curling effort, and they needed a stunning second-half volley from Anderson to rescue a point.

The England international is restricted to sitting in front of the back four when Fernandez is in the side, but has the licence to roam when Bouaddi is deployed beside him.

Aside from scoring a spectacular first Premier League goal for City, Anderson created a match-high three chances and had a 100% success rate from 16 passes in the final third of the pitch.

He also had more touches in the opposition box (7), contested more duels than any other player (17) and no-one else managed to win possession back more times than him.

After dragging his side level, another marauding run into the box was snuffed out by the visiting defenders before Rayan Cherki was brought on, which meant Anderson had to retreat back down the pitch.

Maresca needed to change things in order to apply some fresh impetus in the attacking third, but the substitution of Cherki for Phil Foden seemed to limit Anderson's capabilities.

It has been revealed that "It's not a good result, especially off the back of three losses," former City goalkeeper Joe Hart told BBC Sport. "There was some good stuff in there.

"It felt like Elliot Anderson was going to take the game by the scruff of the neck but then Cherki came on and took his position. So he [Anderson] didn't have as much of an impact on the game.

"I understood the change - when the lines are deep, there were chances of Cherki unlocking Fulham, but they just defended so well and they also offered a threat on the break.".

The trip allowed him to take the opportunity to pay a visit to boyhood club River Plate, but it is at his current club where there are question marks as to how he fits into the side.

Bouaddi's presence provides more insurance in front of the defence and the 19-year-old Moroccan has the ability to keep the ball ticking.

It means Anderson can play his natural game - but where, then, does Fernandez fit in?

The number 10 position tends to be occupied by either Cherki or Foden, while Maresca pointed out in his pre-match news conference that City "won games with Enzo and lost games with Enzo".

But the manager clearly has to work out how to get the best out of Fernandez, who was given the final five minutes against Fulham in place of Bouaddi.

"There is no point in having a brilliant squad like Manchester City if you are not going to take that into account," stated Hart.

"It's very much understandable, if you're going to have a squad and you trust everyone - if someone's done the travelling, he has done the game time, you have got to expect to not use him as much.".

City host Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday followed by a huge trip to Arsenal next Sunday, knowing they could head to the Emirates eight points behind the champions.

Maresca added: "For sure we don't like these kind of moments in terms of four games in a row without a win.

"We also know that, unfortunately, it is normal or part of the path, you go through these kind of moments. It is key that we find a way to keep clean sheets that sometimes you need to win games 1-0 for a small difference.".

Mikel Arteta's men know all about that and City now need to take a leaf out of their book.
