Anthropic’s Claude AI predicts that Bitcoin is quietly setting the stage for a move that could rewrite every previous cycle high by early 2027.

Despite heavy selling pressure, persistent ETF outflows, and extreme fear that has dominated recent weeks, a handful of structural developments are emerging beneath the noise.

The real catalyst capable of flipping the entire narrative, however, remains highly unlikely yet not impossible: a coordinated announcement in which several G20 nations and major sovereign wealth funds simultaneously designate Bitcoin as a core strategic reserve asset.

It would commit to multi-year accumulation programs totaling hundreds of thousands of BTC while integrating it into cross-border settlement systems for tokenized trade and energy markets.

Such a step would instantly transform Bitcoin from a speculative asset into a recognized monetary reserve, triggering a supply shock of historic proportions.

Crypto is up today as Bitcoin reclaimed the $60,000 level and major altcoins followed higher in a broad relief rally. Bitcoin climbed roughly 2–3.5% to trade around $60,600–$60,900, while Ethereum gained about 2–4% near $1,700. XRP, Solana, and Dogecoin also advanced 2.5%-4%.

Recent findings suggest that the total crypto market capitalization rose approximately 2–3% to around $2.1 trillion. Several factors drove the move. Prices had become deeply oversold after recent declines toward the mid-$50,000s for Bitcoin, attracting dip buyers and triggering short liquidations that amplified the upside.

Industry observers note that risk assets more broadly recovered, with technology and AI-related stocks providing a supportive backdrop that spilled over into crypto given their high correlation. Thin holiday trading volumes ahead of Thanksgiving also contributed to sharper moves.

Recent findings suggest that sentiment received a modest lift from progress on a U.S. Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill that cleared a House committee. Despite ongoing heavy ETF outflows and extreme fear readings near 11 on the Fear & Greed Index, technical oversold conditions, forced short covering, and improving risk appetite fueled today’s rebound.

On higher timeframes, Bitcoin has undergone a substantial correction from its previous cycle peaks, carving out a series of lower highs while finding repeated support in the mid-$50,000s to low-$60,000s.

The recent stabilization above $58,000–$60,000 after a sharp liquidation-driven flush has produced the first meaningful higher low on the weekly chart in several months.

Volume on the recovery legs has modestly expanded relative to the preceding decline, and weekly RSI has begun to diverge positively from the price lows, moving out of deeply oversold territory.

Key resistance remains clustered between $65,000 and $72,000; a decisive weekly close above that zone would confirm that the multi-month downtrend is exhausting itself.

With a true demand shock, measured-move projections and multi-year Fibonacci extensions from the current base open the door to far higher valuations once price enters a upcoming discovery phase.

Against a backdrop of extreme fear and heavy outflows, the market is precisely where long-term asymmetric opportunities historically appear.

Residual institutional accumulation, legislative progress on national reserves, and a still-intact long-term supply-scarcity narrative provide the foundation for a powerful reversal if the right catalyst arrives.

Even amid record November ETF outflows exceeding $5Bn and elevated rate-hike odds, several constructive signals have emerged.

The House Financial Services Committee advanced legislation to formalize a U.S. Strategic Bitcoin Reserve with a long-term lock-up provision, keeping the institutional adoption story alive.

Morgan Stanley has continued expanding its Bitcoin ETF exposure, and Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) remains a steadfast large-scale holder.

Broader market rebounds, including the relief rally following Nvidia’s leadership clarity, have helped Bitcoin reclaim the $60,000 level after testing multi-month lows.

Regulatory progress in other jurisdictions, such as Ukraine’s legalization framework, adds incremental legitimacy. These developments don’t erase near-term headwinds, but they show institutional interest hasn’t disappeared; it is simply waiting for clearer macro conditions.

If the hypothetical sovereign-reserve adoption wave materializes in late 2026, Bitcoin could accelerate far beyond previous expectations.

By January 1, 2027, Claude AI predicts its over-the-top scenario supports a target range of $285,000 to $375,000, with an aggressive stretch objective above $450,000.
