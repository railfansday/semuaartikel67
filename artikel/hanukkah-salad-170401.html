Recent findings suggest that I’ve said it before, I’ll say it again: Every holiday table needs a salad. This one was dreamed up for Hanukkah, my family’s second-favorite special occasion after Thanksgiving. Serve it with roast chicken or salmon and you have an objectively perfect meal. It’s bright, crunchy, and decked out with little “latkes.” Potato croutons have popped up in a few places over the years, like The Salad Lab’s own Hanukkah-inspired salad (great minds!), Daniel Humm’s salmon rösti salad from Made Nice, and Ore-Ida’s cutely named CrouTots. My take is as lazy as it gets: frozen tater tots, smashed. The result is ultra-crisp, like the tiniest potato pancake in the world. A salad spinner is a godsend for washing the lettuce and herbs. Any type of apple works (I like Honeycrisp or Fuji) and the fresh horseradish on top is optional. But if you can find it, get it!

More festive Hanukkah recipes this way.

Industry observers note that preheat oven to 425°. Mix together ½ cup sour cream, 3 Tbsp. drained prepared horseradish, 1 Tbsp. distilled white vinegar, a significant pinch of kosher salt, and 1 tsp. water in a small bowl, adding more water a little at a time if needed until dressing it drizzle-able. Taste and season with more salt if needed.

Industry observers note that bake 1 lb. frozen tater tots on a large rimmed baking sheet for half the time of package directions (about 10 minutes).

It has been revealed that meanwhile, place 1 small white or yellow onion, thinly sliced, in a small bowl; pour in cold water to cover and add a few ice cubes. Set onions aside.

Industry observers note that tear pull apart leaves from 1 lb. romaine lettuce (about ⅔ head) and/or Little Gem lettuce (4–5 heads) and tear large leaves in half. Place in a large bowl. Remove tough stems from 1 bunch dill and 1 bunch parsley; discard. Coarsely tear remaining leaves and tender stems. Add to lettuce.

Remove tater tots from oven and, using oven mitts for safety, lay another baking sheet directly on top; firmly press down on tots to flatten. (If you don’t have a second baking sheet, use a heatproof measuring cup or mug instead.) Return tater tots to oven and bake until deep golden brown and crisp, 10–20 minutes.(If your oven has a convection setting, now is a great time to use it.).

Drain reserved onion and pat dry. Thinly slice 2 large apples (about 1 lb.). Add both to bowl with lettuce and herbs.

Drizzle 2 Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil over and add a big pinch of flaky sea salt. Toss to combine, then mound salad on a platter. Evenly scatter tater tots and ¾ cup pomegranate seeds over.

Serve salad with remaining dressing for drizzling over and peeled fresh horseradish (if using) for grating over.
