Bank of America Sees S&P 500 Hitting 7,800 by End of 2027

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bank of America sees the S&P 500 reaching 7,800 by the end of 2027.

The forecast comes after the index suffered an 11.62% correction from its October record.

Several major Wall Street banks have set higher 2027 targets than Bank of America.

According to recent reports, bank of America equity strategist Savita Subramanian sees the S&P 500 climbing back toward 7,800 by the end of 2027. The call comes after the index fell more than 11% from its October record.

The forecast comes as investors weigh AI earnings against inflation, valuations, weaker buybacks and a tougher market ahead.

It has been revealed that the S&P 500 reached a record closing high of 7,818.93 on October 6, when the index was up about 14% for the year. But the rally promptly lost momentum.

Sources indicate that by November 20, the index had dropped to 6,910.39, marking an 11.62% decline from its record. It has since recovered, but remains about 7.9% below the October high.

That pullback is now a key part of BofA’s 2027 outlook.

Strategist Savita Subramanian expects the index to reach 7,800, which would put it close to its previous record and represent about 8.3% upside from today’s level near 7,201.

BofA also sees three possible ranges, i.e 8,500 in a bull case, 7,800 in its base case, and 6,600 if the market turns sharply lower.

But BofA is not expecting an easy climb.

Subramanian expects S&P 500 earnings to grow by about 12% in 2027. The problem is that stronger profits may not translate into the same level of stock gains seen during the earlier rally.

According to recent reports, bofA expects valuation multiples to come under pressure as buybacks weaken and companies concern more shares. That could limit how much investors are willing to pay for each dollar of earnings.

It has been revealed that AI spending is another major factor. Massive investment in AI has helped support earnings expectations across the market, but uncertainty around the size and timing of those returns could keep stocks volatile.

BofA favors communication services, financials, health care and consumer discretionary stocks, while remaining cautious on consumer staples.

The bank also sees the recent correction creating buying opportunities, although it expects 2027 to be a more difficult market.

BofA’s 7,800 target sits below several other major Wall Street forecasts.

JPMorgan, UBS and Wells Fargo each see the S&P 500 at 8,200 in 2027. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are targeting 8,700, while Deutsche Bank has the highest forecast at 9,000.

The Bloomberg-compiled consensus stands at 8,471.
