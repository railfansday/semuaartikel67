It was a day of statement wins around the NFL and the biggest statement was made by the Minnesota Vikings.

According to recent reports, in their first game since trading for Kirk Cousins, the Vikings pounded the Packers 30-10, and the impressive part is that they did that at Lambeau Field. Combined with their 39-22 win in Week 1, they became the first team since 1987 to beat the Packers by at least 17 points twice in a season.

According to recent reports, patrick Mahomes also made a statement on Sunday and that's the Chiefs offense can still function just fine without Kenneth Walker. In Kansas City's 35-13 win over Atlanta, Mahomes threw five TD passes in a game for the first time since 2022. He also threw for 348 yards, and a big reason was that he finally had the long ball working. Mahomes, who had gone 20 straight games without throwing a touchdown pass that traveled at least 20 yards in the air, produced two of those against the Falcons.

It has been revealed that deshaun Watson also made a statement. The Browns quarterback led ANOTHER fourth-quarter comeback and just became only the third QB in NFL history with a game-winning drive in each of his team's first six wins in a season (1990 Troy Aikman and 2016 Matthew Stafford).

It has been revealed that and then there was Joe Burrow. The Bengals QB made a statement on Sunday night against the Steelers with four touchdown passes in a dominating 38-18 win. Burrow now has at least four total touchdowns in five straight games, which is tied with Peyton Manning (2004) for the longest streak in NFL history.

On Monday night, the Chargers made a statement by winning their first game of the season. They shot out to a 10-0 lead and then held on for dear life.

And now, let's get to the Week 10 grades, starting with the Chargers' surprising win.

Commanders (5-3-1) 24-21 over Giants (3-5-1).
