RNA discovery reveals how gut bacterium adapts to life in intestinal mucus

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 28 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (72 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, the mucus layer in the human gut is a tough place for bacteria: nutrients are scarce, and conditions are harsh. How do bacteria adapt to such extreme environments? Researchers at the Helmholtz Institute for RNA-based Infection Research (HIRI) and the Chair of Microbiology of the Julius-Maximilians-Universität (JMU) in Würzburg, working with scientists at Vanderbilt Health in the United States, have identified a key regulator.
