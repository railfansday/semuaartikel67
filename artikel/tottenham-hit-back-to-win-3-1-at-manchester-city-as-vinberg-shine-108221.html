Tottenham hit back to win 3-1 at Manchester City as Vinberg shines

Tottenham Hotspur recovered from conceding in the 10th minute to win at the Manchester Regional Arena on Sunday 22 November. Cathinka Tandberg, Matilda Vinberg and Jessica Naz scored after Khadija Shaw had given Manchester City the lead. Spurs had not beaten City in the previous five meetings between the clubs, and had lost the last four of those in the WSL, including a 5-2 defeat in Manchester on 21 March.

According to recent reports, city struck first. Shaw finished from Jade Rose’s pass in the 10th minute, taking her league tally to 12 goals, 5 more than West Ham’s Asseyi in second place among the WSL’s top scorers.

Tottenham had two players booked before half-time, Julie Blakstad in the 8th minute and Caitlin Dijkstra in the 27th, both for fouls. They still found an equaliser in the 40th minute, when Kirsty Hanson set up Tandberg. It finished 1-1 at the break.

It has been revealed that the visitors took control of the scoreline in the closing quarter. Drew Spence, booked in the 52nd minute, supplied the pass for Vinberg to put Spurs ahead in the 74th. Five minutes later Vinberg turned provider for Naz, who played 19 minutes and scored with one of her 2 shots.

Vinberg finished with a goal, an assist and 3 key passes, rated 8.87, the highest on the pitch. Tandberg, who played 85 minutes, was next on 8.2.

City had 59% of the ball and completed 486 of their 546 passes, yet Tottenham created more. Spurs had 17 shots to 12, put 6 on target to City’s 3 and finished with expected goals of 1.73 against 1. The away side also collected four yellow cards, Eveliina Summanen’s coming in the first minute of stoppage time. Samantha Coffey was City’s only player booked, a minute later.

Sources indicate that the defeat leaves City second on 25 points, two behind leaders Chelsea after 11 games each and two ahead of third-placed London City Lionesses. It is their second loss in three matches in all competitions, after a 1-0 defeat by Chelsea, with a 2-0 win over Häcken in between.

Sources indicate that tottenham are fifth on 20 points, one behind Manchester United in fourth. Martin Ho’s side are now unbeaten in six matches in all competitions, a run that includes a 7-0 win over Leicester City FC. Pelova, in the starting line-up here, has 6 league goals, third in the scoring chart.

Tottenham are back first, hosting Charlton Athletic, bottom of the table with 0 points from 10 games, on Friday 11 December. City travel to Crystal Palace, who are 12th with 9 points, on Sunday 13 December.
