Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando got married on Saturday, and the singer shared the first look at their wedding photos—and her backless Saint Laurent dress—yesterday. Sources also revealed more about the nuptials and vow exchange with Entertainment Tonight and People.

Recent findings suggest that entertainment Tonight reported that Cyrus expressed her hope to start a family with Morando in her vows. “Before I met you I was lost, and in finding you I found myself,” she stated. “Creating with you is my all-time favorite thing, which is why I can’t wait to make a baby! I hope our baby has your hair, but mostly I hope that it has your sweet spirit.”.

In a recent development, morando, meanwhile, told her, “I’m going to be the husband who makes you feel safe and supported. I want to show up for you and our family everyday...I’m a better version of myself because of you.”.

A source told People that Cyrus was emotional during the exchange. “It was such a beautiful ceremony,” the insider mentioned. “Miley was excited and a little nervous beforehand. During the vows, she kept smiling through her tears. She was trying to hold it together, but she was just so happy to be marrying Maxx.”.

Sources indicate that guests included the Bass Persuades singer’s blended family. Her mother, Tish Cyrus-Purcell, came with her husband Dominic Purcell, while her father, Billy Ray Cyrus, attended with his girlfriend, Elizabeth Hurley.

“The most special part [of the day] was seeing Miley’s family all come together to celebrate her,” a source told People. “They wanted her to feel loved and for her to have a magical wedding day. Knowing how much it took to get to that point, seeing them all together was really moving.”.

Cyrus hasn’t said much about her ceremony yet. She captioned the Instagram post with her wedding photos simply: “M + M 🤍 11.21.26”⁠.
