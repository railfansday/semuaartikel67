AC Milan head coach Ruben Amorim has settled on the team that will face Frosinone this evening at San Siro, a report claims.

It has been revealed that milan’s aim is to pick back up where they left off before the break, i.e. to follow up the win over Genoa by beating Frosinone. It will not be straightforward though, given that Alvini’s side have arguably been the surprise package of Serie A so far this season, having been promoted last season.

Recent findings suggest that according to La Gazzetta dello Sport, Amorim will go with a 4-4-1-1 formation this evening. Mike Maignan will be in goal with a four-man defence of Mario Gila, Koni De Winter, Sankhoun Diawara and Davide Bartesaghi ahead of him.

Diego Moreira and Christian Pulisic will be on the right and left flanks respectively, with a midfield double pivot of Luka Modric and Yunus Musah currently tipped to get the nod.

In attack, Adrien Rabiot will once again play in a slightly more advanced role, just behind the striker Goncalo Ramos who is looking to end his month-long goal drought today.

Sky also have the same predicted line-up for the game, as well as a bench that includes the likes of Strahinja Pavlovic (who has returned but is not 100%), Ardon Jashari, Omari Hutchinson, Francesco Camarda and the top scorer Alphadjo Cisse.
