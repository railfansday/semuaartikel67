The climb to Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s wedding has officially begun.

After all, the Hannah Montana alum’s stepsister Lily-Rose Purcell shared special details from what appeared to be the couple’s rehearsal dinner Nov. 20—and it’s hard not to adore the sweet glimpse.

Plus, it’s clear that Cyrus, 33, and Morando, 28, can expect to receive their flowers for the event’s gorgeous set-up as one snap shared to Purcell’s Instagram Stories captured a beautifully set circular table with the event’s dinner menu on full display.

Though the menu included a main course of filet mignon and Maine lobster ravioli, followed by a dessert selection of chocolate caramel layer cake, vanilla crème brûlée and raspberry sorbet, the sweetest element was the “M+M” printed at the top—a nod to the couple’s first initials.

Meanwhile, Purcell—whose father Dominic Purcell tied the knot with Cyrus’ mom Tish Cyrus in 2023—shared even more insight into the candlelit affair as she snapped a photo of her dessert plate, which featured three mini sweet treats.

In a recent development, but what’s a rehearsal dinner without a refreshment to take the edge off? In Purcell’s case, that would be an espresso martini, which was placed beside her plate alongside a glass of water while purple and pink flowers could be seen as centerpieces in the background.

The “Flowers” singer and Liily drummer confirmed their engagement in December 2025 after four years of dating.

And while nobody’s perfect in any relationship, Cyrus shared she had a very specific fear ahead of saying “I do” to the musician.

In a recent development, "We've been together six years, and I've never gotten the ick," she told Zane Lowe in an interview shared by Apple Music Sept. 17. "All of a sudden I got in my head, I was like, ‘Oh my God, we’re about to get married. What if I get the ick?'".

But Morando—who first met Cyrus on a blind date in 2021—had an answer that proved he could be the one.

"I asked him, 'What happens if you get the ick once you’re married?'" she continued. "He’s like, 'You deal with it and you move on and you don’t be a baby. The ick is for when you’re in fifth grade.'".

Even so, the Grammy winner is excited to bring her playful side into the couple’s next chapter.

According to recent reports, as she put it, "I think that’s the thing that maybe generations prior didn’t teach us, that marriage doesn’t need to be a prison. It can actually be a playground.".
