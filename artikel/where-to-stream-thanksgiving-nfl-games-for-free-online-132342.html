In a recent development, for many of us, football is just as much a part of Thanksgiving as turkey and gravy. The NFL is serving up a trio of games this Thanksgiving Day: the Chicago Bears vs. the Detroit Lions, the Philadelphia Eagles vs. the Dallas Cowboys, and the Kansas City Chiefs vs. the Buffalo Bills.

According to recent reports, the Bears and the Lions, both 8-2, kick off Thanksgiving NFL action with a matchup that will decide the NFC North leader and the second-place NFC team after the San Francisco 49ers. The Cowboys (5-6) and the Eagles (3-7) will both be looking for a bragging-rights win against their rivals and an upswing after rough seasons. At the end of the day, we’ll get another substantial matchup in the Chiefs (8-2) vs. the Bills (9-1), who are both looking to stay on top of the AFC.

If you don’t have a way to stream today’s football games, read on. Ahead are a few ways to livestream the Thanksgiving NFL games for free, plus details on all three games.

This year’s Thanksgiving Day NFL slate will include three football games. Here’s the schedule:.

Sources indicate that if you don’t have a cable or satellite proposal that carries CBS, FOX, and NBC, you can watch all Thanksgiving NFL games using a live TV streaming service that carries the three channels. Our favorites are DirecTV, Fubo, and Hulu + Live TV, which all begin with free trials that you can use to stream Thanksgiving football games for free online.

DirecTV carries CBS, FOX, and NBC in its MyNews and MySports Genre Packs and all of its comprehensive Streaming TV Packages. All of these plans start with a five-day free trial that you can use to watch football on Thanksgiving for free. After the trial (and an introductory discount), pricing starts at $44.99 a month.
