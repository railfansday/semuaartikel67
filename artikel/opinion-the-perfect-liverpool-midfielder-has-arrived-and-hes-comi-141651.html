Liverpool are preparing the perfect midfielder who is on his way towards Andoni Iraola.

There's a common consensus among the club's supporters about the Reds needing to reinforce the middle of the park.

Sources indicate that ryan Gravenberch has disappointed for most of the campaign. Curtis Jones left in the summer. Which leaves Iraola with just three reliable midfield options in Alexis Mac Allister, Trey Nyoni and Dominik Szoboszlai.

The latter has also struggled with both injury issues and inconsistencies this season. The aforementioned Nyoni is still a teenager and he can't have too much pressure thrust on his shoulders too soon.

We've all seen what happens with players given too much at that age. Stefan Bajcetic's example should serve as a cautionary tale.

So the consensus is right. But who could Julian Ward target for Liverpool's midfield?

In a recent development, liverpool's midfielder targets have already been confirmedThe Reds are exploring several options at this stage. The blueprint is to go out and buy a midfielder for the long-term this summer. However, if a target becomes available in January, Ward won't hesitate to pull the trigger. He has done so in the past, working hard to get the Luis Diaz deal over the line and securing the future of Cody Gakpo. This time around, the club's main target is Lamine Camara. It's pretty much been confirmed that Liverpool have a long-standing interest in the Senegalese midfielder. Alex Scott is also on the club's radar, but he might be a costly option. Alternatives include Mikel Jauregizar and Sergi Altimira. Manu Kone is also highly appreciated by the club's recruitment team.
