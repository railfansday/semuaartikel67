Investor Steve Eisman noted the so-called AI neocloud stocks will be the biggest losers from the unfolding Nvidia scandal.

Nvidia was essentially a "virtual vertical holding organization and it's unwinding," stated Eisman of "Significant Short" fame on CNBC's "Squawk Box." "I think neoclouds are all in big trouble though because they are part of that vertical holding company and I think they're about to get ejected.".

Neoclouds, which used borrowed funds to build data centers, have been among the hardest-hit stocks during the recent pullback in the artificial intelligence trade. CoreWeave and Nebius are each down more than 50% the last three months.

All eyes on Wall Street are on chipmaker Nvidia as it reports earnings on Tuesday after the bell while grappling with a potential Chinese espionage scandal. Founder and CEO Jensen Huang resigned on Thursday under a cloud of controversary revolving around his affair with a suspected Chinese spy. The Commerce Department has subsequently moved to close a loophole on China's remote use of data centers.

Investors are worried as scrutiny increases on Nvidia's China revenue, it may pull back on its extensive financing that supported the AI industry.

Frontier algorithm organization Anthropic is expected to make its public debut on Thursday as concerns about the company's profitability potential linger amid the rapid and ongoing commodification of AI.

Eisman noted he doesn't think the much-ballyhooed offering is very notable anymore to the AI trade because expectations have come down.

"I don't think anybody really cares," the investor said.

Instead, Eisman is focusing on how much Democrats will restrict data center building when they take control of Congress and various governorships in January.

"I think the biggest complications by far are how much are, how much are local and state governments going to slow AI data center construction? Is it going to be 100% moratorium? That would obviously be — that would put the country into recession overnight. Or is it something like 10% to 30%?" he stated.

In the event of a 10% to 30% slowdown in data center construction, Eisman stated the group of companies within the AI trade that he favors the most are the hyperscalers.

"The group that makes out the best are the hyperscalers because they go cash flow positive again," he stated.

Sources indicate that correction: Nvidia founder and CEO Jensen Huang resigned on Thursday. An earlier version misstated the day.
