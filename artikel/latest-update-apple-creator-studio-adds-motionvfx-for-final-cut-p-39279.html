Last month Apple announced big updates coming to Apple Creator Studio in November, and those updates have just arrived, headlined by the addition of MotionVFX in Final Cut Pro and a lot more.

When Apple unveiled its latest MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR last month, it highlighted several major updates coming to Apple Creator Studio’s app suite.

Logic Pro got its update earlier in November, which introduced new AI features and also the first example of Apple charging for increased AI access via ‘intelligence usage packs.’.

Now today after about a two-week gap, the rest of the Apple Creator Studio updates are rolling out on the App Store.

Pixelmator Pro has added a latest Generate Fill feature, which “lets creators transform selected areas of an image using natural language.” And you can edit images from a Final Cut Pro project using Pixelmator’s tools.

Updates for Pages, Keynote, and Numbers are rolling out too with an Intelligent Assistant feature, as well as smaller releases for Apple’s other pro utilities.
