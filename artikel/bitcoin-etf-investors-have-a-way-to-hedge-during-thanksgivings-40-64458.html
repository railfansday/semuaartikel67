Bitcoin ETF investors have a way to hedge during Thanksgiving’s 40+ hour trading gap

🌐 Source: Cryptoslate • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (548 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Spot Bitcoin ETFs listed on NYSE Arca will miss a full core trading session on Thanksgiving.
  • Sources indicate that arca’s core session closes at 4 p.m.
  • CME claims standard and Micro Bitcoin futures and eligible options trade around the clock on Globex, with weekday maintenance from 4:00 to 4:02 p.m.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Spot Bitcoin ETFs listed on NYSE Arca will miss a full core trading session on Thanksgiving. The exchange’s Friday core session will open 41.5 hours after Wednesday’s core close. During the Thursday closure, traders with access to CME Group’s derivatives market can still use eligible Bitcoin futures to adjust their exposure to Bitcoin’s price.

Sources indicate that arca’s core session closes at 4 p.m. ET Wednesday, Nov. 25, and reopens at 9:30 a.m. ET Friday, Nov. 27, according to the NYSE holiday calendar. On Friday, early trading starts at 4 a.m. ET, the core session closes at 1 p.m. ET and late trading ends at 5 p.m. ET.

CME claims standard and Micro Bitcoin futures and eligible options trade around the clock on Globex, with weekday maintenance from 4:00 to 4:02 p.m. CT. Traders need an account with a futures broker that offers access. Spot-Quoted futures follow a separate schedule.

It has been revealed that an investor holding the Grayscale Bitcoin Trust ETF, which holds Bitcoin and lists its shares on Arca, could sell cash-settled Bitcoin futures to hedge against a decline in Bitcoin’s price. If both the ETF shares and the futures fall, gains from the futures position could offset some of the loss on the shares. The investor keeps the shares, and their price can move by a different amount from the futures price.

The scheduled NYSE Arca and CME events for Nov. 25–27, 2026.

With settlement banks closed Thursday, CME will defer processing of variation payments, cash margin and collateral transfers.

According to recent reports, arca has separate early and late sessions. CME availability depends on eligible contracts, maintenance and futures broker access. CME Clearing gives a day for Thursday submissions to clear.

The 41.5-hour gap adds 24 hours from 4 p.m. Wednesday to 4 p.m. Thursday and 17.5 hours until the 9:30 a.m. Friday core open, all Eastern time. The diagram indicates sequence. CME details follow its product schedule and two holiday notices.

CME states it will skip fresh daily settlement prices for crypto contracts on Thursday. It will still publish fixings, the prices used to determine exercise and assignment for crypto options expiring that day.

Under the CME Clearing holiday advisory, Wednesday’s settlement prices will carry forward for Thursday’s end-of-day settlement variation, the daily debits and credits tied to changes in settlement prices. CME will skip its intraday cycle. It will still recalculate initial margin, the collateral required to carry positions, at the end of the day.

Trades submitted to CME Clearing on Thursday will clear Friday. With settlement banks closed for Thanksgiving, CME claims cash margin, collateral transfers and settlement variation payments will go unprocessed Thursday. A futures trade can change a trader’s exposure when it executes, while clearing and cash movements follow the holiday schedule.

According to recent reports, CME has scheduled Friday’s daily equity and crypto settlement prices for noon Central. At that hour, 1 p.m. Eastern, Arca’s shortened core session will end.
