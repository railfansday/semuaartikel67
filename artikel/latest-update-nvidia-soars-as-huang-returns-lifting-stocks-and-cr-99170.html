Latest Update: Nvidia Soars as Huang Returns, Lifting Stocks and Crypto Shares

🌐 Source: Bitcoinmagazine • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (634 words, 📚 Deep Dive)

  • It has been revealed that several items remain open.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nvidia shares jumped about 35% on Monday after the chipmaker reinstated Jensen Huang as president and CEO, restoring roughly $1 trillion in market value at the open. The stock traded near $169.20 at 10:41 a.m. ET, up from Friday’s close of $125.53, and Wall Street rallied with it.

The board voted unanimously to bring Huang back, effective immediately, after what it called a series of discussions and meetings. It stated it received input from “senior levels of the United States government” and is confident his return “poses no national security risk.” President Donald Trump had urged the move.

It has been revealed that several items remain open. An independent board committee will finish its investigation into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, and Huang mentioned he will cooperate. Nvidia also formed a national security and export control committee and is searching for a chief compliance officer.

Huang resigned on Nov. 11, days after admitting an affair with a woman U.S. officials believe is linked to Chinese intelligence. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress became interim CEO and Harvey Jones became board chair. Nvidia had remarked it was searching for a permanent successor.

Sources indicate that the stock slid for seven straight weeks, the longest such streak since 2008. On Friday it plunged 14% after Reuters reported that the SEC is examining the firm’s accounting practices. Reuters noted that investigations are not evidence of wrongdoing. Huang’s return reverses most of the losses since his affair became public.

Industry observers note that the selloff came even after Nvidia reported better-than-expected quarterly results on Nov. 17. For weeks, the board had stated it was not considering bringing Huang back, which makes Monday’s announcement a sharp turn for the world’s most closely watched chipmaker.

The Nasdaq Composite rose about 7.4% to near 24,300 at 10:41 a.m. ET, and the S&P 500 gained about 5.4% to near 7,280. The Dow Jones Industrial Average climbed about 3.3%, or more than 1,500 points, to near 49,700. The S&P 500 had fallen seven straight days.

Chip stocks followed Nvidia higher. Micron (MU) rose about 21% and Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM) gained about 16%, while Intel (INTC) added about 15%. AMD rose about 9% and Broadcom (AVGO) was up about 3%.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent warned on Sunday that the Fed may be making a “policy mistake” if it overtightens. The central bank meets Dec. 8-9, and a quarter-point increase remains the base case.

Recent findings suggest that the bitcoin price climbed to a Monday high near $64,500, according to Bitcoin Magazine Pro data, and was near $63,000 at 10:41 a.m. ET. That is about $8,000 above the weekend low of $55,106, hit at roughly 1:17 a.m. ET on Saturday. Bitcoin had slid with stocks on Friday.

Crypto-linked stocks jumped. Coinbase (COIN) rose about 9% to near $111, Robinhood (HOOD) gained about 11% and Circle (CRCL) added about 11%. Strategy (MSTR) climbed about 13% to near $88 after it disclosed a purchase of 457 bitcoin last week at an average of about $64,898, lifting its holdings to 851,305 BTC.

Recent findings suggest that the rebound follows a rough stretch for bitcoin. It finished last week down about 6%, its seventh straight red weekly candle, and spot bitcoin ETFs shed about $1.79 billion, their fourth straight week of net outflows. Bitcoin has traded closely with technology stocks during the selloff.

Miners posted the biggest gains. IREN soared about 31%, while CleanSpark (CLSK) rose about 17% and MARA gained about 16%. Core Scientific (CORZ) added about 14% and Riot Platforms (RIOT) gained about 10%.

Bitcoin treasury companies also rallied. Strive (ASST) rose about 16%, Nakamoto (NAKA) gained about 6% and Twenty One Capital (XXI) added about 10%.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $63,000.
