Opinion: Liverpool have found their next Hugo Ekitike in €40m star

Hugo Ekitike has been side-lined due to injury since April now.

The Frenchman managed to tear one the ligaments in his ankle with his return date not yet set in stone.

But, at the start of the season, Andoni Iraola did mention that Liverpool hope the striker is ready to feature in their final two games of the Champions League league stage.

If he can manage to do this, the second half of the Reds' season will certainly look much more positive than the first.

However, if upon his return he isn't back to his usual self, there are some options that Liverpool can explore with a deal for a new striker now emerging.

Gustavo Varela could become Liverpool's next Hugo Ekitike.

Last summer, a lot of people were questioning whether Liverpool had overpaid for Ekitike or not.

In the end, a deal worth around £70 million was agreed for the Frenchman with other clubs such as Newcastle United also interested in signing him.

He's performed fairly well during the 2024-25 season but, during his time with Paris Saint-Germain, things didn't really go to plan.

A lot of people suggested that the striker was too young and too raw for England's top flight but, fortunately, he managed to prove them all wrong.

However, Ekitike's injury set his progress back a bit with some people even suggesting that a upcoming striker should be brought in to plug the gap.

Lewis Koumas eventually took up that role but, that doesn't mean that the Reds' don't have a trick or two up their sleeve, especially if this latest update from Football Insider is to be believed.

"Liverpool will not prioritise a move to sign Monza striker Gustavo Varela in January, sources have told Football Insider," they wrote.

"The 21-year-old has emerged as a target for the Reds, having struck 11 goals from his opening 11 games in Serie A so far this term.

"The Reds are "keeping tabs" on the Portuguese youngster, with several top Italian clubs also having their "eyes fixed on him" at the moment.

"However, sources say that Varela is viewed as more of a long-term option at Anfield, and the club are not likely to make a move for him this winter.

"Monza are relaxed over their frontman's future for now, but they know that further interest from top clubs is inevitable if he continues to perform.".

Gustavo Varela could be Liverpool's next Hugo Ekitike.

Before the 2025-26 season kicked off, Gustavo Varela was a relatively unknown striker.

The 21-year-old joined Serie A outfit Monza for around €2.5 million in the summer with the deal not expected to cause that much of a stir.

However, to the surprise of many, the Portuguese attacker is at the moment the Serie A's top scorer.

He has 11 goals to his name and, despite all of his efforts, Monza are still sitting inside the relegation zone.

Obviously, this could just be a purple patch of form which is why the attacker is more of a long-term target rather than a short-term option.

But, if Liverpool do sign him, he could follow a similar path to Ekitike and prove the doubters wrong.

An asking price worth around €40 million has been mooted for the striker but, it's currently unclear whether the Reds would spend that much on him, even if this form does continue.
