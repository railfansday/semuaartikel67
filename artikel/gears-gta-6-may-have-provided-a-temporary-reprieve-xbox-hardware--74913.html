Industry observers note that xbox hardware sales were up 42% in October in the U.S. year-over-year. It's been donkeys since we've seen positive news attached to this level of stonks coming out of Microsoft, so we'll take it!

Sources indicate that the figures were shared in a monthly report from the statistical boffins over at Circana. Mat Piscatella revealed that US hardware spending was up by 2% compared to October last year. PlayStation 5 sales grew by 20%, Switch 2 dropped 11%, and Xbox Series surged by 42%. Phwoar!

However, Piscatella made sure to point out that "higher pricing was the primary driver of dollar sales growth" for Xbox Series, as "XBS unit sales grew by 10%" whilst the average selling price also bumped up by 29%.

Xbox's planned hardware price increase came into effect back in August, but according to Circana, Gears of War: E-Day and GTA 6 "may have provided at least a temporary reprieve" for the console's sales figures.

"It’s no secret that Xbox Series hardware sales have struggled in recent years. Gears and GTA may have provided at least a temporary reprieve.".

Recent findings suggest that gears of War: E-Day appears to be something of a console mover, and I'd wager help from Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 played a part too. Microsoft has claimed the top two spots in Circana's projected best-selling video games chart for October 2026, taking both physical and digital dollar sales into consideration.

Modern Warfare 4 was the best-selling game of October, which Piscatella also noted makes it the "19th consecutive year that a Call of Duty franchise release has been the best-selling game of its debut month".
