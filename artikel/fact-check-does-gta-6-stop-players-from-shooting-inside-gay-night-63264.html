As players explore Leonida for the first time, misleading claims about Grand Theft Auto 6 have begun circulating on social media as evidence that Rockstar has toned down the series' violence. The two claims I've seen most often are along the lines of:.

Both of these claims are misleading half-truths.

⛔ No, because there is no gay or LGBTQ nightclub in GTA 6.

Sources indicate that it is true that you cannot use firearms inside of GTA 6 clubs, such as Nine1nine and The Vault. Grand Theft Auto 5 had the same restriction. But none of the clubs in GTA 6 are specifically presented as gay or LGBTQ clubs.

It is reasonable to speculate that Rockstar would want to avoid in-game scenarios that closely resemble the 2016 Pulse nightclub shooting in Florida—the state Leonida parodies—but the studio has not stated as much.

⛔ No, unless you've turned on a setting which reduces vehicle damage.

Clips of Grand Theft Auto 6 airplanes smacking into tall buildings without taking any apparent damage have been used on social media to claim that Rockstar is intentionally preventing players from replicating 9/11-like crashes, or that the vehicle physics are shoddy.

The clips of ultra-strong planes aren't fake, but are achieved by turning on "Reduced Vehicle Damage" in the gameplay settings.

In the menu, Rockstar warns players that turning the setting on will "significantly change the balance and experience of gameplay.".

It has been revealed that if you fly into a building without reduced vehicle damage, your plane will explode. It doesn't damage the building, but there's nothing to indicate that Rockstar has manipulated vehicle physics to avoid recalling terrorism.
