Black Friday and Cyber Monday are a blitz of sales on substantial-ticket electronics like TV’s, headphones, and speakers. It all screams “latest and greatest” that gets a bit loud, and the respite sales I’m perusing are e-readers. I was admittedly a holdout who opted for the tangibility of a real book but—let’s be honest— e-readers are much more convenient to tote around than a stack of paperbacks. Right now some of PopMech’s best-tested e-readers are on sale for Black Friday for up to 40 percent off.

Industry observers note that kindle is the household e-reader name because of its wide array of models and features to choose from: They can be as simple as a back-lit tome to rivaling a color e-ink notebook or tablet. Kindle also has a proprietary library that seamlessly integrates with fans of the Amazon ecosystem.

According to recent reports, the e-reader that converted me was Rakuten’s Kobo. The device offers so much more freedom than the Amazon Kindle. It’s a self-hosted option that lets you manage ebooks as you see fit: It integrates cleanly with Calibre (a free, open-source ebook manager), and supports both OverDrive and Libby without any hacky workarounds. It also supports EPUB files, meaning the ebooks are DRM-free and can be read on any device.
