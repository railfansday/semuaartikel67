Players have found that music in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+’s fresh GTA 6-themed secret run was made with Suno, as the free DLC sits at Overwhelmingly Negative recent reviews on Steam.

The run arrived in the same update that brought Repentance+ to PS5, Xbox, and Switch 2 on November 19, a release date publisher Nicalis picked to go head-to-head with GTA 6.

Entering the seed V1CE BSMT unlocks a Vice City-inspired daily run with its own soundtrack, made up of seven songs plus a handful of intros and outros.

A player on the Binding of Isaac subreddit extracted the run’s music from the game files and found several tracks still carrying metadata reading “made with suno,” along with links to the songs on Suno’s site.

The metadata on one track highlights it was created on October 30, 2026, less than three weeks before the patch went live. Other players have since checked the files and confirmed the find.

The run itself is credited to a modder known as Mr. Headcrab, who the original poster notes is “staunchly anti-AI” and had no idea the music was generated.

Valve requires developers to disclose AI-generated content on a game’s Steam page, but Repentance+’s store listing makes no mention of it.

The DLC presently sits at Overwhelmingly Negative recent reviews, with only 10% of 908 reviews from the past 30 days positive, though the same upgrade has also been blamed for breaking mods and causing crashes.

Recent findings suggest that for some fans, the AI music is the final straw. “They used AI when synthwave is like… the easiest genre to make,” one player wrote, in a comment with dozens of upvotes.
