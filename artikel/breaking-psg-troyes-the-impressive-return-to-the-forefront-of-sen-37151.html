Breaking: PSG – Troyes: the impressive return to the forefront of Senny Mayulu is confirmed

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that there are returns that tell much more than a simple return to form. That of Senny Mayulu today looks like a true demonstration of character, talent and maturity. A few weeks ago, the young Parisian was going through a delicate period, removed from the field by an injury and faced with particularly tough competition within the Parisian squad. But far from being defeated, the Titi has patiently rebuilt its dynamic. Since his return, he has had a string of convincing performances, regaining influence in the game and reminding everyone why PSG sees in him an element of the future. And against Troyes (1-0), this Saturday evening, Mayulu once again made an impression. A major performance, which goes well beyond statistics and confirms that a player is changing dimension.

From the first seconds, the tone was set. Mayulu, in a false number 9 position, did not wait to take charge. In the 4th minute of play, perfectly found by a sharp pass from Vitinha in the heart of the area, he created a first huge opportunity. His shot, deflected by Konaté, finally landed on the crossbar. A minute later, on a perfectly felt call and a precise serve from Doué, he ideally brought the ball to himself, but his attempt ended, this time, in the small Trojan net. Two frank situations in five minutes which already illustrated his full range: intelligence of movements, quality of calls, liveliness and ability to project oneself into spaces. But to reduce his performance on these occasions would be to miss the point. Because Mayulu especially impressed with his understanding of the game and his permanent influence on Parisian movements.

Industry observers note that positioned as false number 9, the young Parisian demonstrated particularly interesting tactical intelligence. Very available to his partners, he never stopped stopping, proposing solutions and seeking the right positioning between the lines to unbalance the opposing block. In each of his ball catches, he brought percussion, spontaneity and this ability to accelerate the game which is sometimes lacking in matches where spaces are rare. His technical quality also allowed him to turn around quickly, fix his opponents and free up space for others. In the 38th minute of play, he demonstrated it again with a superb pass for Torres, who finally came up against Konaté from a tight angle. But it was above all a minute later that his influence took on an additional dimension: on a perfectly executed cross, Mayulu placed the ball in the ideal zone and allowed Zabarnyi to open the scoring. An offering rewarding a very high level first period and testifying to his ability to make the difference other than through goals.

And because great players don’t only stand out when they have the ball, Mayulu also made an impression with his defensive investment. His returns regularly allowed PSG to recover from dangerous situations. His activity without the ball, his generosity in pressing and his ability to repeat efforts also particularly caught the attention of Luis Enrique, who did not fail to applaud his protégé. In the 68th minute of play, he again delighted the Parc des Princes with a magnificent feint on a cross from Ruiz, creating a new opportunity for Torres, who unfortunately was unable to finish. One thing is certain, until his exit in the 85th minute, replaced by Dro to the cheers of the Parisian public, he never stopped making himself useful. Nine balls recovered in total: a figure which perfectly sums up this complete performance, where offensive talent was accompanied by remarkable collective investment. “ He is incredible, he can play everywhere, he does everything very well in all positions and that’s why he is one of the best players in the world », even ignited Lucas Beraldo, passing through the mixed zone after his 100th in the Parisian jersey. “He didn’t play much at the start of the season, but since he’s been playing he’s been at the level. And what’s more, he scored tonight.”confided Luis Enrique at a press conference.

In a recent development, finally, the return of Senny Mayulu – author of 3 goals and 2 assists this season – is taking on a whole latest dimension. What initially looked like a good recovery has, at the time of writing, become a real rise in power. Goals, assists, initiative, tactical intelligence and defensive investment, the Parisian Titi seems to tick all the boxes of a player capable of establishing himself permanently in Luis Enrique’s rotation, or even claiming more. His call-up to the Espoirs also rewards this newfound dynamic and confirms that his performances no longer go unnoticed. At only 20 years old, the native of Le Blanc-Mesnil displays a maturity and versatility that are particularly valuable in a team as demanding as that of PSG. Of course, there is still a long way to go and the competition will not do him any favors. But one thing is certain: Mayulu is no longer content to come back. In recent weeks, he has established himself and progressed. And when the Parc des Princes rises to accompany his exit, it is no longer just to salute the potential of a young man trained at the club, but to applaud a player who, evening after evening, is gaining weight.
