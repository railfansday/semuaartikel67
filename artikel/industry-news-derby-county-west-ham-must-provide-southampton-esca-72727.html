Southampton have fought their way back up the Championship table and into play-off contention despite a four-point deduction at the start of the season, as head coach Tonda Eckert may already have one eye on the upcoming January transfer window.

In what is now old news, Southampton were deducted four points at the start of the Championship campaign after being found guilty of spying on opponents last season, and were also thrown out of the play-offs as a result.

This time around, the Saints are looking to claw their way into the top eight legitimately with Eckert still at the helm, and following eight wins, four draws, and three defeats after 15 league games, they are well within play-off contention.

The German boss has stamped his authority at St Mary's following the summer transfer window, though not every addition has necessarily worked out the way he would've hoped.

Southampton saw the likes of Shea Charles, Taylor Harwood-Bellis, Cameron Archer, and Ben Brereton Diaz head out of the St Mary's exit door over the summer, as the likes of Lewis Dobbin, James Ward-Prowse, Keven Schlotterbeck, and Divin Mubama joined in the opposite direction.

Mubama, 21, joined on loan from Manchester City after spending the previous campaign on loan with Stoke City in the Championship, with the England youth international expecting to push Canadian striker Cyle Larin hard for a place in the starting XI.

Divin Mubama Stoke City Championship statistics 2025/26 (Data as per FotMob).

However, despite his 13 league appearances thus far, only one of those has come as a start, with Mubama being limited to just over 200 minutes of league football so far this campaign, struggling to make an impact in those limited minutes.

Naturally, his parent club, Man City, won't be pleased by his lack of game time and subsequent development, as Football League World has taken a look at two Championship clubs that should consider rescuing the 21-year-old this winter.

Derby County find themselves in the midst of a scrap towards the foot of the Championship table as things stand, with two wins, four draws, and nine defeats in their opening 15 league games piling pressure on head coach John Eustace.

A 3-1 home victory over Bolton Wanderers just before the international break has seemingly given Eustace a lifeline at Pride Park, and if his side can start to pick up some form, he may well start turning his attention to the January transfer window sooner rather than later.

Up top, Derby can boast the likes of Carlton Morris, Henrik Meister, and Lars-Jorgen Salvesen, whilst both Patrick Agyemang, Mohammed Fuseini, and Rhian Brewster can also operate in that position; on the face of it, a new centre-forward option is not needed.

However, those six options have just five goals between them at this stage in the season, and whilst numerous factors can be attributed to this, Derby simply cannot afford to settle with what they have got.

The Rams, according to Pete O'Rourke via X, were closing in on a move for Mubama earlier on in the summer window, but for one reason or another, the deal never went through, and the England youth international headed to Southampton instead.

The sort of centre-forward that Eustace loves: quick, strong, capable of holding up the ball and interlinking play as well as getting in behind, and with smart movement, Mubama would be an excellent addition for Derby, and if he can be provided with chances regularly, he will help the Rams look towards the play-offs instead of the relegation zone.

Mubama spent the bulk of his youth playing days in the West Ham United academy, going on to make 18 first-team appearances for the Hammers and scoring once before heading to Man City in the summer of 2024 for just £1.2 million compensation.

West Ham have endured a tricky start to life back in the Championship, with Nuno Espírito Santo being handed his marching orders after 13 games and being replaced by former Fulham, Bournemouth, and Burnley boss Scott Parker.

Parker made over a century of appearances for the East London outfit during his playing days, and it is hoped that he can steer the club in the right direction following his appointment.

The former England midfielder may well already have one eye on the January transfer window, with Alan Nixon having recently reported via Patreon that West Ham are targeting a latest striker and left-back this winter.

Taty Castellanos and Pablo Felipe have not handled the drop into the second tier especially well, as Joel Piroe has emerged as the preferred option up front as things stand, though the Dutchman could do with some competition.

As a cheap loan, and a player who understands exactly what the West Ham DNA is, Mubama would be a solid fit for the Hammers and would offer a completely different profile to Piroe.

If Castellanos and Pablo leave, a newly introduced striker will be needed regardless, and following his lack of minutes with Southampton, Parker has the opportunity to show the Saints exactly what they've been missing out on with a January swoop.
