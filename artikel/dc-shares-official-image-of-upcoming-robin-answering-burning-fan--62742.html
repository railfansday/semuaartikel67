In a recent development, DC just shared a newly introduced image of its latest iteration of Robin, answering a major fan question about this new hero's superpowers. Here's the new art, and everything we know about the latest Boy Wonder.

Sources indicate that DC recently rebooted the iconic Legion of Super-Heroes - a group of superhumans from the 31st Century, inspired to found a new 'Age of Heroes' by the legacy of the modern day Superman. With Brainiac hunting different versions of the Legion across the multiverse, they find themselves beset by an enemy they can't comprehend.

Industry observers note that DC recently confirmed the latest recruit to the Legion would be Bat-Lad - a future version of Robin who follows Batman's legacy rather than the Man of Steel's. The first art of the character raised questions about his powers that DC has just answered.

Sources indicate that DC recently revealed V. Ken Marion's cover for Legion of Super-Heroes #4, showing Bat-Lad in action. The young hero is shown in a dingy street with a shadowy being behind him. This is something fans already saw in Hayden Sherman and Tamra Bonvillain's main cover (below), but it far more clearly communicates the new hero's powers.

While that cover revealed a silhouette behind Bat-Lad, Marion's art makes it clear that the shadow is a physical being controlled by Bat-Lad, with its Venom-like ichor curling around his arm. It's also clear that the original cover isn't obscuring this shadow being - the all-black appearance is its official design.

In a recent development, the new cover appears to confirm that Bat-Lad is essentially a reverse-Robin. While the original Robin is Batman's sidekick, Bat-Lad appears to be able to summon a towering creature of living darkness. It's an awesome twist on the 'Batman and Robin' partnership, showing how Batman's legacy has been warped over the centuries.

Sadly, it seems that Bat-Lad's powers have a major drawback, as the summary for the glitch tells fans to "Welcome a boy and his uncontrollable shadow monster to the world of Legion!" Despite this, DC refers to Bat-Lad as a "latest legionnaire," confirming he'll join the team.

Fans don't yet know much about Bat-Lad other than that he dresses in Robin's colors and can summon a shadow monster. However, it's possible he comes from Planet Gotham. This location was introduced in 2020's Legion of Super-Heroes, characterized as the galaxy's first artificially created planet.

Planet Gotham echoes Batman's iconic city, with a noir aesthetic and ruled over by a president referred to as the 'Commissioner.' The populace are served by robots using the holographic visage of Bruce Wayne's butler Alfred Pennyworth and policed by androids in the style of Victorian 'bobbies.'.

Planet Gotham is characterized as an essentially crime-free utopia, but one with a strict rule of law. Casinos and gaudy luxury are everywhere, suggesting the limits of Batman's ability to truly change Gotham's spirit, no matter the time frame.

DC's 31st Century was canonically rewritten by recent events, so the world of the Legion of Super-Heroes isn't quite as fans remember it. That could mean that Planet Gotham no longer exists, however with each Legionnaire tending to be from a different planet, so it would make a lot of sense for Bat-Lad to call Planet Gotham home.

The Legion of Super-Heroes are known for having a massive membership, making even the Justice League at its height look small. Each era adds a few new heroes, often as commentary on DC lore at the time or on modern-day themes.

Sources indicate that the last major addition to the team was the mysterious Gold Lantern, who became an object of fascination for fans and built on the idea of the multi-colored Lantern Corps. Now, the Robin of the 3st Century is set to join the team, as Bat-Lad heads towards his debut.

Legion of Super-Heroes #4 is coming December 9 from DC Comics.
