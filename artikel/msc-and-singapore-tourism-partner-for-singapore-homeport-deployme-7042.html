📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

MSC Cruises and the Singapore Tourism Board have announced a three-year partnership that will see MSC homeport in Singapore, starting with the MSC Orchestra from Q4 2028.

Gianni Onorato, CEO of MSC Cruises, said: “Asia is an crucial part of our long-term global growth, so we are proud to announce our three-year partnership with the Singapore Tourism Board for winter deployment from this leading cruise hub.”.

“Bringing MSC Orchestra to the region alongside MSC Bellissima strengthens our presence and reflects our commitment to becoming a leading choice for both local and international guests wanting to discover Asia by sea,” added Onorato.

Jean Ng, assistant chief executive, Experience Development Group, Singapore Tourism Board, added: “As one of the world’s leading cruise brands, MSC Cruises’ decision to establish its first homeport deployment in Southeast Asia with a three-year commitment in Singapore speaks to the strength of our cruise ecosystem and Singapore’s robust cruise passenger demand.”.

“Its longer sailing itineraries will offer international visitors and Singaporeans more options to discover destinations in the region. This deployment also creates opportunities for neighboring destinations to welcome more cruise visitors and benefit from increased tourism spending to local businesses,” added Ng.

According to the press release, this marks MSC’s first-ever homeport deployment in the destination.

The ship will offer eight- to 12-night sailings to destinations including Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, Malaysia and Indonesia.

Industry observers note that the MSC Orchestra will join the MSC Bellissima as MSC Cruises’ second ship in Asia from winter 2028-29. The new deployment will further strengthen Singapore’s role as a regional gateway, responding to growing demand for cruise holidays.

Before beginning her deployment in Asia, the MSC Orchestra will undergo a significant refurbishment introducing the award-winning MSC Yacht Club.

Explora Journeys, part of MSC Group’s Cruise Division, will also operate its first cruises from Singapore in 2027.

It has been revealed that following her winter season sailing from Singapore, the MSC Orchestra will relocate to Japan, the Republic of Korea and Greater China. The MSC Orchestra’s latest itineraries from Singapore will be available to book from December 14, 2026.
