The XRP Ledger (XRPL) has scored a major institutional milestone that could bolster its appeal to traditional financial institutions as demand for blockchain-based financial infrastructure grows.

The XRP Ledger Foundation has confirmed that the network is the first public, permissionless Layer-1 blockchain to have its Architecture and Resiliency principles independently verified under the Global Layer One (GL1) Market Infrastructure Toolkit.

According to recent reports, the XRP Ledger’s latest achievement could mark an important step toward strengthening its position in the institutional blockchain market. On November 13, the independent attestation report detailing XRPL’s assessment under the Global Layer One (GL1) Market Infrastructure Toolkit was released, highlighting the network’s willingness to measure its infrastructure against standards designed for traditional financial markets and emerging digital asset systems.

Sources indicate that the toolkit provides a common framework for evaluating blockchain networks across 10 key principles, including governance, legal foundations, settlement finality, architecture, operational resilience, cybersecurity, interoperability, and privacy. These criteria are particularly relevant to banks, asset managers, and other regulated institutions exploring blockchain technology to support tokenized deposits, stablecoins, investment funds, and other financial instruments.

Notably, the XRP Ledger Foundation conducted a self-assessment covering all 10 principles, while blockchain security firm CertiK independently reviewed the supporting evidence for the Architecture and Resiliency principles. Its verification involved reviewing technical evidence—including code, system configurations, logs, and test results—to determine whether selected controls were implemented as described.

The development could give financial institutions a more structured reference point when evaluating XRPL for institutional-grade financial applications. However, the assessment does not amount to full GL1 certification or regulatory approval, meaning prospective institutional people will still need to conduct their own due diligence.

In a recent development, the Global Layer One initiative brings together leading financial institutions and policymakers to establish common standards for digital financial infrastructure. It aims to streamline cross-border transactions involving tokenized assets while ensuring that blockchain networks meet internationally recognized requirements for financial market infrastructure.

It has been revealed that the latest development may strengthen the XRP Ledger’s prospects among traditional financial institutions considering blockchain-based financial solutions. Potential use cases include tokenized deposits, stablecoins, investment funds, and capital-market instruments. The GL1 assessment framework incorporates globally recognized financial market guidelines, drawing on CPMI-IOSCO principles and established digital asset control standards.

According to recent reports, the review also provides institutions with a clearer framework for examining XRPL’s technical infrastructure, operational resilience, and suitability for financial applications. To maintain its relevance, the XRP Ledger Foundation intends to revisit the assessment periodically, taking into account future network improvements and changes in global industry requirements.
