Published: 16:27 EST, 21 November 2026 | Updated: 04:07 EST, 23 November 2026.

Arsenal will be keeping everything crossed ahead of goalkeeper David Raya undergoing tests on what appeared to be a shoulder or arm injury sustained against Newcastle.

The Gunners' No1 went off in the second half in the 2-2 draw at St James' Park, supporting his arm or shoulder before he was replaced by back-up Kepa Arrizabalaga.

It leaves everyone associated with Arsenal nervous, as they face potentially a sustained period of time without a key part of their impressive defence.

Raya appeared to pick up the injury by running into Newcastle's Will Osula after claiming the ball from a cross.

As he attempted to run forward to start an attack, he collided with the striker and hit the ground in pain.

After a brief period of treatment on the field, he was substituted, supporting his arm, and replaced in the game.

"I'm petrified!" Ian Wright and Jamie Redknapp on why David Raya coming off injured vs Newcastle is a massive worry for Arsenal 🧤 pic.twitter.com/F5uAgjFdp0.

Arsenal face a nervy wait to find out the extent of David Raya's injury picked up against Newcastle on Saturday.

Manager Mikel Arteta has been left sweating at the idea of being without his No1 for a busy Christmas period.

'Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is not good news,' Arteta noted: 'He will have an MRI assessment tomorrow, but at the moment I don't know.

Sources indicate that 'It was a knock, I don't know how it happened and what the injury is at the moment. We have to wait and see tomorrow.

'We know how important David is for us. Tomorrow we will have more news.'.

The Gunners will play nine more games before the end of the year as they look to push forward in both the league and Champions League.

That challenge has just got harder, though, and Arsenal fan and former forward Ian Wright has expressed his concern.

'That's a massive worry,' he said on Sky Sports. 'I don't want to say I'm petrified simply because Kepa is in goal, he's the guy who has got to try and keep the goals out for us now.

'But yeah, that is a massive blow. Someone like him, Declan Rice, Gabriel, it is very tough to replace those guys.'.

Arsenal are next in action against Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on Tuesday, before a crunch clash with Manchester City in the Premier League next Sunday.
