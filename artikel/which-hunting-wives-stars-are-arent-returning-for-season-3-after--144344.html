The Hunting Wives has been renewed for season 3 — but fans might not see every cast member returning to the show.

In a recent development, based on May Cobb‘s book of the same name, The Hunting Wives introduced Sophie (Brittany Snow), who found herself drawn to prominent socialite Margo (Malin Åkerman) after moving from Boston to an East Texas town. While some became invested in the murder at the center of the series, the show’s first season also provided plenty of entertainment with guns, drinking and steamy onscreen hookups.

Recent findings suggest that despite the obstacles facing Sophie and Margo, Åkerman hasn’t given up hope on the pair’s future. “They find a way,” she told Us in November 2026 ahead of the season 2 premiere. “They come together and they break apart. It’s the epic, toxic love story that we all adore, so that’s really up to [creator] Rebecca [Cutter] to decide how they eventually, by season 13, run off together and live happily ever after.”.

Åkerman jokingly agreed that things would be easier for them with some men out of the way.

Recent findings suggest that “I agree [that we need to get rid of the men]. We need to because we need to get them out of the way so Sophie and Margo can be together forever,” she said. “And [Margo’s husband] Jed is standing in the way right now. But it’d be tricky.”.

It has been revealed that cutter also spoke to Us about the odds of a happy ending for the couple, saying, “Sophie obviously is the eyes through which we see the show. When she came in, she wasn’t ready for a Margo Banks. She wanted it, but she was in a weakened place in her life.”.

In a recent development, she continued: “Over the course of two seasons, she becomes formidable. She becomes fierce. She becomes worthy and equal [to] Margo. So I think their relationship does get real in season 2, and we have moments, though, where we can really imagine a Margo and Sophie happy ending for them. But of course, they’re both survivors, and that trumps everything.”.
