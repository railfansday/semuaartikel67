Industry observers note that not sure if you’ve heard, but the biggest video game of all time is out today: buying Anthropic stock. Yes, in case you haven’t already blown all of your money on the GTA VI Ultimate Edition, you can try your luck at buying one of the most anticipated IPOs of all time when ANT goes public on the Nasdaq later today.

In a recent development, but you’re likely up, playing the game that is, according to Metacritic, already among the best of all time. It’s presently sitting at a near-perfect 99/100 on the aggregator, with reviewers calling it “a masterpiece” and “gaming’s… greatest love story.” It’s a game in which you can do basically anything you want, from the violent to the vacuous.

More than anything, though, I’m just excited to discover all the different ways the game allows you to find and consume coffee.

As I’m sure happened in every newsroom, the conversation this week turned to whether traffic will meaningfully decline today as up to 1.5 million people “call in sick” to play GTA VI. If you’ve been awake since the crack of dawn trying to meander your way to Vice City, you’re just one of many folks experiencing the rarest of moments in 2026: a singular, unifying cultural event.

My biggest question isn’t whether gamers will play GTA VI but whether normies will. Previous games in the series, particularly GTA V, sold hundreds of millions of copies and still draw countless players today. GTA VI feels even more inviting to those who just want to spend time in its sandbox to do some fishing, dancing, or flying. One reviewer even called it a “cozy game” where he just spent time walking around a zoo.

While the size, scale, and impact of GTA VI are exponentially higher, the last time I heard people talking about a game in this way was during the pandemic, when everyone was hunkering down for a winter of Animal Crossing.

Industry observers note that by the way, GTA VI has a functioning smartphone that can be used for making calls, sending texts, taking photos, looking at “Instagram,” and setting reminders, among other things. It’s yet another minor detail in a game full of them. Plus, it looks like an original Pixel.

According to recent reports, usually with sponsored content, I have to spend the whole time convincing you that the product is worth buying. But RedPocket’s Black Friday deals do all that work on their own.

Not only do you get amazing discounts for the first 12 months of service by prepaying for a full year, but RedPocket’s “Lock-in Low” promise means that you get to stay as close to that promotional price as possible once the first year is over.

There’s no contract and no port-in requirement. You can keep your number, keep your phone, and activate with an eSIM on compatible devices. During the Black Friday sale, there’s even a Refer-a-Friend credit that gets you $25 for every person you bring to the service.

According to recent reports, explore RedPocket’s plans or compare the annual offers. This is one of the best Black Friday decisions you’re going to make this month, and maybe ever.
