Nikocado Avocado is struggling.

Two months after the YouTuber underwent a second vertical torsoplasty following his 250-pound weight loss journey in 2024, he shared insight into the medical complications he's faced in the surgery's aftermath.

"I'm suffering," the mukbanger (real name Nicholas Perry) wrote over a Nov. 19 YouTube Short of a nurse clearing his surgical drains. "I had to get emergency surgery, pulled out veins, pulled out scar tissue. I'm full of regret.".

Since undergoing his surgery in September—in which excess skin and fat were removed from the sides of his torso—he admitted that he hasn't been able to properly rest.

"I haven't slept in weeks, actual f--king weeks," the 34-year-old remarked in a tearful confession in a Nov. 5 video. "I have never been in so much pain in my entire f--king life. This is not OK to be constantly in this condition. I'm in so much pain.".

Perry—who rose to infamy for his extreme eating videos and over-the-top meltdowns—revealed earlier this month that the surgery had "gone wrong" and left him in nonstop pain.

Recent findings suggest that "I'm living a f--king nightmare," he wrote in a lengthy Nov. 1 Facebook post. "I'm going crazy, NOT because of the torsoplasty but BECAUSE OF THE COMPLICATIONS and the SHOOTING pain in my fingers like they're being lit in a Gasoline fire or being sliced off with swords!!!!!!".

"Each night I have dreams of my fingers being sawed off, I'm trapped in a fire, crushed by a car, stepped on, drilled into, etc.," he continued. "I can't even escape this pain in my dreams. I've never been so upset in my entire life, truly.".

Recent findings suggest that perry mentioned doctors initially suspected a nerve compression in his armpit had caused the numbness and pain. He subsequently underwent an additional surgery two weeks ago to remove any sutures, scar tissue and pockets of fluid that could be touching the nerve.

However, the pain has yet to subside, with him adding that he's experiencing weight gain again—which he fears could split the incision—as he struggles with everyday tasks.

"I'm in the same amount of pain today," he wrote. "I can't ride my bike, I'm too unstable to drive, I can't do normal things like unload the dishwasher, fold clothes or type on my phone without these horrible F--KING STABBING in my fingers.".

The influencer also reflected on the toll the complications have had on his mental health.

"I feel so f--king alone, and I've never had to sit in this much pain in my entire life," he added. “Imagine having a really horrible toothache that radiates up to your SKULL…. It's so intense that your brain doesn't allow you to think about anything else other than that.".

Sources indicate that "OK, now multiply that x10 teeth," he continued. "THAT is the level of sheer CRUSHING levels of pain I'm having to go through. I really was hoping this would pass in a week or so, and I wouldn't even need to speak about it, but I'm desperate, crawling in desperation.".
