📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that semaphore is a remote-first software firm building continuous integration and delivery (CI/CD) products for engineering teams. Our customers rely on Semaphore Cloud and our self-hosted enterprise products to build, test, and deliver software securely and reliably.

Recent findings suggest that we have spent years building developer infrastructure at scale. We are now using that foundation to create new products and capabilities for a world in which software is increasingly built by people working with AI coding agents.

We are looking for a senior product engineer who can take an important idea from an early, ambiguous problem to a useful product running in production.

You will work across product and engineering boundaries: understanding the user, shaping the solution, making technical decisions, building the system, and improving it based on real use. Some work will extend mature Semaphore products. Some will start as a small experiment or a latest product. In either case, you will be responsible for getting to a strong outcome, not completing a predefined list of tickets.

In a recent development, AI coding agents are part of our daily engineering workflow. We expect you to use them fluently to explore, build, test, and ship faster. You remain accountable for the work: choosing the right problem, setting direction, making trade-offs, reviewing what is produced, and ensuring that the result is coherent, reliable, and maintainable.

This role is for someone with strong ownership, initiative, taste, and judgment. You should be comfortable moving between product questions, system design, code, infrastructure, and user experience as the work requires.

We care more about the quality and ownership of what you have built than a specific number of years, previous title, or exact match with our stack.

Within your first three months:.

This is a senior individual-contributor role with broad ownership. You will work closely with engineering, product, infrastructure, and manufacturer leadership. Teams are small, communication is direct, and the person closest to the work is expected to exercise judgment.

You will have room to shape both the product and its implementation. In return, we expect you to take responsibility for the whole outcome: what gets built, why it matters, how well it works, and what happens after it ships.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.
