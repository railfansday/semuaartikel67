The ownership of Premier League clubs has never been so crucial. Years ago, football fans probably couldn't care less who owned their club. But now, you must have an owner willing to dip into their deep pockets to fund transfers.

The likes of Newcastle and Manchester City are now essentially being owned by states, while there's an increasing number of billionaires in charge of clubs. If you don't have a rich owner, it's going to be very difficult to be competitive in the greatest league in the world. But which teams presently competing in the Premier League have the richest owners?

Given the fact that football has dramatically changed, and that those who have the most money tend to have the most power, these are how every single Premier League club ranks based on the wealth of their owners.

According to recent reports, brentford fan Mark Benham established his wealth through gambling firms such as Matchbook and Smartodds. He took control of the Bees in 2012 and also owns Danish side FC Midtjylland. He's become a bit of a hero among the fanbase with a statistical model helping Brentford establish themselves as a regular Premier League club, as well as helping build a brand-newly introduced stadium.

Sources indicate that brentford, along with Brighton, are two of the best-run clubs in Europe, never mind just in England. Bringing in young players that can be moulded and grown into stars in the making, and then selling them on to bigger clubs for a huge profit is the way the Bees have operated successfully to become a staple in the Premier League.

In a recent development, in 2022, Acun Ilicali company ACUNMEDYA purchased Hull City. Those in England weren't too familiar with the man, but he's a major deal in his native Turkey. A broadcaster, entrepreneur, international TV producer, businessman and more, the 57-year-old has no shortage of ambition, and this is reflected in his sporting projects.

It has been revealed that he's also had connections to Dutch football team Fortuna Sittard, Irish club Shelbourne, and was briefly on the board of directors at Fenerbahce. His sole focus, however, is now with Hull City, and that bodes well for the newly-promoted club.

Beyond Doug King at Coventry City, there haven't been many better owners in the EFL in recent times. The British businessman became the club’s sole owner in 2023 and has since restructured the club’s financial and operational framework in impressive fashion.

On the pitch, it was a bold call for King to get rid of the long-serving Mark Robins, who was sacked after a fine seven and a half years in charge, and eyebrows were raised when Frank Lampard replaced him. But it turned out to be a stroke of genius, as Coventry clinched promotion to the Premier League after 25 years away. Their net worth could be on the rise in coming years if the Sky Blues manager to cement their position back in the major time.

It has been revealed that tony Bloom made his billions through gambling, specifically poker. He bought his boyhood club, Brighton, in 2009 and has overseen their incredible rise with a clear philosophy and incredible success in the transfer market thanks to a data-driven approach.

Sources indicate that the Seagulls operate by using clever scouting methods to unearth gems from all across the globe to develop before selling them to the highest bidder. The best example of this came when Moises Caicedo was brought in for a nominal fee and became one of the best-performing midfielders in English football. This earned the Ecuadorian defensive midfielder a move to Chelsea for £115 million, making him the most expensive signing in Premier League history.

US billionaire Bill Foley assumed control at the Vitality Stadium in 2023. He's the chairman of Fidelity National Financial, Cannae Holdings and Black Knight Financial Services. He also owns NHL side, Vegas Golden Knights and a stake in French Ligue 1 club FC Lorient.

There was an expectation that the beginning of Foley's time in charge of the Cherries would be in the Championship, but the miracles worked by Gary O'Neil and co in the 2022/23 season ensured the club's top-flight status. There was an influx of signings as a result and the appointment of Andoni Iraola has worked wonders as Bournemouth have pushed towards European qualification - though they must now start a new era with Marco Silva in charge this term.

After several really disappointing years, which saw Sunderland suffer two relegations and languish in League One for a period, the Black Cats have returned to the promised land. In their first season back, they qualified for the Europa League against all odds.

In a recent development, majority owner Kyril Louis-Dreyfus is the youngest chairman in football, at just 28 years old, but he also has one of the lowest net worths in the Premier League. Whether they can keep punching above their weight remains to be seen, but it's been an incredible journey up until this point.
