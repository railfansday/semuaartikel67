Reports in Italy confirm that Mike Maignan took part in the first part of Milan’s training session on Thursday morning, and the latest patch indicates that he should be available to start against Frosinone in Serie A this weekend.

The Rossoneri captain played the full 90 minutes in his home stadium as Italy picked up a 3-1 Nations League victory over France last weekend, but was then forced off at half-time with an injury during his nation’s 2-1 win over Turkey on Sunday evening.

Maignan has checked in for medical tests in both France and Italy over the last few days, and fortunately for Milan, there were no concerns over a serious injury.

It has been revealed that according to Thursday’s updates from Gianluca Di Marzio and various others, Maignan was able to take part in the first portion of Milan’s training session on Thursday. This means that he is likely to be available to start against Frosinone in Serie A on Sunday evening, given that he still has another few days of training before the return of league action this weekend.

Maignan has conceded 16 goals in 14 appearances for Milan across all competitions this season, keeping four clean sheets in the process.

Milan sit fourth in the Serie A table with 24 points from 11 games and four wins in their last five league matches.
