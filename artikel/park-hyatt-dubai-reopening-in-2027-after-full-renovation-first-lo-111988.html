Dubai is a city that has endless hotels at all price points. I don’t have a single favorite hotel in the city, as it really depends on so many factors, from what you’re willing to pay, to the area you want to be in, to how massive you want the property to be, to what you value most.

After all, the concept of hotels in Dubai is a bit different than in other cities, given that alcohol is primarily only served at restaurants in hotels. As a result, hotels are very much the social scenes, both for guests and non-guests.

One thing is for sure — there’s no denying that the rooms were in desperate need of a renovation, given that they hadn’t undergone any meaningful updates since the hotel first opened.
