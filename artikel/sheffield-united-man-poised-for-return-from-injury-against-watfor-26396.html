Sheffield United have been without the attacker over recent times due to a shoulder injury but he is set to return on Saturday which will be a massive boost. SUFC are back in action following the international break with a home clash against Watford at Bramall Lane as they look to return to winning ways.

According to recent reports, bamford, 33, has made 13 appearances for the Blades in all competitions this season, 11 of which have come in the Championship, and he has chipped in with six goals and one assist. He is out of contract in June 2027 but the South Yorkshire outfit hold an option to extend his stay by a further 12 months until 2028.

In this latest patch regarding his condition, The Star report he is poised to be fit to face Watford which is good news for Chris Wilder’s side. They lost 2-1 against Charlton Athletic at The Valley last time out and will be aiming to return to winning ways.

It has been revealed that sheffield United find themselves positioned in 10th place in the table after 15 fixtures. They are two points off the play-offs behind Southampton and QPR.

Sheffield United will be relieved to have Bamford fit again. He is a real threat for SUFC at the top end of the pitch.

Sources indicate that the forward is vastly experienced and knows where the back of the net is. He battles it out with the likes of Tom Cannon, Tyrese Campbell and Jordan Ayew for minutes in Wilder’s starting XI.

Bamford linked up with the Blades on a short-term basis back in November last year and he hit the ground running immediately. He scored 13 times last term before penning a contract extension.

According to recent reports, prior to his switch to Sheffield United, the Grantham-born man spent seven years at Leeds United. He has also had spells with the likes of Chelsea, Derby County, Norwich City and Middlesbrough in the past, among others.

Sheffield United are a better team with Bamford in it. He gives SUFC a real focal point in attack.

The Blades need to find a bit more consistency if they are to establish themselves as genuine promotion contenders this term. Their upcoming match-up against Watford is an ideal chance to pick up a win against a side near the bottom of the league.
