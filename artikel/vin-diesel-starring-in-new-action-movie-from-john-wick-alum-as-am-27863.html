Vib Diesel is about to move beyond his major franchises with a new original action movie.

Per Deadline, Diesel is attached to star in Burial Hill, an action flick penned by Mike McGrale, the writer of the upcoming John Wick spinoff Caine. Jamie Payne, who helmed the two Luther movies for Netflix, will direct, with additional casting now underway.

Burial Hill's script is said to follow "a corrupt State Police unit that pursues a young witness to their crimes into the cemetery which Diesel’s character calls home. He is then forced back into the world he abandoned to save her and bring down the cops determined to bury the truth." Diesel plays a reclusive groundskeeper with a mysterious past in the film, and the intention is to make the character the focus of a new franchise.

Amazon MGM Studios is presently finalizing a deal for most of the international rights for the movie, which is stated to have been a hot property over the past few months. The deal is reportedly in the $35-$40 million range. Talks regarding the film's North American rights are said to be ongoing.
