On the side of Real Madrid, Kylian Mbappé has already set a upcoming goal after finishing second in the Ballon d’Or. According to A.S.the Frenchman now wants to go get his “another Ballon d’Or“: the massive collective titles with Real. With 100 goals in 116 games and 14 goals in 13 games this season, his individual statistics are already there. He now wants to fill the trophy cabinet. And José Mourinho must decide to the right of his defense. With the injury of Fede Valverde, Dumfries and Trent Alexander-Arnold finally have a real opportunity to recover their place. The two specialists have lost a lot of playing time in recent weeks and will must take advantage of this opportunity.

Gianluigi Donnarumma is going through a complicated period. Enzo Maresca, however, continues to show total confidence in him and even spoke of a “Gigio plus ten“, as if to explain that his goalkeeper remained one of the first names on the scoresheet. But the performances do not really follow. Donnarumma is singled out for several recent goals and has now conceded 13 goals in his last seven matches in all competitions. And the context does not help since City has just lost 1-0 against Brighton, their second consecutive defeat in the Premier League after starting the season with seven victories.

In a recent development, “OM, a week in the crash test“. After several complicated weeks, Marseille has finally found a positive dynamic. But Bruno Genesio’s men will now follow Toulouse, Leverkusen in the Europa League then Lens. Three matches which should allow us to know what this OM is really worth. Genesio himself speaks of an opportunity to “to calibrate” facing stronger opponents. And before this series, a question remains open in defense: who will accompany Nayef Aguerd? For the moment, Conrad Egan-Riley maintains a small lead over Bamo Meïté thanks in particular to his quality in the recovery. But the Ivorian is coming back well and his latest performances have completely revived the competition. This improvement above all confirms that Genesio has never abandoned his ideas despite the poor results.
