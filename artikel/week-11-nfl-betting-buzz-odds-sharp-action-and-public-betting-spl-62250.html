Joey Feazel, head NFL trader at Caesars Sportsbook: "Interesting one with the Baker Mayfield news. We reopened Lions -5.5 on Sunday afternoon, it's trickled out to -7. Bad news on the Aidan Hutchinson injury as well. Opened the total at 46.5, down to 44. Really just leaning on that Mayfield news and that Bucs have a solid defense. Right now, more Bucs action, but I anticipate the public being on the Lions, just depends what number they'll lay depending on Mayfield.".

Thomas Gable, sportsbook director at The Borgata in Atlantic City: "This one has moved despite the injury to Hutchinson. This has moved from -5.5, up to -7. Obviously some uncertainty around Mayfield's health. Total has dropped from 46.5 to 44. Some decent early Lions money.".

Feazel: "We've yet to see the Jaguars really stand out as a public team like the Lions or Bengals in attracting that public money. Jacksonville is our best case among AFC teams in the Super Bowl. A productive offense and can compete with the best of them. Two-way action thus far, a little bit more money on the Jaguars. I think we'll need the Giants, but not as much as some of the other top teams in our power ratings. Opened Jaguars -6, been bouncing back between -5.5 and -6. Opened 46.5 on the total and stayed at 46.5. Not the most action on this game.".

Gable: "Jaguars opened -6.5, down to -6. We're probably going to get NY Giants money being situated here in Atlantic City as we have the past few weeks, and that's what it's been in the early going. Total opened 46.5, we're still sitting there. Probably going to end up needing the Jaguars to cover this number.".

In a recent development, feazel: "This is looking like another Kyle McCord experience. Not a lot of action, people seeing Bills -16.5 and not wanting to go there. Little bit of money on the under and a little bit more spread money on the Dolphins. As the public gets involved, probably not going to get a ton of Miami love. Opened at Bills -16.5, it's still there even juiced a little on Buffalo. Opened total 47.5, now down to 45.5. Weather could be a factor.".

It has been revealed that gable: "Biggest spread on the board right now. We opened at Bills -17, down to -16.5. Total has dropped two points, from 47 down to 45. Buffalo is in a ton of money-line parlays as you'd expect — the Bills and the Chiefs are two legs of many parlays. It's actually pretty balanced action on the spread.".
