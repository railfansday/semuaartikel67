YouTuber Nikocado Avocado has revealed he’s suffering from constant pain after a plastic surgery complication allegedly caused nerve damage in his left hand, leaving him struggling to sleep and perform everyday tasks.

The content creator, whose real name is Nicholas Perry, famously lost 250 pounds in secret before revealing his transformation in September 2024.

Since then, he’s undergone multiple skin removal surgeries to address excess skin left behind by his dramatic weight loss.

However, Perry now claims his latest procedure has left him in excruciating pain, with doctors struggling to determine how to resolve the glitch.

In a lengthy Facebook post, Perry explained that he underwent his second vertical torsoplasty in September 2026 to remove another two pounds of excess skin.

According to the YouTuber, complications involving his armpit caused suspected nerve compression, leaving his thumb and index finger numb and experiencing severe shooting pain.

“I’m living a f**king nightmare,” he wrote. “The amount of pain I’m in 24/7 is unbearable.”.

Perry said he endured weeks of pain before undergoing an emergency decompression surgery, during which his surgeon removed sutures, scar tissue, fluid, and a small vein potentially compressing the nerve.

Unfortunately, the procedure failed to provide significant pain relief.

“I’m in the same amount of pain today, TWO WEEKS after the revision surgery as I was in for the entire month after the original surgery,” he explained.

Perry claimed the pain has prevented him from sleeping for more than one or two hours at a time, while even touching everyday objects causes intense discomfort.

He also revealed that medications have provided limited relief and expressed fears that his recovery could take several months, assuming the suspected nerve damage can heal.

“I’ve never cried so much in my life,” he admitted, explaining that he’s now seeking emotional support from others who have experienced similar complications.

On November 13, Perry also shared photos on Facebook showing him in a wheelchair and receiving medical care.

However, he remains uncertain about when the pain will improve, writing: “I regret this so much.”.
