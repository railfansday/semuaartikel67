Marella Cruises is expanding its regional flying program for the winter season, enabling guests to spend Christmas holidays sailing the Caribbean while flying from Newcastle.

Thanks to a direct TUI Airways flight to La Romana, Dominican Republic, departing Newcastle Airport on December 15, 2026, guests can sail a 14-night Caribbean fly-cruise that combines the “Colors of the Caribbean” and “A Caribbean Christmas” itineraries aboard the Marella Discovery.

They can also prolong their stay by combining the “Colors of the Caribbean” itinerary with a one week stay post-cruise at a choice of TUI hotels in La Romana.

“We’re delighted to be expanding our regional flying program from Newcastle Airport for Winter 2026, giving customers across the North East even more choice when it comes to starting their Caribbean holiday closer to home,” said Chris Hackney, CEO at Marella Cruises.

“Regional flying is incredibly important to us at Marella Cruises and the additional La Romana flying means customers can enjoy the convenience of departing from their local airport before experiencing everything our all-inclusive cruises and cruise and stay program has to offer.”.

The itinerary sailing roundtrip from La Romana calls at Roseau, Dominica; St John’s, Antigua; Basseterre, St Kitts; Philipsburg, St Maarten and Road Town, British Virgin Islands during the first week.

The second week visits Isla Catalina, an island off the Dominican Republic; Kralendijk, Bonaire; Willemstad, Curacao; Oranjestad, Aruba before returning to La Romana.

Prices start from £2,868 per person, based on two adults sharing a balcony cabin.

Leon McQuaid, director of aviation development at Newcastle Airport, noted: “Spending Christmas in the Caribbean is on many people’s bucket list, so it’s fantastic that passengers can make that dream a reality with direct flights from Newcastle Airport.

“Marella Cruises’ expanded program makes it easier than ever to swap the cold winter weather for Caribbean sunshine and stunning beaches, while exploring the region’s most spectacular islands.”.
