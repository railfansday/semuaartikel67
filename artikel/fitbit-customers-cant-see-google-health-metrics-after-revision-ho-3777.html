Fitbit customers can’t see Google Health metrics after revision – how to fix it

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that fitbit customers are having trouble seeing several key metrics on the Google Health app. The glitch, Reddit users are speculating, occurs after users revision to the latest Fitbit update 5.12.

It has been revealed that one user reported that Fitbit Air stopped tracking metrics n the Vitals section of the app, including breathing rate, blood oxygen, resting heart rate, HRV, and skin temperature. Many users on the Reddit thread stated they’ve experienced the same problem over the past few days.

Also: Google’s newly introduced Fitbit Edge solves my biggest gripe with the Pixel Watch.

People are complaining that it’s taken Google so long to fix this bug, which appeared in the app for some users about six days ago.

“This is getting extremely frustrating and disappointing that Google, a multi-trillion-dollar tech giant with AI models built to fix these issues and high-tier engineers, can’t even fix a little problem like that,” one user wrote in a thread.

Google acknowledged in a customer support reply that it was aware of the glitch and looking to fix it.

On the Reddit thread, users have suggested logging out and back in, or uninstalling and reinstalling the app, as a solution to the disappearing data concern.

Also: After one week wearing the Pixel Watch 5, here’s what I want most.

Another user suggested clicking through each stat that doesn’t populate and then selecting the monthly view. Doing this, the user reported, should repopulate the main screen with the latest information, without requiring one to log out and log back in. Consumers in the thread noted that this fix helped solve the concern.

Google stated it is rolling out a fix for the problem. “A small number of iOS users were missing metrics like vitals and daily totals for steps, distance and energy burned. Updating to app version 5.12.2 will fix the issue. No user data was lost,” Google said in an email to ZDNET.
