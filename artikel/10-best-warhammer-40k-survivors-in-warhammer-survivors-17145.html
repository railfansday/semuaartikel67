10 Best Warhammer 40k Survivors in Warhammer Survivors

While a lot of survivor-like games offer you plenty of characters to play, Warhammer Survivors, Warhammer's take on Vampire Survivors, takes it to a latest level. With more than 40 characters to play as between both universes, you’re pretty spoiled for choice.

Just because there are a ton of characters to play, though, doesn’t mean they’re all particularly good. Some are a bit tougher to get the hang of than others, and some just have terrible stats.

Industry observers note that this list will give you the ten best survivors to play as in the Warhammer 40k universe, since heroes are not transferable between universes. Each one has their ups and downs, but for the most part, you’ll find one you'll be well suited to.

Of all the characters available in this wild roguelike game, Sister Superior Amalia Novena is undoubtedly the easiest to use. She comes with a weapon that keeps enemies at bay and will mostly wipe out low-level enemies in the early stages.

While she does have an upper limit, her increase in range for the first 30 levels can be a major game changer. She’s a great starter character to get a better feel for levels and for quickly completing objectives, without tricky mechanics to learn.

Recent findings suggest that the thing about the lovely Canoness Aurora Veritas is that she’ll basically burn through waves of enemies like they’re nothing. Better weapons and status effects really help, especially when coming across the bosses with loads of health.

The only real downside is she also has an upper limit and doesn’t scale indefinitely. Her boosted range is helpful, but towards the end of runs, she just doesn’t have enough juice to really do much.

Recent findings suggest that the Chaplain is a pretty average character when things get started, and their weapon isn’t all that reliable, but they can scale well into later game levels. Once you’ve unlocked plenty of weapons, you’ll pretty much always be getting a few followers with every level up.

Sources indicate that the extra Range he gets, combined with the automatically boosted Veterancy, makes him a great candidate for almost any run. If you aren’t very far in your journey, though, he’s likely to be less than helpful.

Max is the definition of a run-and-gun FPS player, as he brings a Combat Shotgun and some serious ability strength with him. Gaining Strength with every level could see him scale incredibly rapidly in the right build, making him stronger than most.

Industry observers note that the fact that his ability has no upper limit means you can combine him with the Gauntlet passive to potentially double your damage. This will let him tear through hordes of enemies without any concern for his safety.

In terms of weapons, Sergeant Furan isn’t exactly rocking the best one there is, but he definitely makes up for it in other areas. If you stay healthy, you’ll be earning a lot more Skill, and if you find yourself in trouble, taking some damage can let you wipe out a horde.

It has been revealed that with no upper limit to his scaling, Furan can get pretty powerful, especially with the right build of weapons and passives. A bit difficult to use when starting, but it does give you more control over the buff you have at the moment.

According to recent reports, there’s nothing better than running your enemies over and hearing their bones crush beneath your treads, which is exactly what Knight-Commander Pask is all about. With bonuses to Strength, Save, and Toughness, he’s a machine of war.

Sources indicate that thanks to that limitless scaling, after just ten levels you’ll be gaining a lot of Wounds back every second to keep you in the fight. He’s also a giant tank, and who doesn’t love driving a massive tank through their enemies?

According to recent reports, saint Celestine might seem like an angelic being, but when it comes to killing, she’s a total demon and can get some absurd builds going. She’s got extra speed and just gets better with every level you manage to acquire.

She even starts with a bonus to Chance and two extra Redeployments, all things that will make a run much easier from the outset. You won’t have an easy time getting the hang of her, but when you do, she’s truly a beast. That movement speed is just valuable.

One of the hardest characters to use thanks to her starting weapon, Aveline still tops the charts when it comes to dealing with enemies. Her weapon is tough to use, but when combined with the bonuses she gets to Range and Move, she can become very powerful very swiftly.

Recent findings suggest that she also comes with built-in Corruption and a free Banish, which definitely helps her in terms of gaining levels and getting what she wants. With a smart build, she can effectively wipe out a massive line of enemies in one go.

In a recent development, daedalosus is a Technoarchaeologist, and he’s bringing the weirdness to the game in more ways than one. His permanent ability to always have four choices at level-up automatically makes him one of the best in the game, since that helps you to round out a build faster. He's amazingly versatile.

It has been revealed that while not exactly the most busted in terms of raw power, he can be a great way to get coins quickly, which is only further improved by his 30% bonus to Tithe. He’ll need a good setup to get going, but once he’s going, he’s hard to stop in this strange roguelike.

As the very last survivor to unlock in the 40k setting, High Abbess Morvenn Vahl is an absolute powerhouse by every standard. With boosts to virtually every single stat in the game, and the extra utility of gaining bonus stats for each Adepta Sororitas follower, they’re made for battle.

Recent findings suggest that in some of the best survivor-like games, the final survivor to unlock can feel underwhelming, but Morvenn lives up to their title all too well. Even though there's only a small number of Adepta Sororitas survivors in the game right now, they’re still almost unstoppable.
