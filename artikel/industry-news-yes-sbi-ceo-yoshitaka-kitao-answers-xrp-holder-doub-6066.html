Yoshitaka Kitao, head of Japanese financial group SBI Holdings, answered "YES" when asked point-blank whether he still trusts XRP.

Recent findings suggest that the question came from an X user. The user wrote that the past few weeks had been "truly exhausting" for XRP holders, and said that token holders feel that Ripple has left them behind.

Industry observers note that kitao answered in English. Most of his recent posts on X are in Japanese, and the rest of them are reposts of other accounts.

Kitao previously worked as an executive at Nomura Securities and later served as CFO of SoftBank. In 1999, he founded SoftBank Investment, which later became SBI Holdings. Today, SBI includes banks, brokerage, and insurance businesses, as well as crypto services.

In a recent development, the partnership began in 2016. SBI joined Ripple's Series B round and released a joint venture with Ripple, SBI Ripple Asia. SBI owns about 60% of the venture, and Ripple owns the remaining 40%.

The same year, SBI Ripple Asia set up a consortium of Japanese banks to test Ripple's technology for payments, and the consortium later grew to more than 60 banks.

Sources indicate that in 2018, SBI launched the interbank app MoneyTap. The same year, SBI's crypto exchange, now called SBI VC Trade, started operations with XRP trading. In 2019, Kitao joined Ripple's board of directors.

From 2020, SBI began handing the token out to shareholders as bonuses, and the payment service SBI Remit released remittances using it with on-demand liquidity (ODL), including in the major Japan-Philippines corridor.

SBI kept working with Ripple during the SEC lawsuit against the company, which lasted from December 2020 until 2025.

It has been revealed that back on February 15, 2026, in rare long-form tweets in English, Kitao denied rumors that SBI holds $10 billion worth of XRP: "Not $10 billion in XRP, but around 9% of Ripple Labs. So our hidden asset could be much bigger." By his account, the combined valuation of Ripple Labs and its ecosystem is "enormous," and SBI owns more than 9% of all of it.

Kitao also talked about XRP at SBI's quarterly briefing on November 2, 2026. He stated that investors buy Bitcoin mostly to hold it, and that the value of XRP depends on its use in payments and business ecosystems.

According to recent reports, in August 2025, SBI VC Trade and Ripple agreed to bring the RLUSD stablecoin to Japan. In October 2025, SBI also invested $200 million in Evernorth, a company that holds XRP in its treasury. By now, SBI VC Trade has more than two million accounts.

At press time, XRP is trading at $1.02.
