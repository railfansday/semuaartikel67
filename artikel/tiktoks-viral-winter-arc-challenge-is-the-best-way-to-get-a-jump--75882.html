TikTok’s Viral Winter Arc Challenge Is the Best Way to Get a Jump-Start on Your Fresh Year’s Goals

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, between holiday parties, happy hours, and meeting your job’s Q4 goals, it may be hard to motivate yourself to lock in on your goals for the remainder of the year. While, yes, you should cut yourself some slack here and there during the busy holiday season, it’s also more important now than ever to finish the year strong.

Industry observers note that that’s where the 90-day Winter Arc Challenge comes into play. This annual challenge, which originally went viral on TikTok back in 2024, starts October 1 and ends January 1 (but if you missed the start, it’s not too late!). The only premise: Stay locked in on your goals for the rest of the calendar year, whether that’s training for a half-marathon, hitting your Goodreads book quota, or mastering a fresh hobby.

This time of year tends to be stressful, but the Winter Arc Challenge “helps to keep the stress in perspective and also helps us to not get off track or out of balance with the bigger picture” in terms of your goals, indicates Chloe Carmichael, PhD, a clinical psychologist and Women's Health advisory board member based in New York City. Now’s the time for you to “be intentional about your energy and what serves you.”.

If you haven’t started the challenge yet, it’s not too late. Whether you try it for 83 or 30 days, the Winter Arc Challenge will set you up for greater success with your New Year’s goals because it’ll strengthen your self-efficacy, or “your sense of belief in your own ability to do what you say that you are going to do,” states Carmichael. Self-efficacy is strengthened through your own behavioral track record, so if you start meeting goals now, you’ll already have momentum heading into January, she explains.

Ready to tackle your own Winter Arc Challenge? Here are some helpful tips to help you make the most of the next few months:.

Maybe as part of your Winter Arc Challenge, you aim to cut your monthly spending in half. But a few weeks after setting this goal, you really go overboard at brunches and parties. Don't sweat it. This is “a completely normal part of the change process,” Carmichael notes. Instead of giving up when you encounter a relapse here or there, blueprint for bumps to happen, then get back on track.

So, if you spend too much money one week, instead of falling into a pattern of negative self-talk, replace your thoughts. Carmichael notes to plan a script of what you can say to yourself when you relapse, like: “I can still get back on track and feel good about my goals. This is my moment to restart.”.

Industry observers note that say you’re planning to work out four times a week until the end of the year. If you know the cold morning weather is going to persuade you to stay under the covers and sleep in, be proactive: Book workout classes online every week so you know they’re in your calendar, and you’re more likely to show up. Also, write down your challenges—like the dropping temps preventing you from leaving your house—to keep yourself accountable, Carmichael adds.

Recent findings suggest that picture yourself on January 1 having been successful with your Winter Arc Challenge, then move backward and think about everything that led up to it. If your massive goal was to do a yoga class every week, visualize yourself walking through the snow to get to your Monday night class, or doing a virtual class at home when there was a bad blizzard. Also think about when you almost skipped classes and fell off the wagon, but you used thought replacements to continue reinforcing your goals. Using imagery will help you mentally prepare for the journey ahead, Carmichael says.

Ask around to see if any of your friends are also doing the Winter Arc Challenge—or if they’re willing to hold you accountable. Then, initiative regular meetups or calls to check in. “Talk about how things went with a promise to be honest and supportive if one or both of you is falling off,” Carmichael indicates. “It will help you to stay consistent when you can leverage your social support network.”.

No matter when you started the Winter Arc Challenge, you’ll see and feel the benefits of bettering yourself in no time. Good luck—you got this!
