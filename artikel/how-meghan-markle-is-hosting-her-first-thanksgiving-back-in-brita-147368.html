📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Meghan Markle may have traded the sunny climes of Montecito for the British countryside, but her penchant for a thoughtful tablescape, a homemade preserve, and a well-appointed guest room remains. As the Duchess of Sussex prepares to celebrate her first Thanksgiving since relocating to the UK, she’s imparting some of her festive hosting wisdom.

In the forthcoming winter issue of Bon Appétit, Meghan shares her approach to holiday entertaining, alongside conversations spanning family, favorite restaurants, and the duchess’s Netflix series, With Love, Meghan.

Recent findings suggest that long before she had her own lifestyle brand (or, indeed, her own wine), Meghan was a college student looking to impress her dinner guests with a little help from Rachael Ray. An avid viewer of 30 Minute Meals, she recalls making a rather sophisticated grilled cheese sandwich in her first apartment, layering fontina, apple slices, and thyme, pressing it all together on her George Foreman grill, and finishing it with olive oil and Maldon sea salt. The wine pairing was Trader Joe’s famously inexpensive Two Buck Chuck. “Early 2000s at its finest,” she recalls.

Sources indicate that while Meghan’s taste in wine has evolved somewhat (last summer saw the launch of her own Napa Valley rosé), her approach to entertaining hasn’t changed all that much. “I still believe little touches are all you need to make something ordinary feel special, especially when you're having loved ones over,” she tells the magazine. “It doesn't have to be over the top.”.

So, what does a stay at the Sussexes’ entail? She likes to lay the table a day in advance, freeing herself up to actually enjoy the tech giant of her guests when they arrive. For those staying overnight, she programs the coffee machine to brew before they wake up, stocks the kitchen with breakfast provisions (think bagels, berries, yogurt, smoked salmon, and capers), and leaves a handwritten note inviting them to help themselves.

And when Meghan is the guest rather than the hostess? She favors something edible and homemade, like a batch of honey-roasted spiced nuts or a blackberry pound cake that can double as breakfast the following morning. Presentation is part of the pleasure too, and she advises using a little kitchen twine, a length of ribbon, or a sprig of rosemary to make things feel more considered. (Both recipes feature in the fresh matter.).

Those who tuned into last year’s festive instalment of With Love, Meghan will be familiar with the duchess’s enthusiasm for the holidays. The Netflix special saw her making personalized advent calendars for her children, decorating her Christmas tree with slices of fig, and preparing cacio e pepe gougères with restaurateur Will Guidara. Her companion lifestyle brand, As Ever, has leaned further into the art of gifting, too. Earlier this fall, Meghan marked the launch of its latest holiday collection with a sweet video of her five-year-old daughter, Princess Lilibet, helping her make raspberry jam.

Sources indicate that this year’s festivities will look a little different for the Sussexes. Having relocated to England in late August after six years in California, Meghan Markle and Prince Harry are celebrating Thanksgiving on British soil for the first time since 2018—and for the first time in the UK with both Archie and Lilibet. Recent weeks have afforded us some glimpses into their fresh life in England, from a family outing to a holiday fair to Meghan’s reunion with her longtime London-based hairstylist and friend George Northwood.

The duchess’s home may have changed, but her brand of Montecito mom domesticity remains intact.
