According to recent reports, it’s a toss-up what thru-hikers think about more: food, or their feet. After all, when you’re on the trail hour after hour, day after day, you can’t replenish calories fast enough. And at that pace, small flaws in shoe design can quickly magnify into a full-blown sufferfest. But with the right footwear—a shoe that provides the perfect combination of stability, durability, and comfort—thru-hikers can stop worrying about their feet and get on with daydreaming about their next cheeseburger.

Recent findings suggest that thru-hikers have already discovered Topo Athletic’s Traverse low-top hiking shoe, and appreciate its traction, toughness, and tailored fit on the longest trails. The latest iteration, the Traverse 2, builds on that foundation with new details geared specifically to multi-month objectives like the Appalachian Trail and Pacific Crest Trail.

Recent findings suggest that extended adventures demand durability, so the Traverse 2 features a new, high-abrasion mesh upper with strategically placed overlays that fend off trail debris without adding extra ounces (which is key when deciding what is and isn’t worth the weight on lengthy thru hikes). Protecting feet from dirt, rocks, and precipitation is also essential for logging long trail miles day after day, so Topo Athletic added its universal gaiter attachment system to the Traverse 2.

In a recent development, the Traverse 2 is built for anything the terrain throws at you. The full-length Vibram ® Megagrip outsole digs into technical, rocky, and wet trails, while a flexible forefoot rock plate beefs up underfoot protection. The three-piece midsole platform is designed for all-day hiking with a heavy pack, with a softer foam on the base of the heel and a firmer foam on the medial arch for maximum stability. Topo Athletic’s FKT insole features TPU beads not only for rebound and comfort, but also to help the shoes drain and dry in a flash. The shoe’s 5-millimeter drop hits the sweet spot to provide a more natural gait across long distances. And of course, the brand’s signature fit combines a roomy toe box with a snug fit around the midfoot and heel for a naturally comfortable feel.

In a recent development, all this comes in a streamlined package that keeps you light on your feet. The Traverse 2 weighs in at 9.2 ounces for a women’s 7 and 11.2 ounces for a men’s 9. It’s the kind of shoe that lets thru-hikers forget about their feet and focus on the adventure (or visions of that next cheeseburger).

Industry observers note that topo Athletic, based in Framingham, Massachusetts, empowers people to move better for life. Rooted in the belief that every step shapes the journey, Topo builds performance-driven footwear with a focus on lasting comfort and durability, all with its signature fit.
