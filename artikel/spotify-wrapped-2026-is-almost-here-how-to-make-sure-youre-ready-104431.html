It’s that time of year again, when people flood social media with year-end reviews and music lovers start counting down to what may be the most anticipated of them all: Spotify Wrapped. Spotify Wrapped 2026 reveals not just your top five songs (you know, those embarrassing tunes you’ve got on repeat), it also stacks your minutes and musical tastes up against the general public—for better or worse. (In past years, it also revealed listeners’ most-streamed songs, favorite artists, and podcasts.) Last year, some 500 million users shared their stories on social media in the first 24 hours, an overall increase of 41% year over year from 2025. When Does Spotify Wrapped drop? While the manufacturer hasn’t officially confirmed the release date for Spotify Wrapped 2026, the streaming platform’s past history reveals some good clues about when to expect it—and that date is coming up fast. Last year, Spotify Wrapped dropped soon after Thanksgiving, on December 3. In 2024, it came out one day later, on December 4. Before that, in 2023 and 2022, it came out November 29 and 30, respectively, near Thanksgiving weekend. However, since 2021, Spotify Wrapped has released on a Wednesday, often right after Thanksgiving, which this year falls on December 2. A look at online prediction market Kalshi shows traders giving Spotify Wrapped 2026 only a 21% chance of dropping before December 2, but forecasting an 88% chance before December 3, and a 90% chance before December 4. How can I see Spotify Wrapped once it releases? Spotify listeners can view their personalized recap in Spotify’s mobile app and share results on social media platforms such as Instagram and TikTok. To get the updated app, scan the QR code to download Spotify.
