In a recent development, BOSTON, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) today confirmed an expansion of its FedRAMP offerings for U.S. federal agencies and contractors. PTC's Windchill+Â® product lifecycle management (PLM) solution is now available on Microsoft Azure Government within PTC Cloud Services, and PTC is introducing its OnshapeÂ® Government Federal solution, a FedRAMP Moderate Equivalent environment for engineering teams working with Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). The two newly introduced offerings also support compliance with the Department of Defense's Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) program for protecting CUI.

Sources indicate that windchill+ is the only direct, full-stack SaaS PLM solution certified on the FedRAMP Marketplace. PTC Cloud Services is certified at the FedRAMP Class C (Moderate) baseline (NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5), so agencies can rely on an existing certification rather than running their own security review.

Sources indicate that onshape Government Federal is the only cloud-native CAD and PDM with a FedRAMP Moderate Equivalent attestation based on NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5. Built on AWS GovCloud (US), it expands its existing support for ITAR and EAR workloads. It also integrates with the ArenaÂ® PLM for AWS GovCloud solution, giving organizations a connected CAD, PDM, and PLM environment across engineering, quality, and supply chain workflows.

"Aerospace and defense organizations build and maintain some of the most complex, mission-critical products in the world," stated Jon Stevenson, Chief Product Officer, PTC. "For nearly a decade, we've invested in cloud solutions that meet federal security requirements, and we aim to continue that investment so these customers can count on PTC from product design through service.".

PTC Cloud Services first achieved FedRAMP authorization in 2017, and PTC has expanded its federal portfolio steadily ever since. In addition to Windchill+ and Onshape, federal agencies and contractors can choose from these PTC solutions to manage product data while meeting federal security requirements:.

It has been revealed that across its portfolio, PTC is delivering on its vision for the Intelligent Product Lifecycle: enabling manufacturers to build a modern product data foundation, extend that data across the enterprise, and unlock AI-powered transformation. Broader use of product data enables companies to bring higher-quality products to market faster, manage complexity at scale, and support new digital business models.
