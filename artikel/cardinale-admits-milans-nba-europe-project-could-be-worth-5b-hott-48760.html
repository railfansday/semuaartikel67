Gerry Cardinale spoke at a conference last night and it’s clear that the AC Milan owner has a lot to say on the NBA Europe matter.

The NBA Europe project has been discussed for some time now, but there has been little movement. As reports have suggested, Milan were one of the first brands/clubs to throw their hat into the ring and that interest has never died.

Now, it seems that some progress could be made with the plans to develop the San Donato land into an arena. However, it still feels like we are a way away from seeing an NBA team in Milano.

Speaking at the SBJ Dealmakers conference, Cardinale sat alongside the NBA Deputy Commissioner and discussed the ongoing discussions about a latest European league, as MilanNews relays from Gazzetta dello Sport.

Sources indicate that “We will continue on our path with or without an agreement because we know that in the end, we will be the ones left standing. I honestly cannot understand how we can operate for 26 years and not be able to make a profit.

“Adam Silver and Mark Tatum [who sat alongside him] are doing everything they can to reach an agreement, but it takes two people to compromise. I am convinced that in the end, the NBA will prevail.”.

The American also discussed the position that Milan are in and the future of the basketball arm of the club.

“Milan is at the moment the hottest market in Europe. When I arrived four years ago, I had two goals, besides winning: one was the stadium, which will finally be built, and the other was to invest in basketball.

“In fact, I had conversations with Olimpia Milano well before the advent of NBA Europe. Where will NBA Europe be in 10 years? Let’s say that if the upcoming NBA franchise in Las Vegas is valued at $13-14 billion, I think that in 10 years, NBA Europe clubs will be worth at least half that.”.
