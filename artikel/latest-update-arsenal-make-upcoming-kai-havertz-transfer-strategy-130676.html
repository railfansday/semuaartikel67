Arsenal are planning to keep Kai Havertz as their first-choice striker despite the questions about his long-term future in the role.

Mikel Arteta is a huge fan of the 27-year-old and what he brings to the squad, regardless of the ongoing concerns about his lack of consistent threat in front of goal.

So far this season, the Germany international has scored just four goals in 16 appearances across all competitions, but that is mentioned to be no concern for the Gunners.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Arsenal are happy with what they've seen from Havertz, even if the stats don't highlight his role.

The forward's job is to bring his teammates into to play, while his exceptional movement can provide opportunities, even if he is not the one putting the ball in the net.

Viktor Gyokeres has seen himself sidelined, despite a better goal record, and despite the doubts over Havertz's future, there are no looks to see him leave Arsenal in the near future.

Football Insider reported on 25 November that Havertz should return to the Arsenal starting XI atfer Gyokeres struggled during their 1-1 draw against Borussia Dortmund.

The coversation surrounding Arsenal's strikers has been ongoing for years at this stage, but it has become clear that the Germany forward is their best option in his position.

Arteta, therefore, has no looks to let the forward leave any time soon despite uncertainty surrounding his future and his contract situation at the Emirates.

"Kai Havertz is an interesting one, because Arsenal still don't have that top-class striker," Brown told Football Insider.

"But, to be honest, I'm not completely convinced they even want one at this stage, because it seems they're happy with Havertz after Gyokeres was cast aside a bit.

"Mikel Arteta loves him, that's pretty obvious, he likes the role he plays even if he's not scoring tonnes of goals every single week.

"He brings his teammates into play, his movement is outstanding, he creates opportunities for other people, even if the stats don't necessarily reflect that.

"Arsenal know what they've got in Havertz, they feel they can rely on him, so even if there are doubts about his contract, I can't see any way they'd let him leave at this stage.".

Speculation about Havertz's contract situation has risen in recent weeks, with Arsenal making efforts to tie their best assets down to long-term deals in North London.

Bukayo Saka and Declan Rice have both penned significant deals in recent months, while Arteta has also extended his stay, and work is now ongoing with other key players.

Football Insider revealed on 24 November that Havertz is on Arsenal's list of players they want to keep around, and talks are scheduled to take place in the near future.

Those talks may be delayed, however, as they prioritise other players in the squad, but there is no doubt Arsenal would like the German to extend his current contract.

Sources say that it is simply a "matter of time" before talks with the German begin, ahead of his current deal expiring in the summer of 2028.
