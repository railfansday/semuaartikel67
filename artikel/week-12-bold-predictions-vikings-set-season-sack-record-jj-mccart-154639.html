Anything can happen in the National Football League, and the league's 2026 Thanksgiving actions emphasized that.

This year's Thanksgiving became the first day in NFL history with at least three games each with a go-ahead score in the final minute of the fourth quarter, per CBS Sports Research. All three games featured a game-winning field goal or touchdown in the final five seconds of regulation, the first such Thanksgiving ever. In the opening game of the day, Caleb Williams threw a six-yard touchdown pass to wide receiver Rome Odunze with three seconds left to secure a 39-35 road win over the Detroit Lions. That marked the latest game-winning touchdown in regulation on Thanksgiving in the Super Bowl era (since 1966), per CBS Sports Research.

Recent findings suggest that in the Dallas Cowboys' home game, they blew a 21-point lead in a 39-38 defeat against the Philadelphia Eagles — the largest on Thanksgiving Day in NFL history. That game ended with Eagles kicker Jake Elliott hitting a 59-yard, game-winning kick with two seconds left. To close out the nightcap between the Buffalo Bills and the Kansas City Chiefs, Bills kicker Tyler Bass drained the longest game-winning field goal (57 yards) as time expired in Buffalo franchise history, according to CBS Sports Research.

That's tied for the most sacks in the first 10 games of a season in Texans history, along with J.J. Watt (2012 and 2015). However, the rest of Houston's defense hasn't been at Anderson Jr.'s level this season. The Texans went from the league's No. 2 scoring defense in 2025 (17.4 points per game allowed) to average (22.2 points per game allowed, 16th in the NFL) in 2026.
