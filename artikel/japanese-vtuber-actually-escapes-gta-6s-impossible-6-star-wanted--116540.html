Japanese streamer Haigure Tamao has managed to escape GTA 6’s supposedly impossible six-star Wanted level by using an elaborate Easter egg that transports players into the original Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.

After ditching the sixth Wanted star in GTA 5, Rockstar brought it back for GTA 6, but with a major twist.

While players can normally escape the police by changing their appearance, switching vehicles or hiding, reaching six stars is supposed to leave them with just two options: death or arrest.

Even trying to escape in a jet just results in missiles being released at you.

However, one player has discovered a clever way to cheat the system with some help from Rockstar’s 2002 classic.

In a video uploaded to YouTube, Tamao, using his VTuber avatar, explained that he wanted to test whether entering the portal featured in GTA 6’s Tommy Vercetti Easter egg would erase his Wanted level.

The secret requires players to solve a puzzle spanning 12 electrical substations across Leonida before accessing a portal inside the Turtle Head Power Station between 7 PM and 6 AM in-game.

Entering it transports Jason to a recreation of the original Malibu Club, complete with PS2-era graphics and an appearance from returning character Kent Paul.

After struggling to survive his initial attempt, the streamer completed an entirely different secret involving the president’s hidden bunkers to get his hands on a powerful tank.

With his newly acquired vehicle, the YouTuber wreaked havoc near the power station, eventually reaching six stars with just minutes to spare before the portal closed.

Sources indicate that upon entering the retro Vice City, his Wanted level immediately disappeared. However, the biggest question was whether it would return once he was transported back to modern-day Leonida.

“The moment of truth,” Tamao said, translated from Japanese.

Much to his delight, the six stars were nowhere to be seen.

“I finally did it! I went from a Wanted level of six to zero!” he celebrated.

Whether Rockstar intended for players to use the Easter egg this way remains unclear, but the discovery adds yet another layer to one of GTA 6’s most elaborate secrets.

Players have already uncovered some incredible surprises since launch, including Dexter Morgan’s apartment and boat and a glitch that enables first-person gameplay.

As it turns out, the best way to escape Vice City’s police might be to leave their generation of GTA altogether.
