Jenny McCarthy and Donnie Wahlberg's son has shared the emotional reality of saying goodbye to his Illinois childhood home—days after it was revealed the couple offloaded the dwelling.

It has been revealed that the singer, 57, and the actress, 53, owned the residence for more than a decade, having purchased it for $1.1 million in 2015. Realtor.com® exclusively revealed that the couple had sold the palatial pad for $2.6 million.

Now, days later, McCarthy's son, Evan Asher, who she shares with ex John Asher, has taken to Instagram to open up about the heartache he feels leaving their longtime home behind, branding it the "saddest moment of my entire life.".

Sources indicate that “I have officially moved away from my home and life just won’t be the same anymore. I’m so sorry, I was very emotional. But when you live somewhere most of your life, have so many friends, and make more nostalgic memories than everything in any other house that you’ve lived in it’s a lot to take in," he mentioned as he wiped away tears.

Recent findings suggest that “I knew I had to share my emotions with you all. This house has done so much for me over the past 11 years and I hope it will do it for the latest owners too. One day. ONE DAY. I will buy this place again and start a family of my own. Thank you house for everything overall. I love you and god bless you," he added.

In the clip, Evan revealed that he wasn't "doing so great" after leaving the home.

“It doesn’t feel the same anymore when someone else owns the place because you’re not living there anymore. It just really hurts. It hurts really bad. I’m just not doing great. I’m just gonna miss all the times I had there. I’m happy, though, for my parents. They made the right decision. But at the same time, it’s just still so hard. But I’ll be okay, we’ll be okay," he admitted.

In the comments section, both McCarthy and Wahlberg penned sweet messages.

“Promise someday you’re gonna knock on that door and buy it for your family. It’s not goodbye. It’s see you later. I love you. Hugging and kissing your heart," McCarthy said.

Industry observers note that wahlberg wrote, “I love you Evan. Glad we got to cry together last weekend.”.

His candid glimpse into his emotions came just days after they sold the property.

Built in 1996, the four-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom home spans 7,236 square feet and sits on 1.26 acres.

Described as "an extraordinary St. Charles estate," the property underwent extensive renovations during Wahlberg and McCarthy's time there, with the couple transforming it into a personalized retreat designed for both entertaining and relaxing.

Inside, the home boasts an expansive living room and family room, a formal dining room, den, game room, recreation space, exercise room, and finished lower level.

The kitchen comes with an island, breakfast bar, walk-in pantry, and butler's pantry, while hardwood and stone flooring can be found throughout much of the main level.

Adding to the home's cozy appeal are six fireplaces, which are spread throughout spaces, including the living room, family room, primary bedroom, and basement.

The primary suite serves as its own private retreat and features an en suite bathroom with a soaking tub, double sinks, and a full-body spray shower.

However, some of the property's most impressive amenities can be found in its entertainment and wellness spaces.

The lower level features a bar and recreation areas, while the home also comes equipped with an exercise room and sauna.

"The reimagined English basement offers an entire recreation level with a home theater, sauna, cold plunge, bar, and generous gathering spaces," the listing read.

Outside, the couple created a resort-style setup with a swimming pool, waterslide, waterfall, spa, putting green, expansive patios, and lush landscaping.

Wahlberg and McCarthy also put their own stamp on the property through a number of highly personalized additions made during their renovations, including door handles shaped like their initials and a custom poker table bearing their names.

The property also holds plenty of sentimental value for the pair. In 2019, they revealed that it was the very first home they toured together while searching for a place where they could embrace the small-town lifestyle they envisioned.

Their decision to sell came after Wahlberg revealed in late 2025 that he and McCarthy had discussed the possibility of relocating to California to be closer to his adult sons, Xavier and Elijah, whom he shares with ex-wife Kim Fey.

At the time, Wahlberg explained that his sons were among the few things that could tempt him back to the West Coast, noting that one of them had repeatedly encouraged him to move closer.

“I have a 24-year-old son and I can’t tell you how many times he’s asked me in the last year to move back to California to be close to him. Probably the only thing that could ever get me back to California is my two sons. And if they keep asking, Jenny and I are going to start going on Realtor.com® and figuring things out," he told People in 2025.

It is not yet clear where Wahlberg and McCarthy plan to settle after selling their Illinois residence.

The couple have called the St. Charles property home for more than a decade, having purchased it one year after tying the knot in 2014.

The $2.8 million listing was held by Cory Jones of eXp Realty.
