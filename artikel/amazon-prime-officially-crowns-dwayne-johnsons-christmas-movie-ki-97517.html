It has been revealed that dwayne Johnson is among a handful of stars who made a successful transition from WWE arena to the big screen. He has given us some versatile movies, from the Jumanji franchise to Moana; the actor always brings fresh concepts to entertain fans worldwide. He broke the mold of being an “action star” by giving a heartfelt performance in Benny Safdie’s The Smashing Machine that even generated Oscar buzz, which certainly comes as a relief after a spell of underperforming films like Red Notice, Black Adam, and such.

Recent findings suggest that one of his movies has redeemed itself in recent years. Red One has emerged as a modern Christmas classic after it failed to excite audiences during its initial run, owing to mixed reviews. Despite a cast with two Marvel stars, it fell short of critics' and the audience’s expectations, and a ballooned budget due to behind-the-scenes drama didn’t help either. Red One, a cooler-than-ice Christmas feature, is directed by Jumanji filmmaker Jake Kasdan. The movie follows the North Pole's Head of Security, Callum Drift (Johnson), who must team up with the world's most notorious hacker, Jack O'Malley (Chris Evans), on a globe-trotting mission to find a kidnapped Santa (JK Simmons) and save Christmas.

According to recent reports, it received mixed reactions upon its release, earning only $185.9 million worldwide on a bloated $250 million budget. The mixed reaction is further evident in its ratings: 29% on Rotten Tomatoes from critics and 89% from audiences, whose consensus notes, “Led by The Rock, the entire ensemble delivers ample servings of naughty and nice that may leave you humming, 'Oh what fun it is to ride in a Red One open sleigh.'”.

It has been revealed that the audience’s love is now catapulting the Christmas movie to latest heights. Red One has returned to Amazon Prime’s streaming charts just in time for the Holiday season. Red One has grabbed the seventh spot on Amazon Prime’s domestic top 10 list, as per FlixPatrol. The feature has consistently appeared on several streaming charts over the years and continues to do so. It has a talented cast including JK Simmons as Santa, Lucy Liu as Zoe, Kiernan Shipka as Grýla, Bonnie Hunt as Mrs. Claus, and more. With its compelling performances and humorously twisted plot, it's no wonder that more and more fans are revisiting the movie on streaming as the Holiday season approaches.
