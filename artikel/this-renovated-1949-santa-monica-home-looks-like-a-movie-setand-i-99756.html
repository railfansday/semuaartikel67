A five-bedroom modern retreat nestled on a tree-lined street in Santa Monica, CA has just hit the market for $6,898,000.

Built in 1949, the 4,746-square-foot residence was renovated in 2022 and now features a sun-soaked open floor initiative with curated finishes, designer lighting, and walls of windows.

Industry observers note that “I love all of the light that comes into the home,” stated listing agent Shelton Wilder of Christie’s International Real Estate Southern California. “It has beautiful skylights throughout and it’s located on the side of the street that gets so much natural sunlight. It looks like a movie set.”.

Designed for grand-scale entertaining and everyday living, the thoughtful layout offers a lavish primary sanctuary separate from a second-story guest wing that could also be used for extended family.

It has been revealed that “The primary suite is so private and very luxurious,” Wilder noted. “It was the primary residence for the sellers, who have owned it for five years. I think the next buyer will use it as a primary as well. It was so special for the sellers who have three kids. It could also be someone with older kids who want to stay there while visiting.”.

A sleek chef’s kitchen has two refrigerators, double ovens, custom cabinetry, a breakfast bar, and a built-in coffee machine. Glass doors from the dining area open to the private backyard with an outdoor shower and a brand-upcoming heated pool and spa.

Architectural details include a sunken sitting room with built-in seating, a living room with a fireplace, and a primary suite with access to two offices, one of which opens to a rooftop deck designed for soaking in the sweeping canyon views.

Industry observers note that “Santa Monica Canyon is a magical place to live,” she mentioned. “You can walk to the beach; Montana Avenue is very close for shopping and restaurants; and Canyon Elementary School is just a few minutes away as well.”.

A bonus playroom could double as a family room, and a commercial-grade fitness center occupies the lower level.

“It’s very rare for a house like this with this amount of square footage, two offices, a pool, and a gym to come up in this area,” Wilder added.
