Ubisoft has laid off 166 staff across its Ubisoft Montreal and Ubisoft Quebec studios, the company announced.

Sources indicate that “Ubisoft is reducing its workforce by 166 positions across its Montreal and Quebec City operations,” a Ubisoft spokesperson said in a statement. “As our portfolio evolves, our production needs are changing, requiring us to adjust the size of some teams to ensure our resources are aligned with current production priorities and business needs.”.

It has been revealed that the spokesperson continued, “We deeply value the contributions of these teams and recognize the impact this may have on our colleagues. We are committed to supporting them through this process with care and respect. Those affected are being provided with severance, extended health care benefits, career transition services and other resources.”.

A source told IGN that the layoffs affect employees whose projects have been canceled or refocused, as well as those who could not be reassigned due to the decrease in the number of active projects.
