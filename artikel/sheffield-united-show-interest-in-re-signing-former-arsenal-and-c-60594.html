Sheffield United have reportedly shown interest in re-signing defender Rob Holding.

The 31-year-old is best-known for his exploits in the colours of Arsenal, who he represented between 2016 and 2023 after cutting his teeth at Bolton Wanderers.

Holding later moved to Crystal Palace, who loaned the centre-back to Sheffield United for the second half of the 2024/25 season.

He helped the Blades reach the Championship play-off final, making 11 appearances before seeing his Palace contract come to an end.

There was talk of a return to Bramall Lane but a reunion did not materialise and Holding went stateside to sign for Colorado Rapids.

He is not under contract for the 2027 Major League Soccer season and according to Sky Sports, Sheffield United have shown interest.

Holding is also noted to have caught the eye of Los Angeles FC and Houston Dynamo, who appear to have been impressed by his MLS showings.

Sheffield United manager Chris Wilder is already familiar with Holding and he would add a wealth of experience to the Blades ranks.

However, the prospect of remaining in the MLS - and living in LA or Houston - could prove tempting.

When Sheffield United recruited Holding in 2025, Wilder described him as “the last remaining piece of the jigsaw”.

He stated: “It is the last remaining piece of the jigsaw and whilst we have had to wait until deadline day to get this one boxed off, I think our patience has been justified by getting Rob over the line.

In a recent development, “He's played in some huge games during his time with Arsenal, so he'll relish the situation he's walking into. He'll compete with the likes of Anel [Ahmedhodzic], Jack [Robinson] and others, and joins a squad which is looking far healthier than the one we had at the start of the window, as we look to attack the remaining part of the season.”.
