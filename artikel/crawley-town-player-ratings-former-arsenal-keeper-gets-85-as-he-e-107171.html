The Reds were involved in a stalemate at the Broadfield Stadium but the Reds did start the brighter of the two sides but failed to test Matthew Cox in the Barnet goal.

Barnet grew into the game and forced Mannone into a couple of saves and they used that momentum into the second dominating the possession and the chance but Crawley were resilient and Mannone denied the Bees a handful of times to extend Crawley’s unbeaten run in the league under Kevin Blackwell.

Vito Mannone - 8.5: The Italian shot stopper pulled off a great stop low down to his left on 25 minutes to keep the score at 0-0. His distribution out to the wide areas was accurate and effective as the Reds were able to win the flick-ons. Made a number of crucial saves in the second half to ultimately claim Crawley a hard fought point.

Kellan Gordon - 7: Delivered a lot of crosses from into the Barnet box and found himself in a lot of space down the right-hand side.

Josh Flint - 6.5: The centre half moved to the right side of the back three and put in a solid shift coming out on top of a lot of his duels.

Charlie Barker – 6: Was moved into central midfield from the back three to fill the hole left by Jude Arthurs and battled hard in the midfield also progressing the ball forward well at times.

Danilo Orsi - 5.5: The ball struggled to stick to Crawley’s number nine and didn’t seem on the same wavelength as some of teammates at times.

Pemi Aderoju – 7.5: His hold up play was second to none and was effective at bringing others into the game.

Lewis Richards – 6: Playing in the wing back role, Richards defended well when required and played a number of decent balls into the channel for Orsi to run onto.

Jonny Russell – 6.5: Moving into the back three from wing-back he didn’t look out of place and dealt with Barnet’s number fast and physical nine Jack Maskell extremely well.

Dylan Morgan – 7: Picked up the ball in some great central positions and used his neat footwork to bypass the Barnet players and move the ball into space.

Kai Jennings – 6: A lack of management seeped into his game at times but he still put in a decent shift in midfield.

Ben Heneghan - 7.5: Marshalled the backline well and headed everything that came his way, winning most if not all his aerial duels and making some crucial interventions.

Stephen Duke-McKenna – 6: Looked dangerous in the channel when he came on for Morgan.

Osman Kamara – 7: Made Crawley’s counter-attacking option more of a threat with his electric pace down the left hand side.

Ade Adeyemo – 6: Performed his defensive responsibilities excellently well.
