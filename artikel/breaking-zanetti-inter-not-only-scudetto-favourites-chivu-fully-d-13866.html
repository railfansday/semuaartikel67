Inter vice president Javier Zanetti feels Cristian Chivu fully deserves his Coach of the Year award, warns they aren’t the only Scudetto favourites, and can still ‘compete’ in the Champions League.

The Nerazzurri boss was given the Panchina D’Oro trophy today for best Italian coach of the year, thanks to securing the Serie A and Coppa Italia double in his first full top flight experience.

“I congratulate him, because it was fully deserved for the work he has done and is still doing,” Zanetti told Sky Sport Italia.

“Cristian is very intelligent, he manages to transmit his passion and clear concepts to the players. He deserves this opportunity and will keep on doing well.”.

Chivu did complain that too many people assumed Inter would follow up their Scudetto by retaining the title, putting undue pressure on the squad.

“We never disguised our ambitions, we want to compete to continue winning,” replied Zanetti.

“Cristian is right when he states we are not the only ones to target certain achievements, because other teams are doing just as well this season. Serie A is very difficult and balanced, so there is danger behind every corner. We’ve got to keep taking it one game at a time.”.

When it comes to the Champions League, the situation is even more complicated, especially after losing to Real Madrid and Club Brugge in the opening fixtures.

According to recent reports, “There are many strong teams like Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain, so we are trying to reduce the gap, but it’s not easy to compete with them. However, with Chivu and the lads working hard, we can aim to be protagonists.

“We had a great performance at the Bernabeu, and sadly the result didn’t go our way, but then we had two good victories and hope to continue like this, because we want to compete in Europe too, or at least we can try,” concluded Zanetti.

The break for international duty has at least given some players an opportunity to recover from injury problems, as Lautaro Martinez is hoping to make his comeback against Atalanta this weekend in Serie A.

The Argentina international has been out of action since October 15 when suffering a thigh strain.
