Gianluca Di Marzio has confirmed Markus Krösche’s imminent arrival, and that he will be given the reins to take AC Milan forward.

Industry observers note that it is not exactly a well-kept secret that Milan have been pursuing Krösche. He was the main target in the summer to come in and lead the management, but Eintracht Frankfurt blocked his exit. Now he has stepped down from his role there, a move to Italy has been in the headlines again.
