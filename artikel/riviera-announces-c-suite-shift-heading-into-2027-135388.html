Riviera Travel River Cruise has announced a C-suite shift as it prepares for another year of anticipated growth.

Travel industry veteran Vicky Billing has been named president of North America, and Stuart Milan has been elevated to chief commercial officer.

Sources indicate that the company noted in a press release that, bringing 30 years of travel industry experience to her fresh role, Billing will oversee Riviera Travel River Cruises’ continued expansion across the U.S. and Canada.

She joins the firm’s multinational executive team, reporting directly to CEO Matt Luscombe, and will oversee all aspects of the company’s North American operations.

Billing succeeds Stuart Milan, who has led Riviera Travel River Cruises’ North American operations for the past three years and will return to the UK, promoted to chief commercial officer.

In his new role, Milan will lead Riviera Travel River Cruises’ commercial strategy, driving profitable customer growth across revenue management, commercial planning, trade, loyalty, CRM and digital commerce.

“North America is a key part of Riviera Travel River Cruises’ future, and we have significant ambitions for the market,” noted Luscombe.

“Billing is an exceptional commercial leader with a real passion for the travel trade, and I have every confidence she’ll build on the momentum already established and help take our North American business to fresh heights.”.

Billing right now serves as Riviera Travel River Cruises’ director of trade, partnerships and loyalty, where she oversees key elements of the organization’s commercial strategy across the UK and Ireland.

“I’m thrilled to take on this opportunity and work alongside our team and partners as we build the next chapter of Riviera’s business in North America,” mentioned Billing.

Industry observers note that “I’m incredibly proud of what the North American team has built over the last three years,” noted Milan. “We’ve established a strong foundation for continued growth, supported by a brilliant team and increasingly strong relationships across the travel advisor community.”.

The press release added that the appointments of Billing and Milan are part of a broader evolution of Riviera Travel River Cruises’ executive leadership team.

Will Sarson, currently product director, has been promoted to chief brand and product officer, overseeing the firm’s brand, product portfolio and guest proposition.

Robin Shaw, currently chief commercial officer, will transition to chief transformation officer, overseeing key strategic initiatives with an immediate focus on the future development of Riviera’s river cruise fleet.

Industry observers note that all changes are effective beginning April 1, 2027. Existing advisor relationships, sales contacts, and trade programs will remain in place throughout the leadership transition.
