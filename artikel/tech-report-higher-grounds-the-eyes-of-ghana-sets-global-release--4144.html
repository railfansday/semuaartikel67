Breakwater Studios and the Obamas’ Higher Ground will stream The Eyes of Ghana on YouTube on November 17 in a novel partnership with social media star IShowSpeed, aka Darren Watkins, who is also a producer on the film.

According to recent reports, the awards contender, officially shortlisted for Best Feature Documentary and Best Score at the 42nd IDA Documentary Awards, follows a Ghanaian cameraman’s extraordinary mission to rescue a hidden film archive documenting the birth of African independence. IShowSpeed (known as Speed) shared official trailer on his channels where it received over 3 million views. Industry observers note that the news follows a breakout year for YouTube creators at the box office as well as continuing experimentation by filmmakers and distributors seeking newly introduced ways to get their films to audiences directly, in this case collaborating with a streaming creator rather than a traditional streaming platform. The doc had a one-day theatrical release, announced by Speed, on October 28 in 100+ theaters in North America, London and Accra, Ghana via Regal, AMC, Cinemark and some indies. He attended the New York premiere showing, livestreaming his arrival.

The social media creator, 21, with a total audience of more than 200 million followers across platforms, was named World’s Most Influential Creator by Rolling Stone in 2025 and one of TIME Magazine’s Most Influential Creators in 2026. He also traces his ancestry to Ghana. During his Speed Does Africa tour earlier this year, the creator visited Accra where he told viewers he was “back home.”.

It has been revealed that “To have the opportunity to use my platform to share this history with the world for free is an incredible moment. After visiting Ghana last year and finding out that my family history can be traced back to West Africa, I know how powerful it can be to feel connected to where you come from,” he said.

“I’m so grateful to the people of Ghana, to Chris Hesse for preserving these incredible images, to Ben Proudfoot and the filmmaking team for bringing this story to life. To be part of this alongside President Obama and Michelle Obama is an honor of a lifetime” he said.

Recent findings suggest that the 90-minute film follows veteran Ghanaian cinematographer Chris Hesse, who served as personal cameraman to Kwame Nkrumah, Ghana’s first president and a leading voice for African liberation. As his eyesight fades, Hesse races against time to rescue more than 1,000 films capturing the continent’s struggle for independence in the 1950s and 60s. Hidden for decades after Nkrumah’s overthrow in a 1966 coup, the archive becomes a bridge between generations to fill in their lost history. Five years ago, after a chance encounter, Hesse asked fellow documentarian Proudfoot to tell his story.

According to recent reports, “Over six decades ago Chris Hesse picked up a camera and documented one of the most pivotal moments in world history,” notes Ethan Lewis, Head of Non-Fiction at Higher Ground. “Now, thanks to iShowSpeed and this historic, precedent setting partnership, this history will be shared for all to see for free.”.

The Eyes of Ghana opened the TIFF Docs program in 2025 and had its international premiere in competition at the BFI London Film Festival. Its honors include the Special Jury Prize and Best Documentary at the Africa International Film Festival in Lagos, Audience Awards at the Hamptons International and Annapolis Film Festivals, and Heartland Film Festival’s Jimmy Stewart Legacy Award.

Cinematographer Brandon Somerhalder (Come See Me in the Good Light) shot The Eyes of Ghana across multiple formats, including sequences captured on Imax 65mm film.

Original score from Academy Award winner Kris Bowers, Proudfoot’s frequent collaborator.

It has been revealed that the film is produced by Proudfoot, Nana Adwoa Frimpong, Moses Bwayo, Anita Afonu, Brandon Somerhalder, Ethan Lewis and Vinnie Malhotra. In addition to President Barack Obama and Michelle Obama, executive producers include Sir John Akomfrah, Kofi Amoo-Gottfried, Paula Madison, Dan and Lian Gill, Max Johnson, Jonathan E. Lim, Michael Risley, Josh Rosenberg, David Treatman and Esther Wojcicki.

Sources indicate that IShowSpeed, was named to the inaugural TIME100 Creators list and ranked No. 1 on Rolling Stone’s 2025 Most Influential Creators list. His honors also include consecutive Streamer of the Year awards in 2024 and 2025.
