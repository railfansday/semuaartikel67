Roma also flexed its muscles at PSG.

Industry observers note that the Giallorossi drew 2-2 at the Parc des Princes against the team that has won the last two Champions League titles. De Roon expressed his satisfaction at the end of the match: “After the 2-1 win, we could have relaxed, but instead we played more. Great performance.”.

Gasperini also commented: “I’m proud of the team’s growth; we’re on the right track.”.

Industry observers note that among the many former players in the match, in addition to Luis Enrique, was Marquinhos. The Brazilian defender remained on the bench and didn’t play a single minute. However, the 1994-born defender made a point of greeting the Roma fans at the end of the match.

At the end of PSG-Roma (2-2), Marquinhos made a point of greeting the Giallorossi fans who filled the away section of the Parc des Princes.

According to recent reports, the former Giallorossi defender hasn’t forgotten his past in the capital. The gesture was particularly appreciated by the Roma fans present, who reciprocated the affection of the 1992-born player: “Marquinhos is one of us.”.
