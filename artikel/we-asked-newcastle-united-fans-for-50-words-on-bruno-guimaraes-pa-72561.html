On Friday, we asked Newcastle United fans for 50 words on Bruno Guimaraes.

Saturday of course sees the former United captain return to St James’ Park.

Bruno Guimaraes and Arsenal on Tyneside for a 5.30pm Premier League kick-off.

Much has been noted about this return some three and a half months after deciding to leave.

We thought we would ask a number of Newcastle United fans for their thoughts,.

Rather than giving any leading pointers as to what to write, we totally left it up to the regular/irregular contributors to The Mag as to what they wanted to come out with.

We simply asked these Newcastle United fans ‘Please give us 50 words on Bruno Guimaraes’….

It has been revealed that I said it on The Mag Pod when he left; Bruno will be remembered as a club legend. Having said that, he’ll be booed today – and deservedly so. He wants a title to his name and that’s the price he’ll pay.

I bear no grudge towards Bruno. He’s among our greatest players in my lifetime and the talisman of the post-takeover era. Do I wish he’d stayed? Of course. Yet I see his decision to chase trophies as disappointing, not treacherous. Booing him would dilute our treatment of the real traitors: then we’re the ones who look pathetic.

This is a player who skippered us to a major trophy and that memory always remains a positive one. His leaving was not enjoyable, but was inevitable. He’s a huge miss. He’s old news now.

Bruno. Our talisman, our captain, the man who lifted our first trophy in generations. A legend? Perhaps, with time. But he engineered the worst kind of departure when we needed him most, betraying us, the fans who adored him. A man desperate to be loved by everyone, he risks being loved by no one. A legend and a traitor.

We loved you Bruno for putting your faith in NUFC. We loved the cheeky goal against Southampton. We loved your gurning and windmillIng after winning a meaningless throw-in. We loved your Brazilian bromance with Major Joe. We loved you. But Gloria Gaynor was right: just turn around now, cos you ain’t welcome any more. We will survive.

It has been revealed that courageous, leader, legend. One of my favourite players to ever pull on the shirt. If it wasn’t for him, we wouldn’t have achieved anything like what we have in the last 5 years. It hurt like hell when he left and I wish he didn’t. But, I get it. If we could’ve matched his ambition, he would’ve stayed. I wish him luck – but not when he’s playing us.

At the end of the day, players aren’t slaves or indentured servants and they have a right to move on. Having a long contract nowadays is more about maintaining value for a potential sale than it is about loyalty. I think Bruno loved the toon and I don’t see it as disingenuous that he kissed the badge. No doubt he looked around when rumours of interest from Arsenal began and wondered where the massive promises from the owners had gone and why Eddie was so disgruntled and other top players were on their way out. He wasn’t leaving the club he’d fallen in love with, he was leaving the club it was becoming!
