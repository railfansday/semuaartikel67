If the previous international window was all sunshine and roses for Greece, their trip to Amsterdam on Friday night must have felt like being caught in a thunderstorm without an umbrella.

Ivan Jovanovic's side beat Germany on their way to collecting eight points in their opening four Nations League matches but were demolished 7-0 by the Netherlands three days ago, despite starting the evening level on points with their hosts at the top of Group A2.

The head-to-head tiebreaker rule means they now can't finish above the Dutch, but they will qualify for the quarter-finals ahead of Germany if Jurgen Klopp's side lose to the Netherlands, or if Greece better Die Mannschaft's result. Jovanovic will be without midfielder Dimitrios Kourbelis for the clash with Serbia.

The visitors' campaign hit a newly introduced low with their defeat and double sending-off against Germany on Friday, although they were already 3-1 down when the red cards occurred.

Despite being bottom of the group and without a point, Veljko Paunovic's side still have something to play for, as a win or a draw would see them avoid automatic relegation and instead go into the League A/B promotion and relegation play-offs.

Paunovic is guaranteed to make at least two changes, with Vanja Dragojevic and Andrija Zivkovic both suspended following their red cards against Germany.

The Greeks have the twin motivations of a quarter-final place and wanting to restore some pride, which should help them get over the line.
