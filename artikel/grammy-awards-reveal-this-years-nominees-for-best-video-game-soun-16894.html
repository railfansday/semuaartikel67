The nominees for the 2027 Grammy Awards were recently announced, and of particular interest to gamers will be the shortlist for Best Score Soundtrack For Video Games and Other Interactive Media.

Five games, released between 31 August, 2025 and 28th August, 2026, are in contention, and two of them come from Sony's first-party efforts.

This could be a second Grammy for Gordy Haab, who jointly won for his work on Star Wars Jedi: Survivor in the 2024 awards.

PS Studios almost always has very strong music, so it's no surprise to see some representation here. Personally I can't speak to Marvel's Wolverine, but the score for Ghost of Yotei is great.

As for 007 First Light, to be honest I struggle to recall any of the music aside from the Bond theme and Lana Del Ray's title track.

It's an interesting group, anyway — I'm not sure I could really call it.
