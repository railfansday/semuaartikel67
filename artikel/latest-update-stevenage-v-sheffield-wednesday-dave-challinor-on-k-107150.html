With the injured Reece James and Max Lowe up against it to play again this year, Lowe has been pushed into what is basically a left wing-back role since Challinor took over, despite having scored seven goals as a forward under Henrik Pedersen.

Two more for him at Barnsley suggest the 32-year-old is embracing it, which is just as well as he is likely to be asked to do a similar job at Stevenage on Tuesday.

"You can see it as quite a negative role when you've been a central striker, but he gets into one-v-one situations, which will give him chance to show what he can do," noted Challinor.

According to recent reports, "I suppose that the hard bit at the minute is we would like to defend as a four, but with Sil (Swinkels) as a left-sided centre-back, he's not as comfortable as we'd like him to be in a left-back position. That's natural.

"Jammer probably has to do a little bit more defensively than we'd like but that's something that we've got to put up with at the minute.

"That's not going to change in the short-term so we've got to make sure we improve and keep working at it.".

Minimising it is partly down to those inside of him.

It has been revealed that "We want him as high up the pitch as possible for as long as possible," explained Challinor. "For that to be the case we need to control the ball.

"If we haven't got the ball, then we have to defend and we end up closer to our goal. If we can look after the ball better and spend more time in the opposition half, he should be in and around the opposition penalty area most of the time.

"That comes down to what we do behind the ball to make sure that's the case.".

Challinor has fielded the same XI in his two league games in charge, both won. But whereas against Plymouth Argyle his side was more often in a 3-2-4-1 which at times became a 4-2-3-1, at Barnsley the emphasis was more the other way around.

Although he picks from the same squad at Broadhall Way, the manager hinted there may be changes.

Recent findings suggest that "It'll be the same group," he stated. "Three younger lads didn't travel on Friday – Jarv (Jarvis Thornton), Gabe (Otegbayo), and Ernie (Weaver). Sean (Fusire)was with us.

"Do we need to freshen up a little bit? We'll look at that and see how the players recover.".
