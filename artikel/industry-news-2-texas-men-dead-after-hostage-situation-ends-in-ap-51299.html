Two men were found dead inside of a home in Gillespie County, Texas, after a hostage situation resulted in an apparent murder-suicide, according to authorities.

The Gillespie County Sheriff’s Office remarked in a press release shared via Facebook that deputies responded to a home on Cemetery Road in Stonewall, Texas, shortly after 5 p.m. on Wednesday, November 18, after they received reports of a disturbance that involved a man with a gun.

Deputies began communicating with the victim inside the residence via phone during the incident, and he told deputies that an armed man would not allow him to leave the home.

Because the incident was treated as a hostage situation, deputies made multiple attempts to communicate with both the suspect and victim so that the conflict could hopefully end in a peaceful resolution.

Authorities stated they lost communication with the men for several hours. Law enforcement then entered the home around 3:30 a.m. on Thursday, November 19, and they found both men dead in a room.

Investigators ultimately determined that the incident was a murder-suicide involving a 35-year-old suspect and a 49-year-old victim.

The Gillespie County Sheriff’s Office mentioned that they worked on the case with the help of the Kerr County Sheriff’s Office Special Operations Unit, Texas Department of Public Safety Special Operations Unit and a Texas Ranger.

It has been revealed that authorities have not yet released the men’s identities as they await to notify next of kin. It’s not right now clear how the two men knew each other or if authorities believe there was a motive in the case.

“More information will be released as the investigation is completed. We will not release any names pending notification of next of kin,” the sheriff’s office concluded in the press release. “Please keep the families in your thoughts and prayers during this very difficult time.”.
