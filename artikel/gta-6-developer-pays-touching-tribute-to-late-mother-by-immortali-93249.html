A GTA 6 developer has paid tribute to his late mother by including a memorial to her in the finished game.

After more than a decade of anticipation, Grand Theft Auto 6 finally launched on November 19, 2026, bringing players back to Vice City and the wider fictional state of Leonida.

Sources indicate that rockstar’s latest open-world game is packed with intricate environmental details, hidden references, and Easter eggs, with players continuing to uncover secrets scattered throughout its sprawling map. A developer who worked on the game has shared a tribute to his late mother that players can find.

In a heartfelt Instagram post, GTA 6 developer Matt Green revealed that his late mother has been honoured with a special memorial just north of the in-game region, Hamlet.

Green’s mother, Bev, passed away approximately eight months before the game’s release, meaning she never had the opportunity to see the project her son had been working on.

However, the developer was able to ensure that his mother would remain part of the game through a permanent tribute within its fictional world.

In a recent development, “I’m incredibly grateful that she’s able to be honoured inside the world of VI – at a special memorial just north of Hamlet,” they said. “Miss you Mum.”.

A post shared by Matt Green (@mattxgreen).

Recent findings suggest that the developer recalled begging his mother to buy him a copy of GTA Vice City as a child. Despite her initial reluctance, she eventually agreed, a decision he believes ultimately set him on the path to working at Rockstar Games.

According to recent reports, “She was super proud and excited to see what I’d been working on but unfortunately I never got to show her,” they wrote. “She’s been gone almost 8 months now and sometimes it still doesn’t feel real.”.
