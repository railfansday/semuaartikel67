For those of you not familiar with Companion Pass, it’s one of the best deals in travel. Those with Companion Pass can have a companion fly with them for just the cost of taxes, regardless of whether paying cash or redeeming points. Essentially think of it as a two-for-one travel pass, which is otherwise pretty unheard of.

Sources indicate that under the current rules, Southwest Companion Pass ordinarily requires 100 flights or 135,000 qualifying points in a calendar year, so this promotion offers quite the shortcut (though also with a limited travel window). We are seeing changes to Companion Pass in 2027, as Companion Pass will become a perk of top tier Platinum elite status, rather than something that’s separately qualified for.
