Tottenham Hotspur return to home soil looking to bounce back from their pre-international break defeat to Leeds United, as Roberto De Zerbi's side chase a third consecutive Premier League victory in North London and a spot in the top half.

Visitors Ipswich Town arrive with just one win from their last six league outings and four points from a possible 15 on the road, though Gary O'Neil's promoted side have repeatedly shown their battling qualities in narrow defeats against Everton, Manchester City and Bournemouth.
