Emmanuel Emegha has revealed that he was robbed after Chelsea’s home drubbing by Leeds on Saturday.

Emegha started his first Premier League game as the Blues suffered a heavy 4-1 defeat at Stamford Bridge, but was the victim of theft in the hours following the game.

According to recent reports, the Dutch striker confirmed the incident on Sunday, writing on Instagram: “Unfortunately after the game I was robbed. I am okay and thankfully no one was hurt. Thanks for the messages of support.”.

Chelsea sources told The Independent that the club is aware of the theft incident involving Emegha and that he has the full support of the club in this matter.

It is understood that the Metropolitan Police has not yet received a formal report regarding the incident and encourage Emegha to contact the authorities via the appropriate channels to allow an investigation to begin.

Emegha scored his first goal for the Blues against Leeds, brilliantly finishing at the near post to open the scoring in the first half before the tide turned heavily against Chelsea.

Noah Okafor equalised three minutes after Emegha’s striker before later scoring again as Leeds ran away with the game after the break, with Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Brendon Aaronson also netting scoring to condemn Chelsea to a damning loss.

Emegha joined Chelsea from sister club Strasbourg in the summer after a deal was agreed to bring the Dutchman to Stamford Bridge in September 2025.

He scored 31 goals in 73 appearances across all competitions in his three seasons in France before making the switch.
