California’s attorney general, Rob Bonta, noted on Thursday that his office has “served an investigative subpoena on Nvidia” that is related to reports that the chip-maker’s co-founder, Jensen Huang, had an affair with an alleged Chinese spy.

“The conduct of Nvidia and its former CEO raises bright red flags, including about the ways in which the firm may have misled investors. My office is investigating whether Nvidia or Jensen Huang’s actions resulted in China obtaining advanced chips or confidential information — and if Nvidia misled investors and the public about this risk, in violation of the law,” Bonta said in a statement. “Nvidia is in a messy, transnational situation but the underlying principle of our corporate securities law is clear: Companies cannot mislead their investors.”.
