The tentpole feature of iCloud+ is cloud storage, but Apple keeps improving and expanding the service to include other perks too. And in iOS 27, iCloud+ has a fresh benefit for high-storage tiers: AI features for HomeKit cameras.

With iOS 27, iCloud+ has added a new perk if you pay for more storage: Apple Intelligence features for compatible Home cameras. Per Apple’s website:.

With Apple Intelligence in the Home app, you can automatically generate video descriptions for recorded clips from compatible Home cameras. You can then search your recordings using natural language.

These AI-generated video descriptions provide a convenient summary of what’s happening in your home, let you browse notable clips at a glance, and search the camera’s recent history of recordings.

In a recent development, the price ladder requirement for the 12TB initiative in order to get 5 cameras is WAY TOO HIGH. I rather dedicate a local mac running some sort of AI model that can do this locally instead of paying that.

All of this is available with a camera that supports HomeKit Secure Video (I recommend this Aqara camera for $35). But because AI features depend on Private Cloud Compute, Apple only offers them on the following iCloud+ intends:.

Apple charges $9.99, $29.99, and $59.99/month for each of these prepares, respectively. So most users probably won’t find the monthly cost worth it for AI camera features alone. But if you already need a lot of iCloud storage, now you’ll get these fresh features thrown in too.

According to recent reports, note: these AI features have additional requirements too. You’ll need a home hub like a HomeView, Apple TV 4K, or HomePod, and your iPhone must support Apple Intelligence.
