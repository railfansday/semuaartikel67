Sources indicate that the AI safety conversation, as Lauren Goode put it, has lost the plot. Will the technology really kill us all? Should each one of us be worried about cyberattacks by rogue agents? Will it bring on total societal collapse? Though there’s room for disagreement about which catastrophe is most salient, experts are aligned on the idea that society is, for one reason or another, in real trouble. This begs one big question: What are industry leaders doing to mitigate the danger they’ve unleashed? Though a pause in training models, a data center moratorium, and other regulation has been proposed, the industry hasn’t exactly made changes that are putting people at ease around AI safety. Meanwhile, critical infrastructure seems more and more vulnerable every day.
