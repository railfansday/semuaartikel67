Sources indicate that bloom's pitch is profitable growth in a market where many chains chase room counts. Kedaara's $75 million will test whether that discipline survives as it scales up and leaves India for the first time.

It has been revealed that bloom, a  hotel brand in India with a user-oriented tech platform, aims to grow to 12,000 rooms over the next few years and to start exploring one or two Southeast Asian markets in the coming quarters. The plans follow a $75 million injection of funds to go straight into the business by  Kedaara Capital.

The company, which notes it has grown about 30% a year for the past five years, is now earning double-digit EBITDA margins as a percentage of revenue, chief product officer Tom Welbury told Skift. Bloom has remarked it expects to operate more than 4,700 rooms by the end of March 2027, when its financial year ends.
