Arriving in Cannes at just 15 years old, Zinedine Zidane helped write one of the most beautiful pages in the history of the Riviera club thanks to the fourth place achieved in the spring of 1991. Alas, the following season turned into a disaster between elimination in the round of 16 in the UEFA Cup and above all a descent to the second division. A relegation that looks like a godsend for the Girondins de Bordeaux as told to The Team its president at the time, Alain Afflelou.

In a recent development, “He had just turned 20. I had seen him two, three times briefly on Téléfoot. I find myself at the end of the 1992 season with Alain Pedretti, the president of Cannes who was going down to D2 while we were moving up with the Girondins, he explained. He needed to downsize because he had a large payroll. I needed to place players because we had a big squad. We make exchanges. ».

Among the most promising players of his generation, Zinedine Zidane inevitably arouses envy, particularly at Olympique de Marseille where Bernard Tapie holds him in very high esteem. However, the former Marseille president never called the native of Castellane and explained in 2016 that this failure constituted “the great regret of my life as president”. The way is therefore clear for the scapular club.

“Pedretti took Philippe Raschke, Rainer Ernst and Patrice Lestage from me. I had negotiated Éric Guérit and Jean-François Daniel. And I noted to him: ‘What are you doing with little Zidane? I saw in L’Équipe that Marseille and Tapie had offered you 8 million francs. He tells me it’s bullshit, continued Alain Afflelou. ‘How much do you want?’ He blurted out to me: ‘5 million francs’. » ».

In a recent development, bordeaux is preparing to do a huge deal. But as a wise businessman, Alain Afflelou is still negotiating to reduce the bill. “I am with Rolland (Courbis), my trainer. He kneed me under the table. I say it’s way too much. With Courbis, we made Pagnol! He blurted out: ‘Perhaps we can make an effort. I feel this boy has a future.’ They looked like two crooks! We took Guérit, Daniel and Zidane for 5 million while valuing Zizou at 3.5 million! “, he revealed.
