Sources indicate that hold your wedding somewhere that charges little or nothing for the space, such as a park hall, a community center, a restaurant’s private room, or a relative’s backyard. Then price everything the place doesn’t include, from chairs and cleanup to drinks and extra hours, before you book, since those extras decide what you pay.

The average reception venue cost $12,900 for couples married in 2025, according to The Knot’s 2026 Real Weddings Study.

Each kind of venue charges for something different, so its fee alone tells you little. The number to compare is the total, which is the fee plus whatever you’d rent, hire, or buy to cover what the venue leaves out.

Industry observers note that say you’re feeding 60 guests. A restaurant’s private room with no rental fee and a $4,000 minimum spend on food covers the room and the dinner for $4,000. A banquet hall that rents for $9,000 covers only the room, so the same dinner there costs $9,000 plus the caterer’s bill.

Each of the 15 venues below comes with one real place’s current price, what that price covers, and the catch.

Before you sign, ask each venue for a written list of what the fee covers, how many hours it includes, and what costs extra, from chairs and cleanup to insurance and permits.
